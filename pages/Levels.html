Levels

A 绝地潜兵's 目的 is to 完成 missions, earning 经验值 points that accumulate to unlock higher 等级 and 更大 奖励.

目录

  • 1 经验值
  • 2 Unlocks
    • 2.1 琐事
  • 3 变更历史

经验值

•  100 for completing 主要目标
•  50  for completing 侧面 目标
•  222  for completing the 摧毁 High-Value 目标 次要目标
•  20  for every surviving 绝地潜兵 onboard the 撤离 运输船.
  ◦ Additionally, the 剩余 任务 timer 百分比 is converted into 经验值. (e.g. 50% 任务 time 剩余 will reward  50)
• Destroying nests, 哨站，或 encampments 奖励:
  ◦  5  for 小型 ones
  ◦  10 for 中型 ones
  ◦  20 for large ones
  ◦  40 for mega nests or fortresses

After a 任务 is completed (无论 success or failure) the total gained will be tallied, then multiplied according to the selected 难度。 倍率 starts at +0% for 简单 up to +300% for 超级绝地潜兵难度.

Unlocks

Early levels provide access to various 战略配备, and every fifth level (or tenth above lvl 50), the 绝地潜兵 will rank up, earning one of 21 titles, which can be equipped under 军械库 → Character → 标题.
