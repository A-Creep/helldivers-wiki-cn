Bug Mine

When stepped on by a 生物 or otherwise damaged, it explodes and releases 酸, a damaging and slowing 效果.

Ice Plant

When stepped on by a 生物 or otherwise damaged, it explodes into a temporary stasis cloud which also deals mild 伤害. Note that only 绝地潜兵 get slowed, other creatures are stunned while in the slowing field.

级别: Flora

Moor Mushroom

On 联络, Releases smoke cloud. On 摧毁, Launches nearby creatures

级别: Flora

Shrub

Slows any 生物 or 绝地潜兵 that moves through it.

级别: Flora

Spike Plant

On 联络, Slows, drains 耐力, and damages creatures. On 摧毁: Knocks back nearby creatures and 施加 the 流血 status 效果

级别: Flora

Emits flammable 毒气. If shot, the 毒气 it emits bursts into flames.
