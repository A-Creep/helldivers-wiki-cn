the 绝地潜兵 Training Facilities is a 超级地球 建筑 on Zegema Paradise.

背景故事

Established on 2185-07-08, this 绝地潜兵 Training 设施 was built to replace the former training 设施 on Mars, which was destroyed during the 超级地球保卫战.

After temporarily being placed at an Undisclosed 位置 during the Battle for 超级地球. Zegema Paradise became the new home for the 绝地潜兵 Training 设施. Responsible for the training of new recruits, all 绝地潜兵 are 必需 to 完成 basic training to earn their cape and 超级驱逐舰.

Once the 设施 was fully functional, the amount of successful recruits 已增加 dramatically, leading to a higher amount of 绝地潜兵 entering the force than those exiting. This resulted in Strategic Order A2-8-4, which led 绝地潜兵 to construct an Element-710 farm on Crimsica. This resulted in a historic 扩展包 of the 超级驱逐舰 fleet, reducing the surplus of in-stasis 绝地潜兵.
