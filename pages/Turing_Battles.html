Background[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

绝地潜兵 received a new 主要命令, it tasked 绝地潜兵 with activating TCS (终结族控制系统). After they secured Fenrir III, 绝地潜兵 decided to 目标 Turing as the 下一个 行星 that will have a working TCS.

策略 and Importance[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

This 战斗 was 重要, as Turing was one of the planets that were a 目标 of a 重要指令.

战斗[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

When Fenrir III was liberated, around 200k 绝地潜兵 were establishing TCS on this 行星 at the same 时间! The 重要 部分 of the 战斗 (that occurred after Fenrir III was liberated) lasted only around 24 hours, then the 行星 was liberated.

Background[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Phase I of 操作 Legitimate Undertaking has failed, but the 终结族 正面 was stable. Suddenly, TCS has failed. 终结族 started to rapidly reproduce, infesting Meridia. Turing could still be rescued.

策略 and Importance[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

The 战斗 was 确实 重要 as it determind if Turing potentially turned into a Super 拓殖地, 点赞 Meridia.

战斗[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

The 战斗 ended on 3rd of 5月, around 50k 绝地潜兵 were deactivating TCS at the same 时间. Around 12 million 绝地潜兵 died during this 战斗.

Turing was saved, avoiding the fate of Meridia.

Background[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

在……上的科学家 Moradesh were working on a new 武器, called Dark Fluid. In 命令 to utilize this 武器, The 绝地潜兵 had to make sure the first phase of 操作 Enduring Peace would succeed. In 命令 to do that, Fenrir III, 天使's Venture and Turing had to be secured.

策略 and Importance[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Turing was 总是显示 a strategically 重要 行星. Located in the south 部分 of Umlaut 分区, it granted access to 天使's Venture and Acamar IV. Also, it was now a 部分 of 重要指令.

战斗[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

The 战斗 ended on 28th of 5月, with the Turing being liberated. The Dark Fluid was ready to be used against 终结族.

Background[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Turing was attacked by 终结族 attempting to escape the Meridia Super 拓殖地 recently. Fortunetly, the 行星 they were escaping from got destroyed. 绝地潜兵 could now focus on cleaning the mess that Super 拓殖地 left in the Umlaut and 锦栖分区s.

策略 and Importance[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Turing is a gateway to Jin Xi 分区, it's the only 行星 that connects there. Liberating this 行星 would 剪切 off Acamar IV. Turing was now also a 部分 of 重要指令.

战斗[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

The 战斗 ended on 4th of 6月. 行星 was successfully liberated and now 绝地潜兵 拆分, and focused on Erata Prime and Acamar IV.
