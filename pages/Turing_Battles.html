图灵上TCS的建立

图灵上TCS的建立
阵营 终结族
Start Date Mar. 12th
End Date Mar. 14th
行星 Turing
操作 操作 英勇 Enclosure
Group Establishment of TCS
Casualties Around 12 million 绝地潜兵 K.I.A.
重要指令 英勇围拢行动：第三阶段
Summary TCS on Turing established.


背景

绝地潜兵 received a new 主要命令, it tasked 绝地潜兵 with activating TCS (终结族控制系统). After they secured Fenrir III, 绝地潜兵 decided to 目标 Turing as the 下一个 行星 that will have a working TCS.

战略与重要性

This battle was 重要, as Turing was one of the planets that were a 目标 of a 重要指令.

战斗

When Fenrir III was liberated, around 200k 绝地潜兵 were establishing TCS on this 行星 at the same time! The 重要 part of the battle (that occurred after Fenrir III was liberated) lasted only around 24 hours, then the 行星 was liberated.

图灵上TCS的停用

图灵上TCS的停用
阵营 终结族
Start Date Apr. 29th
End Date May. 3rd
行星 Turing
操作
Group Deactivation of TCS
Casualties Around 12 million 绝地潜兵 K.I.A.
重要指令 关闭终结族遏制体系
Summary TCS on Turing deactivated.


背景

Phase I of 操作 Legitimate Undertaking has failed, but the 终结族 正面 was stable. Suddenly, TCS has failed. 终结族 started to rapidly reproduce, infesting Meridia. Turing could still be rescued.

战略与重要性

The battle was really 重要 as it determind if Turing potentially turned into a Super Colony, like Meridia.

战斗

The battle ended on 3rd of May, around 50k 绝地潜兵 were deactivating TCS at the same time. Around 12 million 绝地潜兵 died during this battle.

Turing was saved, avoiding the fate of Meridia.

图灵第三次战役——持久和平

Third Battle of Turing
阵营 终结族
Start Date May. 27th (Major Part of the battle, the minor part started a couple of days before)
End Date May. 28th
行星 Turing
操作 操作 "Enduring Peace"
Group
Casualties Around 20 million 绝地潜兵 K.I.A.
重要指令 操作 "Enduring Peace" - Phase 1
Summary Turing liberated.


背景

在……上的科学家 Moradesh were working on a new weapon, called Dark Fluid. In order to utilize this weapon, The 绝地潜兵 had to make sure the first phase of 操作 Enduring Peace would succeed. In order to do that, Fenrir III, Angel's Venture and Turing had to be secured.

战略与重要性

Turing was always a strategically 重要 行星. Located in the south part of Umlaut 分区, it granted access to Angel's Venture and Acamar IV. Also, it was now a part of 重要指令.

战斗

The battle ended on 28th of May, with the Turing being liberated. The Dark Fluid was ready to be used against 终结族.

图灵第四次战役——清理残局

Fourth Battle of Turing

Start Date Jun. 3rd
End Date Jun. 4th
行星 Turing
操作
Group
Casualties
重要指令 Clear Post-Supercolony Infestations
Summary Turing liberated.


背景

Turing was attacked by 终结族 attempting to escape the Meridia Super Colony recently. Fortunetly, the 行星 they were escaping from got destroyed. 绝地潜兵 could now focus on cleaning the mess that Super Colony left in the Umlaut 和 锦栖分区s.

战略与重要性

Turing is a gateway to Jin Xi 分区, it's the only 行星 that connects there. Liberating this 行星 would cut off Acamar IV. Turing was now also a part of 重要指令.

战斗

The battle ended on 4th of June. 行星 was successfully liberated and now 绝地潜兵 split, and focused on Erata Prime and Acamar IV.
