the 感知干扰器 is an 光能者 建筑. For as long as it is standing, it obscures the names and randomizes the inputs of all 战略配备s for 绝地潜兵 within 200 meters of the Disruptor. To 完成 the 目标, 绝地潜兵 must disable the 建筑. This is primarily achieved by destroying the power taps that the 光能者 have placed on nearby structures.

目标步骤

  • 摧毁 光能者 感知干扰器.
    • 绝地潜兵 must 摧毁 感知干扰器 PSUs placed on nearby generators to disable it.

Disclaimer: 每个带数量的高亮部位 a 数量有各自的 生命值. 未标注数量的部位共享 生命值 pools.

1) 生命值标注为 标注为 "主体" 没有各自的 individual 生命值. All 造成伤害 to these parts is 转移到敌人的 主生命值 pool.
2) 请参阅 护甲 values and enemy 耐久度 as defined in 伤害 for more information.
3) 造成伤害 to a part will also be 部分或全部转移 in full to 主生命值. 若设置为 0%, 否 伤害 is transferred.
4) 若设置为 "是", 伤害 transferred 对主血量 is capped by the combined 生命值 and 构造 of the part.
5) 构造 充当 a secondary 生命池 that, 一旦主体或肢体 生命值 has been depleted, 可能会衰减. The first number is the total 构造 and the second is the 衰退速率. 若设置为 0/s 不会衰减 and the 构造 作为额外 生命值.
6) 爆炸抗性 means 爆炸 伤害抗性, and can 射程 from 0 to 100%. 若设置为 100%, the limb cannot take 爆炸伤害; The 爆炸伤害 will instead 目标 主体. 请参阅 伤害 for more information.
  • Note: This 机械师, known as Affected By 爆炸 can only occur once per 爆炸, 无论 100% 爆炸抗性 parts hit, and the 爆炸's 护甲穿透 must match or exceed 主体护甲 to deal 伤害 对主血量. However, 如果相同 爆炸 has already damaged 主体, this 机械师 will not trigger.


战术信息

  • It's 不可能 to 摧毁 the 感知干扰器 itself with any 支援武器 or 战略配备.
  • 轨道武器 Barrages can easily 摧毁 the 感知干扰器 PSUs when thrown towards it.
    • ex. 轨道武器 Napalm 弹幕, 轨道武器 120mm HE 弹幕，以及 轨道武器 380mm HE 弹幕.
  • The randomized codes caused by the Disruptor are not random in the sense that each individual arrow is randomized; rather, there is a list of sequences to draw from. This list not only includes other 战略配备 inputs, but also many humorous ones, such as a code comprised entirely of left inputs or even the 臭名昭著 Konami Code.
