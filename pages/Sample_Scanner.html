Sample 扫描仪
“

The Sample 扫描仪 gives you a 15% chance to 接收 twice the Samples on sample pick up.

— 军械库 描述

Sample 扫描仪 is a 增益 in 绝地潜兵 2. The Sample 扫描仪 gives you a 15% chance to 接收 twice the Samples on sample pick up. It affects all sample rarities.

获取途径

the Sample 扫描仪 强化资源 is unlocked on the 3rd page of the Masters of 仪式 尊享战争债券 for 65 奖章.

战术信息

  • 这个增益是 效果 that's active throughout a 任务, which allows a 绝地潜兵-squad to obtain more samples than the 最大 生成 number.
  • While this 强化资源 is not viable on every deployment, especially for missions that do not contain samples, such as the 撤离高价值资产 任务, it is beneficial if the squad has 绝地潜兵 who need to farm them for 超级驱逐舰 升级.
    • In terms of the 星系战争, it is beneficial for Sample MOs, where an 增加 in their 合集 is 重要 in progressing it. An additional 10 samples can mean a three-任务 操作 can potentially yield the same amount as four* missions. (Depending on the 难度, the extra amount varies)
