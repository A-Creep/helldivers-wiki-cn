常规黑洞

The 常规黑洞 is a completely normal and stable black hole created by the firing of the Star of Peace.

It is the second means by which 超级地球 has used to 摧毁 an entire 行星, the first being the 行星 of Meridia via injections of Dark Fluid.

背景故事

The first 常规黑洞 was created after Penta was fired upon by the Star of Peace at the end of 操作 Free Space following a 48 hour period of voting in which the 绝地潜兵 selected Penta as the 目标 of choice.

The other two choices were Valmox and Zagon Prime.

琐事

  • In a Strohmann News 广播 on a test 火焰 of the Star of Peace, a 小型 moon was shrunk down to the size of a pebble instead of becoming a black hole like Penta would 4 days later.
