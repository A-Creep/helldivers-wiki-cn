详情[edit | edit 来源]

  • Removes all of 耐力 on start
  • Stuns creatures for the entire 持续时间
    • Stunning 效果 only slow 绝地潜兵
  • Tremors do not affect the entire map, and instead occur in a large area around a random player.

Affected Biomes[edit | edit 来源]

  • Desert Cliffs
  • Rocky Canyons
