地震

地震 – Frequent 地震 stun 玩家 and 敌人 alike.

详情

  • Removes all of 耐力 on start
  • Stuns creatures for the entire 持续时间
    • Stunning 效果 only slow 绝地潜兵
  • 地震 do not affect the entire map, and instead occur in a large area around a random player.

Affected Biomes

  • Desert Cliffs
  • Rocky Canyons
