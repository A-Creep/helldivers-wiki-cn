the 巨型者 Bruiser is a 重型 机器人 equipped with a Rotary Munition 发射器 on its left arm, and a Fusion 机炮 on its 正确 arm.

生成[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

巨型者 Bruisers can be first found on 挑战. 巨型者 Bruisers can spawn from 机器人空投, within patrols, guarding 机器人 哨站, 目标, and Minor Places of Interest，或从 Bulk Fabricators.

If a 星球 is invaded by the   喷气 Brigade, its 变体 will be equipped with a 喷射背包.

巨型者 Bruisers do not appear on planets invaded by the   Incineration Corps, and are instead replaced by 巨型者 Firebombers.

行为[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Upon noticing a 绝地潜兵, 巨型者 Bruisers will 任一 火焰 their Fusion 机炮 or Rotary 发射器. The Fusion 机炮 fires a 20 回合 齐射 and the Rotary 发射器 fires a seven 火箭 齐射. They will 火焰 from a 距离 if a 绝地潜兵 is in their line of sight. 巨型者 Bruisers can 提升 and slam one of their 手臂 if a 绝地潜兵 gets too 关闭 to them. If they are missing their 手臂, 巨型者 Bruisers can perform a 践踏 with their leg.

战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

巨型者[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Heavily armed and 有护甲, Hulks are powerful 敌人 that demand 特殊 attention due to their 耐久度 and 重型 武器 载荷, as they are capable of killing 绝地潜兵 in 短 命令.
  • Hulks are covered in  重型 护甲, but they do have two 弱 spots: the glowing 高温 sink on their backs which are  无护甲 II, and their 头部, which only has 250 生命值 and will 击杀 a 巨型者 if destroyed.
    • A Hulks vents can be damaged by 任何 武器, but are rather 耐久. It is advised to use 更高-伤害 支援武器 to 更多 easily 摧毁 the vents. Destroying its vents will 原因 the 巨型者 to go into a bleedout 陈述, where they can be seen hobbling and flailing its 武器.
    • the RS-422 磁轨炮, APW-1 反器材步枪, and S-11 Speargun are capable of destroying its 头部 with a 单发.
  • The 巨型者's slow 移动速度 make them particularly 易受 dedicated 反坦克武器 such as the EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器, LAS-99 类星体加农炮, and GR-8 无后坐力炮 and are capable of destroying a 巨型者 in a 单发.
    • Due to their poor mobility, Hulks can also be easily destroyed by 最多 进攻型 爆炸 战略配备 such as the 飞鹰 500kg 炸弹, “飞鹰”机枪扫射, and 飞鹰 110mm 火箭巢.
    • 单个 G-123 铝热弹 will inflict enough 伤害 to 摧毁 a 巨型者 否 matter where on its 身体 it is placed.
  • While 更多 耐久 than its 弱 spots, the 巨型者's limbs can 经常 be 有效 targets for 摧毁 depending on the situation.
    • The 巨型者's arm joints can be 射击 and destroyed, causing them to 掉落 that arm's respective 武器. 一次 两者 手臂 are destroyed, Hulks attempt to 践踏 on 绝地潜兵 instead.
    • The 巨型者's 腿部 can also be 射击 and destroyed; destroying one leg cripples its movement, and destroying 两者 腿部 will 击杀 the 巨型者.
      • The 巨型者's 腿部 are 经常 许多 更多 convenient targets than its 头部 or 高温 sinks. Destroying a leg will 硬直 it for 几个 秒 and significantly slow its 移动速度.

琐事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The 巨型者 appears to be inspired by various Imperial Dreadnoughts used by 太空 Marines in Warhammer 40,000.
  • Oddly, 巨型者 Bruisers 火焰 their rockets out of what appears to be the 巨型者 Scorcher's 火焰喷射器.
  • 巨型者 Bruisers after 补丁 1.001.100 received an 能量 加农炮, but were reverted 返回 to a 火箭发射器 in the following 补丁 1.001.104. According to the 更新日志, a 绝地潜兵 by the 名称 of "John 绝地潜兵" was responsible for sabotaging their 生产.
    • "John 绝地潜兵" is a popular 名称 in the 绝地潜兵 community originating from the John Darksoul meme, which popularized naming protagonists as "John (游戏 系列 名称)." The same 更新日志 also 参考 the opening 引文 of the A-团队 电视 系列.
