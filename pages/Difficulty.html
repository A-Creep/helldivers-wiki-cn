难度

When selecting a 任务 in 绝地潜兵2 using the 星系战争 控制台, a 难度 can be selected between level 1 简单 and level 10 超级绝地潜兵难度.

Each 难度 level offers a different set of missions. For 示例, some missions offered to the player on 简单 are not offered on 极难.

效果

  • The number of 奖章, 申购点 Slips，以及 经验值 you earn from a 任务 all depends on the 难度 you select.
    • Higher 难度 missions have increasing 百分比 multipliers for 申购点 Slips and 经验值, from +25% on 中型 to +300% on 超级绝地潜兵难度
    • The Eradication 主要目标 base-reward depends on the selected 难度. Each 难度 affects the 奖励 by 20 和 100. E.g. 极难 Eradication-任务奖励 120 和 600，以及 中型 奖励 60 和 300.
  • Enemy forces are enhanced to increasingly 致命 degrees.
    • The number of 敌人 and the 类型 of enemy both depend on the 难度 level set.
    • The 频率 of enemy patrol being spawned 增加 alongside the 难度.
      • In 关系, the 频率 of DSS 鹰式打击 and other tactical actions during missions 减少 with 已增加 难度.
  • The number of 操作 Modifiers present during the 任务.
    • One 修正 is added on 困难.
    • A second 修正 is added on 不可能.
  • The number of Sub-目标 必需 to 完成 a 主要目标 增加 at higher difficulties.
  • The number and size of enemy 哨站 (Bug Nests/机器人哨站/光能者 Encampments)
    • Outpost 生成 can vary quite drastically depending on a number of other factors as well, including 生物群系, 任务 类型, and 阵营.
  • The number of Optional 目标 增加 at higher difficulties.
  • Map size 增加 to accommodate the 已增加 amount of 任务 目标 on the 远征-任务. The size 增加 is lesser on the Metropolis 生物群系 maps.
    • As 难度 增加 once 最大 map-size has been achieved, the 任务 目标 occupy spaces which otherwise might have had Minor Places of Interest being places upon.
  • The number and 类型 of Samples able to be found depends on the set 难度.
    • Commons appear on all difficulties, while Rares first appear on 挑战，以及 Supers on 极难 难度.
    • The 类型 of samples available during an 操作 can be confirmed on the 星系战争 控制台 when selecting an 操作.
