概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Colonies are 大, randomized, grid based biomes. Their 网络 of streets 帮助 连接 a variety of different buildings, parks, and monuments. On missions where a 拓殖地 spawns, at 最少 one 目标, such an an 敌人 encampment, sub 目标, or 主要目标, will be found inside a 拓殖地.

Colonies appear different based on the presiding 阵营:

  • 光能者 occupation leaves the colonies themselves largely untouched, 拯救 for the occasional 建筑 or makeshift 营地 containing parked Warp Ships.
  • 机器人 occupation leaves buildings razed, covering the streets in soot and debris. 机器人 iconography, red 轻型 fixtures, and Bulk 构筑者 哨站 reaffirm their 控制 over the 拓殖地.
  • 终结族 infestation pervades 每个 crevice of a 拓殖地. Roads are overgrown and structures are covered in 终结族 nesting 材质. 大 holes have been dug into the 地面 and the sides of buildings to 形成 Bug Nests.

The perimeter of 每个 城市 is guarded by a 厚, destructible 墙, 偶尔 interrupted by Checkpoint structures. These walls 5月 be destroyed by 爆破 之势 40 攻击, such as the “飞鹰”空袭 and 轨道精准攻击.

Colonies 优惠 predictable geometry and sightlines, but 经常 forces combat encounters to stay in the 关闭 to 中距离. If a substantial 数量 of the 任务 will take place in a 拓殖地, it can be 重要 to 计划 one's 武装配置 accordingly.

  • FRVs can naturally generate inside Colonies, 经常 生成 at 超级地球武装部队 SAM Sites, 超级地球武装部队 大炮，或 Cognitive Disruptors.
  • Their 许多 unique structures and 次要兴趣点s tend to 商店 generous amounts of 弹药, grenades, stims, and other supplies.
