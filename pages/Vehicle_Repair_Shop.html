the 载具 Repair Shop is a 中型 human 建筑, which can be found during missions as a Minor Place of Interest. It consists of a large sign, a garage with 物资, a SEAF 战士 corpse and a Vending Machine, a second garage with a parking space that has a wrecked 载具 (presumably destroyed by a meteorite, crater of which是生成 point of a rare sample), a backyard with lots of spare tires, a few metal drums, more 物资 and 3 SEAF 战士 corpses, and a hidden Cargo 容器 inside the 巨石 formation.
