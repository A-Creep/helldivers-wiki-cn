the 发射洲际导弹 is a 任务 which tasks 绝地潜兵 to activate/回收 various items/structures to launch an ICBM. At 中型 it gains the "回收 Launch Codes" sub-目标, and at 困难 it gains the "reactivate power" sub-目标. All sub-目标 must be done in order to start the 主要目标.

It also exist as an 可选目标 in 特殊 missions on planets located in The Gloom, though only the 发射洲际导弹 step is available regardless of 难度.

取回发射代码[edit | edit 来源]

“

存有导弹发射代码的磁盘遗落在该地区的一座基地里。

— 目标 概述

  • Find the 困难 disk containing the Launch Codes.
    • 绝地潜兵 must go to the Human 建筑 (3 variations; Excavation, Junkyard, ) and find the Launch Codes. It is a blue-ish, big rectangle. It emits a constant sound of static and morse code, getting louder the closer the 绝地潜兵 is to the Disk containing the Codes.
  • The Disk containing the Launch Codes
  • One of the Launch Codes site variations
  • Launch Codes are located at the top of that variation

重新启动发电机[edit | edit 来源]

“

启动这台发电机可以为超级地球武装部队在该地区的设备提供急需的能源。

— 目标 概述

This Sub-目标 only occurs on 困难 or higher

  • 启动终端
    • 绝地潜兵 must interact with the 终端 to activate it.
  • 登录终端
    • 绝地潜兵 must interact with the 终端, then 类型 in the 6 方向-long password
  • 请等待发电机诊断程序完成
    • It takes 15 秒 for the 顺序 to 完成
  • 使用终端重置熔断器
    • 绝地潜兵 must reset all 5 fuses by checking 5 boxes in a similar manner to the SEAF SAM Site
  • 使用终端，校准发电机电压
    • 绝地潜兵 must align the signal to reach 100% 力量, similar to a SEAF 大炮 or Terminate 非法 广播 目标
  • Wait for 发电机 to calibrate
    • It takes 30 秒 for the 发电机 to calibrate
  • 拉动变压器单元操纵杆
    • 绝地潜兵 must move to the 变压器 and interact with the large lever
    • There are large, yellow cables along the ground leading to the 变压器, along with a bright red 轻型 indicating the 位置 of the lever.
  • A 发电机 sub-目标
  • A 发电机's 变压器

启动燃料泵[edit | edit 来源]

“

This fueling 车站 is 离线. It must be reactivated before to initiate fuel 分布.

— 目标 概述

This Sub-目标 only occurs on 不可能 or higher, 偶尔 replacing the Reactivate Power 发电机 sub-目标. However, on 绝地潜兵难度 and above, all 3 sub-目标 will have to be completed.

  • 启动终端
    • 绝地潜兵 must interact with the 终端 to activate it.
  • 登录终端
    • 绝地潜兵 must interact with the 终端, then 类型 in the 7 方向-long password
  • Redirect fuel to 目标
    • 绝地潜兵 must then adjust the three valves located around the fueling 车站 to the right until they glow; the 终端 will also make a loud beep sound to 确认 a valve has been adjusted correctly.
  • 按照终端的提示，调整所有阀门
    • 绝地潜兵 must then adjust the three valves located around the fueling 车站; the valves will turn from a red to yellow 轻型 and the 终端 will make a loud beep sound to 确认 a valve has been adjusted correctly.
    • After correctly turning all valves, 绝地潜兵 must check a box on the 终端 to 确认
  • Redirect fuel to 目标 step

发射洲际导弹[edit | edit 来源]

“

这枚核导弹能以极快的速度击中超远距离外的目标，不给敌人撤退或反击的机会。

— 目标 概述

  • 启动终端
    • 绝地潜兵 must interact with the 终端 to activate it.
  • 登录导弹控制系统
    • 绝地潜兵 must interact with the 终端, then 类型 in the 7 方向-long password
  • Input launch code: (launch code)
    • 绝地潜兵 must interact with the 终端 if not already, then use the directional keys to flip the 5 digit containers to the 正确 number displayed in the 目标 step.
  • 手动解除所有发射井舱门锁
    • 绝地潜兵 must go down to the hatch and manually release all 4 hatch locks by interacting with each one.
  • 使用终端，打开发射井舱门
    • 绝地潜兵 must interact with the 终端 if not already, then using the up 方向 指挥 the 终端 to open the hatch.
  • 洲际弹道导弹正在抬升至发射位置
    • It takes 60 秒 for the ICBM to be fully raised into launch position.
  • 使用终端，锁定目标坐标
    • 绝地潜兵 must interact with the 终端 to select designated 坐标 in the grid using directional inputs. After the cursor moves over the 目标 coordinate, 绝地潜兵 must not move the cursor away from the 目标 coordinate until it has finished calculating.
  • 正在为导弹添加燃料
    • It takes 30 秒 for the 导弹 to be fully fueled.
  • 使用终端，下令发射
    • 绝地潜兵 must finally interact with the 终端, then use the up 方向 to trigger the 10 second 倒计时 until launch.
  • 洲际弹道导弹即将发射
    • 绝地潜兵 are advised to clear the launch area lest they take 伤害 or be ragdolled from the ICBM launch.
  • 任务 Completed: 发射洲际导弹
    • 绝地潜兵 are rewarded with 500 申购点 Slips and 100 经验值, to be awarded after the end of the 任务.
    • 绝地潜兵 are advised to look into the 方向 of the 导弹's 目标, and enjoy the following scenery for 大约 2.4 秒 before continuing to 撤离.
  • Raised ICBM
  • A loyal 绝地潜兵 enjoying their mandatory 2.4 秒 of the fruits of their work
