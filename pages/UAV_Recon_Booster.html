UAV Recon 强化资源
“

扩大所有绝地潜兵的雷达有效范围。

— 军械库 描述

UAV Recon 强化资源 is a 增益 in 绝地潜兵 2. When equipped, all 绝地潜兵 will have 已增加 有效 radar 射程, allowing for easier recon of 地形.

目录

  • 2 获取途径
  • 3 战术信息
  • 4 变更历史

描述

the UAV Recon 强化资源 will 增加 radar 距离 by 大约 50%, giving every 绝地潜兵 a 更大 scanning 射程. This scanning 射程 is shown on the mini-map, which allows 绝地潜兵 to locate patrolling 敌人 faster. These scans are not 可见 to fellow squadmates, so coordination is essential in alerting fellow 绝地潜兵.

获取途径

the UAV Recon 强化资源 is unlocked on the 6th page of the 绝地潜兵 Mobilize 战争债券 for 40 Medals.

战术信息

  • the UAV Recon 强化资源 强化资源 is suited for Stealth 战术, where a 绝地潜兵 is trying to avoid detection.
    • This is further supplemented by using the 侦察盔甲被动, as 绝地潜兵 are harder to detect and can see 敌人 locations by marking a position on the map.
  • When using 侦察 or 不动摇 护甲 passives, radar blips produced by markers placed on the 小地图 are affected by this 强化资源.
  • This 强化资源 will not work in missions with the Atmospheric Spores 操作 修正.
