First Battle of Heeth - Preparations for setting up TCS

First Battle of Heeth
阵营 终结族
Start Date Feb. 8th
End Date Feb. 10th
行星 Heeth
操作 操作 英勇 Enclosure
Group
Casualties Around 5.5 million 绝地潜兵 K.I.A.
重要指令 操作 英勇 Enclosure Phase I
Summary Heeth liberated


背景

终结族 have infested Orion 分区 recently. 超级地球's scientists have created a substance called Termicide, Termicide towers were ready to be activated on 屏障 Planets. To do this, 绝地潜兵 had to liberate Orion 分区 first.

战略与重要性

这场战斗对操作 英勇 Enclosure的推进十分重要，此外，赢得这场战斗意味着终结族将被推回屏障 Planets。

战斗

战斗于2月8日开始，当时重要指令启动。 绝地潜兵 have liberated Heeth on 10th of 2月, which allowed Angel's Venture to 接收 more 增援. Around 5.5 million 绝地潜兵 died during this battle. On 14th of 2月, 绝地潜兵 liberated Angel's Venture, securing Orion 分区.

希斯第二战役——遏制子午线孢子

Second Battle of Heeth
阵营 终结族
Start Date May. 6th
End Date May. 7th
行星 Heeth
操作
Group
Casualties Around 5.5 million 绝地潜兵 K.I.A.
重要指令 Contain Supercolony Outbreaks
Summary Heeth liberated


背景

Recently, TCS on Erata Prime, Fenrir III and Turing was deactivated by 绝地潜兵. Unfortunetly, it was too late to rescue Meridia, because it turned into a Super Colony, that was producing much more spores than other infested planets. Meridian Spores started to infest planets in Orion and Jin XI 星区.

战略与重要性

Orion 分区 was once again in 危险. It is a strategically key 分区, as it directly accesses Celeste 分区, that is just 下一个 to Sol System. 绝地潜兵 decided to focus on 防御 of Heeth.

战斗

The Battle started on 6th of May, 终结族 attacked Veld, Heeth, Acamar IV and Angel's Venture. 绝地潜兵 successfully liberated the 行星. Around 5.5 million 绝地潜兵 died during this battle, it ended on 7th of May.

希斯第三战役——训练设施防御战

Third Battle of Heeth
阵营 终结族
Start Date May. 16th
End Date May. 17th
行星 Heeth
操作
Group
Casualties 1.5 million 绝地潜兵 K.I.A.
重要指令 Hold Planets for SEAF Conscription
Summary


背景

Bilions of citizens across the galaxy were recruited to SEAF. They had to be trained quickly in order to be ready to deploy as fast as possible. Heeth was a 完美 行星 for setting up these facilities. Unfortunetly, 终结族 launched an 特攻 on the 行星.

战略与重要性

绝地潜兵 had to immediately deploy to Heeth, in order to hold the recruitment facilities. At the same time Vernen Wells was attacked, but 绝地潜兵 managed to effectively split between these 2 planets.

战斗

战斗于5月16日开始。
