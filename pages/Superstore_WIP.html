超级商店/WIP

the 超级商店 is an 物品 商店 in 绝地潜兵 2 accessible through the 获取 Center. In it 护甲 sets, 武器, and Cosmetics based on various 战争债券 plus an extra 战马 套装 can be bought using 超级货币. 超级商店 items are exclusive to the 商店 and are unobtainable through the 战争债券 themselves.

The 超级商店 will have one rotating 页面 featuring six different 内容 sets that changes 每个 48 hours, shortening the rotation to 12 days. 全部 the earlier 超级商店 items from older 战争债券 are available to 购买 anytime. When new 战争债券 are released, one of the 当前 rotating sets will be moved to a permanent 页面.
