“

The 终结族 on this 行星 are mutating in 回应 to the Termicide. With every passing minute, they threaten to turn this world in to another Supercolony.
歼灭 the mutated 终结族 eggs and deactivate the corrupted 终结族控制系统 before its too late.

— 任务 Briefing

Deactivate 终结族控制系统 is a 任务 that was released during its namesake Event and is exclusive to the 屏障 planets of the Umlaut 分区 for a 有限 time. It 使用次数 modified versions of the Activate 终结族控制系统 map layouts. The 任务 contains two Main 目标 that can be completed in any order: Deactivate Termicide Tower and 歼灭 Mutated Nests.

绝地潜兵 must quickly deactivate the 终结族控制系统 to prevent further 行星 infestation and 终结族 变异, in addition to destroying some of the eggs surrounding the towers.

目标 Steps[edit | edit 来源]

关闭灭虫终结塔[edit | edit 来源]

  • Deactivate corrupted 电池 silos [0/3]
    • 绝地潜兵 have to go to the 3 spots marked on the map and interact with a button on each 位置.
    • Pressing the button will cause Bug 突破 to continuously occur until 撤离 is available.

彻底摧毁变异巢穴[edit | edit 来源]

  • 摧毁 mutated 终结族 eggs [0/17]
    • 绝地潜兵 have to find 庞大 eggs around the maps and 摧毁 them.
    • Notably, the 弧度-3 电弧发射器 and the 弧度-12 Blitzer are both unable to 摧毁 the eggs.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • When destroyed, the mutated eggs will knock back and 伤害 绝地潜兵 within 10 meters.
  • A 更小 version of the 孢子喷涌虫 建筑 spawns within the area, creating fog of slightly less dense than its larger counterpart. These 小型 喷吐者 can be destroyed to remove the fog
  • Minor Place of Interest can potentially spawn on the map; this notably includes the Super Sample Rock on 自杀任务 and above.
