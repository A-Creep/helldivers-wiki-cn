Assisted 换弹 Rework

问题 we want to solve:

  • The original intent was a tactical action that needed 协作 with a designated reloader that carries the backpack
  • We have reevaluated how this works and with how the game has evolved since launch we reached the conclusion that the assisted 换弹 action requires too much coordination which leads to it rarely happening

Intent and expectation with the change:

  • We want to reduce the complexity with assisted reloading to 推广 using it more.
  • Allow 玩家 to keep their backpack and 换弹 whenever you need, while also allows a teammate to easily help you if the opportunity arises.

The change we made:

  • 绝地潜兵 can now assist 换弹 as long as one of the wielder and/or reloader of the weapon has the 正确 backpack

Balancing

Updated icons for backpack 武器 and expendable 武器 问题 we want to solve:

  • Finding a specific weapon in the 武装配置 can be 困难 to do with all the new 武器 added to the arsenal
  • It can be 困难 to know if a 支援武器 needs a backpack or cannot be reloaded when only looking at the icon.

Intent and expectation with the change:

  • We want to make it easier to find specific 武器 and to plan your 武装配置.
  • It should be easier to know when a weapon needs a backpack or cannot be reloaded when only looking at the icon.

The change we made:

  • 战略配备 武器 that 要求 a backpack now have a backpack icon included on their 武装配置 icons
  • 战略配备 武器 that cannot be reloaded now have a 掉落 weapon icon included on their 武装配置 icons.

已减少 重型 landing 持续时间 问题 we want to solve:

  • The original intent was to make the 重型 landing feel like you recovered from a high fall.
  • The player lost 控制 for a long time when landing on their feet from a higher fall.

Intent and expectation with the change:

  • We wanted to make it smoother to 恢复 from the 重型 landing state and not remove the 控制 for too long.
  • While still making it feel like a 重型 landing 恢复

The change we made:

  • 已减少 the 持续时间 for the 重型 landing 动画 by around 40%

Stim action input smoothing 问题 we want to solve:

  • 玩家 can 经验值 dead input when trying to stim when the stim action was not allowed.

Intent and expectation with the change:

  • We want to lessen the feeling of dead input when using the stim action.

The change we made:

  • We 已更改 so the stim input is remembered for a short 持续时间 when the action is not allowed and will be executed if the stim action is allowed within that 持续时间.
  • This works similarly to how the 换弹 input action 变更 from the last 补丁.

弹药 re-平衡 for Short magazines 配件 and Secondaries 问题 we want to solve:

  • The benefits of the short magazines are too few compared to drum magazines.
  • The benefits of some of the anti-personnel sidearms do not have enough benefits compared to other 副武器 options.

Intent and expectation with the change:

  • Give more benefits to short magazines to make the items more useful compared to drum magazines for the same weapon.
  • Give more benefits to some of the lesser used anti-personnel sidearms to make the items more useful

The change we made:

  • Short magazines got more max spare and 初始弹匣.
  • Some of the anti-personnel sidearms got more max spare and 初始弹匣.
