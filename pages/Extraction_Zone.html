任务-Specific Zones

In certain close-quarters missions (such as the 撤离高价值资产 任务), unique 撤离 Zones will always spawn. 撤离 Beacons will never be present, as 鹈鹕 1 will almost immediately land as soon as the 任务 is 完成.
