the Wretch is a mutated 超级地球 公民, equipped with 庞大 之爪 手臂 and highly agile movement. They accompany Crushers in the 投票 Snatchers 光能者 subfaction.

生成[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Since there are 否 守望者 to 呼叫 增援 on a 行星 occupied by 投票 Snatchers, Wretches will only spawn naturally as patrols and guarding 兴趣点.
    • However, they spawn in 许多 更大 numbers comparatively.
  • They, along with 粉碎者, are spawned from 穿梭舰 during Repel 入侵 Fleet missions on planets where the 投票 Snatchers are active.

行为[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

When a Wretch spots a 绝地潜兵, they will 通常 approach in a straight line. When in 射程, they will 扑击 at 绝地潜兵, dealing significant 伤害.

战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Wretches are 更小, fast 敌人 that will 扑击 at 绝地潜兵 with razor-sharp 螯爪, somewhat mimicking the 行为 of a 追猎虫.
    • (For the host) When a Wretch 扑击 at a 玩家, the only viable ways to avoid 伤害 are to 击杀 it mid-跳跃 or immediately go 卧倒, as they will 追踪 the targeted 绝地潜兵's movement during their 扑击.
    • When facing a 群组 of Wretches and 无票者, 武器 with a 高 数量 of 每秒伤害 such as the MGX-42 子弹 风暴 and MG-43 机枪 are 非常有效 at killing them.
    • Alternatively, crowd 控制 options such as 火焰 or 毒气 武器 can disrupt Wretches, preventing them from closing the 距离 to 绝地潜兵.
      • P-72 Crisper, 冲锋枪/FLAM-34 Stoker flamer 模式, FLAM-66 Torcher, FLAM-40 火焰喷射器, and B/FLAM-80 Cremator in particular are 相当 有效 even against groups of Wretches.
      • Wretches have a 非常 高 stun threshold of 20, can still be staggered, and will be slowed by 毒气 and 火焰.
  • When a Wretch’s 头部 is 已移除, the 生物 will 开始 wildly 斩击 in 全部 directions. 绝地潜兵 are advised to 目标 for the Wretch’s limbs instead of its 头部, particularly its bladed 手臂.

琐事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The Wretch is likely inspired by the Slasher Necromorph from the Dead 太空 Franchise.
    • In the 深 出击: 战斗 for the Gateway 行星 video, their 概述 mentions that their limbs need to be 射击 instead of their 头部, which is a 指挥 nod to the franchise's 著名 引文 "剪切 off their limbs."
