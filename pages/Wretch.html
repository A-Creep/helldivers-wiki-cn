战术信息

  • Wretches are 更小, fast 敌人 that will pounce at 绝地潜兵 with razor-sharp claws, somewhat mimicking the 行为 of a Hunter.
    • (For the host) When a Wretch 扑击 at a player, the 主体 ways to avoid 伤害 are to kill it mid-jump, slide, crouch, or immediately go prone, as they will track the targeted 绝地潜兵's movement during their pounce and 运送 a sideways slash that can easily hit a player mid-dive.
    • When facing a group of Wretches and 无票者, 武器 with a high amount of 每秒伤害 such as the MGX-42 Bullet Storm 和 MG-43 机枪 are 非常有效 at killing them.
    • Alternatively, crowd 控制 options such as 火焰 or 毒气 武器 can disrupt Wretches, preventing them from closing the 距离 to 绝地潜兵.
      • P-72 Crisper, SMG/FLAM-34 Stoker flamer mode, FLAM-66 Torcher, FLAM-40 火焰喷射器，以及 B/FLAM-80 Cremator in particular are quite 有效 even against groups of Wretches.
      • Wretches have a very high stun threshold of 20, can still be staggered, and will be slowed by 毒气 and 火焰.
  • When a Wretch’s head is 已移除, the 生物 will begin wildly 斩击 in all directions. 绝地潜兵 are advised to aim for the Wretch’s limbs instead of its head, particularly its bladed arms.

    • In the Deep Dive: Battle for the Gateway 行星 video, their 概述 mentions that their limbs need to be shot instead of their head, which is a 正面 nod to the franchise's famous 引文 "Cut off their limbs."
