盟友 是一个 战略配备 which calls down a 支援武器 which specializes in disposing of large quantities of lightly 有护甲 targets.

采购

The 盟友 is unlocked at the 爱国行政中心 用于 3500 申购点 Slips 在1级时。

The 盟友 can also be found at some Minor Places of Interest.

战术信息

  • As a 侧面 grade to the MG-43 机枪, the M-105 盟友 specializes in 应对 large crowds of 无护甲 and lightly 有护甲 targets.
    • This makes it particularly 克制 the 光能者, given the abundance of lightly 有护甲, high 生命值 targets.
  • The 盟友 has 几个 advantages as compared to heavier options like the MG-43 机枪 or MG-206 重机枪.
    • It sports a much larger 弹匣 with a 换弹 that, while still lengthy, can be done on the move. It also has much more 储备 ammunition.
    • The weapon also has higher 人体工学 and lower 后坐力, allowing more responsive and 准确 火焰 on 近距离 threats.
  • The 盟友's major drawback is that it possesses only  轻型 护甲穿透, proving less 有效 versus 敌人 with more 护甲.
    • For this reason, primary and 副武器 with  中型 or 更大 护甲穿透 pair well with the 盟友.
    • To leverage the 盟友 against targets of 中型 size or larger, 绝地潜兵 will 通常 have to aim for 弱点 such as 虫窝护卫 bodies or legs, 蹂躏者 heads, 强袭虫 butts, and Hulk heatsinks.
  • The weapon's 射速 can be toggled between 700, 850, and 1,150 发 每分钟.
    • A lower 射速 can be utilized to conserve ammunition when 索敌 distant 敌人 or 小型 weakpoints.
    • A higher 射速 maximizes 伤害 output, best used when faced with crowds or large 无护甲 targets.
  • The 盟友 is one of the few 支援武器 equipped with an 下挂 flashlight, convenient for night missions or 虫窝世界 地下 areas.
