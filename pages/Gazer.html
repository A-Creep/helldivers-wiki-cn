Gazer scans the area around them and locks onto 敌对 targets before firing a beam at them. 绝地潜兵 can avoid this beam by taking note of their tracking laser as it begins to visibly charge up.

Although Gazer fires a beam visually similar to 猎杀器 lasers, they do very 低伤害 每秒, and it is relatively safe to run through one momentarily, although it will set the 绝地潜兵 on 火焰. At shorter ranges, Gazers cannot rotate fast enough to track 绝地潜兵.

Gazer can be quickly destroyed with any 小型 arms 火焰 directly to their eye weak-spot, but the areas surrounding the eye are 受保护  Tank I 护甲.
