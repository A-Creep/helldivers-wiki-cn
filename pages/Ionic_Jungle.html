    这颗星球颠覆性的神奇光合作用是如何让这里色调奇特的植物群维持生长？超级地球最出色的外星生物学家至今仍未解开此谜团。

    The Ionic Jungle is the blue colored triplet of the 火山 Jungle and the Ethereal Jungle primordial biomes.

    Like the other primordial biomes, the Ionic Jungle features dense jungles full of trees, plants and ferns, all of them having blue leaves. Large clearings also fill the 浮出地面, full of blue grass, flowers and rock formations. The 行星 also features beaches, oceans, the occasional lake, and distant 山 ranges.

    The skies barely change color between day and night, this is thanks to the nearby star delivering lower levels of 轻型 during the day. During 离子风暴, the skies begin to crackle with blue storms full of ionized particles, temporarily disabling 战略配备 and the map.

    Yellow dots, similar to real-world fireflies, float around the 浮出地面. It is 未知 whether they are bioluminescent fauna or just spores. Animal sounds can also 偶尔 be heard in the 距离, though unlike the four-winged avian creatures present in the 火山 Jungle, these remain unseen.

    部分星球 tend to have blue rings around them, such as Malevelon Creek 和 Genesis Prime, while others, like Merak, don't have any rings at all.

    浮出地面 Variations

    大气层

    Depending on various factors, the overall color of the 行星's 大气层 may also change. This may be seen when selecting the 行星 on the 银河地图, where the glowing outline of 行星's hologram turns into it's 大气层's respective color.

    • 蓝色/靛蓝色： Planets like Genesis Prime have normal blue colored atmospheres during the day, becoming indigo at night. In the 银河地图, they have a darker blue/purple hue.
    • 青绿色： Planets like Merak have blue atmospheres that have a noticeable turquoise hue during the day, becoming a 轻型 indigo at night. In the 银河地图, they have a lighter blue hue.

    Metropolises

    In parks located in Metropolises, green grassy 地形 can be found with blue grass blades growing out of it. While the 行星 has trees, blue Earth-like trees will generate.
