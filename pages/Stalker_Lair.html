the 追踪虫巢穴 is an 终结族 建筑 and 可选目标 for 终结族 Missions, in which the goal is to 摧毁 a 追踪虫巢穴.

追踪虫 Nests[edit | edit 来源]

A 追踪虫 Nest 建筑 within the limits of a Colony

追踪虫 Nests are a unique 变体 of 终结族 虫穴. While 绝地潜兵 are nearby, the 追踪虫 Nests will regularly spawn the unique 追踪虫 enemy, a large, 耐久, 重型 hitting 终结族 with the 能力 to cloak.

As Stalkers are some of the deadlier 终结族 敌人, closing the nests is a top priority. However, the 追踪虫巢穴 虫穴 can be destroyed in the same manner as ordinary 虫穴, via explosives with a 爆破 force of 20 (if a 手榴弹 is tossed inside) or any weapon with a 爆破 force of 40 or higher such as the GP-20 Ultimatum.

目标 Steps[edit | edit 来源]

  • 摧毁追踪虫巢穴
    • There are 1-2 unique 虫穴 structures around the 追踪虫 Nest. They can be destroyed in the same manner as any other 虫穴.
  • Reward: 申购单 & 经验值

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • 追踪虫 holes can only be destroyed in two ways:
    • Damaging the inside of the 虫穴:
      • With a 最小 爆破 force of 20, which requires an 爆炸 伤害类型.
      • With a 最小 爆破 force of 30, which does not 要求 爆炸伤害 specifically.
    • Damaging the outside of the 虫穴:
      • With a 爆破 force of 40 or 更大.
        • A single well placed TED-63 Dynamite 手榴弹 can 摧毁 2 holes.
  • 绝地潜兵 should immediately 摧毁 these Lairs, as Stalkers are one of the most 危险 敌人 类型.
  • Stalkers Lairs 可以 also spawn 小型 终结族 such as 食腐虫.
  • 追踪虫巢穴 holes are the same holes that can be found in 终结族 Nests, their only difference is a slightly larger 虫穴. They can be destroyed the same way.
  • 绝地潜兵 can easily locate 追踪虫 Lairs by searching in the 方向 Stalkers are coming from, and looking for the orange glow of the 虫穴.
