目标 Steps

建立通信上行链路

“

必须定位并启动本地通信塔，才能向平民进行广播。

— 目标 概述

在极难及以上难度有两个子目标。

  • 启动终端
    • The 终端 takes five 秒 to activate. Once this step appears, 绝地潜兵 will be also able to use the SSSD 运送 战略配备 (). It is recommended to use the 战略配备 as it will be 必需 for a later step.
  • 登录终端
    • On the 终端, enter the five long directional input code.
  • 使用终端打开超级固态硬盘运送舱门
    • On the 终端, press to open the SSSD hatch.
  • 呼叫超级固态硬盘并将其置入舱门
    • Use the SSSD 运送 战略配备 if it hasn't been used and 运送 the SSSD to the hatch. The hatch is located at the base of a 通讯 tower within the sub-目标.
  • 使用终端校准通信信号
    • On the 终端, use directional inputs to raise the signal 力量 to 100%.
  • 使用终端启动通信塔
    • On the 终端, enter the five long directional input code to 完成 the sub-目标.

疏散优先级公民

“

困在紧急掩体里的公民们暂无危险。你必须把他们放出来，护送至该设施的运输船坞，帮助他们撤离。

— 目标 概述

  • 启动运输船坞终端
    • The 终端 takes six 秒 to activate.
  • 登录运输船坞终端
    • On the 终端, enter the seven long directional input code.
  • 确认疏散坐标
    • On the 终端, use directional inputs to adjust the 坐标 to the designated 目标 坐标 located on the right of the screen. It will take the 终端 three 秒 to 确认 the 坐标.
  • 打开运输船坞门
    • On the 终端, press to open the 运输船 bay door.
  • Escort Citizens safely to 运输船 Bay (number of citizens 增加 depending on 难度)
    • 绝地潜兵 must find the highlighted buttons or locate the 撤离 doors with a yellow bar above it; two doors can be found within the 主要目标. Pressing the interact input on the button near the doors will begin 公民 撤离. 绝地潜兵 can evacuate three citizens every 33 秒.
    • Each death of a 公民 by a 绝地潜兵 or environmental 危害 (E.G. 火焰 龙卷风) will immediately subtract 25-申购点 from the 任务奖励 (I.E. before the 任务 难度 倍率 calculations).
  • 任务 Completed: Evacuate Priority Citizens
    • The 主要目标 is completed, and the 撤离 终端 will become available once the last 必需 公民 enters the 运输船 bay.

战术信息

  • 绝地潜兵 are advised to take these factoids into consideration when placing some of the trickier 防御型 战略配备
    • If using Mortar 哨戒炮 (both 高爆弹 和 EMS), it is recommended to place these closer to the 运输船 Bay entrance to possibly allow more pre-emptive clearing around the edges of the 紧急撤离 port area.
    • Depending on the 方向 where 敌人 spawn and enter the 撤离 zone, it is possible to use mine field 战略配备 to block the entrances,
    • While Tesla Towers won't specially 目标 civilians, they can still end up being used as conduits for chain arcing. Therefore, Tesla Towers should be placed over 20-meters away from the 撤离 paths.
  • Planets 与 火焰 龙卷风 environmental 危害 can prove deadly to citizens. 绝地潜兵 should pay attention to their timings of 公民 evacuations if the 危害 is active.
  • A 绝地潜兵 killing 9 平民 will be branded as a 叛徒. And the 超级驱逐舰 will 火焰 380mm HE Shells at the 绝地潜兵 until they are killed.
