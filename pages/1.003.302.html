已知问题

截至 UTC 时间 2028-08-26 19:40。

战争债券代币

We have been made aware that there is an issue with some 玩家 being unable to spend 超级货币 while they are in possession of the 战争债券 Token. We're currently investigating a 修复 for this and we'll keep you posted as soon as it's available.

This means that you may need to hold off on purchases if you want to save your Token for a future 尊享战争债券, such as Dust Devils. If you don’t want to wait, you can use your 战争债券 Token on any other 尊享战争债券 you don’t currently own, and then spend your 超级货币 normally.

ODST MA5C 突击步枪

We are currently investigating an issue regarding the new Halo 突击步枪 which is causing 崩溃 on all platforms. When on the bridge, if all 玩家 equip this weapon from the 军械库 and then the host leaves it will cause your game to crash.

Recommended workaround is to equip this from the 武装配置. We will 更新 you when we have more information!
