生成[edit | edit 来源]

Gatekeepers are part of the   Appropriators sub-阵营 of the 光能者 and can only spawn on worlds where the Appropriators are present. They begin 生成 on 难度 4 and above.

行为[edit | edit 来源]

Gatekeepers rarely spawn, and 通常 present less of a 威胁 than more commonly spawned 猎杀器 on higher difficulties. When they do arrive, they 通常 are positioned between 证真者 at the front and Harvesters at the back, occupying a suppressive midline role on the battlefield.

They have two 攻击 with their twin Plasma Starcannons, which project blue guiding lasers at their 目标:

  • A two-shot, concussive plasma burst that is capable of ragdolling a 绝地潜兵 掩体后. After the 攻击, the ends of the barrels will flare out, closing back in while preparing another 攻击.
  • A much longer plasma volley that can very easily shred apart a 绝地潜兵 if they do not take evasive maneuvers. This is preceded by the Starcannons "pumping", and the barrels rapidly winding up.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • Gatekeepers, unlike 证真者 have  重型 护甲 and prefer to stay further away. They have two 远程 攻击.
    • A charged, large 范围效果 Plasma Starcannon blast with 巨大 伤害 that can ragdoll from a 距离.
    • A telegraphed 中等 伤害, rapid-火焰 volley that slowly tracks the targeted 绝地潜兵, similar to a Gazer.
  • The 驾驶舱 is similar to Veracitor cockpits, but it instead has  重型 护甲. It is also 受保护 by a rechargeable 能量护盾. When destroyed, the pilot is 易受 gunfire and when killed, will also 摧毁 the mech.
    • Note the pilot is 50% 耐久, unlike the Veracitor's pilot, so they may 要求 几个 more rounds to kill.
  • The horn on the Gatekeeper's forehead is the unit's 防护罩生成器, similar to a 猎杀器. When destroyed, the pilot's shield is permanently disabled. It is, however  重型 护甲。
  • Gatekeepers have a 近战攻击, but its use is uncommon and is easily avoided.
  • Firing a single high-伤害 shot (such as from the EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器 or GR-8 无后坐力炮) to the connecting stump that holds the Pilot's 驾驶舱 can instantly 摧毁 said 驾驶舱, killing the machine with it.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • If a Gatekeeper is hit with a stun 效果 such as that from a G-23 Stun or A/M-23 EMS 迫击哨戒炮, then the 监视者 pilot can be seen angrily smacking at the controls to try and boot it back up.
  • According to its 描述, the Gatekeeper is a 变体 of the Veracitor that is made for combat.
