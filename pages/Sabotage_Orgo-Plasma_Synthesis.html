破坏有机等离子合成
“

机器人正在利用有机物质合成某种营养密集型等离子体。不可否认的情报显示，100%的有机物质来自具有高潜力和优秀面部对称性的忠诚公民。这种等离子体的用途尚不清楚，但其生产成本极高。

Disrupt the plasma 生产 and secure samples of the liquid for further analysis.

— 任务简报

破坏 Orgo-Plasma Synthesis 是一个 机器人 任务 in 绝地潜兵 2. 绝地潜兵 are tasked with destroying various Bio-Processors, and extracting a sample of the nutrient-dense plasma.

目标步骤

摧毁生物处理器

“

这里的生物处理器是合成有机等离子的第一步。必须将其摧毁。

— 目标概览

  • 摧毁生化处理器（每个目标最多5个）
    • 绝地潜兵必须摧毁指定数量的生化处理器以完成目标。

提取有机等离子

“

有机等离子在这些槽罐之中合成。抽取样本进行科学分析。

— 目标概览

  • 启动终端
  • 按照终端的提示，调整所有阀门
    • The 绝地潜兵 must turn the valve 下一个 to the pipe leading to the vats of 有机 plasma.
    • The switch on the 终端 can now be flipped up.
  • (A) 燃料运输船即将抵达
    • A 鹈鹕 with cargo space for fluid and a retractable hose will soon arrive. This will take roughly 30 秒 (give or take a few 秒 depending on flight anomalies).
    • Once the 鹈鹕 is in position, it will lower the hose directly into the 传输 pump.
  • Lock the hose into the 传输 pump
    • The 绝地潜兵 must go to the 传输 pump and pull the lever, closing the clamp on the 传输 pump and securing the hose.
  • 正在输送
    • The 传输 will take 15 秒.
  • 卸除软管
    • The 绝地潜兵 must now pull the lever again, releasing the clamp on the 传输 pump and allowing the hose to safely retract back into the 鹈鹕.
  • This 流程 will now repeat one more time, restarting on step (A)
  • 奖励： 500 申购点 Slips 和 100 经验值

战术信息

  • Bio-Processors are completely 无护甲, so they are 易受 all 绝地潜兵 武器，包括基本的小型武器火力。
    • Can be destroyed in one shot from the GP-20 Ultimatum or a single TED-63 Dynamite.
  • 正面 Confrontation with the Outpost can be easily avoided if a 绝地潜兵 is able to throw a high-damaging 战略配备 生化处理器附近，例如一个 Eagle 500kg Bomb.
  • Similar to other Optional 目标 that 要求 绝地潜兵 to 摧毁 a 建筑, a 地狱炸弹战略配备 is provided to 绝地潜兵 when in close proximity to the Bio-Processors.
    • While this method allows 绝地潜兵 to 摧毁 the Bio-Processors without using any of their own munitions or 战略配备, it takes substantially longer to 呼叫 and arm it than if they used their own 装备.
  • A 绝地喷射舱 has enough 爆破力 to 摧毁 a Bio-处理器, which can be achieved by throwing a 战略配备 Ball in the gap between the two parts of a Bio-Processors.

琐事

  • The "撤离 有机 Plasma" 目标 is an altered version of various 终结族 missions, in which 绝地潜兵 are tasked with defending and operating a fluid 车站 to load a 鹈鹕 运输船 with the 任务-specific fluid.
    • Fluid 撤离 missions include Enable E-710 撤离, 撤离 E-711 和 收集 Gloom-Infused Oil.
  • 破坏 Orgo-Plasma Synthesis is the first 机器人 任务 that involves the 撤离 of a fluid from the 任务 map.
  • Despite 等离子 being a 轻型-amber fluid, the fluid spilled on the ground and out of the pipes are colored red, suggesting that there is still red blood cells mixed in it.
