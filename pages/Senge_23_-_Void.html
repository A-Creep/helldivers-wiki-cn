    背景故事

    Exostorm on Senge 23

    On June 2nd 2186,  The 光能者 would begin manifesting an Exostorm on Senge 23 with the goal of plunging the entire system into The Void. Unlike during Rirga Bay's Exostorm, High 指挥 would not send out a dispatch or advisory as the 绝地潜兵 focused on the 防御 of Matar Bay to stop them from reclaiming the Magma 沙漠星球. Additionally, Senge 23 was deep in 光能者 领土, so stopping the Exostorm would've been nearly 不可能.

    With the 绝地潜兵 focused on other planets, on June 9th 2186 The Void would manifest around the Senge 23 system, shunning it from the 银河地图.

    寂域

    On August 14th, 2186, following the creation of the Warp Relay 车站 on Rirga Bay, the 绝地潜兵 were allowed to enter the Void. Here, the 绝地潜兵 re-discovered Senge 23 which had been lightly affected by the Void around it. So as part of 操作: Void Piercer, the 绝地潜兵 received 重要指令 A3-8-6, which ordered the 绝地潜兵 to help study the Void by culling 1,250,000,000 光能者, alongside other tasks within the discovered planets within the Void.

    On August 17th, 2186, the 绝地潜兵 succeeded on Senge 23, leading to the discovery of the 位置 of the voided 行星 哈卓毕亚斯.
