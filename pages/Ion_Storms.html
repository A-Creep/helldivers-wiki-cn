离子风暴

离子风暴 – 离子风暴 intermittently disable 战略配备.

详情

  • During an Ion Storm, 否 new 战略配备 can be deployed (including 增援). The 小地图 is rendered into non-interactable state, hiding all information including nearby radar contacts, 绝地潜兵 positions and 目标 locations. The compass retains the normal functionality alongside already tagged waypoints and nearby undiscovered 小型兴趣点.

受影响生物群落

  • Ionic Crimson
  • Ionic Jungle
