TR-117 阿尔法指挥官

为那些强大自信到不需要任何保证的战士而设计。

TR-117 阿尔法指挥官 is free DLC for 绝地潜兵 2, containing a 中型护甲 set.

获取[edit | edit 来源]

The set was initially only claimable via a finite Twitch 掉落 between 2月 8th - 23rd, 2024.
On 12月 12th, 2024, it was made available to Steam users, in partnership with The Game Awards.[1]
On 12月 19th, 2024, it was made available to 绝地潜兵 across all platforms.

被动能力[edit | edit 来源]

医疗包
增加 initial 库存 and holding 容量 of stims by +2.
增加 stim 效果 持续时间 by 2.0s.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • The familiar 军事 green with metallic black accents and the number “117” allude to John-117 and his MJOLNIR 护甲 from the 光环 series. Moreover, both “Alpha” and “指挥官” are synonymous with his rank, Master Chief.
  • The 护甲 set is primarily a CM-14 Physician reskin, with the exception that the left shoulder canisters are replaced by a second shoulder pad.
