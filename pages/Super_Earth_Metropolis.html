描述[edit | edit 来源]

“

炫目的灯塔，以民主之光照亮宇宙群星。真正效忠联邦的爱国公民心中的乐土。为整个银河系描绘了未来乌托邦的蓝图。

This 生物群系 consists of familiar green grass, oak trees, and similar vegetation. No native fauna can be found in this 生物群系.

Stars cannot be seen in this 生物群系's skyline, implying 极难 轻型 污染.

超级地球 only offers operations in Metropolis regions, and shares many of its attributes as a result. Like other cities, 超级地球 cities 特性 giant skyscrapers and buildings, large parks and playgrounds (which can hide 行星 防御 Cannons), and are connected with large networks of roads in polygonal patterns.

These roadways are distinct from the Colonies seen on colony words, as they are not all grid-based.
