— 行动修正描述

Poor Intel 是一个 行动修正器 in 绝地潜兵 2 exclusive to the 机器人 前线。

详情

  • Hides the exact 位置 of Missions 目标 on the map, requiring 绝地潜兵 to search within a radius for a 任务 目标.
    • Practically all 目标-markers are turned into "Elimination"-markers such as seen in 消灭机器人巨型者-任务.
    • Topographics on the 小地图 remain unchanged, enabling sharp-eyed 绝地潜兵 to scout and easily spot larger 目标 such 洲际弹道导弹发射区
    • Pinging the 目标 will 显示 its precise 位置 on the 小地图. Such as pinging the tower of the Air Base 控制 Tower.
