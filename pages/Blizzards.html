暴风雪

暴风雪 – Intense 暴风雪 reduce mobility and moderately reduce visibility for both enemy and friendly units.

详情[edit | edit 来源]

  • 降低 visibility
    • Things past 5m are more 困难 to see
    • Things past 25m are not 可见
  • Detection-ranges of 敌人 and 战略配备 索敌-射程 are also 已降低 大约 by ~60-百分比 (E.G. AX/LAS-5 "Guard Dog" Rover 索敌 射程 is 已降低 from 30-meters to 12-meters during the storm)
  • 锁定 ranges for guided 武器 such as the FAF-14 Spear or P-92 Warrant are unaffected by 天气 conditions.
  • Slows down 绝地潜兵, both normal walking and sprinting. Does not affect sprint 持续时间.
  • Functionally identical with 沙尘暴

Affected Biomes[edit | edit 来源]

  • Icy Glaciers
