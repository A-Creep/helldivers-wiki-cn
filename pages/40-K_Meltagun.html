战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • The Meltagun acts similarly to the LAS-98 激光大炮 but trades 射程 for 庞大 每秒伤害 and highly 有限 弹药.
  • The Meltagun has a short 蓄力 time before firing, leaving a short window of 弱点 for users. To effectively 火焰 it, 绝地潜兵 should 维护 some 距离 between 敌人 in order to charge up the weapon and land shots.
  • Once the Meltagun is charged, it fires continuously for roughly 1.4 秒 before needing to be charged again.
    • The Meltagun can be fired 3 times before needing to 换弹.
  • With 3 rounds per 弹匣 and 4 spare, the Meltagun can be fired 15 times before running empty.
    • This can be alleviated by equipping 护甲 with the Siege-Ready 被动, adding one more spare 弹匣.
  • The Meltagun is 极其 克制 重型 敌人 such as 湮灭坦克 和 强袭虫, capable of killing them in 1 to 2 shots anywhere.
  • The Meltagun has a 有限 射程 of 15m. 绝地潜兵 are advised to position themselves carefully and consider the 蓄力 time when using this weapon.
  • Being ragdolled does not cancel the firing 顺序 of the Meltagun.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • The 40-K Meltagun, despite being based on the Warhammer 40,000 热熔枪 and sharing its 外观, is notably 更小 in game than it is on miniatures. The size of it is more akin to the Melta 卡宾枪 primarily fielded by the Tempestus Aquilons, which are not in this crossover.
  • The 制作人 of this weapon is ST Construction, referencing the Warhammer 40,000 标准 Template Constructs. These STC Systems are equivalent to technological time capsules, serving to preserve blueprints and knowledge from the Dark Age of Technology. They are the only way the Imperium of Man can make any technological advancements, progression and 发明, as they have outlawed it.
