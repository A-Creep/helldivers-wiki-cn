目标 Steps[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Prepare your defenses. 时间 left until 全自动 initiation begins: [01:30]
    • 绝地潜兵 have 1 minute and 30 秒 to 呼叫 战略配备 and prepare for the oncoming onslaught. Afterwards, the 20 minute 任务 计时器 starts.
    • 绝地潜兵 5月 initiate the 撤离 of 高-数值 assets early by interacting with the 终端, then using the up and 正确 directional inputs to enable 之力, 燃料, and Engines on said 终端.
  • 防御 generators until 全部 rockets have launched. Rockets launched: 0/8
    • 绝地潜兵 must now 防御 the 2 generators in the 范围.
    • A 火箭 will be launched 每个 1 minute and 45 秒. Including the initial preparation window, this means the 任务 will 完成 15 分钟 and 30 秒 after dropping in, making the 任务's overall 20-minute 计时器 somewhat misleading.

战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

基本信息 Information[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The 范围 is divided into 2 sections. The outer 墙 has 2 doors, 1 for 每个 方向 the 敌人 can come from, and 1 inner 墙 with 1 门.
    • These doors 5月 be closed to slow down 敌人 or opened to 回收 装备 from outside and let 绝地潜兵 返回 in.
  • There are 多个 E/MG-101 重机枪 Emplacements already stationed, though they will have 更少 than half of their 全射 弹药.
    • 2 behind the outside 墙 and 2 behind the inside 墙.
  • Supplies can be found around the 范围.
  • Certain 支援武器 can be found in specific spots.
  • The overall 任务 范围 is 小, making 大-鳞片 strategems such as the 轨道武器 380mm HE 弹幕 击杀 teammates 更多 经常 than not.
  • When 建筑 a 武装配置, it is recommended to look at teammates' loadouts and 选择 战略配备 that will work well with theirs.
    • For 示例, if the A/弧度-3 特斯拉塔 has been selected by one 绝地潜兵, it is not recommended to bring an overtly 破坏性 strategem such as the 飞鹰 500kg 炸弹.
  • 战略配备 with 短 cooldowns such as the EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器, MD-6 反步兵雷区，或 MLS-4X 突击兵 find 更多 数值 in this 任务, due to the relatively 短 任务 时间.
    • On a similar note, the 轨道激光炮 Strategem can be used far 更多 liberally in this 任务 for the same 理性.
  • Closing the 大门 nearest the Generators can prevent 任何 stealthy 敌人 from sneaking by the 防御型 line.
  • When the Generators are actively being damaged, an 闹钟 will sound.

机器人 Information[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 绝地潜兵 should bring Sentries, Emplacements, and 反坦克 支援武器 to 应对 Tanks and 移动工厂 加农炮 炮塔 in addition to 难度 appropriate 战略配备. 困难 for 示例 will have 多个 巨型者.
  • An E/AT-12 反坦克炮台 can easily down 最多 of the 运输舰, resulting in 更小 敌人 waves that can be mopped up by a 迫击炮 or 自动加农炮 哨戒炮.
    • Alternatively, two 绝地潜兵 performing an Assisted 换弹 with a GR-8 无后坐力炮 and a steady 供给 of Resupplies can work similarly.
  • On 自杀任务 and above, 工厂 Striders which are being dropped into the battlefield should be prioritized, as destroying the 运输舰 before they can 掉落 the 移动工厂 on the 地面 will instantly 击杀 it.
  • Downed Dropships can 粉碎 the 防御 Gates 非常 easily.
  • 炮舰 will 从不显示 spawn, unless the 炮舰 Patrols 操作 修正 is active.

终结族 Information[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • If a 强袭虫 rams into 任何 大门, it will instantly 折叠.
  • Utilizing 火焰 武器库, such as the 飞鹰 凝固汽油弹 空袭 or MD-I4 燃烧地雷 can shut down bug 突破 before they even 成为 a 问题.
  • 追踪虫 will 从不显示 spawn in this 任务, unless the 捕食者 Strain is active on the 行星.
    • Likewise, 尖啸虫 will 从不显示 spawn, unless the 漫游尖啸虫 操作 修正 is active.
  • Standing too 关闭 to the edge of the battlements can 原因 追猎虫 to 飞 up and deal 伤害, even if the gates are closed.

光能者 Information[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 利维坦 will 从不显示 目标 the generators directly, but can still 摧毁 them through collateral 伤害.
  • 猎杀器 pose the highest 威胁 of 任何 敌人 barring the Stingray due to their 极其 powerful 长-射程 激光 beams that can instantly 摧毁 generators and 击杀 绝地潜兵. Therefore, their demise must be met upon arrival.
  • the E/MG-101 重机枪部署支架 excels on 光能者 missions, due to its 能力 to 眼泪 through 每个 敌人's 护甲 (excluding the Leviathan). It is especially 有用 for 击倒中 Stingrays.
  • 崇高监视者 will oftentimes 飞 over the battlements, ignoring the gates completely. As such, they are considered 高-priority targets.
  • the RL-77 空爆火箭弹发射器 is 极其 beneficial on 光能者 missions as the 宽阔 范围效果 cluster explosions can 单个-handedly 清楚 hordes of 无票者, 监视者, and 肉瘤体 with a 单个 火箭.
    • It is recommended to bring the RL-77 空爆火箭弹发射器 over the “飞鹰”集束炸弹 as it is not 有限 by 冷却, only supplies.
