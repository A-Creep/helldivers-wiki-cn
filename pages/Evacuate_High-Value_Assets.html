撤离高价值资产 是 任务 目标 that was released during the Reclamation Event and is exclusive to Defend 行星 events only with an exception for 光能者 controlled Regions. The 光能者 version is re-titled "Defend 撤离 Site".

目标 Steps

  • Prepare your defenses. Time left until 全自动 initiation begins: [01:30]
    • 绝地潜兵 have 1 minute and 30 秒 to 呼叫 战略配备 and prepare for the oncoming onslaught. Afterwards, the 20 minute 任务 timer starts.
    • 绝地潜兵 may initiate the 撤离 of high-value assets early by interacting with the 终端, then using the up and right directional inputs to enable Power, Fuel, and Engines on said 终端.
  • Defend generators until all rockets have launched. Rockets launched: 0/8
    • 绝地潜兵 must now defend the 2 generators in the area.
    • A rocket will be launched every 1 minute and 45 秒. Including the initial preparation window, this means the 任务 will 完成 15 分钟 and 30 秒 after dropping in, making the 任务's overall 20-minute timer somewhat misleading.
  • Reward: 500 经验值 & 申购单

战术信息

基本信息 Information

  • The area is divided into 2 sections. The 外圈 wall has 2 doors, 1 for each 方向 the 敌人 can come from, and 1 内圈 wall with 1 door.
    • These doors may be closed to slow down 敌人 or opened to 回收 装备 from outside and let 绝地潜兵 back in.
  • There are 多个 E/MG-101 HMG 炮台 already stationed, though they will have less than half of their total 弹药.
    • 2 behind the outside wall and 2 behind the inside wall.
  • Supplies can be found around the area.
  • Certain 支援武器 can be found in specific spots.
  • The overall 任务 area is 小型, making large-scale strategems such as the 轨道武器 380mm HE 弹幕 kill teammates more often than not.
  • When 建筑 a 武装配置, it is recommended to look at teammates' loadouts and select 战略配备 that will work well with theirs.
    • For 示例, if the A/弧度-3 特斯拉塔 has been selected by one 绝地潜兵, it is not recommended to bring an overtly 破坏性 strategem such as the Eagle 500kg Bomb.
  • 战略配备 with short cooldowns such as the EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器, MD-6 反步兵雷区，或 MLS-4X 突击兵 find more value in this 任务, due to the relatively short 任务 time.
    • On a similar note, the 轨道激光炮 Strategem can be used far more liberally in this 任务 for the same reason.
  • Closing the gate nearest the Generators can prevent any stealthy 敌人 from sneaking by the 防御型 line.
  • When the Generators are actively being damaged, an alarm will sound.

机器人 Information

  • 绝地潜兵 should bring 哨兵, 炮台, and 反坦克 支援武器 to 应对 Tanks 和 移动工厂 cannon 炮塔 in addition to 难度 appropriate 战略配备. 困难 for 示例 will have 多个 巨型者.
  • An E/AT-12 反坦克炮台 can easily down most of the 运输舰, resulting in 更小 enemy waves that can be mopped up by a 迫击炮 or 自动加农炮 sentry.
    • Alternatively, two 绝地潜兵 performing an Assisted 换弹 with a GR-8 无后坐力炮 and a steady supply of 补给 can work similarly.
  • On 自杀任务 and above, 工厂 Striders which are being dropped into the battlefield should be prioritized, as destroying the 运输舰 before they can 掉落 the 移动工厂 on the ground will instantly kill it.
  • Downed Dropships can crush the 防御 Gates very easily.
  • 炮舰 will never spawn, unless the 炮舰 Patrols 操作 修正 is active.

终结族 Information

  • If a 强袭虫 rams into any gate, it will instantly 折叠.
  • Utilizing 火焰 武器库, such as the Eagle Napalm 空袭 or MD-I4 燃烧地雷 can shut down bug 突破 before they even become a 问题.
  • 追踪虫 will never spawn in this 任务, unless the 捕食者 Strain is active on the 行星.
    • Likewise, 尖啸虫 will never spawn, unless the 漫游尖啸虫 操作 修正 is active.
  • Standing too close to the edge of the battlements can cause 追猎虫 to fly up and deal 伤害, even if the gates are closed.

光能者 Information

  • 利维坦 will never 目标 the generators directly, but can still 摧毁 them through collateral 伤害.
  • 猎杀器 pose the highest 威胁 of any enemy barring the Stingray due to their 极其 powerful long-射程 laser beams that can instantly 摧毁 generators and kill 绝地潜兵. Therefore, their demise must be met upon arrival.
  • the E/MG-101 重机枪部署支架 excels on 光能者 missions, due to its 能力 to tear through every enemy's 护甲 (excluding the Leviathan). It is especially useful for 击倒中 Stingrays.
  • 崇高监视者 will oftentimes fly over the battlements, ignoring the gates completely. As such, they are considered high-priority targets.
  • RL-77 空爆火箭弹发射器 是 极其 beneficial on 光能者 missions as the wide 范围效果 cluster explosions can single-handedly clear hordes of 无票者, 监视者，以及 肉瘤体 with a single rocket.
    • It is recommended to bring the RL-77 空爆火箭弹发射器 over the “飞鹰”集束炸弹 as it is not 有限 by 冷却, only supplies.
