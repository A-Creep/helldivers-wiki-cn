战术信息

  • Operating similarly to the GP-31 榴弹手枪, the Ultimatum fires a single powerful 反坦克 爆炸 round and has to be reloaded after every shot.
    • Also like the 榴弹手枪, equipping the Ultimatum means forgoing a reliable 副武器 for 应对 近距离 threats.
  • The Ultimatum is capable of killing most 敌人 in a 单发.
  • The Ultimatum's 高伤害, 护甲穿透, and 爆破 force of 40 allows it to demolish various structures such as 虫洞 (including 吐酸泰坦虫穴) and 机器人 Fabricators from the outside.
    • 停靠的跃迁舰 can also be destroyed from the outside due to taking 已增加 爆炸伤害.
  • A weakness of the Ultimatum is its very short 射程.
    • When standing still and firing parallel to the ground, the 投射物 has a 射程 of 大约 17 meters. When firing at a 45° angle, it is 已增加 to 大约 40 meters.
    • The user's 速率 is transferred to the 投射物 when fired. Diving, using the LIFT-850 喷射背包, riding in a M-102 Fast 侦察载具, or using the Raise Weapon emote can greatly influence its 射程.
  • Another significant weakness is its low 弹药 容量.
    • Regardless of any 护甲 Passives, its 储备 ammunition can never be 已增加.
    • 弹药箱 disproportionately 受益 the Ultimatum, granting full 储备 ammunition.

琐事

  • The GP-20 Ultimatum is likely inspired by the German Kampfpistole, a World War II flare pistol of the Wehrmacht that was later modified to fulfill the role of a short-远程 榴弹发射器 by firing a specially designed 步枪 手榴弹 known as a Sprengpatrone in a low-弧度 similar to the in-game Ultimatum, but with much less 爆炸 and penetrative power.
