Sample 类型

There are three 类型 of obtainable samples: 普通, 稀有，以及 Super. These Samples are capped at 500 for Common, 250 for Rare and 100 for Supers. You will need to spend 3,830 Common, 2,920 Rare, and 305 超级样本 to unlock 所有 30 舰船模块.

普通

常见样本 常见样本 have a green icon and may be found on any of the ten 难度 levels. When found in missions, they can be identified by the icon of a white circle split down the middle.

稀有

稀有样本 稀有样本 have an orange icon, and may be found on 挑战 难度 or higher. When found in missions, they can be identified by the icon of a white square with the bottom-left corner split off. There are also undocumented 稀有样本 found at 次要兴趣点, specifically inside Pods, Containers, and Bunkers. Undocumented 稀有样本 do not count towards the Sample 反制 on the top right corner HUD during a 任务.

All 稀有样本 emit constant background noise that manifests as a constant ringing/shining sound. Legendarium ingots and Black Saffron flowers (see the table below) can be reliable detected by sound.

Super

There is an in-depth Guide on acquiring 超级样本 solo.

超级样本 超级样本, also known as 超铀, have a pink icon, and may be found on 极难 难度 or higher. When found in missions, they can be identified by the icon of four white squares. They always appear as a pool of liquid metal on the ground, with a hovering blob above it. The also make constant noise similar to gurgling/blooping/bubbling sounds, but it is quiet enough to be 无用 when hunting for 超级样本

超级样本 are often found at Super Sample Rocks; a rock vaguely resembling a chicken drumstick with silvery veins. This rock may rarely spawn below 极难难度 without 超级样本, but on 极难 and above, it is guaranteed to appear and always contains them. Experienced 绝地潜兵 can quickly spot this rock on the 小地图. There is a similar-looking rock on 终结族 missions that do not contain 超级样本, only 物资. The number of 超级样本 varies based on the 难度:

  • 极难: 2-3
  • 自杀任务: 3-4
  • 不可能: 5
  • 绝地潜兵难度: 6
  • 超级绝地潜兵难度: 7-8

备注

  • There is one 成就 associated with 普通样本: 'Science is done by 数量' awarded for extracting with 15 普通样本 from a single 任务. Samples carried by squadmates will not count.
  • There is one 成就 associated with 稀有样本: 'Samples are a diver's best friend' awarded for your squad extracting with 15 稀有样本 from a single 任务.
  • Excess 普通样本 may be donated to the Democracy Space 车站 to fund the Eagle Storm 战术行动, while 稀有样本 are used for 重型 法令 分布.
  • 绝地潜兵 can cancel the 动画 of picking up a sample by initiating a sprint while walking, or double-tapping sprint while already sprinting to stop first and then re-engage it, allowing for the rapid successive pickup of 多个 samples in one area.
