功能[edit | edit 来源]

The VG-70 Variable, as its name suggests, is a weapon system with variable 射击模式 and rpm options.

  • Auto: Fully-全自动 with three available rpm options; 300, 550 & 750.
  • Volley: Fires one round from each barrel simultaneously, totaling 7 rounds on 目标 with one trigger pull.
    • This firing mode is 半自动, but is affected by the selected rpm. Produces significant 后坐力.
  • Total: Fires all 剩余 ammunition in the 弹匣.
    • Using this mode will deal 15 伤害 to the 绝地潜兵's right arm and ragdoll them if not prone and when firing more than 22 rounds at once.
    • This mode has the added usecase of acting as a mini 喷射背包 if the 绝地潜兵 dives while firing towards the floor. This is most useful for getting up some 小型 ledges or over city walls when already on an 高处 position (such as a garbage bin).

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • The Variable, as implied, comes with 3 火焰 modes.
    • Auto: Fully-全自动, like a 标准 突击步枪, with 85 normal 伤害 每发.
      • Recommended for infantry or fodder.
    • Volley: Fires one round from each barrel simultaneously. 595 伤害 (85 x 7). It deals 161 伤害 to 100% 耐久 enemy parts.
      • Recommended for tough 中型 敌人 (eg. 阿尔法指挥官 heads, 胆汁喷涌虫 mouths, 蹂躏者 stomachs or 狂暴者 stomachs).
      • This firing mode suffers from high 后坐力. This can be mitigated by aiming down sights, crouching, going prone, and using armors that reduce 后坐力, such as 加固 和 工程套件.
    • Total: Fires all 剩余 loaded ammunition from all barrels. Maxes out at 4,165 伤害 (85*49) when fully loaded. On 100% 耐久 enemy parts the 最大 耐久伤害 of TOTAL mode is 1,127 伤害.
      • Recommended for the weak spots of 重型 敌人, like Hulk vents, 强袭虫 butts, 穿刺虫 heads, Fleshmob heads or 猎杀器 eye sockets.
      • Firing while leaning out of the FRV's doors will not ragdoll 绝地潜兵, but will still deal 伤害.
      • Diving backwards and firing or using a 喷射背包 and firing backwards midair in this mode enables 玩家 to "jump" much 更大 distances thanks to the 后坐力, useful when a quick escape or a large gap must be crossed. With skill, jumps can be made in excess of 50 meters, though significant 伤害 may be incurred.
  • The Variable's projectiles have much higher drag than other 特攻 rifles, resulting in a noticeable 减少 in 伤害 per bullet over longer ranges. This is slightly compensated by the Variable's 射击模式 that 火焰 more bullets at a time.
  • The Variable suffers from very low 人体工学. Armors with the 巅峰体魄 被动 can help mitigate this.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • The Variable's seven-barrelled configuration resembles the 18th 世纪 Nock gun, a flintlock muzzleloader.
  • The Variable's functionality appears inspired by the "Bertha" 原型 volley gun developed by Metal Storm, which electronically fires projectiles superposed inside each of its barrels. Like the Variable, Bertha could adjust its 射速, even to the point where it could 火焰 all of its ammunition in a 分数 of a second.
