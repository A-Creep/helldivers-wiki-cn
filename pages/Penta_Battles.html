Summary Penta liberated. RL-77 空爆火箭弹发射器 unlocked.


Background

操作 Legitimate Undertaking has just begun. The first phase ordered 绝地潜兵 to liberate Penta or Choohe (or both). Many brave civilains were trapped on this planets. On Penta, there were civilians who worked on a new 支援武器 Strategem.

策略 and Importance

The battle determined if 主要命令 would be completed or not. Also, it determined if 绝地潜兵 will be supplied with a new weapon.

战斗

The battle was a success. Civilians were rescued and the RL-77 空爆火箭弹发射器 is thanks to this victory now avaible for all 绝地潜兵 above level 15.
