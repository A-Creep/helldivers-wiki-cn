战术信息

  • 爆炸弹可以伤害密集聚集的敌群，以及任何离爆炸太近的绝地潜兵。
  • Without the 绝地喷射舱 Space 优化 强化资源, it comes with 3 out of 5 possible magazines. The 蓄势出击 护甲 被动 slightly alleviates Scorcher's 弹药 经济 with +1 弹匣.
  • The Scorcher's 特性 make it viable against nearly every enemy in the game. The exploding plasma can bypass 中型护甲 present on 敌人 like Hive Guards, Scout Striders, and Devastators. The blasts are also highly 克制 the 耐久 butts of Chargers, 穿刺虫 faces, the vents on 机器人 Hulks, and Tanks.
    • Note that while the 爆炸 can penetrate  中型 护甲, the plasma 投射物 only has  轻型 护甲穿透. Careful aim against 有护甲 敌人 is still 重要 to make full use of every shot.
  • The Scorcher is capable of eliminating Gunships by shooting at the thrusters because the explosions are  中型 护甲 penetrating.
  • 追猎虫 and Pouncers may 频繁 complicate the use of this weapon, as they can quickly get into 近战 射程 of the shooter and render the Scorcher unsafe to 火焰. However meleeing and quickly backstepping to shoot is a good tactic to avoid self-伤害.
  • 它拥有所有爆炸武器中最低的硬直量；所有脆弱到能被爆炸内半径有意义地硬直的敌人，无论如何都会被直接杀死。
