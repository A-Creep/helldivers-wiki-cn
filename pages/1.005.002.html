基本信息 changes 概述[edit | edit 来源]

Suppressors are now more 有效[edit | edit 来源]

  • Intent is to make suppressors more valuable in stealth 玩法 and attract less attention from 敌人
  • Noise radius of firing suppressed 武器 roughly降低50%。
  • 敌人 detection radius when being fired at, but not being hit, is 已降低 in some units

将军[edit | edit 来源]

  • Detection 射程 of 危险 (基本上 being shot at but not getting hit) 已降低 for 敌人 like Devastators, 狂暴者, 喷吐者, Chargers, 穿刺虫, Observer
