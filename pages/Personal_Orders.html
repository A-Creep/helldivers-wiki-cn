个人命令

个人命令 （缩写为 PO) are optional orders specific to each player that rotate every 24-hours, and give 15 Medals when completed. They expire once the time is up, but can be completed if the 任务 is initiated before the reset.

玩法

In order to 完成 a 个人指令, 绝地潜兵 must dive down to a 行星 (once or 多个 times) and 完成 the specific task. Depending on the 个人指令, the 要求 may 要求 different loadouts so 绝地潜兵 should 平衡 their 装备 accordingly.

While 个人命令 are individually completed, any 绝地潜兵 in the squad can 贡献 to other's PO goal (i.e. a 盟友 user contributes to all squad members, including the 绝地潜兵 equipped with AT). Despite this, 绝地潜兵 should plan accordingly since squad 贡献 may not be as 高效, and may need to proactively 贡献 to 完成 the PO in a timely manner.

备注

  • Only 致命 hits (to 主生命值-points / HP) are counted as kills (E.G. 虫穴 and thrusters of Dropships won't count), but 构筑者 和 Spore 喷吐者 count as enemy kills.
    • The 致命 hit kill on structures is only counted with the proper depletion of 生命值-point; 爆破 will not count as a kill (E.G. 摧毁 of a 构筑者 via vents).
  • During 撤离, any kills gotten with 鹈鹕 1 is credited to the 绝地潜兵 who finished the input-code. 全自动 and Emergency calldown 鹈鹕 1 kills are only credited towards overall collective kill-count of SEAF.
    • The first rule 施加 also to other armed transportation shuttles such the one delivering M-102 Fast 侦察 载具.
