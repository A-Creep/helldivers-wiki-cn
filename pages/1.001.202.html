概述[edit | edit 来源]

  • 崩溃修复
  • Raise Flag 目标 难度 fix
  • Miscellaneous fixes

修复[edit | edit 来源]

  • 崩溃修复与软锁
  • 已解决 a crash that could occur when a player or hot-joiner first spawns into a 任务
  • Fixed a rare crash triggered by interacting with carriable items while viewing the 小地图

任务[edit | edit 来源]

  • 已降低 enemy 生成速率 during the "Raise Flag" 目标 in 传播民主 missions to make the 目标 easier to 完成

Miscellaneous fixes[edit | edit 来源]

  • Fixed an issue where holding the last 战略配备 input would prevent movement on 默认 PC controls
  • SOS beacons will now look for additional 玩家 for the remainder of 每次任务. They are no longer destroyed when the squad is full. Instead they will deactivate - until someone leaves and the SOS beacon starts looking for additional 玩家 again. The SOS beacons will still be destroyed on host migration - this is to allow the new host to decide if they want a new SOS beacon or not
  • Fixed an issue where the SOS beacons could not be deployed in the same or subsequent missions under certain circumstances
  • Fixed an issue where deploying an SOS beacon could result in your lobby not being made public and couldn't be joined by other 玩家. The intended design is that deploying an SOS beacon makes your lobby public for the 持续时间 of the 任务
  • Fixed the 掉落 wheel, emote wheel and comms wheel snapping the cursor to the outer edges in the radial menus when using a mouse and keyboard
  • 已解决 an issue where users with a large number of in-game friends couldn't see some of their friends' in-game status properly, preventing them from being able to be invited or join your game
  • Fixed an issue with player names and chat text disappearing

已知问题[edit | edit 来源]

这些问题要么是由本次补丁引入的且正在修复中，要么来自之前的版本且尚未修复。

Top Priority[edit | edit 来源]

  • Excessive ragdolling can cause a feeling of helplessness and frustration
  • 玩家 are unable to emote when flying through the air
  • 战略配备 balls bounce unpredictably off cliffs and some spots
  • the Reinforced 侦察纵步者 does not telegraph its rocket 攻击

中型 Priority[edit | edit 来源]

  • The 机器人 Barrager Tank's turret continues to work despite its body being destroyed
  • Firebomb Hellpods can no longer 摧毁 larger enemy 类型 such as tanks
  • 玩家 are unable to shoot properly while in the air using a 喷气背包
  • 增援 may not be available for 玩家 who join a game in progress
  • 玩家 can get stuck on 鹈鹕-1’s ramp during 撤离
  • 高伤害武器 will not 引爆 Hellbombs already present on the map
  • 玩家 using the Microsoft IME languages are unable to 类型 Korean, Japanese and Chinese 语言 characters in chat
  • 武器 with a 蓄力 机械师 can exhibit unintended 行为 when firing faster than the RPM (Rounds 每分钟) limit
