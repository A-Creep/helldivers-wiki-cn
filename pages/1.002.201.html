修复[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

崩溃 修复, Hangs and 柔软-locks:[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已修复 a 崩溃 that could occur when a 大 数字 of particles were displayed
  • 已修复 a rare 崩溃 when aiming scoped 武器
  • 已修复 一些 崩溃 when playing bot convoy missions
  • 已修复 a 崩溃 that could occur when receiving Microsoft Teams messages while playing the 游戏

武器 and 战略配备[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已修复 an 问题 where the 武器 flashlights were flickering while using ADS
  • 已修复 an 问题 for 能量 based 武器 not charging or overheating properly
  • 已修复 an 问题 with 错误 drag setting on the GL-21 榴弹发射器 projectiles that made them lose 速率 更快 than they should have
  • 已修复 an 问题 where shrapnel from 浮出地面-hits was forced in a roughly south-west 方向. Shrapnel should now properly 飞 out in a 180-ish 弧度 in the 方向 of the 浮出地面 hit

LAS-58 Talon[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已修复 an visual 问题 when reloading in ADS
  • 已修复 an 问题 for not being able to 换弹 without overheating
  • HUD 修复 to properly 显示 正确 弹药 陈述
  • Now properly resets the 温度 to 0 when reloading
  • 已修复 a visual 效果 问题 when holding down the 火焰 button
  • 已移除 a minor charging 延迟 to firing projectiles

AR-23C 解放者 Concussive[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

一些 unintentional changes made their way onto the 解放者 Concussive and the Breaker during the last 补丁. This is a revert of those changes.

  • Starting 弹匣 数量从2提升至4
  • 最大 spare 数量 of magazines从5提升至6
  • Ergonomic 效果 已增加 from 0 to -10

SG-225 Breaker[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Starting 弹匣 数量从6降低至5
  • 最大 spare 数量 of magazines从10降低至7

社交 & Multiplayer 修复[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已解决 an 问题 where the 账户 ID was not 可见 in the Options tab

Miscellaneous 修复[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已修复 an 问题 for the 毒气 武器, throwables and 战略配备 inverting the movement 输入 for the 绝地潜兵
  • The Jarndiver override for the hoverpacks have been turned off 有效 today. This is reserved for 基本信息 Brasch and other Super 10 之星 Generals(已修复 a bug with the draw and kick emotes that allowed 玩家 to stay floating in the air)
