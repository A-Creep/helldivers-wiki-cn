⚖️ 平衡性调整[edit | edit 来源]

Exo suits rebalance[edit | edit 来源]

We agree with the community that the 外骨骼机甲 can take too little 伤害 and break too easily, especially against the 机器人 阵营. Therefore we have made a bigger 平衡 pass to 增加 its 耐久度.

  • 已增加 主生命值 pool from 850 to 1600
  • The Exo suit will only die if the main healthpool is depleted. Before it could die if specific healthzones were depleted.
  • 已增加 弱点 to 酸 攻击 by 50%
  • Fixed so parts of the 驾驶舱 healthzones are not blocked by the ragdoll actor. Now the 驾驶舱 zones will be damageable in the right way.
  • 已增加 Exo suit 手臂生命值 from 350 to 600
  • Exo suit arms now also have a 50% 爆炸抗性 (just like the main body)
  • 已移除 构造 and bleedout on the Exo suit arms.
  • Exo suits no longer lose all mobility when both legs are broken.
  • Can now be staggered, have 硬直 力量 45. 硬直 does not affect the 能力 to shoot.
  • 已移除 most 生命值 zones that do not affect the 主生命值 and are mainly visual 效果. The reason is to 改进 performance. For 示例 tow cables and similar items can not be damaged or destroyed anymore.

Enemy 伤害 against 载具[edit | edit 来源]

We think 机器人 and illuminates do 伤害 reasonably well against the 载具 especially now with the changes to the Exo suits, the 终结族 however have fallen behind a bit in their 能力 to 应对 载具 so we have made some buffs to their 伤害 against 耐久 targets.
