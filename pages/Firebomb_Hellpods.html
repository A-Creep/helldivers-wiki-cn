火焰炸弹绝地喷射舱
“

Lines all Hellpods with volatile incendiaries that 引爆 upon impact, igniting any units in vicinity of the 掉落 site.

— 军械库 描述

Firebomb Hellpods is a 强化资源 in 绝地潜兵 2. When equipped, all Hellpods (manned or unmanned), will 引爆 upon impact with the 浮出地面. Due to the nature of the 强化资源, and its potential 伤害, notify all squadmates of its use in order to minimize 友军误伤.

描述

Firebomb Hellpods is a 强化资源 designed to 伤害 any entity that is near a 绝地喷射舱 掉落 site. Upon 联络 with the 浮出地面, a 小型 爆炸 will occur, dealing both 爆炸 and 燃烧弹 伤害. Since it affects every 绝地喷射舱 deployed by the squad, caution must be taken in order to prevent 友军误伤 or accidents, espcially during initial deployments, when the entire squad is equipping themselves.

采购

The Firebomb Hellpods 强化资源 is unlocked on the 3rd page of the 自由烈焰 尊享战争债券 for 60 奖章.

战术信息

  • The Firebomb Hellpods is not recommended for 更小 missions areas, such as 撤离高价值资产, where space is 有限 and the necessity to deploy 多个 绝地喷射舱-deployed 战略配备 are 必需 for 有效 coverage.
    • 所有哨戒炮都通过绝地喷射舱部署，因此在彼此附近部署它们会导致附近的哨戒炮被摧毁。
  • 如果装备了它，请通知队友其效果，以便他们调整投掷，要么确保最大程度消灭敌人，要么防止意外的友军误伤，尤其是在呼叫支援类战略配备时。
