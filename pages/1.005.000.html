Into the Unjust: 5.0.0

补丁 Highlights

  • For this 补丁, we have tackled a bunch of items from our 已知问题 list, including audio issues, XP modifiers, and 几个 崩溃.
  • We have made more optimizations and improvements!
  • Balancing changes that raise weapon 伤害 and fixes to weapon FOV.

玩法

Keybind options for quick weapon 功能:

  • New, unbound keybind options have been added for quickly swapping weapon functions (like changing 火焰 modes or 弹药).
  • The intent is to give 玩家 better ease of use, especially for 武器 with 多个 弹药 类型 like the Halt, 机炮, and the new "One Two" AR.

Updated First Person View (FPV)

  • We updated how First Person View (FPV) works with the 意图 of improving the aiming 经验值 across different FOV settings (Field of view).
  • The intent is that the size and 距离 of the scope will be more consistent with different FOV settings.
  • We also updated how 人体工学 works in FPV with the intent to make it more responsive.
  • Fixed instances of flickering textures in various city locations
