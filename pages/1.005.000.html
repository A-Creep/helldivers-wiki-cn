已减少 人体工学 penalty on magnifying scopes 武器 attachments The intent is to make magnifying scopes 更多 a matter of preference by decreasing the penalty they impose on a 武器's 人体工学 (低 人体工学 makes the 武器 muzzle 关注 the crosshair 更慢).

  • The 手枪 发 re-平衡 基本信息 intent is to make them 更多 有效 at 近距离 while not 更多 有效 at 远距离 then for 示例 特攻 or 射手 rifles

2x Tube Red Dot

  • 已减少 ergonomic 修正 from (-3) to (-1)

4x Combat Scope

  • 已减少 ergonomic 修正 from (-6) to (-2)

10x 狙击手 Scope

  • 已减少 ergonomic 修正 from (-9) to (-4)
  • APW-1 反器材步枪 is also effected by this 变化 so it will get an 已增加 人体工学 from 26 to 31

Keybind options for quick 武器 功能:

  • New, unbound keybind options have been 已添加 for quickly swapping 武器 functions (点赞 changing 火焰 modes or 弹药).
  • The intent is to give 玩家 更好 ease of use, especially for 武器 with 多个 弹药 类型 点赞 the Halt, 机炮, and the new "One Two" AR.

Updated First Person 查看 (FPV)

  • We updated how First Person 查看 (FPV) works with the 意图 of improving the aiming 经验值 across different FOV settings (田野 of 查看).
  • The intent is that the 尺寸 and 距离 of the scope will be 更多 consistent with different FOV settings.
  • We also updated how 人体工学 works in FPV with the intent to make it 更多 responsive.
  • 已修复 instances of flickering textures in various 城市 locations
