Stealth as a 策略[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Utilizing stealth during missions is a 形成 of 优化 developed from the following observations:

  1. Combat slows down 任务 completion and risks deaths (and 亏损 of 装备/samples) without contributing 经验值, 申购点 or 任务 progress (outside of 闪电战/Eradication missions).
  2. Certain 敌人 can spawn aggro'd onto a 绝地潜兵, including 追踪虫, 尖啸虫, 蟑龙, 刺魟, and 炮舰.
  3. Being seen by 敌人 can 原因 them to 呼叫 增援, (运输舰, 穿梭舰, and Bug 突破), which contain 更多 and 更多 困难 敌人 to engage the 绝地潜兵.

The majority of this 引导 is therefore oriented towards minimizing 敌人 增援, as these are 最多 threatening to 绝地潜兵 and have the potential to snowball: 压力 from 高-威胁 敌人 spawned as 增援 提供 太空 and 时间 for 更小 敌人 to 呼叫 更多 增援. The 更多 敌人 present in an 范围, the harder it is to de-aggro them through stuns or line of sight blocking because of their 更大 map coverage, which will likely 掩护 目标 or samples.

By contrast, a 大 群组 of infantry is 相当 高 风险 to 攻击, given the likelihood of 剩余 members calling 增援 after one or two targets are killed. Their 武器 have 非常 低 指挥 威胁, but they represent and project the 更大 威胁 of the 敌人 they can summon.
