As they are the 敌人 with the most fleshed out 机制 regarding to stealth 玩法, this 条款 will focus largely on the  机器人 Legion.

Stealth as a 策略

Utilizing stealth during missions is a form of 优化 developed from the following observations:

  1. Combat slows down 任务 completion and risks deaths (and loss of 装备/samples) without contributing 经验值, 申购点 or 任务 progress (outside of Blitz/Eradication missions).
  2. Certain 敌人 can spawn aggro'd onto a 绝地潜兵, including 追踪虫, 尖啸虫, 蟑龙, 刺魟，以及 炮舰.
  3. Being seen by 敌人 can cause them to 呼叫 增援, (运输舰, 穿梭舰，以及 Bug 突破), which contain more and more 困难 敌人 to engage the 绝地潜兵.

The majority of this guide is therefore oriented towards minimizing enemy 增援, as these are most threatening to 绝地潜兵 and have the potential to snowball: 压力 from high-威胁 敌人 spawned as 增援 提供 space and time for 更小 敌人 to call more 增援. The more 敌人 present in an area, the harder it is to de-aggro them through stuns or line of sight blocking because of their 更大 map coverage, which will likely cover 目标 or samples.

Enemy 行为

敌人 detect 绝地潜兵 by sound and then sight. 敌人 通常 become alert to a 绝地潜兵’s presence by sight in their vision cone. This can vary wildly depending on 距离, the height of the enemy, and the stance of the 绝地潜兵. 绝地潜兵 that are sprinting are most 可见, while 绝地潜兵 that are prone are the least 可见.

Nearby 敌人 will join in, and will begin closing in on their 目标 after a short delay. 进攻型 战略配备 (Eagles and Orbitals) automatically escalate 被动 敌人 to this level, although they will not automatically know the exact position of the 绝地潜兵, only the 基本信息 方向 the 战略配备 came from.

Patrols

It is 重要 to note that Patrols are player-biased, 含义 they will spawn marching towards a 绝地潜兵 but not aggroed. As a 策略, patrols are best left unbothered as killing even one member of a patrol will alert the entire squad, likely leading to a 增援 call. Enemy 增援

The goal of stealth is to minimize 增援, but they will inevitably still occur as the result of occasional mistakes. The 增援 动画 can, however, be interrupted. 机器人 have a subtle but long 动画 of pulling out and preparing their flare which can be interrupted. 终结族 have a distinct retching audio cue and 动画 and will start to 生产 a thin orange trail before a 虫族入侵 is opened. 光能者 要求 a 守望者 to call a 增援, with a very long audio and 动画 cue that can be easily interrupted.

Units will only call for 增援 after visually detecting and engaging either a 绝地潜兵 or one of their proxies (哨戒炮, 外骨骼机甲, Tanks, etc.).

Some 目标 (notably the 地质勘探 和 升起旗帜) will cause 增援 to spawn when initiated.

Decision Making

The key factor in determining whether to 消除 a given enemy in a stealth 任务 is the risk of them calling 增援 if left alive. 绝地潜兵 will want to 目标 the 敌人 that can call 增援 first, such as Watchers and 轻型 机器人 units (Commissars, Troopers, etc.). 敌人 that are capable of quickly dispatching a 绝地潜兵 (工厂 Striders, Tanks, Cannon 炮塔, etc.) are best left alone and at a great 距离, as they can 有时 aggro silently and from considerable 距离, proceeding to kill 绝地潜兵 unaware.

This has some immediate implications. Groups of 敌人 with only one infantry member capable of calling 增援 are relatively low risk to 攻击. If that enemy is eliminated first the 剩余 敌人 can easily be contained and eliminated. By contrast, a large group of infantry is quite high risk to 攻击, given the likelihood of 剩余 members calling 增援 after one or two targets are killed. Their 武器 have very low direct 威胁, but they represent and project the 更大 威胁 of the 敌人 they can summon.

What to do when caught

When 敌人 are alerted and 绝地潜兵 are not in the immediate vicinity of an 目标, the most prudent 策略 is to 撤退.
