概述

The flag of The Collective

The 机器人 are mindless, bloodthirsty robots, coded for nothing but murder and socialist violence. Their origins are a 谜团, but their unthinking hatred of Freedom makes them a 威胁 to all citizens of 超级地球.

— Playstation Store 描述

the 机器人 - colloquially known as Bots - are intelligent robotic beings created by the 生化人 before the events of the 第二次银河战争. [1] [2] [3] They are fully capable of feeling emotions - such as sorrow and anger[4] - and having independent thoughts. They predominantly utilize fusion 武器, rockets, and 近战 武器.

the 机器人 first appeared in the south-western 星区 of the 银河地图 with a 先锋 force. Their main force would only arrive later during The Reclamation, settling in the Valdis 分区 and re-uniting with their creators on Cyberstan.

In terms of 玩法, the 机器人 utilize various 类型 of 远程 武器库 and heavily-有护甲 units to overwhelm the 绝地潜兵.

背景故事

Origin

“

Vessel 00 is indicated to be a spaceship that departed from the 行星 Cyberstan in 2180, 大约 4 years prior to the initial 机器人 攻击. The cargo and destination of that voyage is 未知, but it is referred to as the final one the ship made. Vessel 00 is referred to in exultation - 证据 of 机器人 programming to express reverence towards it. It is additionally referred to as their seed or origin. This intel raises many troubling questions. The origin of the 机器人 has been a top intel priority since their initial barbaric 特攻. This information is the first to definitively 连接 the 机器人 to Cyberstan, a rehabilitation 行星 invaded by them in the early days of the war.

— 重要指令 A2-10-1 结果 briefing

According to intell gathered from 数据库 One, the 机器人 originated from Cyberstan. In 2180, a spacecraft known as Vessel 00 departed from Cyberstan to an 未知 destination, said vessel being referred by the 机器人 as their seed or origin.

机器人 Social 媒体 Takeover

See 主页: 机器人 Social 媒体 Takeover

Between 2月 5th and 2月 9th, 2026, all 官员 绝地潜兵 2 social medial channels were completely rebranded to 机器人 versions. The social platforms adopted a pro-机器人 stance and sent various messages in binary calling for the citizens of the 超级地球 联邦 to stop the ongoing push to Cyberstan, which was part of 操作 Valid Pretext. Various altered versions of 绝地潜兵 宣传 were also posted during the event.

The event ended on 2月 9th with the reveal of the   Cyborg Legion and the Machinery of 压迫 更新 trailer. All platforms then gradually reverted back to normal.
