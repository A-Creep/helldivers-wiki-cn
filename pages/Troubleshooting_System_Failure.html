Overclocking and undervolting directly alter the time it takes for these logic gates to charge and 放电 their 电力 signals. If the voltage is dropped too low, the 电力 signal takes slightly longer to build up. As a result, the gate attempts to read the data before the state switch has fully completed. When this happens, the 电脑 processes corrupted data or mathematical nonsense. A Blue Screen of Death (BSOD) is simply the operating system's 软件 防御 mechanism responding to a catastrophic breakdown in the hardware's logical consistency shutting down the system before these corrupted calculations can corrupt your OS configuration or other data.

Or to make it simpler: Imagine a water system that 使用次数 latches to 运输 water (voltage) from A to B. In order to 继续 you need to open and close latches; you can only open latches if the water 压力 (电力 charge) is high enough. If you need water quickly but there's low 压力 (undervolting) then the latch simply cannot be opened quickly enough so the water doesn't get where it needs to be in a timely fashion.
