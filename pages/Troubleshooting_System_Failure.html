A system freeze is a situation in which the system gets "stuck"; you are unable to enter commands, move the mouse, close the 当前 window or conduct any other operations without the system returning a BSOD. 电源

The other big factor when it comes to sudden shutoffs (or even system 冰冻!) is 电击. This can occur when a "transient spike" triggers the Power Supply's (PSU) Over-当前 保护 (OCP) or Over-Power 保护 (OPP). What happens here is that the sudden draw of power trips the safety mechanism of your PSU. Technically the system does what it's supposed to do, however it shouldn't trigger such an event simply because it is strained by a game.

瞬态尖峰

In order to understand how a transient spike trips the overcurrent 保护, let's take a look at that:[1]

All hardware employs some kind of 能量 saving technology. This means your hardware will run less power-hungry and much cooler during phases in which only little performance is needed. However, when you start up a game, your hardware will suddenly have to kick up a gear and therefore 要求 a lot more power in order to reach certain speeds. When this happens, we are looking at a "transient spike" - a sudden, rapid microsecond power demand that causes the wattage to spike. In a few milliseconds the hardware will have to pull in a lot of power to perform much faster.

Now this is even more pronounced if you are playing a game. A game almost never has a linear way of functioning. Loads differ from second to second. One moment you run over an empty desert and the 下一个 moment an 轨道武器 弹幕 hammers down on the map, things break, 敌人 die, etc. The GPU has to load in new textures or calculate 轻型 particles which were not in the memory before, a sudden surge of 敌人 requires more CPU calculations and so on. Pair this with modern boost technologies which 要求 an 增加 in core voltage in order to perform well under load and you will see that the system has to draw in a lot more power at times at a very rapid pace. On the other hand you will see that the hardware will clock down when things are idling, so as to reduce power draw, heat 世代 and noise.

AVX

This is even more of a stress-test for any system, as 绝地潜兵 2 utilizes AVX (Advanced Vector Extensions), a CPU 指示 set that is utilized to do a lot of very 复杂 math at a rapid pace. In order to do all of these mathematical calculations (as 绝地潜兵 2 simulates almost everything), the 电脑 will use a wider bus (256 bit or 512 bit) inside the CPU and load heaps of data straight from the RAM into the CPU. Such a 重型 load requires a lot of power, which requires a lot of power and therefore 增加 the transient spikes even further (not as intense as GPUs but still noticeably).

When the CPU engages AVX, it first encounters a slight 减少 of internal voltage in the CPU. Such a 流程 is called "vDroop" and occurs whenever a CPU has to change from idle to full load. During this 流程 the internal voltage in the CPU will slightly 减少, making the CPU 极其 unstable. If the PSU or the VRMs of the Motherboard aren't working properly, the CPU will simply crash.

It's also 重要 to note that AVX will stay on during your time playing. AVX draws a 庞大, sustained 当前 the entire time it runs, heavily heating up the silicon and dropping the safety margin for 稳定性. 更小 issues therefore can quickly cause 崩溃 when a 流程 使用次数 AVX opposed to 软件 that does not utilize it.

Furthermore motherboards employ something called an "AVX-Offset", which will cause the CPU to reduce it's clock 速度 (e.g 200 - 300 MHz, maybe more or less) when an AVX 指示 is used as the steep power draw results in 庞大 heat 世代 (which is why your CPU runs hotter during 绝地潜兵 2 opposed to other video games). This combats potential overheating and 崩溃 from thermal / 当前 limits.

功耗

Modern GPUs can cause 极其 high transient spikes - modern NVIDIA RTX 50-series and AMD RX 9000-series GPUs can generate 150% of their rated TDP in transient spikes lasting 100 microseconds. As an 示例: A RTX 5090 rated at 575W can momentarily spike to 860W when a transient spike occurs during a scene transition or shader compilation step. Such a change occurs in under a millisecond, requiring the PSU to 运送 this wattage without the 12V rail sagging. Older PSUs simply were not designed to handle such loads without shutting down.

Especially aging, underpowered or budget PSUs can 经验值 problems 相关 to transient spikes, resulting in emergency shutdowns to 保护 your components from an 电力 surge. Alternatively it's also possible that the 当前 for CPU, RAM or GPU 减少 just slightly below the 致命 最小, which immediately causes the hardware to lose 稳定性. As a result you see that the image freezes up and the PC suddenly stops reacting altogether.

ATX 3.0 和 3.1 规范的现代电源明确要求电源在 100 微秒内处理 200% 的瞬时偏移，而不触发任何保护机制。

超频/欠压

Overclocking and undervolting can create both Blue Screens and sudden shutoffs, which is why it's recommended to turn all overclocking and undervolting off. Especially very 激进 settings can easily trigger problems.

超频和降压包括（但不限于）：

  • Precision Boost Overdrive (PBO)
  • Intel 200s 加速
  • 华硕多核增强
  • XMP / EXPO / DOCP
  • 出厂 GPU 超频。

Why this matters: 无论你是将时钟速度推得超出当前电压所能承受的范围，还是在降低电压的同时保持时钟不变，对芯片的物理影响都是相同的：处理器实际上被饿死了电能。

In 技术信息 terms, digital processors consist of billions of microscopic logic gates that must switch states synchronously according to an internal timer. Overclocking and undervolting directly alter the time it takes for these logic gates to charge and 放电 their 电力 signals. If the voltage is dropped too low, the 电力 signal takes slightly longer to build up. As a result, the gate attempts to read the data before the state switch has fully completed. In the hardware industry, this is formally known as a "Timing Violation" or a "Logic Race 条件".

When this happens, the 电脑 processes corrupted data or mathematical nonsense. A Blue Screen of Death (BSOD) is simply the operating system's 软件 防御 mechanism responding to a catastrophic breakdown in the hardware's logical consistency shutting down the system before these corrupted calculations can corrupt your OS configuration or other data.

Or to make it simpler: Imagine a water system that 使用次数 latches to 运输 water (voltage) from A to B. In order to 继续 you need to open and close latches; you can only open latches if the water 压力 (电力 charge) is high enough. If you need water quickly but there's low 压力 (undervolting) then the latch simply cannot be opened quickly enough so the water doesn't get where it needs to be in a timely fashion.

超频

Overclocking requires more voltage to 维护 higher speeds. In turn this generates more heat. An unstable overclock can lead to a BSOD. In simpler terms it fails to operate in a safe 电力 or thermal window, trading lifespan and 稳定性 for 速度.

降压

Undervolting too much can potentially trigger either a BSOD or an UVP (Undervoltage 保护)[3].

  • A GPU is rarely stressed during the initial system boot or desktop idling. Therefore, an unstable GPU undervolt will almost never prevent your PC from turning on. Instead, it will running fine on your desktop, only to instantly crash to desktop or freeze once a 重型 3D load like 绝地潜兵 2 is applied.
  • A CPU manages the low-level Windows kernel initialization, driver sorting, and cryptographic checks from the very first millisecond. An unstable CPU undervolt can trigger an immediate failure during startup, locking your system in an infinite bootloop, 冰冻 before the desktop loads, or throwing early Blue Screens.

Be aware:

  • 如果你遇到突然关机，那就是电源的 UVP（欠压保护）被触发了。
  • If the CPU is starved for voltage you instead are presented with a BSOD with an error message such as WHEA_UNCORRECTABLE_ERROR or DPC_看门狗_VIOLATION.
