描述[edit | edit 来源]

The Damaged Hellbomb is a 类型 of 危害 consisting of a Hellbomb lodged into the 地形. It may be triggered by dealing any 伤害 to it; after a short time, it will 爆炸 with a 庞大 25m blast radius. However, any 攻击 with a 最小 拆除力 of 30 will instead 引爆 them immediately. It is best used to easily dispatch any crowds of 敌人 or high-生命值 units that are nearby. The 爆炸 is similar, if not the same, in 效果 as a regular Hellbomb 战略配备. Damaged Hellbombs may be found around the map during 挑战 missions or higher, or in the Deadlands 生物群系 on all difficulties.

次要特殊地点[edit | edit 来源]

Damaged Hellbombs can 偶尔 be found as a Minor Place of Interest. 支援武器 such as the FLAM-40 火焰喷射器 和 LAS-90 激光大炮 can 有时 be found 下一个 to it. Additionally, inscribed words may be found on the Hellbomb (see Words Scribbled on Bomb).
