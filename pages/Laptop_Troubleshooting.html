Basic Performance & 稳定性[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Laptops have stricter thermal and 之力 limits than Desktops. 绝地潜兵 2 is demanding enough to expose those limits and can potentially 原因 throttling, 崩溃 or shutdowns.

基本信息 技巧:

  • Plug your Laptop directly into the 墙.
    • Do not run the 游戏 using the 电池.
    • Avoid 弱 之力 strips.
    • 套装 Windows 之力 计划 to "平衡" (Recommended for 最多 Laptops)
  • Ensure the 使用 of the 正确 GPU.
    • Hellbomb Script -> Graphics Options 菜单 -> 选择 正确 GPU.
    • Manually: Go to "Windows Graphics Settings" -> 套装 绝地潜兵 2 to "高 Performance".
    • Use the recommended GPU 驾驶员.
  • Please 监控 temperatures under 负载.
    • You can use HWiNFO64 for 传感器 logging. If the 软件 reports overheating, it's imperative to clean the vents and ensure proper airflow.
  • 安装 the 游戏 on a 固态硬盘 / NVMe (not HDD!).
    • Keep at 最少 20% free 太空 for 最好 performance.
  • Make sure your pagefile is enabled and 套装 as 星系 Managed on at 最少 one disk.
    • This is an absolute MUST for 16GB or 更少 RAM.

  • iGPU-only systems are below 最小 spec and 最多 likely will 任一 not 开始 at 全部 or perform 非常 badly.
  • Older 4-核心 CPUs 经常 原因 bottlenecks.
    • See User Settings Config FPS 优化 for 帮助 with settings.

Stuttering[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

If you are on a Laptop and 经验值 stuttering despite 全部 other 故障排除 steps, please try the following:

  • 下载 GPU-Z
  • 选择 your 高-结束 GPU, then go to the "Sensors" tab.
  • Launch the 游戏 until you encounter stuttering.
  • Scroll down to the "PerfCap 理性" 田野.

Should this be the 案例, the quickest way to 解决 this 问题 is to perform a CMOS 重置 by holding the laptop's 之力 button for 30 to 60 秒.

This can happen because of 几个 reasons:

  • Aftermarket or 低-wattage 强袭虫
  • Laptop 之力 管理 (EC/firmware) getting stuck in a 差 陈述.
  • 电池 degradation or 之力 运送 issues.
  • 长 periods of being constantly plugged in (can 有时 贡献 to EC 陈述 issues).

Others possible warnings:

  • Thrm - Laptop is overheating.
    • Clean fans/vents; if needed, replace thermal 粘贴.
  • Util - GPU 使用 is too 低
    • Likely CPU 瓶颈 or scheduling 问题 (检查 故障排除 Stutters/短 Freezes as well)
  • VRel / VOp - Normal 行为
    • The 问题 is likely elsewhere (CPU, drivers, background tasks, etc.)
