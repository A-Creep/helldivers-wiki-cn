战术信息

  • The TX-41 Sterilizer functions similarly to the FLAM-40 火焰喷射器. It spews out caustic 毒气 that slows and confuses 敌人.
    • The Sterilizer has a 射程 of 30 meters, measured in first person.
    • 毒气 expelled from the Sterilizer has a 每秒伤害 and 耐久 每秒伤害 of 25, lingers for 大约 0.25 秒 in the air, and cannot stick to 浮出地面, unlike flames and napalm.
    • Unlike the 火焰喷射器, the Sterilizer doesn't have a firing delay due to the lack of a pilot 轻型.
    • 敌人 under the 效果 of the caustic 毒气 are slowed by 25% and become confused, causing them to run into different directions while uncontrollably attacking anything nearby, potentially hitting their own allies.
    • The Sterilizer's canisters contain enough 毒气 for roughly 16 秒 of continuous spraying.
  • With its low 每秒伤害 and strong 困惑 效果, the Sterilizer is a purely crowd-controlling tool that is best used in short bursts - quickly spraying 毒气 to inflict the 效果 and changing to another weapon to finish off the slowed and confused 敌人.
  • The Sterilizer's spray is affected by wind, which can cause 毒气 to drift away from the point of aim or even back toward the operator. Wind 方向 can be estimated by observing 绝地潜兵 capes, rainfall, or 环境 objects like foliage and flags.
  • Diving directly backwards while shooting the Sterilizer will deal 伤害 to the 绝地潜兵, due to their legs passing in 正面 of the nozzle. This 伤害 can be 已降低 by equipping armors with the 高级过滤能力 被动技能。
