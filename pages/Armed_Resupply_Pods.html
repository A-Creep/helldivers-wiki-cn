Mounts a modified AR-23 解放者 to all 重新补给 pods, allowing them to double as 全自动 炮塔.

The Armed 重新补给 Pods are a 强化资源 in 绝地潜兵 2. When equipped, all 重新补给 Pods come mounted with an automated custom AR-23P 解放者 Penetrator lasting until ran out of bullets or the pod itself getting demolished.

类似于哨戒炮战略配备，该武器会瞄准并射击30米内的任何非绝地潜兵敌对目标。
