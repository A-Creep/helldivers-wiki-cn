奖章

奖章 are a 货币 used in 绝地潜兵 2. They are used for unlocking 战争债券 内容.

采购

The 最大 number of 奖章 any 绝地潜兵 can have is 250. Any additional 奖章 earned beyond this limit are discarded.

奖章 can be obtained on any 难度 through various means:

  • 1-3 can be collected from cases found at Minor Places of Interest, inside pods, containers, or bunkers.
    • These are instantly added to every present 绝地潜兵's 账户 upon pickup. 任务 completion is not 必需.
  • 1-15 can be earned by completing Missions based on the chosen 难度.
  • 15 are awarded for completing 个人指令.
  • 35-100 from completing 主要命令.

战场情报

  • 特殊 loot containers containing 奖章 are more likely to be found on difficulties 简单 through 中型, since their spawns are not taken up by 稀有样本 in those difficulties.
