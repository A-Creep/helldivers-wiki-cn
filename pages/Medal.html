Medals are a 货币 used in 绝地潜兵 2. They are used for unlocking 战争债券 内容.

获取途径[edit | edit 来源]

The 最大 number of medals any 绝地潜兵 can have is 250. Any additional medals earned beyond this limit are discarded.

Medals can be obtained on any 难度 through various means:

  • 1-3 can be collected from cases found at 次要兴趣点, inside pods, containers, or bunkers.
    • These are instantly added to every present 绝地潜兵's 账户 upon pickup. 任务 completion is not 必需.
  • 1-15 can be earned by completing Missions based on the chosen 难度.
  • 15 are awarded for completing 个人命令.
  • 35-100 from completing 主要命令.
