概述[edit | edit 来源]

In the 第二次银河战争, 玩家 take the role of The 绝地潜兵 and fight across various 星球 in order to secure 领土 for the  超级地球 联邦, as well as following 命令 issued by High 指挥. By completing Missions 和 Operations 玩家 are able to deal 伤害 to a 行星's 生命池, eventually depleting it and resulting in a successful campaign, securing 控制 over the 行星.

机制 Summarized[edit | edit 来源]

A quick summary of keywords and relevant 机制.

  • 绝地潜兵 Impact: At the end of each successful 任务, a certain amount of 影响 is generated depending on the 任务's 难度, higher generating more. Only the 主要目标's completion matters for 影响.[1][2][3] This value is multiplied by the Impact 修正 and then dealt to the 行星's 生命池 as 伤害.
    • Impact 修正: A value that scales inversely proportional to the number of missions completed (successful and failed) within a 30 minute window. Effectively this means that: Many active 绝地潜兵 = less impact 每次任务. Fewer active 绝地潜兵 = more impact 每次任务.
  • Planets: 每颗星球 has an individual 生命池 -varying on a 行星-to-行星 and Campaign basis- which must be depleted in order to secure a successful 占领/防御 of the 行星.
    • Warp Links: Commonly known as "Supply Lines", connects planets together. In order for a Campaign to be launched or stay active, the 目标 行星 must be linked to at least one 超级地球-controlled 行星, or be under 超级地球 控制 itself if it is a 保卫战役.
  • Campaigns: In order to fight for a 行星, a campaign must be active on it. Different campaigns provide different 机制.
    • 解放 Campaigns: Campaigns with no 时间限制. 绝地潜兵 must 克服 Enemy 抗性 in order to deplete the 行星's 生命池. Campaign will immediately end in loss if no Warp Links to 超级地球 held planets remain.
      • Enemy 抗性: Commonly known as "Decay". The % of 行星's 生命池 regenerated every hour. Due to 星球生命值 pools being different, the % may be misleading when comparing with other planets' 抗性.
    • 防御 Campaign: Campaigns with a 时间限制 and NO Enemy 抗性. 绝地潜兵 must deplete the 行星's 生命池 before the timer expires.
      • Gambit: Capturing the 来源 of a 保卫战役 (indicated by arrows on the Warp Link) will immediately end the connected 防御 in the 绝地潜兵' favor.
    • High Priority Campaign (HPC): Campaigns with their own exclusive 机制 and exclusive missions. 通常 utilized during key story points.
  • Regions: Commonly known as "Mega City" or "City". Each region has its own individual 生命池 AND Enemy 抗性, 分离 from the 行星's. Upon 占领, the 行星's 生命池 is dealt a significant amount of 伤害, varying with the Region's 生命值.


机制 in Detail[edit | edit 来源]

As in the first game, The 绝地潜兵 participate in a dynamic 星系战争 against enemy 阵营.

The 第二次银河战争 in 绝地潜兵 2 is notably different from its 前任 in a few key ways.

  • The war is persistent, does not reset, and is managed by a human Game Master.
  • 每颗星球 has its own 生命值/解放 meter, not the 分区.
  • 主要命令 are utilized to 运送 a narrative across the state of the 星系战争.

The 基本信息 idea is simple. The 终结族, 机器人 and the 光能者 have seized 控制 of 超级地球's valuable worlds, and the 绝地潜兵 must take them back and prevent the enemy from taking any more planets.

The 银河地图[edit | edit 来源]

The available planets to play on in 绝地潜兵 2 are determined by the 阵营 that currently controls them, and which planets they are connected to via Warp Links.

When two planets connected by a Warp Link are controlled by different 阵营, one 行星 can mount a 行星 攻击 on the other 行星 on said Warp Link, which will trigger a 战役。

Whenever a 行星 is being liberated or defended, that is referred to as a 战役。

Campaigns[edit | edit 来源]

In order for a 行星 to be playable, it needs to have a Campaign active. There are various 类型 of campaigns, each with their own intricacies.

解放 Campaigns[edit | edit 来源]

In 解放 Campaigns the 目标 is to liberate the 行星 from enemy 控制. The 行星 has an internal value known as 星球生命值, which is not to be confused with 解放 百分比, the value shown in game.

For a 行星 to be liberated, 超级地球 has to mount a 行星 攻击 from one of the Supply Line connected planets under their 控制.

The 目标 is simple. Reduce the 行星's 生命值 to 0. Each successful 任务 and on completion of all missions in an 操作 the 操作 bonus chips away at the 行星's 生命值, with higher 难度 missions/operations dealing even more 伤害 to the 行星. Should the 行星 be liberated, then new campaigns will launch from the liberated world.

However, should 超级地球 lose all avenues of 攻击 to a 行星, then the 解放 Campaign will end in failure.

Operations in a 解放 Campaign are often subjected to one or two 操作 Modifiers, depending on the 难度.

星球生命值[edit | edit 来源]

Without any Regions, 每颗星球 has a 最大 生命值 of 1,000,000. Should a 行星 have regions, however, this max 生命值 will 增加 depending on the number 和 类型 of regions.

  1. 每个 定居点 (★) adds an additional 100k Max HP
  2. 每个 城镇 (★★) adds an additional 200k Max HP
  3. 每个 城市 (★★★) adds an additional 400k Max HP
  4. 每个 特大城市 (★★★★) adds an additional 600k Max HP
Decay Rates[edit | edit 来源]

Of course, the 敌人 of 超级地球 are not going to just let the 绝地潜兵 walk over them. As such, they will actively unliberate the planets by a fixed amount every hour known as the 衰退速率. This value is shown as a 百分比 as Enemy 抗性 ingame, and can also be seen via third party applications such as 绝地潜兵 Companion.

Efforts to liberate a 行星 are wasted if too 小型 a 百分比 of the 绝地潜兵 人口 is performing missions at sufficient Difficulties on a 行星 to overtake the 衰退速率. This 最小 number of active 绝地潜兵 varies depending on how many 玩家 are online, their 比例 across 每颗星球, and how many of said 比例 is performing missions.

The 衰退速率 is not 相关 in any way to how 困难 each 任务 is on a 行星. This is a common misconception.[4]

Sieging[edit | edit 来源]

Should an enemy controlled 行星 be completely surrounded by 超级地球 controlled planets, the 衰退速率 of these planets will turn 负面, and will begin to self-liberate. This is referred to as a 围城.

High-Priority Campaigns[edit | edit 来源]

有时, a 行星 in game is marked as a 首要战役, such as during 操作 Enduring Peace and the first gloom 远征. High Priority Campaigns behave a bit differently from your regular 解放 Campaign.

These are 有时 called Story Campaigns by the community. Clearing these campaigns requires following along with the 当前 主要命令.

通常, these are accompanied by an absurdly high 衰退速率. This 衰退速率 is lowered as 通常 未知 milestones are hit, provided a large majority of the playerbase actively participates in the 重要指令.

防御 Campaigns[edit | edit 来源]

基本信息 百分比 of active 绝地潜兵 needed to successfully defend a 行星 based on 防御 HP.

The Enemy 阵营 can also mount 攻击 on 超级地球 worlds, triggering a 防御 Campaign.

Unlike 解放 Campaigns, 防御 Campaigns have their own internal 生命值 value that varies depending on the 力量 of the enemy force attacking the world. 通常, it's far less than a 解放 Campaign's usual 1,000,000 生命值, and there is no active 衰退速率 while the 防御 is active. The 防御 Campaign also has a 截止日期. For the 平均 行星 this is 24 hours. If a Megacity or Megafactory is present this goes up to 48 hours, unless the 攻击 is lead by the Jet Brigade who will bring the 截止日期 back down to 24 hours. 岩浆, Deciduous/秋季 Forest, and 绿洲 planets, despite not having Megacities or Megafactories, all have have their deadlines 已增加 to 48 hours. This includes 攻击 led by the Jet Brigade.

The 目标 is to reduce the 防御's HP to 0 to repel the enemy 攻击. Failure to completely liberate the 行星 by the 防御 Campaign's 截止日期 will result in the 行星 being lost to the enemy, and a new 解放 Campaign will launch for the fallen world should there still be a connected 行星. The fallen 行星 will begin at 0% 解放 after a 防御 Campaign no matter the progress achieved before the timer expires.

Missions played on planets undergoing a 防御 Campaign will never have any 操作 Modifiers, though Environmental Conditions will still be present.

Gambits[edit | edit 来源]

Of course, that's not the only way to win a 防御 campaign. Since the Enemy 阵营 must mount 攻击 from a 行星 they own, the 绝地潜兵 have the option to simply liberate that 行星 before the 防御 ends. This is referred to as a Gambit by the community.

Gambits, while preferred to completing the 防御 normally, are not always viable. If, for 示例, the attacking 行星 has too much 生命值 or a high 衰退速率, it becomes infeasible for the 绝地潜兵 to retake the 行星 before the 防御 campaign ends.

It should be noted that the Democracy Space 车站's 轨道武器 Blockade does NOT auto-gambit attacking planets. It can only prevent 攻击 from being mounted from a specific 行星.

亟待解放[edit | edit 来源]

Urgent 解放 campaigns are 防御 Campaigns in reverse. Instead of an enemy 阵营 attacking a 超级地球 controlled 行星, 超级地球 攻击 the enemy 行星.

The same rules for 防御 Campaigns apply, but 玩家 must take the 行星 before the timer runs out.

入侵 Campaigns[edit | edit 来源]

入侵 Campaigns were a campaign 类型 exclusive to the 光能者 in the first months of their arrival. They were similar to the 防御 Campaign, differing in some factors.

入侵 Campaigns often had 操作 Modifiers and did not possess an 攻击 origin via Warp Links. If the 绝地潜兵 lost an 入侵 Campaign, the 行星 still remained under 超级地球's 控制. 入侵 Campaigns predominately featured semi-urbanized environments described in Colonies.

Currently, only The 光能者 have initiated 入侵 Campaigns.

Recon Campaigns[edit | edit 来源]

超级地球不会解放侦察战役中的星球，但只要该分区坚持反抗，这些星球就仍有希望。

Regions[edit | edit 来源]

Planets have a varying amount or no Regions built onto them.

  • Each Region has its own 抗性 rating independent of the 行星 抗性. This 抗性 can also be present during 防御 campaigns.
  • Successfully liberating / fully securing back a Region deals 伤害 to the 行星's HP equivalent to 1.5 times the Region's HP.
  • Some Regions can be locked out depending on the 行星's overall progression. Their availability depends on:
    • 绝地潜兵 控制 over the 行星 during 解放 campaigns; Some Regions are locked behind the 行星's 解放进度.
    • Time left during 防御 campaigns; More Regions become 脆弱 as time passes.
  • Modifiers can be tied to Regions, for 示例: The 光能者 has the Leviathan Blockade 修正 present in Regions under their 控制 starting at 不可能 难度.

Whenever a squad successfully completes a 任务 on the 行星, a value known as 任务 influence is calculated. See the table to the left.

更高 难度 missions have much more influence than lower 难度 missions. If an entire 操作 is completed, the 任务 Influence for the final 任务 will be multiplied by four.[5]

In both cases, it will then be multiplied by a changing value known as the Galactic Impact 修正, resulting in a value called Impact.

Impact is then subtracted from the 当前 行星 or event 生命值.

The exact formula for the 'Galactic Impact 修正 is 未知. It used to be said that it scaled based on how many successful missions are completed across a rolling 30-minute window, although this can be simplified to the 比例 of player online.

This means that 行星 伤害 is based on where the 比例 of 玩家 currently online and preforming missions are, not the raw 玩家人数.

Only missions started by a host will count towards 行星 解放. Kicking 玩家 midway does not create new 伤害 instances.

Ramp Up Time[edit | edit 来源]

解放 is only generated by completed missions and operations, just sitting on a 行星 doesn’t liberate them.

Ramp up time is the period of time between when the bulk of 绝地潜兵 move to a new 行星 and when the majority of 绝地潜兵 have begun to 完成 operations and deal 可见 解放 to the 行星.



Some Takeaways[edit | edit 来源]

  • Spamming 简单 missions does NOT help the war effort. Due to the impact 倍率, it 实际上 hinders the overall performance on 每颗星球.
  • Higher 难度 missions are more worthwhile to the war effort.

FAQ and Common Misconceptions[edit | edit 来源]

Q: Is a 行星's in-game Enemy 抗性 相关 to how 困难 it is/how many enemy spawns on it?

  • No. Enemy 抗性 is another name for 衰退速率, and is unrelated to 任务 难度. This was clarified by Arrowhead 支援.[4]

Q: Do 防御 campaigns have decay rates?

  • No. The enemy decay % per hour seen in companion apps is purely 装饰.

Q: If we completely cut off a 行星's Warp Links, will we automatically win that 行星

  • No.

Q: If we completely cut off a 行星's Warp Links, will its 衰退速率 掉落 to zero?

  • Yes and no. It's 衰退速率 will go 负面, slowly liberating itself. 通常 around 0.5% per hour. Previously this was done manually by ArrowHead, but was 已更改 to be 全自动 around June 16th 2025.

Q: Do we get more 解放 if 玩家 spam 简单 missions?

  • No. Higher 难度 missions are weighed more favorably to low 难度 missions.

Q: Do we get more 解放 from rushing only a 任务's primary 目标

  • No. The impact 倍率 is based around instances of 伤害 in a certain window, if everyone speedran the missions the impact 倍率 will 掉落 to compensate, erasing any 效果 speedrunning your missions would accomplish.

Q: We have Y 百分比 of 玩家 on X 行星, but we're still losing. What's happening?

  • It may be that the 衰退速率 is too high, or everyone on the 行星 is still completing missions.
    It's 重要 to wait a few hours for the Ramp Up Time to end before making any judgements on 解放速率.

Q: Why don't we try to win X gambit instead of trying to win Y 防御(s)?

  • Gambits are not always viable. In most scenarios, it's often better to focus on the associated 防御 campaign instead.
    This can be due to a variety of reasons, such as the gambit 行星's 衰退速率 being too strong or difficulties coordinating 玩家.

Q: What if we got everyone to...

  • Unfortunately, it's not a 写实 plan to get the entire playerbase to do something. There are simply too many people playing 绝地潜兵 2, and many are entirely uninterested or unable to engage with the game's community and/or the 星系战争, be it due to a 语言 屏障 or lack of time.
    It's better to focus on plans that need only a reasonable majority of the playerbase or plans that have a 小型 amount of 玩家 贡献 a lot, like through DSS actions.

Q: If X 玩家 didn't exclusively dive on 终结族 planets, we wouldn't have lost Y!

  • The Game Master knows that there are large amounts of 玩家 who only play against certain 阵营, and accounts for that.
    Please don't blame other 玩家 for strategical mishaps.

Q: Why doesn't Arrowhead split the 解放 cap by 阵营

  • This wouldn't change much, as we have never once reached any kind of upper 解放 cap. If we ever do reach said cap and encounter problems, perhaps then would be the time to discuss said system.
