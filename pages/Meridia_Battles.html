背景

绝地潜兵 received a new 主要命令, it tasked 绝地潜兵 with activating TCS (终结族控制系统). After they secured Fenrir III 和 Turing, 绝地潜兵 decided to 目标 Meridia as the 下一个 行星 that will have a working TCS.

战略与重要性

This battle was 重要, as Meridia was one of the planets that were a 目标 of a 重要指令.

战斗

When Fenrir III and Turing were liberated, around 250k 绝地潜兵 were estabilishing TCS on this 行星 at the same time! The battle lasted 3 days. Around 13 million 绝地潜兵 died during this battle. Unfortunetly, the estabilishment of TCS on Meridia, led to 完成 infestation of this 行星 on April 29th.

背景

彻底消灭终结族的时刻已到…… Supercolony completely. The 绝地潜兵 will deploy to the 浮出地面 of Meridia to deploy partial payloads of Dark Fluid across the 行星. Once a 致命 质量 is reached, the Dark Fluid will accrete into a superdense 质量, triggering total 行星 implosion. The Supercolony, and its hyper-reproductive 终结族, will be destroyed.

战略与重要性

Meridia was a Super Colony, that produced 庞大 amounts of spores. If not destroyed quickly, Meridia sooner or later would turn all nearby 星区 into infested, ugly, undemocratic and brutal worlds.

战斗

The Battle started on 30th of May and ended on 2nd of June. Merdian Supercolony turned into a blackhole, because of Gravitational Anomaly, caused by Dark Fluid. Right before the end of this battle, hordes of 终结族 escaped to Fenrir III and Acamar IV. Around 38 million 绝地潜兵 gave their last breaths during this battle.
