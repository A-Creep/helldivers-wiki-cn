the 喷气旅孵化厂 are an 机器人 Legion 位置 containing 多个 factories capable of producing units for the Jet Brigade.

背景故事

The 喷气旅孵化厂 are 机器人 structures specialized in producing Jet Brigade units. These are highly-危险 structures, as it allows the 机器人 to 生产 more 危险 敌人 to the battlefield.

Construction

1月 30th, 2185 - The 喷气旅孵化厂 were discovered on Chort Bay, following the dispatch from a 主要命令. 绝地潜兵 were tasked to liberate the 行星 in order to prevent the 机器人 from rebuilding Jet Brigade. It是未知 how long they were online, but it is assumed it was long enough for the 机器人 to rebuild the Brigade and ship them off-world.

April 3rd, 2185 - The 喷气旅孵化厂 resurfaced on Aurora Bay, a 行星 deep behind 机器人 lines. It has been producing the Jet Brigades ever since.
