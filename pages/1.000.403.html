1.000.403 was a hotfix 更新 released for 绝地潜兵 2.

目录

  • 1 补丁 01.000.403

补丁 01.000.403

🌍 概述

对于本次补丁，我们对以下领域进行了改进和更改：

  • Crash 修复 相关 to the FAF-14 Spear
  • 基本信息 fixes

将军

  • Japanese 语言 voice-overs are now available globally on PS5 (also on PC).

🔧 修复

崩溃

  • 修复 for crash happening when 玩家 with unique 绝地喷射舱 patterns leave during 绝地喷射舱 launch cutscenes
  • Crash 修复 while aiming with the FAF-14 Spear

杂项修复

  • Fixed corrupted text showing "?" for some characters when having Traditional Chinese 语言 selected
  • 修复 for Plasma Punisher being unable to shoot out of the SH-32 防护罩生成包 and the FX-12 防护罩生成器
  • Fixed so the 类星体加农炮 has the 正确 change to its heat when on hot and cold planets
  • 修复了以下问题： 孢子喷涌虫 would appear purple on 特定星球
  • Fixed some cases where pink 问题 marks would appear in missions on 多种星球
  • 已修复 健壮体格 护甲 被动 not properly affecting weapon 人体工学
  • Fixed issue where available Operations were reset after player reconnected from getting kicked due to inactivity

🧠 已知问题

这些问题要么是由本次补丁引入的且正在修复中，要么来自之前的版本且尚未修复。

  • Sending friend requests via friend code in game currently does not work
  • 玩家 may be unable to be joined or invited to the game
  • 玩家 added to the Recent 玩家 list will appear in the middle of the list
  • 玩家 may 经验值 delays in 奖章 and 超级货币 payouts
  • 敌人 that 流血 out do not progress 个人指令 and 歼灭 missions
  • Deployed mines may 有时 become invisible (but remain active)
  • 电弧武器 有时 behave inconsistently and 有时 misfire
  • Most 武器 shoot below the crosshair when aiming down the sights
  • 战略配备 beam might attach itself to an enemy but will deploy to its original 位置
  • Hand Carts 舰船模块 does not reduce 防护罩生成包's 冷却
  • 卓越封装方法 舰船模块 does not work
  • 吐酸泰坦 有时 does not take 伤害 to the head
  • 玩家 may become stuck in the 武装配置 when joining a game in progress
  • 增援 may not be available for 玩家 who join a game in progress
  • 行星 解放 reaches 100 % at the end of every Defend 任务
  • Raise Flag of 超级地球 目标 does not show a progress bar
  • 任务 count in the Career tab is being reset to zero after every game 重新开始
  • Some 武器’ descriptions are out-of-date and don’t reflect their 当前 design
