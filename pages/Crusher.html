  • 碾压者非常耐打且生命值极高，这意味着常规攻击通常需要大量弹药才能击倒一只碾压者。
    • Crushers possess the 能力 to regenerate 生命值 for all limbs and 主体, except for their 头盔 and head, at a rate of 30 生命值 每秒 0.5s after taking 伤害, incentivizing 绝地潜兵 to 应对 the 威胁 quickly lest they become a 问题 again later.

    • 弹道武器 with  重型 护甲穿透 or higher (such as the APW-1 反器材步枪, P/40-K 爆弹手枪, and RS-422 磁轨炮) are all 有效 at destroying a Crusher's 头盔, destroying it in one to two shots.

  • Crushers mainly 攻击 绝地潜兵 by slamming their 庞大 club to the ground; this will instantly kill 绝地潜兵 that are hit directly, and ragdoll those in a 小型 radius around it. They will also 有时 use a sweeping 攻击 and will try to stomp 绝地潜兵 that get too close to them.

  • Crushers are weak to 爆炸 武器库; a single G-123 铝热弹 手榴弹 is capable of killing a Crusher. Additionally, the GL-21 榴弹发射器, RL-77 空爆火箭弹发射器, and StA-X3 W.A.S.P. 发射器 are highly 克制 Crushers.
