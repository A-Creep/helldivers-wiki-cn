穿刺虫
“

Back by popular demand: the 穿刺虫! This big guy from the original 绝地潜兵 creates some new challenges. 否 longer can you run from the bugs when the 穿刺虫 is around–big, imposing, and 破坏性, it demands to be dealt with.

— Playstation.Blog, 绝地潜兵 2 Escalation of Freedom 更新

The 穿刺虫 is a large 终结族. Impalers burrow their "head" of three bladed tentacles, causing them to emerge near 绝地潜兵 to impede and 攻击 them.

生成

Impalers can be first found on 困难 as the 任务 目标 of the 消除 穿刺虫 任务.

Starting on 极难, Impalers can spawn from Bug 突破 and can be found patrolling the map on its own. Higher difficulties will 增加 how often they can spawn.

Starting on 超级绝地潜兵难度, Impalers can be found guarding Mega Nests.

行为

When encountered outside of a 虫族入侵, Impalers will 通常 remain still. Upon noticing 绝地潜兵 movement or sounds caused by them, they will turn to face where a 绝地潜兵 or the sound was. When in a combat or encountered in a 虫族入侵, Impalers will often immediately burrow its "head," using its three tentacles to 攻击 绝地潜兵 in its line of sight. The tentacles can be damaged to force them to retract. After some time, losing sight of 绝地潜兵, or upon death, Impalers will retract all of their tentacles.

巡逻时，穿刺虫也会停下脚步，转向附近任何移动或声音。

近距离时，穿刺虫可以利用其前部重量碾碎前方的一切。

4) 若设置为"是"，转移到主生命值的伤害将以部位的生命值与构造之和为上限。
5) 构造充当次级生命池，一旦主体或肢体的生命值耗尽，可能会开始衰减。第一个数字是构造总量，第二个是衰减速率。若设置为 0/秒，则不会衰减，构造将作为额外生命值。
6) 爆炸抗性指爆炸伤害抗性，范围可为 0 至 100%。若设置为 100%，肢体无法承受爆炸伤害，爆炸伤害将转而攻击主体。请参阅伤害以获取更多信息。
  • 注：此机制称为\"受爆炸影响\"，每次爆炸只能触发一次，无论命中多少个 100% 爆炸抗性的部位；且爆炸的护甲穿透必须达到或超过主体护甲才能对主血量造成伤害。但如果同一次爆炸已经伤害过主体，此机制将不会触发。

战术信息

  • 穿刺虫钻入触手攻击时，会暴露其无护甲的头部，这是绝地潜兵应利用的弱点，可快速击杀它。
    • Impalers can be easily killed in this state, so locating them is essential in defeating them. Some of the 最佳武器 to dispatch them in this state are 支援武器 with high 耐久伤害 and firepower such as the GR-8 无后坐力炮, AC-8 机炮, or 进攻型 战略配备 such as the Eagle 500kg Bomb, “飞鹰”机枪扫射, 轨道炮攻击, and 轨道精准攻击 . This can be 困难 to manage, as 绝地潜兵 will have to avoid their tentacles whilst entering the 战略配备 input, and will have to avoid being knocked over, as this will cause you to 掉落 the 战略配备 ball on your position.
    • 高每秒伤害 武器 such as the M-105 盟友 and MG-43 机枪 can quickly incapacitate an 穿刺虫 by 索敌 their legs once the 护甲 has been destroyed.
  • When a tentacle has breached the ground to 攻击 绝地潜兵, it can be damaged. Enough 伤害 will make the 穿刺虫 retract the tentacle
    • 这在紧急情况下最有用，例如命中不可避免或会杀死绝地潜兵时。
    • 火焰能有效迫使穿刺虫缩回触手，并削减其生命值，让绝地潜兵专注于更大的威胁。
    • 哨戒炮可以瞄准它们的触手，迫使穿刺虫缩回触手。
  • The 穿刺虫 has a unique interaction when destroying the 头部护甲; it will 摧毁 the RIGHT 侧面 躯干护甲 exposing the 内圈 flesh.
  • The 穿刺虫's legs are covered in 护甲 which can be broken, exposing the 脆弱 flesh underneath. If an 穿刺虫 does not reveal its head, destroying a leg is the 下一个 best way to 消灭 it.
  • Additionally, 绝地潜兵 shoot their 无护甲 underside. However, this part has much more 生命值 and 通常 requires 重型 武器库 to be 有效.
  • Impalers are especially 危险 towards 载具 like the M-102 Fast 侦察 载具, EXO-45 爱国者 and EXO-49 Emancipator 外骨骼机甲 as their large frames have a 困难 time avoiding tentacles, and 外骨骼机甲 can easily be dismembered by their 攻击.
  • If Impalers don't expose their faces, 绝地潜兵 can walk up to them and bait a 近战攻击. During the 近战 动画, the 穿刺虫 will lift its head and reveal its mouth—hitting this 弱点 with 武器 like the R-36 Eruptor or CB-9 Exploding Crossbow can quickly kill it.
  • 穿刺虫本身的硬直抗性为45，而触手只有35的抗性。
    • As a result, 武器 like the AR-23C 解放者 Concussive, SG-8 Punisher, JAR-5 Dominator and SG-22 Bushwhacker are capable of interrupting their 攻击.

琐事

  • The 穿刺虫 was revealed in the Escalation of Freedom 公告 Trailer on July 23rd, 2024, and appeared in-game on August 6, 2024.
  • The 穿刺虫 shares the same musical theme as the 吐酸泰坦 if it is in combat.
  • The 穿刺虫 has twenty seven eyes hidden behind its tentacles. Thirteen on its right 侧面 and fourteen on its left, making it one of the few asymmetrical 终结族 in the game.
