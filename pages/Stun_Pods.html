描述

Stun Pods is a 强化资源 designed to stun any entity that is near a 绝地喷射舱 掉落 site. Upon 联络 with the 浮出地面, a 小型 爆炸 will occur.

采购

the Stun Pods 强化资源 is unlocked on the 2nd page of the Force of Law 尊享战争债券 用于 55 奖章.
