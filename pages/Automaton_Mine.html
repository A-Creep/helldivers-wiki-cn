机器人 Mine (officially 水雷), is a red and black 联络 mine constructed by the  机器人 Legion. It is an Environmental 危害 found scattered around perimeters of the 机器人 occupied zones, such as 哨站 and most Minor Places of Interest 自杀任务 onwards.

Locations[edit | edit 来源]

This 危害 can only be found on 机器人 Missions. As mentioned above, mines may be expected near any specific landmark where 机器人 have stationed defenders.

Mines become increasingly common as 难度 rises, to the point that they may be discovered in open 地形.

结构解析[edit | edit 来源]

机器人 mine with lights off, in second stage trigger.

The 机器人' mines are volatile enough that a detailed breakdown of their 伤害 zones would be redundant. There is a two-stage trigger state. If you move close enough (1-2m) you can trigger the first stage and get the lights to shut off. [1]

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • Using the 'tag 位置' 指挥 while pointing one's reticle at a 联络 mine will place a temporary marker above that mine and issue a generic "危险" vocal call out.
  • It is 极其 unlikely to survive stepping on a 联络 Mine without the Democracy Protects or 加固 护甲 被动 效果.
    • A 加固 重型护甲 such as the FS-11 行刑者 will leave its wearer with roughly 1/3 of their 生命值 if they step on a mine while in 完美 条件.
    • Using the SH-32 防护罩生成包 has the potential to negate the 效果 of one mine. This will fully drain the shield pack, necessitating a 充能 period.
  • 机器人 tend to place their mines in logical positions that impede traffic toward areas that 绝地潜兵 would find desirable.
    • The alternative entrances of 重型 哨站, Fortresses, and 探测器 Tower hills are all 极其 likely to be mined. Areas adjacent to the primary entrances may also be mined.
    • Colonies will often be mined at 突破 in their outer wall.
    • Alleyways and intersections may be mined inside of occupied Mega Cities and Colonies.
    • The 更小 地下 变体 of the Bunker place of interest tends to be heavily mined on higher difficulties.
  • 机器人 Mines are 极其 sensitive to 伤害. Gunfire and explosions can 引爆 them easily.
    • Beam 武器 such as the LAS-5 are preferable for mine clearing due to their uninterrupted stream of 伤害 and (hypothetically) infinite ammunition.
    • 机器人 forces will attempt to avoid stepping on their own mines, but may still pass close enough to be damaged by one if detonated by an outside force.
