描述[edit | edit 来源]

定位混淆 is a 强化资源 in 绝地潜兵 2. When equipped, the time between the call for spawned enemy encounters/增援 (in the form of Bug 突破, or Incoming Dropships) will be 已增加. This 已增加 time gives 绝地潜兵 more breathing room, allowing them to react more to the incoming 敌人.

获取途径[edit | edit 来源]

the 定位混淆 强化资源 is unlocked on the 1st page of the 遥遥领先 尊享战争债券 for 奖牌.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**It should be noted that 定位混淆 DOES NOT 会影响 spawn 机制 or rate of enemy patrol 生成!!**

定位混淆 DOES NOT delay the time for the 增援 to arrive once an encounter event occurs nor does it reduce the amount of 增援 that 回应 to the call.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

  • An encounter (增援 call) is another name for a 机器人空投, 虫族入侵，或 incoming 光能者 ships.
    • There is an internal timer that starts when an encounter is triggered, ranging anywhere from around 2-5 分钟 depending on 难度. This timer acts as a 冷却 period and must be completed before another encounter can be triggered. The length of this timer is 已修复 and does not change during the length of the 任务.
      • 定位混淆 增加 the internal 增援/encounter timer's threshold by ~15% [1][2]
  • For 示例:
    • Without 定位混淆 equipped, the threshold for the 增援/encounter 冷却 timer on 难度 9 as a solo diver is 2 分钟 and ~30 秒. [1][2]
    • 使用 定位混淆 equipped, the threshold for the 增援/encounter 冷却 timer on 难度 9 as a solo diver is ~2 分钟 and 52.5 秒. [1][2]
      • I.E. 定位混淆 has been demonstrated to 增加 the encounter 冷却 timer by ~22.5 秒 as a solo diver on 难度 9.
