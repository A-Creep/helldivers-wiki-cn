描述

定位混淆 is a 强化资源 in 绝地潜兵 2. When equipped, the time between the call for spawned enemy encounters/增援 (in the form of Bug 突破, or Incoming Dropships) will be 已增加. This 已增加 time gives 绝地潜兵 more breathing room, allowing them to react more to the incoming 敌人.

获取途径

the 定位混淆 强化资源 is unlocked on the 1st page of the 遥遥领先 尊享战争债券 for 18 奖章.

战术信息

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**It should be noted that 定位混淆 不会 会影响 spawn 机制 或速率 enemy 巡逻队生成!!**

定位混淆 不会 delay the time for the 增援 to arrive once an encounter event occurs nor does it reduce the amount of 增援 that 回应 to the call.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

  • An 遭遇 (增援 call) is another name for a 机器人空投, 虫族入侵，或 来袭的光能者舰船.
    • There is an internal timer that starts when an encounter is triggered, ranging anywhere from around 2-5 分钟 depending on 难度. This timer 充当 a 冷却 period and must be completed before another encounter can be triggered. The length of this timer is 已修复 和 不会改变 during the length of the 任务.
      • 定位混淆将内部增援/遭遇计时器的阈值提高约15% [1][2]
  • 例如：
    • 没有 定位混淆 equipped, the threshold for the 增援/encounter 冷却 timer on 难度 9 as a solo diver is 2分钟 和 约30秒. [1][2]
    • 使用 定位混淆 equipped, the threshold for the 增援/encounter 冷却 timer on 难度 9 as a solo diver is 约2分52.5秒. [1][2]
      • I.E. 定位混淆 has been demonstrated to 增加 the encounter 冷却 timer by ~22.5 秒 as a solo diver on 难度 9.
