地面全地形采集钻机 (GATER)

地面全地形撤离钻机（缩写为 GATER）是一种载具，搭载有机炮炮塔，是"进行移动式 E-711 撤离"等任务的主要目标的一部分。

Once the 任务 has been accomplished, a 鹈鹕 will take the 后部 fuel canisters, and the 载具 will self-destruct, rendering it unusable for the rest of the 任务.

控制项

  • 默认 keybinds for the GATER on PC are the same as the M-102 Fast 侦察 载具.
    • The headlights cannot be turned off. They will always be turned on while there is a player in the driver's seat.
    • C to change between driver and gunner seats, provided that there is free space.
    • F to honk the horn.
    • Left CTRL to shift down and Left SHIFT to shift up gears.
    • 太空 to activate the handbrake.
  • The GATER 使用次数 an R-N-D-1-2 gear selector layout.
    • By 默认, the GATER will automatically brake and shift between the Reverse, Neutral, and Drive gears based on movement inputs when appropriate. This can be 已更改 in Accessibility settings.
    • Manually shifting into positions 1 and 2 will 限制 the 载具 to the respective gear ratios, thereby reducing the top 速度 achievable with the throttle.
    • To activate the parking brake, hold the handbrake button and do not press any throttle inputs as you exit the GATER. Otherwise, the GATER will freely roll down slopes.
  • Driver and gunner can perform few actions that they normally can on foot.
    • 武器 can not be reloaded while inside the GATER; you must exit from the 载具 to perform any 换弹 actions.
    • 所有 occupants can stim themselves while inside the GATER.
      • The driver must have their 小地图 closed in order to stim.
  • The mounted 机炮 turret fires shells that are identical to the A/AC-8 自动哨戒炮

战术信息

  • The GATER is immune to most forms of 伤害 from 终结族 敌人, but certain 攻击 like the 吐酸泰坦's vomit are capable of destroying it. 霸王虫 and 出现中 爆裂强袭虫 are also capable of launching the GATER off the map for 全自动 任务 failure; therefore, it is highly recommended to 摧毁 Hive Lords quickly or have teammates draw away the 霸王虫 before attempting to drive the GATER, as it is egregiously slow with a max 速度 of 31km/h.
  • The 机炮 turret, located on the left 侧面 of the 载具, have 有限 aiming capabilities: it can't shoot at ground 敌人 closer than 24 meters, struggles to kill near flying 尖啸虫 and fuel canisters interfere with firing at the ground enemy behind a GATER.
    • As the 机炮 弹药 cannot be replenished, gunners should prioritize which targets to expend its 有限 弹药 on.
  • The GATER is capable of crushing any 虫穴 it drives over. As many 撤离 sites have 虫穴 close to them, it is recommended to crush those holes with the 载具 first before initiating 撤离, as it will render the 载具 immobile, or use the 机炮 mounted on top.
    • It is also capable of crushing 尖啸虫巢穴 not located on hills.
    • The GATER has a high center of 质量 and can easily take 重型 伤害 from being flipped over, so caution is advised around 虫穴 遗骸 that stick above the ground and other large 障碍 that aren't completely crushed by its wheels.
  • 敌人 can be damaged by colliding with the GATER.
    • 轻型 and 中型 敌人 such as 食腐虫 和 战士 are easily killed at 中等 speeds, while 敌人 such as 虫窝护卫 和 阿尔法指挥官 can be easily staggered without losing momentum.
    • 重型 敌人 and above such as 强袭虫, 穿刺虫 will stop the GATER in its tracks, much like colliding with map geometry, and take little to 否 伤害.
    • The GATER has 'scrubbing chips' installed to preventing accidental 锁定 by smart 武器库.

琐事

  • The GATER is the first 任务-相关 载具, where a 载具 is 必需 to 完成 the 任务, and can only be used for its 目标.
  • The GATER plays the 超级地球 anthem during the E-711 撤离, which is the same anthem heard in flag 目标, like in 传播民主 任务。
