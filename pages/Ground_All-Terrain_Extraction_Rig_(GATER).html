the 地面全地形采集钻机 （缩写为 GATER} is a 载具 with mounted 机炮 炮塔. It acts as 部分 of the 主要目标 for Conduct Mobile E-711 撤离 missions on 虫窝 Worlds and 撤离 Anomalous 材质 missions on planets within the Void, which also appears as a 次要目标 on the 光能者 正面 within the Void.

一次 the 任务 has been accomplished, a 鹈鹕 will take the 后部 燃料 canisters, and the 载具 will self-destruct, rendering it unusable for the rest of the 任务.

控制项[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 默认 keybinds for the GATER on PC are the same as the M-102 Fast 侦察 载具.
    • The headlights cannot be turned off. They will 总是显示 be turned on while there is a 玩家 in the 驾驶员's seat.
    • C to 变化 between 驾驶员 and 炮手 seats, provided that there is free 太空.
    • F to honk the horn.
    • Left CTRL to shift down and Left SHIFT to shift up gears.
    • 太空 to activate the handbrake.
  • The GATER 使用次数 an R-N-D-1-2 装备 selector 布局.
    • By 默认, the GATER will automatically brake and shift between the Reverse, Neutral, and Drive gears based on movement inputs when appropriate. This can be 已更改 in Accessibility settings.
    • Manually shifting into positions 1 and 2 will 限制 the 载具 to the respective 装备 ratios, thereby reducing the top 速度 achievable with the throttle.
    • To activate the parking brake, 按住 the handbrake button and do not press 任何 throttle inputs as you exit the GATER. Otherwise, the GATER will freely 翻滚 down slopes.
  • 驾驶员 and 炮手 can perform 少数 actions that they normally can on foot.
    • 武器 can not be reloaded while inside the GATER; you must exit from the 载具 to perform 任何 换弹 actions.
    • 所有 occupants can 兴奋剂 themselves while inside the GATER.
      • The 驾驶员 must have their 小地图 closed in 命令 to 兴奋剂.
  • The mounted 机炮 炮塔 fires shells that are identical to the A/AC-8 自动哨戒炮

战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The GATER is 免疫 to 最多 forms of 伤害 from 终结族 敌人, but certain 攻击 点赞 the 吐酸泰坦's vomit are capable of destroying it. 霸王虫 and 出现中 爆裂强袭虫 are also capable of launching the GATER off the map for 全自动 任务 failure; therefore, it is highly recommended to 摧毁 虫窝 Lords quickly or have teammates draw away the 霸王虫 before attempting to drive the GATER, as it is egregiously slow with a 最大 速度 of 31km/h.
  • The 机炮 炮塔, located on the left 侧面 of the 载具, have 有限 aiming capabilities: it can't 射击 at 地面 敌人 closer than 24 米, struggles to 击杀 near 飞行 尖啸虫 and 燃料 canisters interfere with firing at the 地面 敌人 behind a GATER.
    • As the 机炮 弹药 cannot be replenished, gunners should prioritize which targets to expend its 有限 弹药 on.
  • The GATER is capable of crushing 任何 虫穴 it drives over. As 许多 撤离 sites have 虫穴 关闭 to them, it is recommended to 粉碎 those holes with the 载具 first before initiating 撤离, as it will render the 载具 immobile, or use the 机炮 mounted on top.
    • It is also capable of crushing 尖啸虫巢穴 not located on hills.
    • The GATER has a 高 center of 质量 and can easily take 重型 伤害 from being flipped over, so caution is advised around 虫穴 遗骸 that stick above the 地面 and other 大 障碍 that aren't completely crushed by its wheels.
  • 敌人 can be damaged by colliding with the GATER.
    • 轻型 and 中型 敌人 such as 食腐虫 and 战士 are easily killed at 中等 speeds, while 敌人 such as 虫窝护卫 and 阿尔法指挥官 can be easily staggered without losing momentum.
    • 重型 敌人 and above such as 强袭虫, 穿刺虫 will 制止 the GATER in its tracks, 许多 点赞 colliding with map geometry, and take 少量 to 否 伤害.
    • The GATER has 'scrubbing chips' installed to preventing accidental 锁定 by smart 武器库.

  • The GATER plays the 超级地球 anthem during the E-711 撤离, which is the same anthem heard in 旗帜 目标, 点赞 in 传播民主 missions.
