生成[edit | edit 来源]

Alpha 指挥官 can be first found on 不可能, completely replacing Brood 指挥官. They can spawn from Bug 突破, within patrols, and guarding 目标, Bug Nests, and Minor Places of Interest.

行为[edit | edit 来源]

Alpha 指挥官 behave similarly to 虫族指挥官.

Alpha 指挥官 have slightly more 生命值 and 耐久度 compared to Brood 指挥官, but otherwise behave the same. The 阿尔法指挥官 can instead summon a trio of Alpha 武斗虫, which are faster and potentially deadlier 武斗虫. Once the 阿尔法指挥官 summons the Alpha 武士 Trio, it will either engage in combat alongside them or 撤退 to a safe 距离 to 继续 生成 additional units. 有时 the 阿尔法指挥官 summons 虫窝护卫, which 通常 indicates that a large number of Hive Guards and other heavily 有护甲 敌人 will spawn during the 任务.

Alpha 指挥官 are able to release a cloud of 信息素 into the air that causes a 虫族入侵.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

Alpha 指挥官 can be taken out with the same 战术 used against the 虫族指挥官.

  • Alpha 指挥官 have peculiar 行为 where if not directly engaged in a patrol they will "撤退" then come back and 攻击 the 绝地潜兵.
  • Alpha 指挥官 can 呼叫 a 虫族入侵, even without their head.
  • Alpha 指挥官 are capable of summoning Alpha 武斗虫 and 虫窝护卫 even without their head.
  • While any weapon is capable of killing Alpha 指挥官,  中型 护甲-penetrating 武器 and explosives are more 克制 them due to their  轻型 有护甲 heads and high 耐久度.
    • Their head, claws, and body have slightly thicker 护甲, reducing 伤害 from  轻型 穿透 武器.
      • Destroying both claws will kill the 指挥官.
    • Their legs are not 有护甲, making them 易受  轻型 穿透 武器库. Destroying a leg will significantly reduce its movement and charge 速度, with further reductions for additional legs destroyed.
      • Even while missing a leg, the 阿尔法指挥官 is still able to "charge," inflicting 伤害 and its 效果; this is despite not appearing to be 实际上 charging.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • The 阿尔法指挥官 was first revealed in the Escalation of Freedom 公告 Trailer on July 23rd, 2024, and began appearing in-game on the 更新's release on August 6th, 2024.
  • Alpha 指挥官 may summon Hive Guards 偶尔. This is confirmed to be an unintended bug.
