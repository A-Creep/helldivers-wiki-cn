the 阿尔法指挥官 is a 更多 耐久 虫族指挥官. The trio of 阿尔法 武斗虫 it summons are 更快 than their original counterparts.

生成[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

阿尔法 指挥官 can be first found on 不可能, completely replacing 虫群 指挥官. They can spawn from Bug 突破, within patrols, and guarding 目标, Bug Nests, and Minor Places of Interest.

行为[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

阿尔法 指挥官 behave similarly to 虫族指挥官.

阿尔法 指挥官 have slightly 更多 生命值 and 耐久度 compared to 虫群 指挥官, but otherwise behave the same. The 阿尔法指挥官 can instead summon a trio of 阿尔法 武斗虫, which are 更快 and potentially deadlier 武斗虫. 一次 the 阿尔法指挥官 summons the 阿尔法 武士 Trio, it will 任一 engage in combat alongside them or 撤退 to a 安全 距离 to 继续 生成 additional units. 有时 the 阿尔法指挥官 summons 虫窝护卫, which 通常 indicates that a 大 数字 of 虫窝 Guards and other heavily 有护甲 敌人 will spawn during the 任务.

阿尔法 指挥官 are able to release a 云 of 信息素 into the air that causes a 虫族入侵.

战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

阿尔法 指挥官 can be taken out with the same 战术 used against the 虫族指挥官.

  • 阿尔法 指挥官 have peculiar 行为 where if not directly engaged in a 巡逻 they will "撤退" then come 返回 and 攻击 the 绝地潜兵.
  • 阿尔法 指挥官 can 呼叫 a 虫族入侵, even without their 头部.
  • 阿尔法 指挥官 are capable of summoning 阿尔法 武斗虫 and 虫窝护卫 even without their 头部.
  • While 任何 武器 is capable of killing 阿尔法 指挥官,  中型 护甲-penetrating 武器 and explosives are 更多 克制 them due to their  轻型 有护甲 heads and 高 耐久度.
    • Their 头部, 螯爪, and 身体 have slightly thicker 护甲, reducing 伤害 from  轻型 穿透 武器.
      • Destroying 两者 螯爪 will 击杀 the 指挥官.
    • Their 腿部 are not 有护甲, making them 易受  轻型 穿透 武器库. Destroying a leg will significantly 减少 its movement and 冲锋 速度, with further reductions for additional 腿部 destroyed.
      • Even while missing a leg, the 阿尔法指挥官 is still able to "冲锋," inflicting 伤害 and its 效果; this is despite not appearing to be 实际上 charging.

琐事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The 阿尔法指挥官 was first revealed in the Escalation of Freedom 公告 Trailer on 7月 23rd, 2024, and began appearing in-游戏 on the 更新's release on 8月 6th, 2024.
  • 阿尔法 指挥官 5月 summon 虫窝 Guards 偶尔. This is confirmed to be an unintended bug.
