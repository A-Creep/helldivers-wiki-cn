战术信息

  • The Leveller is most comparable to the MS-11 Solo Silo. Both are high-power, single-use 反坦克 支援武器 with identical 爆炸半径 and 爆破力. The Leveller is much quicker to deploy and has a 33% shorter 冷却, while the Solo Silo has 更大 power and 射程.
    • Due to its 巨大 伤害 and 爆炸半径, the EAT-411 Leveller can be used for both large crowds and 庞大 敌人 like Bile Titans or 工厂 Striders.
  • The Leveller's 投射物 shares the same drag and 重力 characteristics of the GP-20 Ultimatum, but with significantly higher 速率 at 50 m/s compared to only 20m/s.
    • 在平坦地形上垂直向上发射时，投射物可飞行约80米。
  • Similar to the EAT-700 Expendable Napalm, the EAT-411 Leveller has a 基础冷却 of 140 秒. However, only one 发射器 will be sent down per call-in.
  • The weapon's 40 爆破力 allows it to kill enemy spawners from the outside and close 吐酸泰坦 虫穴.
    • This is not enough to 摧毁 objects such as 战略配备 Jammers or 探测器 Towers
    • 城市围墙和建筑可以被摧毁。
    • Grounded Warp Ships will be destroyed on a 正面 hit anywhere on their 外圈 hull, even with the 护盾 fully intact.

琐事

  • In l33tspeak, EAT-411 translates to EAT ALL.
  • The EAT-411 Leveller and how it operates appears to be partially inspired by the Panzerfaust 消耗性反坦克武器 weapon in real life.
    • In real life, the Panzerfaust was an inexpensive, single-shot, pre-loaded recoilless 反坦克 weapon featuring a super-caliber warhead propelled by a black powder charge. It was designed for use by a single infantryman.
  • In terms of 破坏性 firepower and the overall 外观, the Leveller appears to be partially based upon the Davy Crockett tactical nuclear recoilless gun.
    • 戴维·克罗克特是一种单兵武器系统，设计用于发射当量10至20吨TNT（0.01-0.02千吨）的核弹头，旨在通过初始的高能中子脉冲击溃或威慑坦克的集团冲锋，因此它被视为一种中子弹。
