暴政 Park 2 was a Galactic Point of Interest on Bore Rock, and the second iteration of 暴政 Park. Like its 前任, it was also destroyed following a 终结族 爆发.

背景故事

暴政 Park 2 was established by 超级地球 as a 庞大 recreational and educational 设施 to “showcase the most dire threats to 管理式民主.” Those facilities were also expanded to include more 敌对 终结族 变体, such as Gloom-相关 specimens.

初始建造

暴政 Park 2 was established after August 13th, 2185, on Bore Rock, to be bigger, better, and safer installation than its original 前任. Like the original 暴政 Park, it was an amusement park designed to 显示 the various species of 终结族 to the citizens of 超级地球.

公园扩建与余波

After its successful construction, the 超级地球 planned an 扩展包 for 暴政 Park 2 named “The 被禁止 Gloom.” This 扩展包 would showcase Gloom infested planets and the Spore Burst Strain 终结族. This 扩展包 was successful, and the Park's roster was expanded.

On August 28th, 2185, in addition to large-scale 终结族 攻击, 暴政 Park 2 suffered a catastrophic malfunction in its 巨兽级强袭虫 Enclosure, leading to the entire Park being overrun by 终结族. While 绝地潜兵 attempted to recapture the 行星 soon after, it was not successful.

时间线

  • 2185 年 8 月 13 日——暴政公园 2 在博尔岩建立。
    • 一项次要指令将公园扩建，纳入孢子爆发株系终结族
  • 2185 年 8 月 28 日——暴政公园被终结族淹没并摧毁，大部分/全部人员

琐事

  • 最初的暴政公园只是更大行星保护区的一个较小分区，而暴政公园 2 则是自己的银河兴趣点，其重要性甚至超过原公园。
  • During the 重要指令 to 建立 暴政 Park 2, 绝地潜兵 were ordered to kill various 重型 敌人, including Chargers. Due to the MO 反制 not counting its 变体, the count had a lower growth rate, which projected an MO failure.
    • Despite this discrepancy, High 指挥 did agree that 强袭虫 Behemoths were considered as Chargers, and their kills were added to the MO 反制. This retroactive addition led to the MO to be considered a success.
