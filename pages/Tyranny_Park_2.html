暴政 Park 2 was a Galactic Point of Interest on Bore Rock, and the second iteration of 暴政 Park. Like its 前任, it was also destroyed following a 终结族 爆发.

背景故事

暴政 Park 2 was established by 超级地球 as a 庞大 recreational and educational 设施 to “showcase the most dire threats to 管理式民主.” Those facilities were also expanded to include more 敌对 终结族 变体, such as Gloom相关 specimens.

初始建造

暴政 Park 2 was established after August 13th, 2185, on Bore Rock, to be bigger, better, and safer installation than 其原始前身. Like the original 暴政 Park, it was an amusement park designed to 显示 the various species of 终结族 to the citizens of 超级地球.

公园扩建与余波

After its successful construction, the 超级地球 planned an 扩展包 for 暴政 Park 2 named “The 被禁止 Gloom.” This 扩展包 would showcase Gloom infested planets and the 孢裂变种终结族. This 扩展包 was successful, and the Park's roster was expanded.

On August 28th, 2185, in addition to large-scale 终结族 攻击, 暴政 Park 2 suffered a catastrophic malfunction in its 巨兽级强袭虫围场, leading to the entire Park being overrun by 终结族. While 绝地潜兵 attempted to recapture the 行星 soon after, it was not successful.

时间线

  • August 13th, 2185 - 暴政 Park 2 was established on Bore Rock.
    • A Minor Order expanded the Park to include Spore Burst Strain 终结族
  • August 28th, 2185 - 暴政 Park was overrun and destroyed by the 终结族, with most/all of the personnel

琐事

  • While the initial 暴政 Park was a 更小 subsection of the larger 行星 Preserve, 暴政 Park 2 was its own Galactic Point of Interest, deeming it an even larger relevance than the original park.
  • During the 重要指令 to 建立 暴政 Park 2, 绝地潜兵 were ordered to kill various 重型 敌人, including Chargers. Due to the MO 反制 not counting its 变体, the count had a lower growth rate, which projected an MO failure.
    • Despite this discrepancy, High 指挥 did agree that 强袭虫 Behemoths were considered as Chargers, and their kills were added to the MO 反制. This retroactive addition led to the MO to be considered a success.
