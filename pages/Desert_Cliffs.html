大气层[edit | edit 来源]

Depending on atmospheric composition and outside factors like the color of the system's star, the overall color of the 行星's 大气层 may change. This may be seen when selecting the 行星 on the 银河地图, where the glowing outline of 行星's hologram turns into it's 大气层's respective color.

  • Blue: Planets like Erata Prime have a normal blue 大气层, similarly to deserts found on Earth.
  • Orange: Planets like Polaris Prime have an orange 大气层, changing the sky color and makes the orange sand much more vibrant.

Metropolises[edit | edit 来源]

In parks located in Metropolises, the usual sandy 地形 can be found. Although the 生物群系 seemingly has no trees, trees 通常 seen in Primordial biomes (such as the 火山 Jungle) will generate, having green leaves in this 生物群系.

The Gloom[edit | edit 来源]

The Gloom changes the skies into a much more noticeable yellow. The night skies still 特性 the bright orange Gloom spores from space, reflecting 轻型 back down onto the 行星 itself and giving it a more green night color.

Unlike on most biomes, The Gloom does not disable any environmental conditions.

Exostorms[edit | edit 来源]

On Desert Cliffs planets that harbor an Exostorm, distant fog will appear and the skies will become dustier. As the Exostorm lifts sand into the air, the 浮出地面 will become darker for each Exostorm 级别 until daytime looks like dusk. Notably though, the Tremors and 极暑 environmental conditions aren't present whatsoever, and are instead replaced by a constant effectless and less-potent sandstorm.

The purple streaks that have been radiated from the 行星 into orbit can be seen from the ground between the clouds both during daytime and nighttime and 偶尔 "blink", giving the 浮出地面 a more purple color before dimming.
