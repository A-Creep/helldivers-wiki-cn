    沙漠悬崖

    The Desert Cliffs is a Sandy 类型 生物群系 that can be found on 多种星球 in 绝地潜兵 2.

    描述

    “

    A desert 行星 prone to unpredictable and 危险 sand twisters.

    The Desert Cliffs 生物群系 is composed of rocky and sandy 地形, more often than not being very hilly, however, it still features flatter areas, and 偶尔 a set of misty springs. Large rocks cover the 浮出地面, dividing parts of the map and providing cover. In between the rocks and the sand, 有时 tall, dead shrubs can be found.

    地表变体

    大气层

    Depending on atmospheric composition and outside factors like the color of the system's star, the overall color of the 行星's 大气层 may change. This may be seen when selecting the 行星 on the 银河地图, where the glowing outline of 行星's hologram turns into it's 大气层's respective color.

    • Blue: Planets like Erata Prime have a normal blue 大气层, similarly to deserts found on Earth.
    • Orange: Planets like Polaris Prime have an orange 大气层, changing the sky color and makes the orange sand much more vibrant.

    大都市

    In parks located in Metropolises, the usual sandy 地形 can be found. Although the 生物群系 seemingly has 否 trees, trees 通常 seen in Primordial biomes (such as the 火山 Jungle) will generate, having green leaves in this 生物群系.

    阴霾

    The Gloom 变更 the skies into a much more noticeable yellow. The night skies still 特性 the bright orange Gloom spores from space, reflecting 轻型 back down onto the 行星 itself and giving it a more green night color.

    与大多数生物群系不同，“阴霾”不会禁用任何环境条件。

    异界风暴

    On Desert Cliffs planets that harbor an Exostorm, distant fog will appear and the skies will become dustier. As the Exostorm lifts sand into the air, the 浮出地面 will become darker for each Exostorm 级别 until 白天 looks like dusk. Notably though, the 地震 and 极暑 environmental conditions aren't present whatsoever, and are instead replaced by a constant effectless and less-potent sandstorm.

    从地面望去，在白天和黑夜的云层之间都能看到从行星辐射到轨道的紫色条纹，它们偶尔还会"闪烁"，在变暗之前让地表呈现出更紫的色泽。

    寂域

    On Desert Cliffs planets encompassed by The Void, not many 变更 to the geography can be seen. Notably, nights seem to be brighter than normal, with the skies being filled with puffy clouds and enough sand to color parts of the skies yellow/orange.

    环境条件

    • 极暑不会在夜间出现。

    极暑 – Periods of high temperatures 增加 耐力 drain and 速度 up heat buildup in 武器.
    地震 – Frequent 地震 stun 玩家 and 敌人 alike.

    环境危害

    • 偶尔出现的雾气弥漫的泉水，无害，不过会减慢任何决定在其中泡澡的绝地潜兵的速度
    • Tall dead Ferns, slowing any creatures walking through down
