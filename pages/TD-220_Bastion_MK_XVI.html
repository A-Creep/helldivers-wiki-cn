Driver Controls:

Gunner Controls:

  • Press C - switch seats between the Gunner, the Left Passenger, and Right Passenger positions (in that order).
  • Press M1 - fires the main cannon.
  • Press R - reloads the main cannon. This 流程 takes 5 秒, and will be interrupted if the Gunner vacates the position.
  • Hold M2 - fires the 重机枪.

  • the TD-220 Bastion MK XVI is a Tank 驱逐舰 (TD) that is crewed by two 绝地潜兵 (a 驾驶员，以及 炮手) and can hold two Passengers:

  • 炮手 - accessed in the rear.
    • Controls the main armament(s) - the 120mm Main Cannon, and a coaxial 重机枪.
    • Can switch between this seat, and the two Passenger positions.
  • Passenger(s) - accessed on each side of the tank (casemate roof).
    • Can switch between these seats, and the Main Gunner position.
    • Able to use personal 装备, such as primaries, secondaries, 支援武器, and 战略配备. Also able to stim, and use throwables. Cannot aim down sights.
    • Can hunker inside the the TD for safety, in case of enemy 火焰.
