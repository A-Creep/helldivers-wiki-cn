你为什么要降级 GPU 驱动程序？

The latest GPU drivers of both AMD and NVIDIA have been causing issues. Especially on AMD Vega-based Hardware (dedicated GPUs and APUs) newer drivers will cause consistent crashing on missions with 终结族.

⚠ 重要 message about the recently added driver check! ⚠

The recently added driver check is potentially bugged and will flag the currently installed GPU driver as outdated while providing a non-existing driver number. However, the GPU drivers listed below have been chosen due to 稳定性 and are known to work with the game. If you can play the game without 崩溃 or other issues, you can skip the 警告 and 继续 to play normally. In case you do encounter 崩溃 or graphical issues, a GPU driver 更新 might be able to help.

我应该选择哪个驱动程序？

⚠ 警告: Due to recent 安全 flaws discovered on all GPU drivers we 否 longer recommend using older GPU drivers! ⚠

Due to the recent news surrounding severe GPU driver 安全 flaws, older drivers can 否 longer be recommended in good conscience. The reason for this is simple: An attacker could potentially exploit the 弱点 discovered in those drivers to gain access to your system. Therefore it is highly advised to use the latest drivers in the meantime.

The issue with this lies in the drivers themselves and is present, 无论 the 软件 you use your 电脑 for; so even in the event that you are not playing 绝地潜兵 2, these recommendations should be followed.

This issue of course is a predicament for 玩家 who use AMD APUs and dGPUs based on the AMD VEGA; they were 必需 to use older drivers to avoid crashing on the 终结族 正面 but this older driver has the aforementioned 安全 flaw. It is advised to prioritize your PCs 安全 over being able to play 绝地潜兵 2.

For more information, please check these sites:

  • 安全公告：NVIDIA GPU 显示驱动程序 - May 2026
  • AMD 图形驱动程序漏洞 – 2月 2026
  • Intel 图形建议通告 - INTEL-SA-01385

如何降级你的驱动程序

⚠ If you have BitLocker enabled, please keep your BitLocker Key ready as you will be prompted to unlock it. ⚠

如果你在遵循以下步骤时遇到困难或需要额外信息，这里有一份关于如何使用 DDU 的教程。

  1. 下载 the recommended driver version for your GPU.
  2. Please 下载 the 显示驱动程序卸载程序 (DDU).
  3. Reboot your 电脑 into safe mode. [2]
  4. Click "是" at the "User 账户 控制" prompt.
  5. If it's your first time running DDU, click "Okay/Close" on the pop-up full of checkboxes that will appear. If you've run DDU before this will not appear.
  6. On the right 侧面 you will see two 掉落 boxes. 选择设备类型 in one (select GPU) and 选择设备 should automatically select to your GPU brand, if you have an iGPU or APU as well as a Discrete GPU make sure the 正确 brand is selected for your 独立 GPU.
  7. On the Top Left you will see three grey boxes, select the top one "Clean and 重新开始".
  8. Once your PC has rebooted you should be back in the Regular Desktop 环境 and can 安装 the GPU Driver of choice.
  9. 请通过以下步骤清理你的 Shadercache 文件夹.

If you are still in Safe-Mode after the reboot please check this Microsoft 指南 on how to 地址 this and then 继续 with the installation of the GPU Driver of your choice as seen above.
