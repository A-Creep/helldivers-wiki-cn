目标 Steps[edit | edit 来源]

占领精炼厂[edit | edit 来源]

“

这座精炼厂将原矿加工成高品质铂金。夺取控制权。

— 目标 概述

  • 定位并解锁系统访问端口
    • 绝地潜兵 must find and interact with a SSSD 容器 found inside the 精炼厂
  • Use 战略配备: SSSD 运送
    • 绝地潜兵 must call down the SSSD by inputting:

  • 收集 the SSSD 硬盘
  • 将超级固态硬盘置入插槽
    • The receptacle can be found 下一个 to the 精炼厂.
  • Hack into the 终端 to takeover 精炼厂
    • 绝地潜兵 must find and interact with the 终端 found inside the base
  • 绕过终端安全系统
    • 绝地潜兵 are presented with a puzzle, requiring them to match 机器人 letters into the 正确 order, shown by the key on the right side of the 终端.

  • 使用终端接管精炼厂
    • The 绝地潜兵 must flip 3 switches (crushing, smelting, purification).
  • Wait for 流程 to finish
    • Transferring data from the SSSD will take 45 秒.
  • 使用终端完成占领
    • Flip 1 switch (安装 rootkit).
    • Overwrite core protocols.
      • The 绝地潜兵 must input a randomized 5-input code into the 终端.
  • Reward: 200 申购点 Slips 和 50 经验值

吞并采石场[edit | edit 来源]

“

机器人正在这座采石场内从我们公民的手中窃取这颗星球的矿物。将资源夺回来。

— 目标 概述

  • If no 绝地潜兵 is within the 25-meter 射程
    • 绝地潜兵 must move closer to the 目标 位置.
  • Use 战略配备: 超级地球旗帜

()

  • 升起旗帜
    • 绝地潜兵 must wait and defend the flag from constant 机器人空投 the entire one minute and forty 秒 until the 目标 is 完成.
    • 敌人 near the 目标 must be all killed once the flag has fully raised to 完成 the 目标.
    • Should all 绝地潜兵 leave the area of the flag before the 目标 is completed, the flag's progress will begin reversing.
  • Reward: 200 申购点 Slips 和 50 经验值

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • The main 精炼厂 Crusher located at the "Appropriate 精炼厂" 目标 will insta-kill any 绝地潜兵 entering it, regardless if its active or not.
  • Seize Industrial 复杂 is currently the only 任务 that has its icon change after a certain 难度 (changing from a 精炼厂 icon to a Flag onto a rock icon).
