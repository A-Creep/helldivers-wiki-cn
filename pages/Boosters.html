强化资源

Boosters

Claimed in various 战争债券, 增益 are 效果 beneficial to your squad that you can add to your 武装配置 before dropping into a 任务. They provide extra 生命值, 耐力, or a tactical 优势 in the battlefield. Each 绝地潜兵 can equip their own 强化资源 up to a 最大 of four unique ones 每次任务.
