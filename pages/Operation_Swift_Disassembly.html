操作 Swift Disassembly was a 军事 操作 against the 机器人 during the 第二次银河战争 in 绝地潜兵 2. It was a significant 任务 aimed at completely eradicating the 机器人. Each phase was designed to serve a different 目的 to 达成 that goal.

Phase I: Disrupt Communications[edit | edit 来源]

The goal of phase I was to disrupt enemy communications, vital for crippling their coordination and intelligence capabilities. It would also allow obtaining 致命 intelligence about an 未知 机器人 plan called "The Reclamation." High 指挥 managed to 识别 a long-射程 communications array on the 行星 Troost in the Trigon 分区, deep in 机器人 space. The 任务 was a success and a mysterious 广播 was discovered being sent outside of the Galactic Frontier. Unfortunately, the bots managed to delete further information and 超级地球 analysts were unable to determine more.

Phase II: Reduce Non-有知觉 参战者 生产[edit | edit 来源]

Phase II of the 操作 aimed to 摧毁 the 生产 centers of enemy combatants. The most significant 机器人 工厂 was identified to be located on the 行星 Tibit in the Severin 分区, and promptly a 庞大 攻击 was launched to reclaim the 行星. Unfortunately, due to indecision, thousands of 绝地潜兵 remained on other fronts, fighting the bots on Malevelon Creek or containing the bugs on 终结族-controlled worlds. As a result, the 行星 was not claimed on time and the 机器人 managed to move their 生产 to other locations. 操作 Swift Disassembly was briefly put on pause in order to focus on the 解放 of the 臭名昭著 Malevelon Creek, which fell in mere hours under the focused might of the 绝地潜兵.

Phase III: Hold Ground[edit | edit 来源]

After the heroic 解放 of Malevelon Creek, it was time to 继续 操作 Swift Disassembly. The 机器人, anticipating their impending doom, had put their whole 剩余 resources into launching a large scale 反击. To prevent their spread back into 超级地球 领土, phase III was planned to defend the strategically located planets of Malevelon Creek, Ubanea, and Draupnir. The bots devised new 类型 of units (炮舰 and 移动工厂) to try and overwhelm 超级地球 forces, but their efforts turned out to be in vain as 绝地潜兵 managed to valiantly defend their homeland.

Phase IV: Annihilation[edit | edit 来源]

Having pushed off the 机器人 攻击, 绝地潜兵 were ready to take the fight back to them. High 指挥 ordered the 解放 of the last 剩余 机器人 worlds of Tibit, Durgen, and Maia. Eventually, the last bot fell and the 机器人 威胁 was no more, at least for the time being.

Aftermath[edit | edit 来源]

Initially, 操作 Swift Disassembly appeared to be a success, with the 绝地潜兵 eliminating all 可见 机器人 threats, which led to a brief moment of 庆祝 as it seemed that peace had been restored. However, this victory was short-lived, as the 机器人 soon returned in 更大 numbers, revealing that the initial forces were just a 先锋. A much larger fleet has launched an 特攻 on 多种星球, including Cyberstan, in an effort known as the Reclamation.
