克里姆西卡第一次战役

First Battle of Crimsica
阵营 终结族
Start Date March 15th
End Date March 21st
行星 Crimsica
操作
Group
Casualties
重要指令 相关; 解放 of Zagon Prime and Fori Prime
Summary Crimsica was not liberated.


背景

Crimsica was a 行星 originally owned by the 终结族 in the "quarantined zone" found in the Draco 分区. 绝地潜兵 engaged on this 行星 briefly in their effort to 完成 the 重要指令 of liberating Fori Prime & Zagon Prime.

战略与重要性

Crimsica was a 终结族 controlled 行星 in the "quarantined zone" close to Fori Prime which was easily accessible from one of the 终结族控制系统 屏障 Planets - Meridia, in the Umlaut 分区. 控制 of this 行星 or Estanu meant that 绝地潜兵 can focus on liberating Fori Prime. Many 绝地潜兵 chose to liberate Estanu first. This 行星 continued to be unimportant for the rest of the 重要指令.

战斗

The majority of 绝地潜兵 forces were focusing mainly on Estanu rather than Crimsica to liberate Fori Prime during the 重要指令. Since Zagon Prime was effectively unobtainable with the time available, some 绝地潜兵 chose to deploy to Crimsica in an attempt to gain a foothold on the 行星. By the end of the 重要指令 at the time, Crimsica had 大约 16% 解放.

克里姆西卡第二次战役

Second Battle of Crimsica
阵营 终结族
Start Date March 21st
End Date March 24th
行星 Crimsica
操作
Group
Casualties
重要指令 保护 of Biomass 合集 Teams
Summary Crimsica was liberated and successfully defended on the same day.


背景

Fori Prime & Estanu had been liberated. Crimsica was the last 行星 left to be liberated by the 绝地潜兵 in the Draco 分区. The 重要指令: 保护 of Biomass 合集 Teams, 必需 Crimsica to be finally liberated and under 控制 as Biomass-合集 Teams processed inert 终结族 for crude E-710.

战略与重要性

Crimsica, Estanu, Fori Prime, and Hellmire were all 重要 planets for the 合集 of E-710 efforts. Estanu & Fori Prime in the Draco 分区 were already under Super Earths 控制 at the start of the 操作, leaving Crimsica as the last 行星 to be liberated from the 分区. This would mark the first time that Crimsica - and by extension, the whole of the Draco 分区 - would be under 超级地球 控制 since the start of the 第二次银河战争.

战斗

解放 was swift, only taking 大约 1 day and 20 hours to be liberated by the 绝地潜兵. As it was the last 剩余 行星 in the Draco 分区 yet to be liberated at the time, it attracted a large amount of 绝地潜兵 activity. On the same day 2 hours after its initial 解放, 终结族 attempted an 攻击 to reclaim the 行星. This was quickly dealt with by the end of the day in roughly 2-3 hours of combat. Crimsica remained under 超级地球 控制 for the time being.

克里姆西卡第三次战役

Third Battle of Crimsica
阵营 终结族
Start Date March 31st
End Date April 8th
行星 Crimsica
操作
Group
Casualties
重要指令 Safe E-710 Farm Construction
Summary Crimsica loses its initial 防御; keeps a foothold; and sees eventual 解放.


背景

As the 防御 of Fori Prime falls, Crimsica is left 易受 an 攻击 by the 终结族. Most of the 绝地潜兵 forces are focused on the 机器人 at this time as 操作 Swift Disassembly is underway. A 英勇 防御 attempts to push back as to prevent the 终结族's attempt at retaking the Draco 分区. After the fall of the 机器人 during 操作 Swift Disassembly, Crimsica was one of the three planets selected to be a new, safer, and more 高效 来源 for E-710 as mentioned in the 重要指令: Safe E-710 Farm Construction.

战略与重要性

Since 操作 Swift Disassembly was underway to focus on the eradication of the 机器人, Crimsica held little importance for the 重要指令 at the time. Even though its 能力 to provide another avenue of 攻击 to 支援 Fori Prime is useful, as long as Estanu did not fall as well, Fori Prime still remained in reach - which made it acceptable to lose ground at Crimsica. As the 绝地潜兵 kept a presence on the 行星 and 操作 Swift Disassembly finished, new 主要命令 were issued including Crimsica as one of its 主体 goals. Crimsica, Estanu, & Hellmire were all 重要 planets in constructing E-710 farms for the 重要指令.

战斗

At the start of the Third Battle of Crimsica, a marginal amount of the 绝地潜兵 deployed to Crimsica to mount a 防御 at an attempt to not lose the entire 行星. Although the initial 防御 failed to fully hold the 入侵, it prevented the entire 行星 to fall to the 终结族 looking to retake the Draco 分区. Shortly after gaining a foothold on Crimsica, Estanu was attacked by 终结族. Crimsica boasted a high amount of 绝地潜兵 activity 下一个 to Estanu as the Draco 分区 witnessed back and forth efforts in reclaiming all three of its planets throughout the week. The 8th of April saw a considerable amount of 绝地潜兵 增援 as 操作 Swift Disassembly came to a close and the 重要指令: Safe E-710 Farm Construction was underway. In a matter of hours, Crimsica was quickly liberated. There was a sudden unexplained spike of 解放 % near the end of the long and daunting battle on Crimsica as 超级地球 finally reclaimed 控制 of the 行星 in the Draco 分区.

Summary Initially loses 防御 on Crimsica; sees 解放 a few days later.


背景

Even after recently establishing new E-710 farms, Crimsica was attacked again on the 11th of April. Most 绝地潜兵 are focused on the efforts of establishing the Menkent Line, leaving Crimsica to only have a piecemeal 抗性 to 维护 控制 of the 行星.

战略与重要性

Although Crimsica housed new E-710 farms vital to operations, the 机器人 situation was more pressing at the time. Crimsica was held as lower priority.

战斗

超级地球 once again loses its 防御 on Crimsica against the 终结族 on the 12th of April but maintained a foothold on the 行星. 增援 arrived to help liberate Crimsica on the 14th of April, slowly liberating the 行星 by the 15th of April. Crimsica was once again reclaimed by 超级地球 through the efforts of the 绝地潜兵. The Draco 分区 was fully under 控制 of 超级地球 武装力量, until another 爆发 was present by the end of the day at Fori Prime.

Fifth Battle of Crimsica
阵营 终结族
Start Date April 29th
End Date April 30th
行星 Crimsica
操作
Group
Casualties
重要指令 否
Summary Crimsica is lost.


背景

After enjoying rather peaceful days, Crimsica is hit with a thunderous 攻击 by the 终结族 a little over a week after its last successful 防御 on the 15th of April. With the 终结族控制系统 out of 控制, 超级地球 武装力量 along with a sparse amount of 绝地潜兵 were all that remained for 防御. Meridia becomes a Supercolony and cuts off Supply Lines to Crimsica.

战略与重要性

Without the Supply Lines that Meridia provided, along with the high amount of 终结族 backlash, it was highly unlikely that 超级地球 would be able to 控制 Crimsica much longer. In addition, many 绝地潜兵 were too busy managing the situation on the 屏障 Planets that the 防御 of Crimsica was once again pushed to be a low priority. Any 防御 efforts at Crimsica focused on quick 撤离 and the saving of lives of any surviving personnel still on the 行星.

战役

撤离 missions were underway to save as many lives as possible still on Crimsica. On the 30th of April, Crimsica fell to the 终结族. Estanu is then attacked an hour after Crimsica falls.

克里姆西卡第六次战役

Sixth Battle of Crimsica
阵营 终结族
Start Date June 3rd
End Date June 16th
行星 Crimsica
操作
Group
Casualties
重要指令 Hive Break 终结族 Nurseries
Summary Crimsica was eventually liberated.


背景

Meridia succumbs to the Dark Fluid and is effectively destroyed in 操作 Enduring Peace, opening permanent Supply Lines to Estanu and Crimsica. In over a month's time, Crimsica sees battle once more. A 重要指令 involving Crimsica was 已公布 on the 15th of June.

战略与重要性

Although Crimsica is open for 解放, it is of little importance at this time as the 当前 主要命令: Clear Post-Supercolony Infestations calls for the 解放 of Erata Prime, Turing, Acamar IV, & Phact Bay. Crimsica is now much easier to reach because Meridia is an unattackable Supply Line. On the 15th of June, Crimsica became one of the first planets to gain a 重型 activity from the 绝地潜兵 due to the new 重要指令: Hive Break 终结族 Nurseries - calling for the 解放 of Hellmire, Estanu, Nivel 43, Fori Prime, and Crimsica for resettlement efforts.

战役

On the 3rd of June, Crimsica once again has 绝地潜兵 on the ground. Although not a large contingency of them due to the 当前 重要指令, the battle was underway regardless. Crimsica did not gain any 解放 % during those early deployments. The battle was a stalemate for a long time, even though almost 25 thousand 绝地潜兵 tried to liberate the 行星. Fortunetly, on 15th of June, High 指挥 issued a new 重要指令 that tasked 绝地潜兵 with liberating many planets, Crimsica was a part of this 重要指令 - being the first 行星 of five to 接收 focused 解放 efforts and was shortly liberated on the 16th of June.
