死亡冲刺

Boosters
死亡冲刺
“

通过消耗生命值，让绝地潜兵在耐力耗尽时仍能继续冲刺。

— 军械库描述

死亡冲刺 是一个 增益 in 绝地潜兵 2. When equipped, all 绝地潜兵 will be able to keep sprinting after they're exhausted, though it will eat though your HP.

描述

死亡冲刺 allows 绝地潜兵 to cross longer stretches of land without using Stims or interrupting their sprint to regain their 耐力.

When used outside of combat, 死亡冲刺 saves time and potential Stims by allowing 绝地潜兵 to 继续 sprinting, allowing their 生命值 to drain and only using a Stim by the end of their hike from point A to B. It also condenses the amount of time spent sprinting in a situation where one was going to use a Stim regardless.

In active 冲突, it effectively allows 绝地潜兵 to kite 敌人 without stopping so long as their 生命值 allows for it, in a way trading potential enemy 攻击 for the minor chip 伤害 inflicted by the 强化资源.

采购

the 死亡冲刺 强化资源 is unlocked on the 1st page of the 真理执行者 尊享战争债券 用于 35 奖章.

战术信息

  • Drains 大约 3.6%[1] 生命值 (or ~1% with Vitality Enhancement) 每秒 spent sprinting.
  • Allows 25 秒 of continuous sprinting once out of 耐力, and stops once 生命值 reaches 5%.[2]
  • 死亡冲刺 won't activate when below the 5% 生命值 threshold, and does not have the 能力 to kill 绝地潜兵 should their 生命值 reach 1.
  • Vitality Enhancement greatly 降低 the 生命值 drain incurred from 死亡冲刺, allowing for 120 秒 of total sprint once out of 耐力.[3]
