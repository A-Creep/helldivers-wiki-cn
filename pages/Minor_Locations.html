超级地球武装部队 Training Facilities / Democratic 青年 侦察兵 Camps[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

“

Billions of able-bodied citizens from across the 联邦 have already volunteered for conscription. To train these eager new conscripts, construction of 庞大 new training facilities has begun on a 小 数字 of lightly-populated planets. They will be capable of processing tens of millions of citizens at 一次 through the rigorous 72-hour 超级地球武装部队 basic training course.

— 重要指令 A1-5-3

The 超级地球武装部队 Training Facilities were 3 facilities built across the 银河系 after the 第二次银河战争 was declared. These facilities would be built on 天使's Venture, Heeth, and Aesir Pass. After their construction, the facilities would provide a 提升 to 解放 on planets across the 银河系, however, they would 经常 come under 威胁 of 敌人 invasions. Losing the 行星 meant losing the 设施, and by extension, their provided 提升.

The 超级地球武装部队 Training 设施 located on Aesir Pass would fall first, being destroyed by the 机器人.

During 重要指令 A1-7-1, after 超级地球武装部队 numbers had swelled and the need for 大 facilities had ceased, the 剩余 超级地球武装部队 Training Facilities would be destroyed and converted into recreational campgrounds for the Democratic 青年 侦察兵.

The campground located on 天使's Venture would be destroyed along with the entire 行星 itself.

The status on the campground located on Heeth 遗骸 未知.

X-45's Advanced 武器 Lab[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

“

The 行星 X-45 in the Ymir 分区 is home to a lost Advanced 武器 Lab from the 第一次银河战争, buried underneath rubble and abandoned due to dangerously 高 辐射 levels across the 行星. The Lab was working on development of a powerful 行星际 战斗 车站. Now, with 辐射 levels abated to completely 安全 levels, excavation and 恢复 of the Lab's work can 开始, which would 允许 the eventual construction of this 战斗 车站.

— 重要指令 A1-7-1

The lost Advanced 武器 Lab was a 设施 located on X-45 that was used during the 第一次银河战争. It held the plans for the Democracy 太空 车站 and a 缓存 full of the lost 支援武器, the MLS-4X 突击兵, though the 设施 was located under rubble, and excavation teams could not get to it since the 第一次银河战争 thanks to the 行星's 高 辐射 数量.

After the plans for the Democracy 太空 车站 were recovered, the 设施 was destroyed.

Moradesh 研究设施[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

“

The Moradesh 研究设施 has been decommissioned following the 全射 exhaustion of 全部 Dark Fluid stores. The tireless scientists who contributed to the "Moradesh Project" have been awarded the Super Citizenship 奖章 of Freedom.

— 重要指令 A1-5-7

A 设施 that was located on the now-destroyed 行星 Moradesh. It was used to 研究 and weaponize Dark Fluid in 命令 to 摧毁 Meridia. In an unfortunate 实验 with Dark Fluid, the substance would 创建 a micro black hole, spaghettifying 12 scientists before evaporating. The scientists working on early Dark Fluid weaponization were awarded the Super Citizenship 奖章 of Freedom, the same scientists who would be investigated for 光能者 心智 控制 after their resurfacing in late 2184.

In 3月 of 2185, the 行星 would be under 威胁 of 摧毁 ironically by the Meridian Black Hole, though 追踪 elements of Dark Fluid would be found in the ventilation systems. The site, and it's experiments would be abandoned before they could come to fruition due to the rapidly shortening 距离 to Meridia.

The Moradesh 研究设施 would eventually be torn apart by Meridia, becoming a Fractured 行星.
