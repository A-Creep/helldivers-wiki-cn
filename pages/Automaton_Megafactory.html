Megafactories are a 类型 of megastructure, unique to Regions that are found on 部分星球. They are exclusively found on planets 机器人 控制, and 生产 the   Cyborg Legion.

    • 概述

    Megafactories, unlike 超级地球 Metropolises, do not cover the entire 任务 area. They leave a 章节 (about 20-30% of the 任务 area) as the original 行星's 生物群系. These exterior portions 通常 contain the 撤离 Zone, and have a 生成几率 a handful of 哨站 and the occasional 可选目标, such as Upload Escape Pod Data. The 地形 inside the Megafactories also 遗骸 relatively as it was, excluding mountains, large rock formations, and any kind of ravine.

    Inside the Megafactories stand tall, brutalist industrial buildings and machinery which 偶尔 have entry points, allowing both 绝地潜兵 and 机器人 units to walk through them. Enemy 哨站 are constantly found here alongside enemy Optional 目标. Monuments to the   Cyborg Legion can also be found.

    The 机器人/Cyborg 语言 is printed on most of the large 工厂 buildings. 更小 prints, such as signs, 显示 sentences （翻译见本页面的图库，或 机器人#宣传). Larger prints, either on the sides of buildings or along pipes, 显示 numbers.

    Between the Megafactory and the normal rural area stands a large, thick wall, separating them from each other with only a few access points, marked by icons on the map:

    • 涵洞： 偶尔 along the wall, the 地形 will suddenly dip lower to reveal a large culvert with a broken grate.
    • 破损墙壁： Some segments of the wall are destroyed, creating an opening into the Megafactory.
    • 大门： 庞大 gates are commonly found across the wall. Though they are closed from the start, they can be destroyed by dealing enough 伤害 to them.
      • 机器人 units can also open and close the gate itself when they get close.

    环境危害

    Outside the Megafactory wall, a 行星 may retain its 生物群系's 环境危害; for those, see the 行星's 生物群系 page. This 章节 is about hazards found in every Megafactory 无论 生物群系.

    • Large 混凝土 cutouts full of Lava can be found across the 浮出地面. Stepping on them will deal 伤害 and set the 绝地潜兵 on   火焰.
    • Occasional spills of   酸 can be found on the ground. Stepping on them will deal 伤害 over time.
    • Tar slicks 偶尔 cover the floor near structures, particularly around Lava pools. Stepping on them slows down 绝地潜兵 and 敌人.
    • Ventilation systems that release steam can be found across most buildings. Staying near them upon steam release will deal minor 伤害 and knock 绝地潜兵 down.

    战术信息

    • Due to the 有限 entrances to the Megafactory, caution must be taken to ensure 绝地潜兵 are able to escape outside of them if the number of 敌人 starts overrunning their position.
      • These entrances are relatively 小型, making them 极佳 咽喉点 to 消除 敌人. Be aware that heavier 敌人 (工厂 Striders, Vox Engines, etc) are able to pass through them as well.
    • Due to the relatively open nature of the 地形, especially compared to Metropolises, 绝地潜兵 must be aware of their surroundings or risk being flanked by 敌人.
      • These are even more 危险 with close combat 敌人, such as Agitators, 狂暴者, and Radicals.
    • The use of 进攻型 战略配备 is highly advised, though the 已增加 presence of 防空 defenses means that Eagle 战略配备 may be affected. 绝地潜兵 are advised to seek and 摧毁 them whenever possible, even if they do not use Eagle 战略配备.
    • Gate entrances can either be destroyed or opened by 机器人. A slow, but simple way to open them is to 火焰 into the air and bait them into opening it.
      • Finding an alternative entrance/exit (through a culvert or a broken wall) is more advisable and 高效.
