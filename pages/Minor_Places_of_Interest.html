Minor Places of Interest (m兴趣点) are minor locations scattered around on 远征*-任务 maps containing various items of 数值, 一些 depending on the 难度 level.

*远征-任务 being random-generated-maps, I.E. not 已修复 歼灭 or 撤离-任务-maps.

These are not to be confused with 银河系 Places of Interest.

Locations and Icons[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Minor Places of Interests can be found in 多个 ways:

  • 全部 mPOIs are displayed onto the topographical 小地图. Spotting or otherwise recognizing 一些 of the mPOIs can be tricky due the numerous variations of the topographical drawings varying between different biomes or otherwise having different randomly selected sharpness and other quality variations.
  • The compass displays a 问题 mark ("?") when 绝地潜兵 is within 60-metres 半径 of undiscovered mPOI-markers; this mark functions identically to pinged locations being bright while in 田野-of-查看 and transparent at the edges of the compass.
  • Completing the 雷达站 次要目标 highlights 全部 POIs on the map.
  • 偶尔 the marking-cursor on the mini-map can reveal 一些 (and 非常 很少 全部 mPOIs) with a 次要特殊地点 文本-pop-up. NOTE: This method becomes functional only after first playing an 远征-类型 (not 歼灭 or Defence)-任务 during 每个 玩法-session / ".exe-boot".

When found, the icons of Minor Places of Interests will 变化 on the 小地图 if it was sufficiently looted of 全部 samples (ammunition and 支援武器 通常 do not 数量):

  • Contains Samples / un-explored mPOI.
  • Does not contain Samples/全部 Samples collected.

An exception to note is that if there was a 救援 Pod at the mPOI, and it did not contain a 支援武器, then the 图标 will not 变化 from the gem after successful looting, even if 全部 样本 and 货币 have been collected. If it did contain a 支援武器, then it will 变化 after picking it up. This is unique to 救援 Pods, and does not apply to 地下 Caches or other 类型 of mPOIs.

Guards at mPOIs[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

On 挑战 and above, 敌人 can be present at 最多 Minor Places of Interests. On 自杀任务 and above, 敌人 can occupy them, along with their appearances changing, such as adding structures 点赞 倒刺铁丝网 or 加农炮 炮塔.

Minor Places of Interest Within Cities[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

The 世代 of mPOIs within colonies / cities has 一些 exceptions:

  • 全部 eligible mPOI candidate "tiles" will be generated regardless of if they 结束 up being a mPOI or not, such "Checkpoints" and "Customs / 集装箱 Platforms".
    • This 规则 allows a 几率 of 拓殖地-tileset being the 最多 densely populated by mPOIs. E.G. There can be 几个 救援 Pods placed within 少数 steps of 每个 other due to the 小 浮出地面-范围-要求 of these mPOIs.
  • 最多 importantly mPOIs inside 超级地球 colonial cities / urban 定居点 zones are only marked as a mPOI if there is at 最少 a 样本, 支援武器, or a 特殊 战利品 容器 (including bunkers).
    • E.G. 集装箱 范围 / customs will only be marked as a mPOI if there is a lootable (shipping) 容器.

The above rules however only affect the colonial / offworld cities: Mega 城市 / 超级地球 Metropolis-mPOI-世代 works for the 最多 部分 identically to other non-城市 mPOIs. I.E. the Mega 城市 mPOI-assets are fully tied to the mPOI itself and cannot exist as non-mPOI, even if they exist as a "scenery-prop" akin to empty "地面 Stash"-mPOIs. Metropolis-biomes also spawn 两者 decorative and proper 地下 bunkers; these bunkers are 通常 located below a lone saluting 雕像 of a 绝地潜兵.

What decides the 数量 of mPOIs placed on a map?[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

The 数量 of mPOIs spawned (if 任何 to 开始 with) on the map hugely fluctuates depending on how "敌对" to mPOIs the generated map ends up being.

This is not only 有限 to 位置 and the 尺寸 of the main-目标 but also the 已修复 任务-难度 variable 数量 of 哨站 / Nests / Encampments alongside Optional 目标, 全部 which do occupy potential mPOI-slots. Therefore, it is 更多 likely to encounter far 更少 mPOIs on 更高 difficulties especially on mPOI-敌对 biomes.

E.G: 机器人 超级绝地潜兵难度 (10) on Icy Glaciers with plentiful 数量 of slippery 冰 and bodies of thawed 水 might not even have enough 太空 for a singular mPOI.

The larger 更多 浮出地面-范围 occupying mPOIs (E.G. Valleys) will also 减少 the 数量 of mPOIs on the map.

Theoretically, an 光能者 挑战-任务 map consisting only of 拓殖地 / 城市-tiles has the potential of to have over twenty (20-)mPOIs due to the 最大-map-尺寸 (400-meter 半径) to lowest 任务-目标 比率 (1 or 2-Main 目标 and 3-Optional 目标, and four spacious (4) Encampments). This potentially can also 允许 tens of "救援 Pod"-特殊-战利品-containers being placed near proximity of 每个 other, if not 正确 下一个 to 每个 other (cross-roads, sidewalks, 城市-corners, etc).

Of the mPOI Availability 每次任务[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Outside the 拓殖地 / 城市 and Metropolis biomes, 通常 only one of 每个 mPOI-布局 can exist on a 任务 map. E.G. There can be only one Solar 农场 and one 载具 修理 商店 and Junkyard.
    • mPOIs with different 布局-variants 通常 will co-exist on the same map, such as the 案例 with L Shaped House with a 矿场 and without one.
    • 农场 / Greenhouse have the same 布局 with different props, thus only 任一 one can exist a map.
    • Of the "wilderness" mPOIs, 地面 Stash and mPOI-marked Damaged Hellbombs are exceptions: These can exist in 多个 counts and 内容 variation, E.G. 全部 can be 任一 empty or have supplies.
  • The mPOIs also have their own "placement-命令 / 重量"-数值 of being placed on the map. The map-发电机 will at 最小 attempts to place these mPOIs (respectively) on the map if given enough 太空 and is allowed by the ruleset of 生物群系:
    • Burial Grounds (non-existent on Icy Glaciers 生物群系)
    • 地面 Stash
    • Damaged Hellbomb
    • L Shaped House without a 矿场
    • Solar 农场
    • 农场 / Greenhouse
    • 地下 / 小 Ditch 碉堡
    • Crashed 舰船 (on the 陆地)
    • L Shaped House with a 矿场
    • 地下 缓存
    • Destroyed House
    • Crashed 舰船 (into a 岩石 丘陵)

特殊 战利品[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

最多 Minor Places of Interests 特性 a 特殊 战利品 vessel. A Pod has 太空 for 1, a 货物 容器 has 太空 for 2, and a 碉堡 has 太空 for 3 with possible additional Supplies inside.

特殊 战利品 5月 be one of the following:

  • a 支援武器
  • a pile of 10, or (很少) 100 Syntax Error 超级货币
  • a slip 100, or (很少) Syntax Error 申购点
  • a 案例 of 1-3 Syntax Error 奖牌
  • one (1) 单个 Syntax Error 稀有; collecting these additional "undocumented" specimens won't despawn or prevent collecting the pre-generated "wild" units. Thus one can 撤离 with E.G. 23/20 稀有样本; the latter 数字 only indicates the 数量 of generated "下限"-units.

救援 Pods[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Pods can be opened by pressing the 互动-button and holding still for ~1-second after which the hatch will pop 打开; a standing 绝地潜兵 performs 休闲 Salute-emote. You can also salute from up to 5 米 away without interacting using the emote.

While pods are still closed, they emit a 信号 光束 which can be observed from 11-metres and beyond; the further away a 绝地潜兵 is, the 更高 reaching is the 光束. There is also 脉冲-sound emitted which can be faintly heard already from 65-米 away.

On 超级地球 Metropolis 生物群系-maps an additional 救援 Pod is placed within specific secondary 目标, E.G. Cognitive Disruptors and 雷达 Stations.

掩体[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

掩体 要求 two (2) coordinated 绝地潜兵 to 打开 海湾 门 by simultaneously pressing the unlock button.
