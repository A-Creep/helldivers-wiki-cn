背景故事[edit | edit 来源]

最大 安全 City (MSC) Construction Sites were established after 重要指令 A2-7-5, dedicated to the construction of the 最大 安全 Cities. These construction sites are the locations on which new MSCs are built to serve the citizens of 超级地球.

Equipped with high-定义 facial recognition cameras pre-installed in every block and 建筑, 已增加 foot and drone patrols, and dedicated 监视 satellites, 最大 安全 Cities are built to be impregnable to dissidents and thoughtcriminals alike.
