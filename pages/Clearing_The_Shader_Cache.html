What Is The Shader 缓存?[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

the shader 缓存 is a dedicated 存储 文件夹 that saves the results of shader compilation for reuse in 未来 sessions.

Shaders are mini-programs executed directly by your graphics card (GPU) to calculate visual elements, such as lighting, shadows, particle 效果, etc. By caching the results on your 存储 drive, the 游戏 引擎 can then instantly fetch the ready-to-use 数据, instead of re-compiling shaders over and over 再次. This can prevent severe 车架 空投 and ensuring a 光滑 经验值.[1]

This is especially 是 after a new 游戏 更新 or GPU 驾驶员 刷新 when the older cached files 否 longer 比赛 the new 软件 法典. Therefore it is recommended to 删除 the cached files after a 游戏 补丁 or 驾驶员 更新.

Corrupted shaders can 原因 a variety of problems, including:

(Linux) Shader Pre-Caching[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

By 默认, Steam on Linux utilizes a 特性 called "Shader Pre-Caching".[2] The idea behind shader pre-caching is to 下载 pre-compiled shader pipelines pre-optimized for your specific GPU architecture instead of compiling shaders locally 每个 时间 you 开始 the 游戏.

This 流程 is significantly 更少 computationally expensive for your hardware. Instead of experiencing stutters, dropping framerates and a reduction in 稳定性 you only need to 下载 更多, which is an 增加 in background 下载 traffic, as Steam 频繁 updates these shaders.
