“

The 机器人 have gained 地面 in this region, constructing a 广阔 网络 of Fabricators which 允许 them to replicate 极其 rapidly. We have detected a 狭窄 window of 维护, giving us a 几率 to 打击 at their facilities. 摧毁 全部 that you can.

— 任务 Briefing - 闪电战: 搜索与摧毁

the 机器人哨站 is an 可选目标 contained within a 群组 of 机器人 structures. They contain a 数字 of 机器人 Fabricators and/or 巨型构筑者. These 5月 periodically 创建 机器人 of various 类型 depending on the 难度 of the 任务. 次要兴趣点 cannot spawn near structures. It also contains 机器人 MG Emplacements, Despotic Explosives, Barbed Wire and Proximity Mines. To 完成 this 可选目标, 绝地潜兵 must 摧毁 全部 机器人 Fabricators, after which 机器人 cease 生成 from them. It will 总是显示 have Samples within it.

可选目标[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

While in the 范围 of 操作, 绝地潜兵 5月 optionally 摧毁 机器人 Fabricators. When a 绝地潜兵 enters the 机器人哨站, they will 呼叫 out that they have discovered it (if it wasn't marked on the map already), and the 次要目标 of "歼灭 机器人 Fabricators" pops up on the 正确 侧面 of the HUD. The task of the 次要目标 is "摧毁 全部 Fabricators". Successful 摧毁 of an 机器人哨站 is rewarded in 形成 of Syntax Error 经验值 and Syntax Error 申购点 at the 结束 of the 任务, scaled depending on how big the Outpost was.

Destroying 哨站[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • There are various methods to destroying Fabricators. 绝地潜兵 are advised to stand 清楚, because the 构筑者 will 爆炸.
    • An 爆炸 with a 最小 爆破 之势 of 20 can be thrown into or 射击 inside of the vents of a regular and Bulk 构筑者.
    • 两者 构筑者 类型 are 易受 to exterior 伤害 from  坦克 I 战略配备 such as 鹰式空袭 and an 轨道精准攻击, or 支援武器 such as the GR-8 无后坐力炮 and MLS-4X 突击兵.
    • 机器人 哨站 are particularly 易受 to 轨道武器 bombardment, making the 轨道游走火力网, 380mm HE 弹幕, and 120mm HE 弹幕 exceptional at 基地 clearing.
  • 机器人 Fortresses are well-defended against infantry 特攻, with a multitude of 碉堡炮台, 加农炮台, and MG Emplacements positioned across the 基地.
    • A 探测器 塔 can also be placed in the middle of the 堡垒, which can 呼叫 增援 to 保护 the 基地. 绝地潜兵 are advised to bring a 战略配备 capable of 爆破 50, as a Hellbomb will not be available to 呼叫 to 摧毁 it.
  • The 摧毁 of half of the 哨站 in the entire 范围 of operations will 原因 an 增加 of 巡逻 spawns.
    • 绝地潜兵 are advised to weigh the potential 优点 and 缺点 of clearing an Outpost in proximity of their 位置 vs having 更多 巡逻 spawns during their 任务 in 更高 difficulties.
