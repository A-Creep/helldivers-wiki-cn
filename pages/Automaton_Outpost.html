the 机器人哨站 is an 可选目标 contained within a group of 机器人 structures. They contain a number of 机器人 Fabricators and/or 巨型构筑者. These may periodically create 机器人 of various 类型 depending on the 难度 of the 任务. 次要兴趣点 cannot spawn near structures. It also contains 机器人 MG 炮台, Despotic Explosives, Barbed Wire and Proximity Mines. To 完成 this 可选目标, 绝地潜兵 must 摧毁 all 机器人 Fabricators, after which 机器人 cease 生成 from them. It will always have Samples within it.

可选目标

While in the area of 操作, 绝地潜兵 may optionally 摧毁 机器人 Fabricators. When a 绝地潜兵 enters the 机器人哨站, they will call out that they have discovered it (if it wasn't marked on the map already), and the 次要目标 of "歼灭 机器人 Fabricators" pops up on the right 侧面 of the HUD. The task of the 次要目标 is "摧毁 all Fabricators". Successful 摧毁 of an 机器人哨站 is rewarded in form of 经验值 和 申购点 Slips at the end of the 任务, scaled depending on how big the Outpost was.

战术信息

Locations

  • The 近似 位置 of 机器人 哨站 may be found on the 小地图 via red marked spots.
  • The 机器人 堡垒 will already be marked on the 小地图 with an 机器人 emblem surrounded by a large red area.

Destroying 哨站

  • There are various methods to destroying Fabricators. 绝地潜兵 are advised to stand clear, because the 构筑者 will 爆炸.
    • An 爆炸 with a 最小 爆破 force of 20 can be thrown into or shot inside of the vents of a regular and Bulk 构筑者.
    • Both 构筑者 类型 are 易受 to exterior 伤害 from  Tank I 战略配备 such as 鹰式空袭 and an 轨道精准攻击, or 支援武器 such as the GR-8 无后坐力炮 和 MLS-4X 突击兵.
    • 机器人 哨站 are particularly 易受 to 轨道武器 bombardment, making the 轨道游走火力网, 380mm HE 弹幕，以及 120mm HE 弹幕 exceptional at base clearing.
  • 机器人 Fortresses are well-defended against infantry 特攻, with a multitude of 碉堡炮台, 加农炮台，以及 MG 炮台 positioned across the base.
    • A 探测塔 can also be placed in the middle of the 堡垒, which can 呼叫 增援 to 保护 the base. 绝地潜兵 are advised to bring a 战略配备 capable of 爆破 50, as a Hellbomb will not be available to 呼叫 to 摧毁 it.
  • The 摧毁 of half of the 哨站 in the entire area of operations will cause an 增加 of patrol spawns.
    • 绝地潜兵 are advised to weigh the potential pros and cons of clearing an Outpost in proximity of their 位置 vs having more patrol spawns during their 任务 in higher difficulties.
