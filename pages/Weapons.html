概述

武器 have different 伤害类型. Most have 后坐力.

获取

Most personal 武器 are unlocked through 战争债券 using Medals, with some exceptions.[1]

最多 辅助武器 are purchased through the 舰船管理 with 申购单, with some exceptions.[2]

支援武器

支援武器 are selected as part of your 武装配置 during deployment. Once on the battlefield, 绝地潜兵 can summon these 武器 by inputting a unique 战略配备 code and throwing a beacon on the ground. They are then delivered from the 驱逐舰 to the 行星's 浮出地面 via Hellpods. Certain supports are either exclusive to or also available at various POIs.
