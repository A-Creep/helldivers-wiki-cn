First Battle of Menkent

Background

Menkent was a 行星 under 控制 of 超级地球 in the Hydra 分区 ever since the 第一次银河战争. The 行星 was engulfed in 冲突 on April 9th due to the 机器人 efforts in The Reclamation. The enemy swept the entire Valdis 分区 under their 控制 in mere hours, and easily took 控制 of Choohe in the Lacaille 分区 before Menkent saw its first battle in 大约 100 years.

策略 and Importance

Menkent has possible Supply Lines to two planets in the Lacaille 分区: Choohe & Chort Bay. Holding this 行星 at the time meant limiting enemy advancement options. Since the 主要命令 at the time was to successfully defend 5 planets, Menkent became a hotspot for 绝地潜兵 activity.

战斗

The majority of 绝地潜兵 deployed to Menkent's 防御 during start of The Reclamation. Because of this, Menkent was first 行星 to be successfully defended from the 机器人 during the 重要指令. The 绝地潜兵 continued their vigilance after their first victory on the 10th of April. Now that Lesath & Chort Bay were taken, it was only a matter of time until more waves of 机器人 would 继续 the 特攻 on Menkent in the near future.

Background

Not long after a successful 防御, Menkent finds itself 遭受攻击 by another wave of 机器人. Another 行星 in the same 分区, Vernen Wells, is also 遭受攻击 at this time. The 主要命令: Halt the 机器人 Advance is 已公布 on the 12th of April.

策略 and Importance

Menkent continued to stay an 重要 行星 in holding back the 机器人 advance, however, the 绝地潜兵 sent the majority of their forces to Vernen Wells initially. On the 12th of April, a new 重要指令 was issued to 建立 the Menkent Line. Menkent & Lesath had to be liberated and completely defended for 超级地球 武装力量 to 安装 rudimentary 轨道武器 defenses to deter the 机器人 advance into 超级地球 领土.

Battles

Since the majority of the 绝地潜兵 were deployed in Vernen Wells, the 剩余 forces left on Menkent focused on 撤离 and the crippling of 机器人 forces 标题 to Vernen Wells. At around 午夜, the initial 防御 of Menkent was lost as the majority of the 行星 belonged to the 机器人. As Vernen Wells saw a successful 防御, 舰船模块 were expanded due to the 增加 of 威胁 level, and a new 重要指令: Halt the 机器人 Advance was issued. This revitalized efforts in liberating Menkent and a surge of 增援 to 建立 the Menkent Line arrived to reclaim the 行星. In the early hours of the 14th of April, Menkent was fully liberated and the creation of Menkent Line was in progress. This would later be disrupted by another 机器人 特攻 on the same day.

Background

After a successful 解放 of Menkent, the majority of 绝地潜兵 quickly moved in to take Lesath in the Lacaille 分区 according to the 主要命令: Halt the 机器人 Advance. While the Menkent Line was being started on Menkent, another wave of 机器人 arrived on the 行星 and began their 特攻 on the same day a few hours after it was liberated.

策略 & Importance

Menkent was pivotal in the success of the 当前 重要指令 at the time. The 绝地潜兵 needed to not only liberate Menkent and Lesath to succeed, they needed to also hold the planets until the end of the 操作's 持续时间 to give 超级地球 武装力量 enough time to 建立 an 有效 轨道武器 防御. If 超级地球 did not 建立 a 防御型 formation here, it would be even more 困难 to mount an 有效 进攻型 later.

Battles

Since the majority of the 绝地潜兵 were focused on their efforts to liberate Lesath at the time, a 更小 but capable contingency stood by and defended Menkent. Their goal was to ensure that the whole 行星 was not taken over by the 机器人. The battle was fierce, but the moment that Lesath was liberated, Menkent started to 接收 增援 - almost doubling the number of forces defending the 行星. In just a few hours, Menkent succeeded in its 防御 against the 机器人 and the enemy was forced to 撤退 away from the newly formed Menkent Line on the 15th of April.

Fourth Battle of Menkent

Fourth Battle of Menkent
阵营 机器人

End Date April 23rd

操作
Group
Casualties
重要指令 防御 on Two Fronts
Summary Menkent fails its 防御.


Background

Amidst the fall of Lesath, a large 营 of 机器人 set their sights directly towards Vernen Wells which was acting as a forward base supporting Lesath and Menkent. At this time, Menkent was the only 行星 left with rudimentary 轨道武器 defenses which discouraged the enemy from directly engaging a battle on the 行星 itself. Additionally on the 21st of April, the 摧毁 of long-远程 通讯 arrays on Vernen Wells caused 解放 % to not 显示 correctly for the 绝地潜兵. After the failed 防御 of Vernen Wells, Menkent saw battle on the 22nd of April as it was surrounded by 敌对 planets from 多个 sides.

策略 & Importance

Although the Menkent Line was an 有效 way of staving off the 机器人's advance, the fall of Lesath punched a hole through the 防御. The enemy focused on Vernen Wells itself - exploiting this 优势. Menkent's 轨道武器 defenses was able to discourage 敌人 from laying siege to Menkent itself for a few days. That gave 绝地潜兵 time to focus on the 防御 of Vernen Wells which was held as high priority at the time. Although many 绝地潜兵 rallied to the 防御 of Vernen Wells, its initial 防御 eventually fell, and the 机器人 were able to gain a sizable foothold on the 行星. Menkent now facing 入侵, once again held the role to 继续 to give time for the 绝地潜兵 to liberate Vernen Wells, as with its 轨道武器 defenses, it would've been 困难 for the 机器人 to 征服 the 行星 easily.

战斗

During the Fourth Battle of Menkent, many 绝地潜兵 arrived to 增援 the 行星. Unfortunately, as the 主要命令: 防御 on Two Fronts stated, planets threatened by the 终结族 and 机器人 were threatened across the galaxy and the 绝地潜兵 were 散射 too thin. Even though the 防御 of Menkent by the 绝地潜兵 was 英勇, the presence of the newly 质量-produced 移动工厂 combined with the 机器人 sending 攻击 from 多个 sides was simply too overwhelming. Eventually, the 防御 fell on the 23rd of April as the 机器人 gained a large amount of ground on the 行星.

Fifth Battle of Menkent

Fifth Battle of Menkent
阵营 机器人

End Date May 21st
行星 Menkent
操作 操作 Legitimate Undertaking
Group
Casualties
重要指令 Phase I: 公民 Rescue ; Decommission 机器人 for Mines ; Hold Planets for SEAF conscription ; Kill and Dismantle Freedom's 敌人
Summary Menkent eventually fell into 机器人 控制.


Background

Not long after losing a large amount of ground on Menkent, the 机器人 tightened their grip on the 行星. The 解放 % continued to 掉落 to dangerously low levels. By now, the 轨道武器 防御 system installed on the 行星 否 longer functions. Vernen Wells is finally liberated once more near the end of the day on the 24th of April, lightening some of the 压力 on Menkent. Meanwhile, the 主要命令: 防御 on Two Fronts is successful on the 早晨 of the 25th of April as Ustotu saw 解放 from the 机器人 - further allowing 绝地潜兵 to 增援 a position on Menkent.

On the 26th of April, the 主要命令: 操作 Legitimate Undertaking, Phase I: 公民 Rescue was 已公布, making Menkent a point of interest for 绝地潜兵 wanting to liberate Choohe for 抢先体验 to the MD-17 反坦克地雷 and saving the scientists responsible for its creation.

After the conclusion of 操作 Legitimate Undertaking, Phase I: 公民 Rescue an emergency 广播 stating the failure of the 终结族控制系统 and the creation of Meridia into a Supercolony was 已公布. 绝地潜兵 focused efforts in stabilizing the situation until the 10th of May, when a new 重要指令: Decommission 机器人 for Mines was 已公布.

After that 重要指令 was concluded, Menkent continued to see 冲突 as the 机器人 kept most of the 行星 under their 控制. The 重要指令 of the creation of SEAF training facilities was 已公布 on the 16th of May. On the early hours on the 21st of May, Vernen Wells loses its 防御 against the 机器人 during the 重要指令: Kill and Dismantle Freedom's 敌人 which has cut off Menkent's Supply Lines.

策略 & Importance

After initially losing the 防御, the 绝地潜兵 put the 解放 of Menkent on low priority as the 重要指令 of the time needed 绝地潜兵 to focus on the 防御 of planets rather than 解放. Estanu, Mort, Varylia 5，以及 Ustotu were given higher priority as they needed to have successful defenses to accomplish the 重要指令. The moment Vernen Wells was liberated, it became favorable for 绝地潜兵 to start liberating Menkent.

Presumably, the new 重要指令 highlighted the fact that Menkent had possible Supply Lines to Choohe - a 行星 which evidently housed the scientists responsible for creating the 反坦克地雷. Despite this, only a 小型 number of 绝地潜兵 in comparison reinforced Menkent and opted for the RL-77 空爆火箭弹发射器 - by deploying into Lesath instead. Menkent continued to be largely unimportant and low priority for the rest of that particular 重要指令.

When the 重要指令 to Deactivate the 终结族控制系统 was 已公布, Menkent's 解放 was put into low priority and slowly lost ground as 绝地潜兵 scrambled to help contain this new 终结族 situation. Even after the completion of the 重要指令, Menkent continued to be put at low priority as another 重要指令 pertaining to the 终结族 was 已公布 shortly afterwards concerning the Supercolony's 能力 to create spores and 散射 outbreaks in other planets.

Around the 10th of May, the 重要指令: Decommission 机器人 for Mines was 已公布 and Menkent was finally given a slightly higher priority once Vernen Wells was liberated.

However, this was short lived. The new 重要指令 to hold Aesir Pass and Vernon Wells to help create SEAF training facilities drew many 绝地潜兵 away from Menkent, lowering its priority. This downward trend of 解放 continued unto the 下一个 重要指令: Kill and Dismantle Freedom's 敌人. Vernen Wells lost its 防御 on the 21st of May - cutting off all available Supply Lines to the 行星.

Battles

During the 23rd to the 24th of April, Menkent slowly lost ground. It gained a 小型 number of 绝地潜兵 增援 when Vernen Wells was liberated, following an even bigger spike in 增援 once the 防御 on Two Fronts 重要指令 was completed around the 25th of April. There was a trend of 正面 解放 up until the 公告 of the 下一个 重要指令.

When the 重要指令: 操作 Legitimate Undertaking was 已公布, many of the 绝地潜兵 chose to deploy to Lesath then continued onwards to Penta for access to the 空爆火箭弹发射器 战略配备. This action resulted in only a minimal number of 绝地潜兵 assigned to Menkent's 解放. Chort Bay was also chosen by many 绝地潜兵 instead of Menkent for 解放 to access Choohe at the time for its favorable conditions in comparison to Menkent. Unfortunately, this decision caused a failure in the 重要指令 and 研究 on the 反坦克地雷 was halted on the 29th of April. By the end of this 重要指令, Menkent saw slow but steady progress in its overall 解放.

However, the rise of a potentially 致命 situation in the form of the failure of the 终结族控制系统 forced many 绝地潜兵 away from Menkent on the 30th of April. The slow progress that was made to liberate Menkent was slowly becoming undone by the 机器人 during this time. By the 1st of May, 超级地球 was only able to hold about 30% of the 行星's 解放 in combination with the meager number of 绝地潜兵 present on the 行星. Menkent continued to lose 解放 % all the way into the end of the 重要指令 and the following 重要指令: Contain Supercolony Outbreaks which continued focus away from the 行星's 防御.

At this point, 解放 was at an all-time low on Menkent, with 超级地球 only controlling less than 1% of the 行星's 领土 during the 4th of May to the 9th of May. The 绝地潜兵 that remained on Menkent continued to gain 经验值 on the fiery 行星 while clinging to the 小型 stretches of liberated land until the day more 增援 would arrive. Eventually, on the 10th of May, a spike of 绝地潜兵 增援 would arrive after the successful 防御 of Vernen Wells. The new 重要指令: Decommission 机器人 for Mines called for the 摧毁 of 2 billion 机器人. This allowed some 绝地潜兵 to choose 解放 efforts on Menkent and on the 14th of May, Menkent found itself on a gradual gain of 解放 - currently having 大约 14% by the time of the end of the 重要指令.

Unfortunately, with the rise of a New 重要指令: Hold Planets for SEAF conscription, on the 16th of May, Menkent once again saw a decline of 绝地潜兵 activity as it became more 重要 to 保护 Vernen Wells and Aesir Pass. This 已降低 the 解放 well below 1%. Despite successful defenses of Vernen Wells during the last 重要指令, it still loses its 防御 on the 21st of May during the new 重要指令. This crippled any 剩余 解放 efforts happening on Menkent even further. Menkent eventually finds itself unavailable for 解放 as the failed 防御 of Vernon Wells stops all 绝地潜兵 access to the 行星. The long and gruesome fifth battle of Menkent was lost on the early hours of the 21st of May.

策略 & Importance

Menkent is a 行星 in the Hydra 分区 that connects to 几个 planets in the Lacaille 分区. It is currently not the 目标 of any 重要指令 so the priority to liberate the 行星 is low.

Battles

绝地潜兵 made 联络 with the enemy the moment Vernen Wells was liberated. At that time, around 2,000 绝地潜兵 attempted to 维护 a slow and steady 解放速率 despite the majority of the 绝地潜兵 focusing on Marfark. The battle is ongoing.
