the Ground Stash is a tiny 建筑, which can be found during missions. It is noticeable from a 距离 due to a 轻型 shining across it. The 轻型 comes either from above or inside the stash.

次要特殊地点

The Ground Stash may be found as a Minor Place of Interest. It may have a 背景 Interactable called "Hastily Written Note" depending on the variation.
