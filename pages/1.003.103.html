概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 战斗 For 超级地球 commemorative 披肩!
    • Please note: Capes 5月 take 几个 hours to 完成 deployment to 全部 绝地潜兵.
  • 平衡 adjustments to 尖啸虫 Mega Nests and the Leviathan
  • 修复 galore! Including a 修复 for the 崩溃 in the 武器 customisation screen

Balancing[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已增加 the 生命值 of Mega 尖啸虫 nests. This 变化 was made to make the 目标 更多 挑战 and provide 更多 varied solutions to destroying them.
  • Leviathans now have a shorter sight 射程 and will mark their 目标 with 轻型 a 少数 秒 before they 火焰. This 变化 gives 玩家 a fair 警告 before being fired upon.

修复[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

崩溃 修复, Hangs and 柔软-locks[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已修复 崩溃 that occurred when changing between menus in 武器 customisation too quickly.
  • 已修复 崩溃 that would occur for 玩家 fighting bots with a poor 连接.
  • 已修复 a freeze when generating bug 城市 missions.
  • 已修复 崩溃 that could occur when 击倒中 光能者 Overships.

武器 and 战略配备[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已修复 武器 with selectable 弹药 (无后坐力炮, 机炮, etc) having their icons overwritten by the Warrant’s “Guided/Unguided” 模式.
  • P-92 Warrant
    • will 否 longer lock 一些 targets when in unguided 模式.
    • 已修复 an 问题 where 锁定 would be 困难 in 一些 environments.
    • 已修复 一些 Z-fighting with lights on the 武器.
    • Corrected animations for the 手枪's sight, it should now fold away nicely when in unguided 模式.
  • AX/LAS-5 “守卫 Dog” Rover: will 否 longer 回报 to backpack to “换弹.”
  • AR-32 Pacifier: 已修复 spent casing not ejecting from the ejection 港口.
  • 已添加 Videos for the K-9 守卫 Dog and Gl-52 De-Escalator.
  • One 是 旗帜: 已修复 an 问题 where planting the 旗帜 would 原因 目标 items to clip through the 地面.

敌人[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已修复 一些 issues where 敌人 could clip into certain 地形 elements.
    • Note: We aren’t done resolving 全部 of these issues and 继续 to 调查. Please keep the reports coming!

任务[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已修复 an 问题 where 成群 missions would spawn at Difficulties 更低 than 5, which would 打破 一些 functionality.
  • 已修复 an 问题 where certain bug missions would not spawn during operations.

Miscellaneous 修复[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 已解决 问题 with 载具 skins not having a scroll 酒吧 and 已增加 全射 数字 of skins that can be displayed.
  • 所有 cities will now 显示 in the 城市 availability UI.
  • 已修复 a bug that would 显示 0 玩家人数 on 一些 cities.
  • 已添加 更多 logging to 追踪 desyncs and 崩溃.
  • 已修复 Borderline 正义 armours to be 更多 准确 to their 属性, e.g. slight 改进 to draw 速度, etc.
