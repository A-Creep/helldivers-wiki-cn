击沉统御舰
“

The Overship is the 指挥-and-控制 nexus of 光能者 forces in this 分区. Its oppressive presence must be destroyed.

A 行星 防御 Cannon in this area can penetrate its 护盾. Activate the Cannon and use it to 击落 the Overship.

— 任务 简报

击沉统御舰 is a 任务 which tasks 绝地潜兵 with retrieving various items in order to operate and 火焰 an 行星 防御 Cannon capable of eliminating the 光能者统御舰.

目标步骤

取回访问代码

“

Access codes for the 行星 防御 Cannon are held at this 位置.

— 目标 概述

  • Find the 困难 disk containing the Access Codes.
    • 绝地潜兵 must go to the Priority Emergency Bunkers then figure out which door is the 正确 one by opening them and then retrieving Access Codes from the shelves inside. It是杰出 by a gray-ish 矩形 外观 with a purple circle, identical to the 外观 of Access Codes in 发射洲际弹道导弹 missions. It emits a constant sound of static and morse code, getting louder the closer the 绝地潜兵 is to the Disk containing the Codes.
  • The Disk containing the Access Codes

击沉统御舰

“

Activate the 行星 Defence Cannon and use it to 击落 the 光能者统御舰.

— 目标 概述

  • 启动终端
    • 绝地潜兵必须与终端互动以激活它。
  • 输入访问代码：（访问代码）
    • 绝地潜兵必须（如果尚未）与终端互动，然后使用方向键将5位数字容器翻转到目标步骤中显示的正确数字。
  • 手动解除发射井掩体锁
    • 绝地潜兵必须下到舱门处，并逐一与两个锁交互，手动释放它们。
  • 使用终端，打开掩体舱门
    • 绝地潜兵必须（如果尚未）与终端互动，然后使用向上的方向命令终端打开舱门。
  • 使用终端抬升行星防御加农炮
    • 使用方向键输入来启动升降台，随后需要 60 秒，行星防御加农炮才会完全升入开火位置。
  • 瞄准行星防御加农炮
    • 绝地潜兵必须使用加农炮内部的俯仰和水平控制器来瞄准。
      • 将加农炮对准离它最近的通气口，可一发击落超舰。
  • 使用加农炮，击沉统御舰
    • 绝地潜兵最终必须与终端交互，然后按向上方向键触发 5 秒倒计时，直至其开火。
      • 如果没有绝地潜兵瞄准，加农炮会径直向前射击，很可能打中建筑物。

战术信息

  • When the 光能者统御舰 is shot, it will retaliate by summoning Warp Ships on the 绝地潜兵. It currently has 否 other 效果 on ground assets.
  • Aiming the barrel of the 行星 防御 Cannon at the closest Port hole of the Overship will 1-shot the 目标 once the 护盾 have been disabled, requiring 2 shots. This drastically 降低 the time needed to 摧毁 it.
  • The Overship's Shield can regenerate if 绝地潜兵 take too long firing the 行星 防御 Cannon in-between shots. Additional shells may need to be fired in order to deactivate the shield again.
    • 尽管用行星防御加农炮射击护盾是最直接的方式，但护盾也可以被常规武器伤害。
    • 护盾仍完好时，如果20秒内未受到伤害，它会以每秒500点生命值的速度开始恢复。
    • 护盾被击破后，如果届时统御舰尚未被摧毁，护盾会在60秒后重新出现并开始恢复。

琐事

  • The 超级地球 行星 防御 Cannon is the first manually operated large weapon in the game. Unlike other SE Defenses, which are remotely operated, the SE 行星 防御 Cannon requires manual aiming and firing.
  • Unlike other 目标, the steps on 终端 for the SE 行星 防御 Cannon can be repeated 多个 times before being locked out. This is due to the 主要目标 being tied to the 摧毁 of the Overship, which needs 多个 shots to be destroyed.
  • After the Overship has been destroyed, the SE 行星 防御 Cannon can still be fired. This can be used to 摧毁 other large 敌人 such as a nearby Leviathan.
  • The SE 行星 防御 Cannon itself seems to be based off the 40.6 cm SK C/34 naval/coastal 防御 gun, or the “Adolfkanone”.
  • Although the 任务 was initially exclusively available on 光能者 city missions, they became available outside of cities in 特殊 "Quell Exostorm" operations and later in 特殊 "调查 Void 行星" operations within the The Void.
