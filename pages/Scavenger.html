食腐虫
“

The brainless 食腐虫 is the lowliest of 终结族. Upon encountering an agent of Liberty, it emits a 穿刺 scream that attracts nearby bugs in a reflexive attempt to 摧毁 Freedom.

— 绝地潜兵 2 PlayStation Store Page

The 食腐虫 is the weakest and most common of the 终结族, using its claws to 攻击 绝地潜兵 at 近距离.

生成

Scavengers can be found on all difficulties, 生成 from Bug 突破, within patrols, guarding Bug Nests and 目标, or from 虫穴.

Starting on 挑战, they can be found guarding Minor Places of Interest.

行为

Scavengers will simply approach 绝地潜兵, climbing over 地形 and 障碍 in an attempt to 攻击 with their claws. After striking, they will 撤退 a short 距离 before approaching again.

少数情况下，当绝地潜兵超出其攻击范围时，它们会钻入地下，然后突然冒出攻击绝地潜兵。

Scavengers are able to release a cloud of 信息素 into the air that causes a 虫族入侵.

武器配置

Tearing Pincers

Living up to their name, the 食腐虫's pincers are capable of 压制中 and picking apart the 遗骸 of prey that had already been mauled by larger 终结族 with alarming 效率, easily stripping flesh from armour and bone alike.

战术信息

  • Due to their low 生命值, Scavengers can easily be dispatched by any weapon.
    • Individual Scavengers can be safely killed with a single 近战攻击 to the head, which conserves ammunition.
  • When encountering a patrol or group of 终结族, it is recommended to kill Scavengers quickly before they can cause a 虫族入侵.
    • The AX/LAS-5 "Guard Dog" Rover and 爆炸 武器库 can be 克制 swarms of Scavengers or other 小型 终结族.
