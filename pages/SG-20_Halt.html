SG-20 止息
“

A humane compliance weapon with two 分离 magazines: one for 护甲-penetrating flechette 发 and one for stun 发.

The SG-20 Halt is a dual-tube, 泵动式 Primary 霰弹枪. It can switch between  中型-护甲 penetrating flechettes and  轻型-护甲 penetrating stun pellets.

采购

The SG-20 Halt is unlocked on the 1st page of the 真理执行者 尊享战争债券 for 20 奖章.

战术信息

  • The SG-20 Halt's 弹匣 tubes are independent from each other. Swapping the tubes by using the 武器轮盘 can help avoid a 换弹 in dire situations.
    • 因此，建议绝地潜兵在接战前确保两根弹管都已装填。
    • Shells share the same 储备 弹药 pool. 绝地潜兵 should prioritize what shells they immediately need if they're low on 弹药.
  • 在需要装填镖弹弹管时，眩晕弹可以有效拖住敌人。
    • Using stun 发 on decapitated charging Brood/Alpha 指挥官 is a good tactic to completely halt their 狂暴 charge, providing time to switch to a 支援武器 to finish them off or to 撤退.
    • Using stun 发 on any form of 监视者 will give you plenty of time to switch to the P-4 参议员, APW-1 反器材步枪, or even RS-422 磁轨炮 and line up a shot at their head, killing them instantly. At 近距离, the Halt's own flechette shells can 达成 the same 效果 if enough flechettes hit the head directly.
  • The Halt has significant 散射 which is slightly better than the SG-225IE Breaker 燃烧弹. Therefore, it is ill-advised to engage 敌人 at 中距离 as most of your pellets will not hit your 目标.
  • The Halt benefits greatly from being able to swiftly switch between its flechette and stun 射击模式. For PC 玩家, it is advisable to set an accessible keybind to "Quick 武器轮盘: Left" to easily swap between the weapon's two modes without needing to engage the 武器轮盘 every time a different mode is needed.

琐事

  • Both the SG-8S Slugger and SG-20 Halt are the only shotguns to 特性 rifled barrels for better 投射物 速率 and 精度. However, the Halt does not 火焰 any slug 发, making its rifling redundant.
    • In real life, rifling is 实际上 detrimental to shotguns that are not firing slugs - as rifling is known to cause a 涂装 akin to a donut. While the in-game 霰弹枪 does not have such a 涂装, its lower overall 精度 could be due to the fact it has a rifled barrel.
  • The 外观 of the SG-20, like all Punisher 变体, is loosely based on the UTS-15.
