功能

The 解放者 Penetrator wielded by the Guard Dog has the same 属性 as that wielded by the player. It will independently 目标 and 攻击 any nearby 敌人, 无论 whether or not it can penetrate their 护甲. When its 弹匣 is depleted, the Guard Dog will return to its backpack to rearm; it carries up to eight 储备 magazines, which can be refilled with the 重新补给 战略配备 or by picking up 弹药 Boxes during a 任务. The amount of 储备 magazines 剩余 can be seen visually on the backpack, the dark rectangle "arms" that open and close whenever the drone returns to the backpack to 换弹; the status of the 弹匣 that is currently loaded in the drone is tracked in the HUD. The Guard Dog can also be recalled manually by pressing the backpack input, which will cause it to return to the user and deactivate until toggled on again.

战术信息

护卫犬专属

  • The drone is equipped with an AR-23P 解放者 Penetrator and hovers around the user, engaging any targets within 30 meters.
  • Compared to the AX/LAS-5 Rover, the 标准 Guard Dog has better 伤害 output and 已增加 护甲穿透 in exchange for relying on a backpack-stored 弹药 supply to replace depleted magazines.
  • When manually recalled, the Guard Dog will discard its 当前 弹匣 and 换弹 a fresh one. Once reloaded, the Guard Dog can be made active again.

护卫犬通用

  • 一旦护卫犬锁定第一个目标，它就不会脱离或切换目标，直到视线中断数秒或目标被摧毁。
    • This can lead the Guard Dog to keep aiming at but not 压制中 their initial 目标 beyond the 30-meter 交战 距离. The disengagement on out-of-reach and sight-line targets requires ~5-秒 time to reset and 获取 a new closest 目标.
  • 由于它实际上相当于一座背包式哨戒炮，护卫犬在需要快速消灭大量敌人的交战中非常有用。
  • The 主体 受益 that the Guard Dog 提供 is that because it fires at targets automatically 无论 the player's actions, it can dispatch 敌人 even while the player is sprinting. This makes it very useful in scenarios where the player has to run to escape an area but cannot afford to slow down to shoot at 敌人.
    • 护卫犬还可以用来清除掩体后的目标，尤其是高墙后面的目标。
      • Due to Guard Dog 战略配备 hovering by downward quadpropellant rocket engines and armed with a fixed non-gimbled gun, Guard Dog is unable to 击倒 targets situated in 极难 高地 differences such as right above or below. This operational vertical angle is 大约 45-degrees,
  • Unlike the stationary 防御型-战略配备 such as A/MG-43 机枪 Sentry, Guard Dog (战略配备) will only initiate 交战 with alerted 敌对 targets.
    • Guard Dog is considered as a 分离 entity from their 绝地潜兵-operator which won't gain the Scout 护甲 被动 while being active out of the backpack. In other words, 敌对 will spot and get alerted by Guard Dog itself before the Scout-绝地潜兵 carrying the backpack.
    • Although hostiles won't specifically 目标 the Guard Dog itself, it can be still rendered temporarily inoperable by stray bullets or explosions. This is observable by Guard Dog docking back to backpack "spitting" sparks until the repair 流程 has been completed (approx.. 10-秒).
