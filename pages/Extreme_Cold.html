效果[edit | edit 来源]

极寒 is applied depending on the time of day and 天气 conditions. On 特定星球, it's always active.

  • 射速 is 已降低 by about 10%.
  • Heat-based 武器 lose heat faster, contrary to the in-game 描述 stating that it delays heat buildup. This means that once overheated, it takes a shorter amount of time for the weapon to cool down, 通常 by a factor of 33% 亲戚 to its normal 冷却 持续时间.[1]

Affected Biomes[edit | edit 来源]

  • Boneyard
  • Icy Glaciers
  • Moon
  • Desert Dunes
