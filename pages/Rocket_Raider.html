生成

Rocket Raiders can be first found on 容易。它们可以从……生成 机器人空投，出现在巡逻队中，守卫 机器人 哨站 and 目标，或从 机器人 Fabricators.

Starting on 挑战, they can be found guarding Minor Places of Interest.

Rocket Raiders spawn more 频繁 on higher difficulties, mostly replacing Troopers.

行为

Rocket Raiders will raise and point their Portable 发射器 at 绝地潜兵 and 火焰 one rocket before reloading for about three 秒. They will often 火焰 from 中距离, and can 火焰 from afar if they have a 绝地潜兵 in their line of sight, and have been alerted to their presence. Rocket Raiders will slowly 距离 themselves from 绝地潜兵 if they approach, and will punch them if they get too close. Like all 装甲兵 变体, the Rocket Raider will attempt to toss a 手榴弹 if a 绝地潜兵 is hidden within cover.

When alerted to a 绝地潜兵's presence, Rocket Raiders can 火焰 a flare to 呼叫 a 机器人空投.

战术信息

火箭奇袭者

  • Like other 轻型 机器人, 压制中 the Rocket Raider will reduce its 精度. Due to its slow 换弹 and firing, this can significantly reduce their lethality in group firefights.
  • Due to their 小型 size and tendency to 火焰 from 射程, Rocket Raiders can be an annoyance. Their rockets can launch and ragdoll 绝地潜兵, which can leave them 易受 other 机器人.

轻型机器人

  • 全身轻型 机器人 左臂中隐藏着信号弹发射器，可以用来呼叫 机器人空投.
    • Destroying them before it can finish launching the flare will prevent a 机器人空投 from being called in.
  • 手榴弹和爆头可以稳定一击秒杀所有轻型机器人。

琐事

  • Rocket Raiders were particularly deadly before the 1.000.200 补丁, as 绝地潜兵 would take 爆炸伤害 多个 times.
