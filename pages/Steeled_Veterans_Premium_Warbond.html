概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

玩家 can use 奖牌 to 声称 various items, including a 强化资源, 5 武器, 3 armors, 7 cosmetics, and 300 超级货币. These 奖励 are spread across three pages, 每个 requiring increasingly 更多 奖牌 (ranging from 2 to 80). 玩家 开始 on 页面 one, unlocking subsequent pages as they 花费 更多 奖牌, gradually expanding their customization options.

琐事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • the 铁血老兵 尊享战争债券 was previously included in the Super 公民 Edition DLC. This was later replaced with a 尊享战争债券 Token which entitles the 玩家 to 1 free 尊享战争债券 of their choice. 传奇 战争债券 are not eligible.
  • The 战争债券 名称 is likely a pun based on a scene from the original 星河战队 电影. In the recruitment scene, Rico is congratulated on his placement in the Mobile Infantry by a 三重-amputee 军官 played by Robert David Hall (a 双重-amputee in 真实 life). He says "好 for you. Mobile Infantry made me the man I am today." as the 摄像头 conspicuously pans down past his prosthetic arm to his missing 腿部.
    • The 战争债券 includes the first instances of 护甲 which gives the 玩家 character prosthetic limbs.
