概述[edit | edit 来源]

玩家 can use Medals to claim various items, including a 强化资源, 5 武器, 3 armors, 7 cosmetics, and 300 超级货币. These 奖励 are spread across three pages, each requiring increasingly more Medals (ranging from 2 to 80). 玩家 start on page one, unlocking subsequent pages as they spend more Medals, gradually expanding their customization options.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • the 铁血老兵 尊享战争债券 was previously included in the Super 公民 Edition DLC. This was later replaced with a 尊享战争债券 Token which entitles the player to 1 free 尊享战争债券 of their choice. 传奇 战争债券 are not eligible.
  • The 战争债券 name is likely a pun based on a scene from the original 星河战队 film. In the recruitment scene, Rico is congratulated on his placement in the Mobile Infantry by a triple-amputee 军官 played by Robert David Hall (a double-amputee in real life). He says "Good for you. Mobile Infantry made me the man I am today." as the camera conspicuously pans down past his prosthetic arm to his missing legs.
    • The 战争债券 includes the first instances of 护甲 which gives the player character prosthetic limbs.
