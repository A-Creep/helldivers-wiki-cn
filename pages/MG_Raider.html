生成[edit | edit 来源]

MG Raiders can be first found on 容易。它们可以从……生成 机器人空投，出现在巡逻队中，守卫 机器人 哨站 and 目标，或从 机器人 Fabricators.

Starting on 挑战, they can be found guarding Minor Places of Interest.

MG Raiders spawn more 频繁 on higher difficulties, mostly replacing Troopers.

如果星球被……入侵  Jet Brigade, MG Raiders will begin being replaced by their Jet Brigade 变体 on 容易.

行为[edit | edit 来源]

MG Raiders will 火焰 12 shots from their 轻型 Fusion Repeater if a 绝地潜兵 is in its line of sight before quickly reloading. MG Raiders will keep some 距离 from 绝地潜兵, backing up if they approach, and punching them if they are too close. MG Raiders will toss a 手榴弹 if 绝地潜兵 are 掩体后. Their powerpack can unintentionally 爆炸 if damaged, instantly killing the MG Raider but significantly damaging and 燃烧 nearby 机器人 or 绝地潜兵. If it is 被压制 by gunfire, it will erratically move its 步枪, significantly reducing its 精度.

When alerted to a 绝地潜兵's presence, MG Raiders can 火焰 a flare to 呼叫 a 机器人空投.

它的  Jet Brigade 变种可以使用喷气背包，从而不断缩短与绝地潜兵之间的距离。

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

机枪奇袭者[edit | edit 来源]

  • The 轻型 Fusion Repeater used by MG Raiders can 火焰 almost continuously, pausing only briefly after 12 shots, making them very 危险 at 中型-近距离.
    • MG Raiders can be potentially 准确 enough to severely 伤害 a 绝地潜兵 in one burst. 绝地潜兵 should consider firing, even inaccurately, near MG Raiders to 压制 and lower their burst 精度.
  • MG Raiders can be immediately recognized by the two heat sink fins rising from their back-mounted powerpack.
    • The powerpack can still potentially 爆炸 on their 遗骸, but it can still be shot to blow it up prematurely.

轻型机器人[edit | edit 来源]

  • 全身轻型 机器人 左臂中隐藏着信号弹发射器，可以用来呼叫 机器人空投.
    • Destroying them before it can finish launching the flare will prevent a 机器人空投 from being called in.
  • 手榴弹和爆头可以稳定一击秒杀所有轻型机器人。
