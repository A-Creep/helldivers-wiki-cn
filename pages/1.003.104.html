敌人[edit | edit 来源]

利维坦
We have heard your feedback and are attempting to 平衡 Leviathans to more manageable levels.

  • The Leviathans no longer cause ragdolling and deal less impulse 伤害 to 载具
  • Adjusted 生成速率, so you encounter less 每次任务
  • Added an additional 1 second delay between shots for 目标 locking
  • Leviathan cannons can now be destroyed

Miscellaneous Fixes[edit | edit 来源]

  • Fixed a bug that caused 任务 items to disappear when entering 载具
  • Fix for chat interactions causing weapon switches while using a mounted weapon, emotes and victory poses
  • Fixed weapon grip issues and 近战攻击 issues after using stims or emotes
