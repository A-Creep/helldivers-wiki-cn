Earn Your 绝地潜兵 Cape is a 任务 in 绝地潜兵 2. It is the tutorial of the game and new 绝地潜兵 will be automatically launched into this 任务. To 完成 this 任务, 绝地潜兵 must learn the basics of the game. It takes place on Zegema Paradise.

目标 Steps[edit | edit 来源]

努力赢得绝地潜兵披肩[edit | edit 来源]

  • 使用地图前往绝地潜兵训练基地
  • 前往基地大门
  • 完成绝地潜兵障碍训练
    • The 绝地潜兵 has to crouch under the barbed wire fences, then climb some big steps, and then finally jump over some 混凝土 barricades.
  • 通过高度逼真的模拟战场
    • The 绝地潜兵 has to approach the Highly 写实 Battlefield Simulator, then dive into it, and stay prone until they have moved past the two A/G-16 加特林 哨戒炮.
  • 启动战场损伤模拟器
    • The 绝地潜兵 must activate the Battlefield Injury Simulator by approaching and interacting with it.
  • 从损伤模拟器中拾取治疗剂
    • The 绝地潜兵 must pick up a stim by using the interact button.
  • 使用治疗剂疗愈伤口
    • The 绝地潜兵 must use the Stim button to heal themselves
  • 前往实弹靶场
    • The 绝地潜兵 must walk to the 下一个 area.
  • 拾取次要武器
    • The 绝地潜兵 must walk to and interact with the weapon
  • 拾取弹药
    • Staying at the same 位置, the 绝地潜兵 must now use the interact button again to pick up the 弹药
  • 给武器装弹
    • The 绝地潜兵 must now load the weapon with the 弹药 by pressing the 换弹 button
  • 击杀终结族
    • The 绝地潜兵 must kill the 终结族
  • 拾取榴弹
    • The 绝地潜兵 must now go back to the 运送 spot and press the interact button on the 手榴弹 Case
  • 投掷 手榴弹 into 人造 虫穴
    • The 绝地潜兵 must 摧毁 the 虫穴 with a 手榴弹, using the quick 手榴弹 button. Holding the 手榴弹 in hand cooks it, so it's advised to not keep it in the hand.
  • 继续 to 战略配备 射程
  • 使用 轨道精准攻击 战略配备 to kill 敌人
  • 前往增援训练区
  • 使用增援战略配备复活阵亡的队友
  • Pick up the AR-23 解放者
  • 拾取补给，为“解放者”装弹
  • 投掷 手榴弹 into 人造 虫穴
  • 前往支援战略配备训练区
  • 使用机枪战略配备，呼叫支援武器
  • 使用重新补给战略配备，呼叫补给物资
  • 前往终极考验
  • 启动终端
  • 在终端上输入代码
    • The 绝地潜兵 has to use the 5 方向 code displayed on the 监控 and then wait the entire 5 秒 to 继续.
  • 取回超级地球旗帜
  • 在指定区域竖立旗帜
  • 向超级地球旗帜敬礼
  • 击杀所有终结族
  • 前往毕业礼堂，成为绝地潜兵
  • Reward: 500 申购点 Slips 和 100 经验值

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • There is a 特殊 voice line for failing to 完成 the turret 章节 5 consecutive times.
  • This 任务 has unique music that can't be heard anywhere else in the game.
  • If the 绝地潜兵 destroys the 绝地潜兵 Dummy before 基本信息 Brasch can finish his speech, he will immediately jump to the 下一个 line.
  • It is possible to shoot and 摧毁 the 哨戒炮 on top of the walls.
  • The sign right outside the training 设施 that reads "See you on the other side" has a 小型 copy of itself on the other side.
  • If the 绝地潜兵 continues past the end of the 任务, on the right, they can find a burnt human corpse with a usable FLAM-40 火焰喷射器 nearby.
  • Prior to the Heart of Democracy 更新, this 任务 would take place on Mars, rather than the 当前 行星 of Zegema Paradise.
