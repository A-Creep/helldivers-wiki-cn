功能

  • The backpack's 能量 恢复 rate is 6% 每秒, and each teleport consumes 33%, 含义 teleporting every 6 秒 is absolutely safe.
  • By holding the climb button, you will teleport 10 meters in the 方向 your 绝地潜兵 is moving in. 能量 is consumed after teleporting.
  • The Orb on the backpack indicates its 能量 level and warp safety, additionally it will 生产 a noticeable amount of steam out the bottom back end while in 冷却.
    • If it is purple (>85% 能量), warping is safe.
    • If it is orange (56% - 85% 能量), warping will cause 伤害.
      • The Warp Pack will choose which limb will take 伤害 in a random 涂装, choosing between arms, legs and head. It can also 伤害 the same limb on the 下一个 warp, even if it is already broken. If 无 of the limbs are chosen, the warp pack will ignite the user.
        • The Warp Pack will deal 35 伤害 if it hits either arm, 45 if it hits either leg, and 10 if it hits the head.
        • 伺服辅助 被动 or Vitality Enhancement 强化资源 allow a 绝地潜兵's limbs to survive at least one warp before it's broken; 强化肩章 被动 can also avoid limb breaking by warping; 护腿为上 被动 will prevent any leg injury.
    • If it is red (<56% 能量), warping is guaranteed death that 两者都不 民主护佑 or 肾上腺素除颤器 can save, and the warp pack will be destroyed as well.

战术信息

  • Warping can bypass any 地形, buildings, 敌人, and Bunker doors. Warping can also position you upwards or downwards a max of 5 meters.
    • Warping as you dive/prone will allow you to bypass certain areas that you normally cannot. For 示例: some bunkers are 困难 or 不可能 to warp into; this trick allows you to easily warp into those bunkers.
  • Warping can be used in mid-air to travel through a canyon from above.
  • Warping causes 爆炸伤害 to everything at the destination that can kill most 小型 敌人, other 绝地潜兵 and 炮台. It has sufficient 爆破力 to open Cargo Containers. It also triggers 火山 Plants.
  • Warping into most large map icon 敌人, excluding fleshmobs, will result in the player instantly dying. The warp pack will still deal its normal 伤害 if this occurs.
    • This 瞬间 death can be prevented by 民主护佑, and will also result in the player taking 否 伤害, making it a decent failsafe.
    • While risky, since the 范围效果 can reach farther than the player's hitbox, the Warp Pack can be an 有效 重型 伤害 dealer against large 敌人.
  • A warp pack can be used while prone or crouched, but will NOT work when swimming.
  • One of the 主体 utilities you can use is to quickly position yourself behind 敌人 to get a better position on enemy weak spots or to avoid 攻击. This can also help disorient 敌人 that may have been aiming at you.
  • Warping into anything 爆炸 like mines, bile bugs, or 爆炸 barrels can kill or 伤害 you.
  • If the RS-422 磁轨炮 is about to 爆炸 in Unsafe mode, activating the Warp Pack will 保护 from almost all 受到伤害.
  • Activating the Warp Pack while falling can reduce the 距离 of your fall, up to 5m, potentially negating any fall 伤害 and 硬直 that would have occurred.
  • Any 蓄力 weapon will keep its charge, even if the Warp Pack is actively being used. Although warping during a 换弹 will interrupt it.
  • When in either the passenger or gunner seat of an FRV or manning any 阵地, it is possible to use the warp pack as an alternative method of disembarking.
    • This cannot be done while driving the FRV or piloting an 外骨骼机甲.
    • Warping from a passenger seat can be a safer alternative to exiting a moving FRV.
      • Warping out of a Bastion tank's gunner seat while the 载具 is in motion will kill the user.
