Summary X-45 successfully defended. Most 绝地潜兵 went to Meissa, where they begun the Second Battle of Meissa.


Background

机器人 conquered the whole Ymir 分区, with Wezen (that was 遭受攻击 during the First Battle of X-45) and X-45 being the only planets that survived all invasions on the 分区.

策略 and importance

X-45 is a home to a lost Advanced 武器 Lab from the 第一次银河战争, buried underneath rubble and abandoned due to dangerously high 辐射 levels across the 行星. Holding this 行星, would allow 超级地球's scientists to work on a powerful 行星际 battle 车站

战斗

The Battle started on 3rd of June and ended on the same day few hours later. 绝地潜兵 quickly destroyed 机器人 fleet that attacked X-45. After the successful 防御, most 绝地潜兵 moved to Meissa.
