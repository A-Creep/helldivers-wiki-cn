描述

集成炸药
护甲 explodes 1.5秒 after the wearer dies.
增加 initial 库存 and holding 容量 of throwables by +2.

解释

1. the martyrdom style 爆炸 deals 2,000 伤害 with a 穿透 of and a 爆破力 of 30—potentially enough to finish off even some of the heaviest units and 摧毁 all spawn structures, except for 吐酸泰坦 holes.
