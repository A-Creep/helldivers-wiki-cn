武器 Customization, which allows 玩家 to 变化 武器 attachments, was introduced in 补丁 1.003.000. Syntax Error 经验值 is needed to unlock attachments, and Syntax Error 申购点 are needed to 购买 them.

Additionally, purely 装饰 武器 patterns are earned and purchased the same way as attachments.

武器 经验值[编辑 | 编辑 来源]
