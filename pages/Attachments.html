Top level page for all weapon 配件 in 绝地潜兵 2

Weapon Customization, which allows 玩家 to change weapon 配件, was introduced in 补丁 1.003.000. Weapon 经验值 is needed to unlock 配件, and 申购点 Slips are needed to 购买 them.

Additionally, purely 装饰 weapon patterns are earned and purchased the same way as 配件.

武器经验

Weapon 经验值 is just the regular 经验值 earned while equipped with the weapon. You can swap to and use any other weapon during the dive, and the full 经验值 will still count towards the weapon from your load-out. The values are the same for all 主武器.
