莱萨斯第一战役

First Battle of Lesath
阵营 机器人

End Date Apr. 10th
行星 Lesath
操作
Group The Reclamation
Casualties 4 million Hellldivers K.I.A.
重要指令 相关 / The Reclamation
Summary Lesath captured by 机器人


背景

After the annulation of the 机器人 menace in the Severin 分区 following 操作 Swift Disassembly, the 联邦 of 超级地球 believed they had concluded the war with the 机器人. Celebrations were held all across 联邦 space, while 绝地潜兵 across the stars cried out with rejoice over the defeat of their enemy. The 总统 of 超级地球 had made plans to provide an 官员 公告 to the end of the 冲突. However, the Ministry of Intelligence held a different story. They had received disturbing reports of cloaked 机器人 vessels in hiding at the furthest corners of known galactic space. An 调查 was conducted but ultimately the Ministry of Intelligence was too late. Only one day later, the 机器人 launched a full-scale 惊讶 入侵 on planets in the Valdis and Lacaille 星区. 超级地球 武装力量 were caught off guard and in mere hours 几个 planets swiftly fell in every 分区 to the cold 机械 hands of the 机器人.

这些受害者中有拉凯欧分区内的尾宿八。

战略与重要性

Originally 小型 colonial world, Lesath became of grave importance due to its proximity to Menkent and Vernen Wells, the latter of which, if captured, would cut off 增援 and 致命 物资 to the 防御 of Menkent.

因此，守住尾宿八成为优先事项，以确保佛农井不会遭到机器人部队攻击。

战斗

The first battle for Lesath came following initial SEAF High 指挥 orders to halt the 机器人 advance through the Lacaille 分区 and into the Hydra 分区. However, two of the four systems within the Lacaille 分区 had already fallen, with 增援 being directed to the 防御 of Menkent in the Hydra 分区. Thus the First Battle of Lesath ended with staggering losses. Those 绝地潜兵 that deployed to Lesath did their best to evacuate what essential personnel and civilians they could, but they were unable to 守住防线 against the 机器人 forces and were ordered to withdraw within the following day.

The First Battle of Lesath ended with over four million SEAF casualties. However, the 防御 of Lesath was enough to weigh down the 机器人 advance onto Vernen Wells, allowing for its successful 防御.

莱萨斯第二战役

Second Battle of Lesath
阵营 机器人
Start Date Apr. 10th
End Date Apr. 12th
行星 Lesath
操作
Group The Reclamation
Casualties 6.5 million 绝地潜兵 K.I.A.
重要指令 否
Summary 机器人's continued to hold Lesath


背景

Across the region, worlds were falling left and right. Lesath was among these casualties. SEAF and 绝地潜兵 forces attempted every 策略 to hold and reclaim worlds in a desperate attempt to buy time for High 指挥 to 生产 a 回应 plan.

战略与重要性

While already captured to 机器人, many 绝地潜兵 desperately attempted to break their 要塞 over the 行星 to prevent further 机器人 assaults towards Vernen Wells. However many considered Lesath a loss and refused to take the 行星 due to their presence being 必需 elsewhere.

战斗

The Second Battle of Lesath was considered by many to be a waste of valuable resources and assets. Despite this, many 绝地潜兵 took to the field in a desperate attempt to reclaim Lesath to prevent Vernen Wells continued 特攻. However, 机器人 forces had dug deep into the 行星 and established well defended garrisons too strong for such a 小型 force of 绝地潜兵 to take. The battle was thus considered a loss.

莱萨斯第三战役

Third Battle of Lesath
阵营 机器人
Start Date Apr. 12th
End Date Apr. 14th
行星 Lesath
操作
Group
Casualties 15 million 绝地潜兵 K.I.A.
重要指令 Halt the 机器人 Advance
Summary Lesath liberated, Menkent Line establishment.


背景

Following the staggering losses across the Valdis, Lacaille, Hydra and Andromeda 星区, SEAF High 指挥 issued a 重要指令 that would focus 绝地潜兵 efforts to shore up the defenses within the region and begin reestablishing dominance over nearby worlds. The plan was to 建立 the Menkent Line an effort to not only retake key worlds vital for future operations, but also prevent further 机器人 advance into core 联邦 worlds.

战略与重要性

尾宿八在此计划中至关重要，因为它与库楼三一起被选中进行收复，并加装多种轨道防御，以防止机器人进一步入侵。

战斗

The Third Battle of Lesath was an 进攻型 one. 绝地潜兵 were directed to 特攻 the 行星 and 歼灭 机器人 garrisons to make way for SEAF Engineers to develop the 轨道武器 defenses for the Menkent Line. The foggy, tundra world of Lesath once again became the battleground between 绝地潜兵 and 机器人, with the 绝地潜兵 particularly making use of the cold to cool their 能量武器 rapidly as they fought through the 机器人 infested landscapes.

Ultimately Lesath would be taken shortly after the initial seizure of Menkent. With 增援 pooling in from Menkent, Lesath was successfully seized and became key to the establishment of the Menkent Line. Those 绝地潜兵 that arrived after their deployment of Menkent found the conditions of Lesath much more preferable, though remarked they wished they brought their wooly capes after garrisoning the 行星.

莱萨斯第四战役

Fourth Battle of Lesath
阵营 机器人
Start Date Apr. 19th
End Date Apr. 20th
行星 Lesath
操作
Group The Reclamation
Casualties 3.5 million 绝地潜兵 K.I.A.
重要指令 防御 on Two Fronts
Summary Lesath contested to 机器人 特攻.


背景

在尾宿八成功解放和库楼三防线建立后，尾宿八受到超级地球武装部队轨道防御网络的保护，以帮助防止机器人进一步入侵。

战略与重要性

While initially held, Lesath would come 遭受攻击 a second time by a 机器人 strike force that managed to break through the Menkent Line with brute force. Lesath became their 目标 and would fall under 轨道武器 bombardment from the 机器人 invaders. 绝地潜兵 and SEAF forces worked tirelessly to defend Lesath, in order to prevent the 机器人 from gaining a foothold behind the Menkent Line.

战斗

Lesath came under swift 攻击 from the 机器人 breakthrough fleet. 轨道武器 defenses failed, allowing the 机器人 to land ground troops that began cutting through SEAF engineers and civilians alike. 绝地潜兵 were swiftly deployed but could do very little as the 机器人 forces were simply outnumbering them. 增援 were unable to reach them due to an even worse fate falling over the region as a whole, as shortly after Lesath was attacked, the Menkent Line fell, and all hell broke loose across the region. The Forth Battle of Lesath was ultimately a loss. Despite this, 绝地潜兵 continued fighting upon the world to evacuate as many as they could and hold what 领土 upon the 行星 they still had.

莱萨斯第五战役

Fifth Battle of Lesath
阵营 机器人
Start Date Apr. 26th
End Date Apr. 27th
行星 Lesath
操作 操作 Legitimate Undertaking
Group The Reclamation
Casualties
重要指令 是
Summary Lesath liberated from 机器人 forces.


背景

Shortly after Lesath's 入侵, both the 机器人 and 终结族 launched a colossal joint 攻击 in both respective regions of the known galaxy. 绝地潜兵 and SEAF forces were 散射 thin across both regions, leaving less-strategically-sound planets such as Lesath to the mercy of the 机器人. Shortly after Lesath's fall, others would soon follow, with Vernen Wells 暴露 to 攻击, Menkent suffering the same fate as the 轨道武器 defenses failed, and other nearby planets in nearby 星区 began falling as well.

战略与重要性

The importance of Lesath would become apparent during the Fifth Battle of Lesath, in which 操作 Legitimate Undertaking was enacted. SEAF High 指挥 discovered that weapon caches located on the worlds of Penta and Choohe in the Lacaille 分区. Lesath was once again selected to become a major staging ground for a 重要指令, as Lesath stood before the Penta System, which housed a 研究设施 that was constructing a new 支援武器 for 绝地潜兵 forces, known as the RL-77 空爆火箭弹发射器. In order to 获取 this weapon, Lesath had to be taken first.

战斗

The Fifth Battle of Lesath was one of the largest 解放 campaigns ever conducted. Hundreds of thousands of 绝地潜兵 stormed the 行星, swiftly cutting down the 机器人 who had once again garrisoned the world and liberated it from the cold robotic hands of the 机械 menace. As swiftly as Lesath fell, so too would it be reclaimed. The Fifth Battle of Lesath thus concluded in a 联邦 victory. From then on, Lesath became a staging ground for a number of 绝地潜兵 units, particularly those who'd participate in Legitimate Undertaking to secure their newest 支援 武器库.

莱萨斯第六战役

Sixth Battle of Lesath
阵营 机器人
Start Date May 10th
End Date May 12th
行星 Lesath
操作
Group The Reclamation
Casualties
重要指令 相关
Summary Lesath assaulted by 机器人 forces.


背景

Lesath was believed to be well defended now after so many incursions. However, with the 爆发 of the 终结族 Supercolony on Meridia, the majority of 绝地潜兵 forces were directed towards the 终结族 正面. This gave ample time for the 机器人's to regain their 力量 and 收集 their forces, which gave way to the Sixth Battle of Lesath.

战略与重要性

Having not been apart of a 重要指令 since the enactment of 操作 Legitimate Undertaking, Lesath became of less strategic value following the seizure of Penta. This was proven 正确 when 机器人 forces launched a strike against Lesath, once again attacking SEAF Engineers and other restoration forces deployed to the 行星. 绝地潜兵 were quick to 回应, but without 目标 目的, few came to the 防御 of Lesath. Lesath would fall to the 机器人's once more, however, the following day saw High 指挥 release a new 重要指令. 机器人 were to be scrapped and used as parts to restock much needed 物资 for 平民 engineers who were designing the MD-17 反坦克地雷, the blueprints of which were recovered despite the unfortunate failure to reclaim Choohe.

这一重要指令为绝地潜兵重新部署到机器人前线提供了正当理由。

战斗

Having just been taken by 机器人, Lesath became the primary focus for redeploying 绝地潜兵. Once again, 绝地潜兵 stormed Lesath to seize it from the 机器人. Although this time they were taking Bots for their own purposes now rather than just leaving them to freeze in the snow. Lesath was swiftly reclaimed by 绝地潜兵, thus the Sixth Battle of Lesath ended in an 杰出 victory despite the initial loss of the 行星.
