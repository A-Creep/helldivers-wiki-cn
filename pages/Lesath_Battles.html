背景

After the annulation of the 机器人 menace in the Severin 分区 following 操作 Swift Disassembly, the 联邦 of 超级地球 believed they had concluded the war with the 机器人. Celebrations were held all across 联邦 space, while 绝地潜兵 across the stars cried out with rejoice over the defeat of their enemy. The 总统 of 超级地球 had made plans to provide an 官员 公告 to the end of the 冲突. However, the Ministry of Intelligence held a different story. They had received disturbing reports of cloaked 机器人 vessels in hiding at the furthest corners of known galactic space. An 调查 was conducted but ultimately the Ministry of Intelligence was too late. Only one day later, the 机器人 launched a full-scale 惊讶 入侵 on planets in the 瓦尔迪斯 和 拉卡伊星区. 超级地球 武装力量 were caught off guard and in mere hours 几个 planets swiftly fell in every 分区 to the cold 机械 hands of the 机器人.

Among these victims was Lesath within the Lacaille 分区.

战略与重要性

Originally 小型 colonial world, Lesath became of grave importance due to its proximity to Menkent 和 Vernen Wells, the latter of which, if captured, would cut off 增援 and 致命 物资 to the 防御 of Menkent.

Holding Lesath thus became a priority to assure Vernen Wells could not fall 遭受攻击 from 机器人 forces.

战斗

The first battle for Lesath came following initial 超级地球武装部队 High 指挥 orders to halt the 机器人 advance through the Lacaille 分区 and into the Hydra 分区. However, two of the four systems within the Lacaille 分区 had already fallen, with 增援 being directed to the 防御 of Menkent in the Hydra 分区. Thus the First Battle of Lesath ended with staggering losses. Those 绝地潜兵 that deployed to Lesath did their best to evacuate what essential personnel and civilians they could, but they were unable to 守住防线 against the 机器人 forces and were ordered to withdraw within the following day.

The First Battle of Lesath ended with over four million SEAF casualties. However, the 防御 of Lesath was enough to weigh down the 机器人 advance onto Vernen Wells, allowing for its successful 防御.

Those 绝地潜兵 that arrived after their deployment of Menkent found the conditions of Lesath much more preferable, though remarked they wished they brought their wooly capes after garrisoning the 行星.
