The 补给背包 is a backpack 战略配备 allows 绝地潜兵 to carry 物资 to supplement yourself or the team's ammunition, grenades and stims.

采购

The 补给背包 is unlocked at the 爱国行政中心 (in the 工程港 subsection) for 4000 申购点 Slips at Level 1.

功能

When called in, the 补给背包 arrives fully stocked with four 重新补给 boxes. Each of these functions the same as a 重新补给 box from the 重新补给 战略配备, though it does not 受益 from the 卓越封装方法 舰船模块 升级. Pressing the "Interact" button on a teammate (PC 默认 press E, PlayStation 默认 press X) will 重新补给 their ammunition, stims, and grenades in the same fashion. The 补给背包 can also be used by its wearer to 重新补给 their own stocks, this is done by pressing the "Use Backpack 功能" button (PC 默认 press 5, PlayStation 默认 press D-Pad Down).

The 补给背包 can only be refilled by picking up a supply box from the 重新补给 战略配备. One 重新补给 box will refill one box on the 补给背包, 无论 whether or not the 卓越封装方法 升级 has been unlocked. Stray 弹药 Boxes, 手榴弹 Cases, and Stim Crates found within a 任务 cannot refill the 补给背包. Refilling the 补给背包 will also refill the wearer's other 物资 as normal.

战术信息

  • The primary 功能型 of the 补给背包 is to be able to provide 物资 to oneself and to teammates on the move, without having to call down the 重新补给 战略配备. This can be particularly useful when the team is split up in such a way that a 重新补给 战略配备 cannot be used by all teammates, or a team member has a particularly 弹药-hungry weapon that needs to be constantly replenished.
  • The number of supply boxes carried in the 补给背包 is 可见 on the wearer's back. This makes it 容易 to visually keep track of how many spare 物资 are on hand.
  • It is most supply-高效 to carry supply-hungry 装备 alongside the 补给背包, such as the 工程包 or Med Kit 护甲 Passives, or the 榴弹手枪. This way, refilling the 补给背包 from a 重新补给 will help to top off the 已增加 threshold for 最大 Grenades and Stims on 工程包 and Med Kit 护甲, respectively.
  • 留意团队的物资状况，了解他们何时需要补给。
    • When their supply icon in the lower-left corner of the HUD is flashing red, it means they are low in that 类别. If it is solid red, they are fully depleted. However, be aware that the backpack icon status may be misleading in the case of 背包 like the 防护罩生成包 and 喷射背包, which are not crew weapon ammunition 背包.
    • You can also visually 确认 how much 储备 ammunition certain 支援武器 like the Spear or 机炮 have by looking at their 背包.
  • 在呼叫重新补给和拾取补给箱时要讲究策略，把队友的需求放在心上。
    • As with normally calling down 重新补给, it is ideal to do so when the team is not in immediate combat. This not only allows teammates to refill their 物资 at their leisure, but also grants an opportunity to replenish the 补给背包 without depriving teammates of 物资. 有效 通讯 is key.
    • Because a supply box refills your 物资 while also adding a box to the 补给背包, it is doubly 高效 to pick up a 重新补给 box to refill your 物资 and then directly 重新补给 teammates. However, be aware of whether or not 卓越封装方法 has been unlocked, as giving a teammate a 重新补给 from the 补给背包 will give them less 弹药 for their 支援武器 than if they picked up a box from the 重新补给 绝地喷射舱 themselves. Be mindful of the situation, and communicate with teammates prior to taking more than one supply box.
    • After the team is finished resupplying, pick up the 剩余 supply boxes from the 重新补给 绝地喷射舱 to replenish your 补给背包. It is better to carry 物资 with a team member in a known 位置 than to leave the boxes strewn around where they may never be found or used again.
  • If a team's 物资 are low in a situation where the 重新补给 战略配备 is not enough, and the 补给背包 战略配备 is off 冷却, it can be useful to call down another 补给背包 in a pinch. Refill everyone's 物资 with the currently-equipped 补给背包 to deplete it, and then replace it with a fresh one via calldown.
    • 请记住，补给背包的冷却时间比重新补给长得多，因此只应在紧急情况下这样做。
    • As with other backpack 战略配备, 呼叫中 a spare one for a teammate who has an empty backpack slot can 受益 the team as a whole. Two 玩家 with the 能力 to carry supply boxes around and 重新补给 their teammates can significantly augment the team's overall robustness, though both 背包 still need to be refilled individually from 重新补给 calldowns.
  • 如果是双人组队，可以让一名绝地潜兵携带支援背包武器和补给背包，另一名作为装填手背支援背包，因为射手可以给装填手补充弹药。
