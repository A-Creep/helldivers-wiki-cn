Hulk Firebomber 是 重型 机器人 属于……的一部分  Incineration Corps subfaction. It is a heavily modified Hulk equipped with a Rotary 发射器, firing Phosphorus Shells, alongside a 重型 Flamer unit.

生成

Hulk Firebombers can be first found on 挑战 on planets invaded by the Incineration Corps. They can spawn from 机器人空投, within patrols, guarding 机器人 哨站, 目标，以及 Minor Places of Interest，或从 Bulk Fabricators.

行为

Hulk Firebombers will sprint towards 绝地潜兵, firing two White Phosphorus Shells, once every two 秒; these shells will leave lingering 火焰 where it lands. It will then pause for five 秒 before firing two more Shells again. Hulk Firebombers will often aim their Rotary 发射器 up into the air, allowing the Phosphorus Shells to 弧度 over cover and 地形.

At 25m and closer, Hulk Firebombers will use their 重型 火焰喷射器; they can also use their 重型 Flamer right after firing their Rotary 发射器 if 绝地潜兵 are within 射程.

If they are missing their arms, Hulk Firebombers can perform a stomp with their leg.

战术信息

巨型烈焰轰炸者

  • It is highly recommended for 绝地潜兵 to move opposite of where they were initially running after a Hulk Firebomber fires its shells to avoid them.
    • Due to this, Firebombers are 极其 危险 within groups of 机器人, as they can significantly disrupt a 绝地潜兵's movement, or can lead to their deaths.
  • A 正面 hit from a Phosphorus Shell can instantly kill 绝地潜兵. It can also trip 绝地潜兵 and cause them to burn to death within the lingering 火焰.
  • 对付……时 Incineration Corps，绝地潜兵可以装备带有……的护甲 防火 和 应变自如 护甲被动 以减少火焰伤害。
  • If the Hulk Firebomber is within 射程 to use its 重型 火焰喷射器 it will stop using its Rotary 发射器 entirely, making it relatively safer to 应对 up close with 铝热剂 or other means if 绝地潜兵 dive through the flames.
    • The Hulk Firebomber also has a different 近战攻击 where it does a backhand with its 重型 火焰喷射器. This 攻击 is slower compared to other Hulks and is easier to dodge.

巨型者

  • Heavily armed and 有护甲, Hulks are powerful 敌人 that demand 特殊 attention due to their 耐久度 and 重型 武器 载荷, as they are capable of killing 绝地潜兵 in short order.
  • Hulks are covered in  重型 护甲, but they do have two weak spots: the glowing 散热器 on their backs which are  Very 轻型, and their Head, which only has 250 生命值 and will kill a Hulk if destroyed.
    • A Hulks vents can be damaged by any weapon, but are rather 耐久. It is advised to use higher-伤害 支援武器 to more easily 摧毁 the vents. Destroying its vents will cause the Hulk to go into a bleedout state, where they can be seen hobbling and flailing its 武器.
    • the RS-422 磁轨炮, APW-1 反器材步枪，以及 S-11 Speargun are capable of destroying its head with a 单发.
  • The Hulk's slow 移动速度 make them particularly 易受 dedicated 反坦克武器 such as the EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器, LAS-99 类星体加农炮，以及 GR-8 无后坐力炮 and are capable of destroying a Hulk in a 单发.
    • Due to their poor mobility, Hulks can also be easily destroyed by most 进攻型 爆炸 战略配备 such as the Eagle 500kg Bomb, “飞鹰”机枪扫射，以及 Eagle 110mm 火箭巢.
    • 单个 G-123 铝热弹 will inflict enough 伤害 to 摧毁 a Hulk 否 matter where on its body it is placed.
  • While more 耐久 than its weak spots, the Hulk's limbs can often be valid targets for 摧毁 depending on the situation.
    • The Hulk's arm joints can be shot and destroyed, causing them to 掉落 that arm's respective weapon. Once both arms are destroyed, Hulks attempt to stomp on 绝地潜兵 instead.
    • The Hulk's legs can also be shot and destroyed; destroying one leg cripples its movement, and destroying both legs will kill the Hulk.
      • The Hulk's legs are often much more convenient targets than its head or heat sinks. Destroying a leg will 硬直 it for 几个 秒 and significantly slow its 移动速度.
