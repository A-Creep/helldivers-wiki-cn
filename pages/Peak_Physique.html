描述[edit | edit 来源]

巅峰体魄
增加 近战 伤害 by 40%.
Improves 武器 操控性 with less drag on weapon movement.

解释[edit | edit 来源]

1. Your CQC becomes 40% more deadly — whether it's butt-strokes, pistol-whips, bayonet thrusts, 近战 swings, or kicks. This 伤害 增加 affects both normal 伤害 as well as 耐久伤害.
2. Your 人体工学 属性 增加 by 30 points, allowing you to snap between targets more quickly—especially noticeable when using heavier 武器 like the 主宰, 爆裂铳, or most 支援武器.

Note 
阵地 and FRV 炮塔 (unconfirmed: as well as 外骨骼机甲) are also handled better.
