升级效果

迫击炮 哨兵 prioritize 火焰 at marked targets.

要求

  • 爆炸吸收
  • 250 Common Sample
  • 200 Rare Sample
  • 30 Super Sample
  • 35,000 申购点 Slips

受影响的战略配备

  • EMS迫击炮哨戒炮
  • 迫击炮哨戒炮
  • 瓦斯迫击哨戒炮

战术信息

  • Both 迫击哨戒炮 变体' 最大 射程 is 125m.
    • This 最大 射程 is always 有效 when utilizing the ping regardless if the 迫击哨戒炮 itself has a 正面 line of sight with the enemy.
    • However, smoke 效果 cannot be bypasses by the this 升级 so mortars will still have a 最大 射程 of 50m while concealed (though they will still be able to 火焰 upon 敌人).
  • Only the owner's ping is considered, not allies'.
    • Responding to an ally's ping triggers it.
  • The priority persists so long as a 目标 遗骸 marked.
    • Both 变体 switch targets only after completing their 当前 firing 顺序.
    • the A/M-12 is less responsive due to its 4-round burst, requiring it to 完成 its 齐射 before refocusing.
  • The mortar 哨戒炮 only 目标 构筑者 if they have 正面 line of sight; they 回应 inconsistently compared to 典型 pinged 敌人.
