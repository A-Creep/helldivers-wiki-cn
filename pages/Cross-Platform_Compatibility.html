战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 两者 迫击哨戒炮 variants' 最大 射程 is 125m.
    • This 最大 射程 is 总是显示 有效 when utilizing the ping regardless if the 迫击哨戒炮 itself has a 指挥 line of sight with the 敌人.
    • However, 烟雾弹 效果 cannot be bypasses by the this 升级 so mortars will still have a 最大 射程 of 50m while concealed (though they will still be able to 火焰 upon 敌人).
  • Only the owner's ping is considered, not allies'.
    • Responding to an 盟友's ping triggers it.
  • The priority persists so 长 as a 目标 遗骸 marked.
    • 两者 variants 切换 targets only after completing their 当前 firing 顺序.
    • the A/M-12 is 更少 responsive due to its 4-回合 连射, requiring it to 完成 its 齐射 before refocusing.
  • The 迫击炮 sentries only 目标 构筑者 if they have 指挥 line of sight; they 回应 inconsistently compared to 典型 pinged 敌人.
