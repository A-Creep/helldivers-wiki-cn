1.002.002 是为《绝地潜兵2》发布的一次热修复更新。

概述

  • 崩溃修复

修复

  • Fixed crash when a player leaves while ragdolling and throwing grenades
  • Fixed potential crash when hotjoining 光能者 missions
  • Fixed crash when large amounts of 摧毁 is taking place
  • 修复 for crash when reloading a 霰弹枪 and entering the FRV MG
  • Fixed some potential 崩溃 on joining or leaving other player's ship"
  • Fixed crash that could occur on certain explosions
  • Fixed potential crash when calling 载具 战略配备
  • Fixed crash that could happen when fighting in a colony map.

已知问题

这些问题要么是由本次补丁引入的且正在修复中，要么来自之前的版本且尚未修复。

Top Priority

  • 玩家 are unable to emote when flying through the air
  • 战略配备 balls bounce unpredictably off cliffs and some spots
  • Balancing and functionality adjustments for DSS
  • 匹配 improvements to reduce wait time during peak hour

中型 Priority

  • Firebomb Hellpods can 否 longer 摧毁 larger enemy 类型 such as tanks
  • 玩家 can get stuck on 鹈鹕-1’s ramp during 撤离
  • 高伤害武器 will not 引爆 Hellbombs already present on the map
  • 玩家 using the Microsoft IME languages are unable to 类型 Korean, Japanese and Chinese 语言 characters in chat
  • 武器 with a 蓄力 机械师 can exhibit unintended 行为 when firing faster than the 发/分钟 (发 每分钟) limit
  • 玩家 who use the “this is democracy” emote on their ship might unintentionally send their fellow 绝地潜兵 on unplanned spacewalks
