概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

玩家 can use 奖牌 to 声称 various items, including 2 武器, 战略配备, and armors, 6 cosmetics, and 300 超级货币. These 奖励 are spread across three pages, 每个 requiring increasingly 更多 奖牌 (ranging from 2 to 110). 玩家 开始 on 页面 one, unlocking subsequent pages as they 花费 更多 奖牌, gradually expanding their customization options.

目录[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Different 类型 of 武器 than in 上一个 战争债券—namely, two 战略配备 and a supportive 次要—with 否 强化资源.

  • 灭菌器 sprays caustic 毒气 while holding down the trigger.
  • “守卫 Dog” Dog Breath is a 无人机 that flies near its 背包 航母 and sprays nearby 敌人 with 毒气.
  • the 治疗剂手枪 can hit fellow 绝地潜兵 with stims from afar.
  • the G-4 毒气 releases a 云 of 毒气 after a 2.9 second fuse 时间.
  • the 身体 armors provide you with 毒气 抗性.
