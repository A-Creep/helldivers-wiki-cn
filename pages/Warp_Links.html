Warp Links

Warp Links[1], or 供给 Lines as they tend to be called by the community, are a dynamic 网络 of pathways between 每个 冲突-threatened 行星 in the 第二次银河战争. 供给 Lines dictate which planets are 打开 for 解放 and which planets are at 风险 of an 攻击 by the 终结族 and 机器人.

机制[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Warp Links critically 允许 for 解放 and 防御 campaigns to occur via the mounting of 行星 攻击.

An 敌人 行星 cannot be liberated unless it is connected to a 超级地球 controlled 行星 via warp 链接, and 敌人 阵营 cannot launch a 防御 campaign on a 超级地球 controlled 行星 without a warp 链接.

If a 超级地球 controlled 行星 is connected to an 敌人 controlled 行星 via Warp 链接, the 超级地球 行星 will mount a 行星 攻击 on said 敌人 行星, triggering a 解放 campaign.

When 超级地球 fully liberates an 敌人 controlled 行星, new 行星 攻击 are immediately mounted from the newly liberated world to 任何 connected 敌人 controlled planets.

Moreover, if 超级地球 happens to lose 控制 of a 行星, 全部 行星 攻击 mounted from said 行星 will cease. Unless another 超级地球 owned world is attacking an 敌人 行星 being liberated, the 解放 campaign for said 敌人 行星 will 制止.

防御 campaigns are triggered when an 敌人 阵营 mounts a 行星 攻击 from one of their controlled planets to a 超级地球 controlled 行星 via Warp 链接.

Should 超级地球 fail to 防御 the attacked 行星 before the 防御 campaign's 截止日期, said 行星 will be lost to the attacking 阵营. 任意星球 攻击 mounted from the lost world will cease.

However, a 防御 campaign can 结束 early if the 行星 mounting the 攻击 towards the 超级地球 行星 is liberated before the 防御 campaign's 截止日期. This is 通常 referred to as a Gambit by the community.

    • This 已更改 following the 补丁, where the 供给 Lines would be displayed on the in-游戏 银河地图.

详情[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Warp Links are dynamic, they can 变化 at 任何 时间, 通常 depending on the active 重要指令 or manually.
  • 敌人 阵营 will 通常 关注 the 供给 Line rules when launching 攻击 on 超级地球 worlds, however there are exceptions.
    • This includes when Meridia turned into a Supercolony, and when the Valdis 分区 was invaded and captured during the 机器人 second wave.
  • On occasion, new Warp Links have been 已添加 before the 终结族 and 机器人 launch 攻击 on 超级地球 worlds. However, 超级地球 cannot launch a 解放 campaign via these new Warp Links until a 防御 campaign is won, or if the 行星 in 问题 is lost and re-liberated.
  • The method of how Meridia Outbreaks occurred while the Supercolony was active is 未知, as it was observed that the 攻击 did not properly 关注 the 供给 line rules.
  • If a 行星 bordering a dense Gloom 云 is liberated, then 解放 campaigns will still 开始 for 任何 planets within the dense Gloom 云 if they are connected via a 供给 line. This is despite said planets being unselectable from the map screen.
    • This has been observed 两次 on Hellmire while the 行星 was enveloped by the dense Gloom 云. 两者 times, the active 解放 campaigns had to be manually cancelled.
