超级商店

the 超级商店 is an item 商店 in 绝地潜兵 2 accessible through the 获取 Center. In it 护甲 sets, 武器，以及 Cosmetics based on various 战争债券 plus an extra 战马 set can be bought using 超级货币. 超级商店 items are exclusive to the store and are unobtainable through the 战争债券 themselves.

变更历史[edit | edit 来源]

补丁
描述

1.006.202
2026-04-28
(Mid-补丁)

Rotating pages 特性 已移除, all 内容 sets now have permanent pages.

1.003.000
2025-05-15
(Mid-补丁)

New 内容 added alongside Masters of 仪式 尊享战争债券. The 超级商店 will have one rotating page featuring six different 内容 sets that changes every 48 hours, shortening the rotation to 12 days. All the earlier 超级商店 items from older 战争债券 are available to 购买 anytime. When new 战争债券 are released, one of the 当前 rotating sets will be moved to a permanent page.

1.002.200
2025-03-20
(Mid-补丁)

New 内容 added alongside Borderline 正义 尊享战争债券, pages and rotations re-ordered.

1.002.105
2025-02-18

Pages re-enabled.

1.002.101
2025-02-06
(Mid-补丁)

  • The rotation 已更改:
    • From chronological to reverse chronological order.
  • Pages added.
    • Pages disabled, a visual bug had the potential to cause unintended player purchases.

1.002.003
2024-12-28
(Mid-补丁)

The rotation 已更改:

  1. from a mixed 涂装—beginning with the latest entries through the seventh, then progressing chronologically through the sixth and resetting with each new release.
  2. to a fully chronological order.
