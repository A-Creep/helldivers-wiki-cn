生成[edit | edit 来源]

Cannon 炮塔 can be first found in 机器人 哨站 on 中型. They can also spawn in 哨站 in the Neutralize 轨道武器 Defenses 任务, guarding the 机器人' 轨道加农炮.

Starting on 自杀任务, they can be found guarding Minor Places of Interests. 机器人 Fortresses on 超级绝地潜兵难度 can also be guarded by Cannon 炮塔.

Notably, certain Cannon 炮塔 will be built on shorter pillars, allowing it to 火焰 at 绝地潜兵 that are close to it.

行为[edit | edit 来源]

Cannon 炮塔 will idly point towards the sky until a 绝地潜兵 walks in front of its barrel (its point of view), or if gunfire is shot near or at it. The Fusion Battle Cannon will flash a red 轻型 before firing a second later, before reloading for about eight 秒.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • The Cannon Turret may not always rotate towards a 绝地潜兵 who has engaged 机器人 without shooting near the turret itself. However, it will try to retaliate when shot at.
  • Due to being mounted atop a large pillar, the Cannon Turret cannot 火焰 at anything that gets too close, as its gun barrel cannot depress far enough down. This is not the case for Cannon 炮塔 atop short pillars.
  • Its 弱点 is the glowing orange heatsink on the rear.
  • As this turret 使用次数 爆炸伤害, even a near-miss can launch 绝地潜兵 into the air or knock them prone.
  • Beware their 远距离 and seemingly indecisive 索敌 行为; 炮塔 may seem to ignore a 绝地潜兵 in one instance, only to aggressively 锁定 and open 火焰 at 极难 ranges in others. Fortunately, their 投射物 速率 is relatively low, so they can be avoided at 远距离 by staying mobile. Most larger 机器人 哨站 have at least one turret on higher difficulties, 有时 two.
  • Completing the 妥协 机器人 Defenses 可选目标 disables the Cannon Turret's 能力 to 目标 绝地潜兵, making it practically harmless.
