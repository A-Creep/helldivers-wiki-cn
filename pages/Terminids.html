the 终结族 are one of three 当前 阵营 the 绝地潜兵 can fight against, with the others being the 机器人 and the 光能者.

They have three sub-阵营: The   捕食者 Strain, the   Spore Burst Strain, and the   破裂 Strain.

概述

The ravenous 终结族 are guided solely by an instinctive urge to reproduce, spread, and 摧毁 Democracy. They are a fast-evolving plague, a shapeshifting infestation that threatens to devour and disenfranchise every single child on every single 行星.

— Playstation Store 描述

The 终结族, insect-like creatures referred to as 虫族 before their scientific name was given, are rapidly-reproducing, swarming aliens. Much of the 终结族 攻击 using their claws and acidic bile. They now swarm the planets north-east of the Sol System.

酸

酸, also known as Bile, is the preferred method of dealing 伤害 for some 终结族, namely, the 胆汁吐沫虫, 抚育喷涌虫, 胆汁喷涌虫 和 吐酸泰坦。 Bug Mine also 施加 酸 to creatures that step on it. 酸 blobs deals 伤害 on hit, depending on the size of it, and then 施加 a slow and 伤害 over time 效果.

战术信息

  • 终结族 频繁 use 酸 伤害。 蟑龙 in particular also make use of 火焰 伤害. 绝地潜兵 should plan accordingly.
  • 虫洞 lack an apparent hitbox, which has a few implications:
    • They can only be destroyed using 爆破 Force, unlike 机器人 Fabricators 和 停靠的跃迁舰, which can be destroyed by any weapon with an 护甲穿透 of 5.
    • Paired with them being recessed within the ground, it can be tricky to gauge if an 爆炸 will be close enough to demolish them and can result in Nests surviving bombardments that it seems they shouldn't. 绝地潜兵 should make sure to either be precise with their 爆破 or make missing a statistical improbability by volume of 火焰.
  • Despite providing Hellbombs, certain 终结族 目标, such as Spore 喷吐者 和 尖啸虫巢穴, only have an 护甲 Value of 2. Because of this, 绝地潜兵 can 摧毁 these structures with any weapon, albeit slowly, due to their high 生命值 and 耐久度.
