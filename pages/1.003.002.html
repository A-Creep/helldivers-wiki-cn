1.003.002 was a major 更新 released for 绝地潜兵 2, titled "Heart of Democracy". The main 特性 of this 更新 was the 机制 and availability of 超级地球 as a playable 行星, in 轻型 of the 光能者 入侵 of 超级地球.

Introduction[edit | edit 来源]

绝地潜兵, it’s time to defend the Heart of Democracy! Fight back against the 光能者 on the streets of 超级地球. The worst nightmare of all those who fight for 管理式民主 has been made a grim 现实.

Despite the 英勇 efforts of our 绝地潜兵 to push back the fleet, the 光能者 have reached 超级地球. Now they are swarming our cities, terrorizing citizens, and destroying our 基础设施. It’s time to rise up and take arms and fight for the precious foundations and soil of Democracy manifest. 绝地潜兵, return to our 行星 and defend what is rightfully ours.

超级地球… OUR HOME[edit | edit 来源]

The urban sprawl of 超级地球 is the site of the 冲突. Fight among the towering skyscrapers and crowded streets of our home turf as the 光能者 rain down 摧毁 all around you.

Inside the city biomes, you will tackle operations and work to liberate cities together, having a significant impact on 行星 campaigns.

REPEL THE 入侵[edit | edit 来源]

The 绝地潜兵 are tasked with a new 目标: to repel the 光能者 入侵 by gaining ground over the squids as they fight to 控制 areas where the fleet is landing. It won’t be a walk in the park, divers. Like a game of intergalactic tug-of-war, you will struggle against the incoming forces, gaining and losing 控制 quickly.

ARM THE CANNONS[edit | edit 来源]

Even in the face of such 危险, we are not 无助, and all is not lost. The might of 超级地球’s arsenal lies hidden beneath our peaceful, democratic streets. With your help, we must activate our 行星 防御 Cannons and 击倒 the 光能者 fleet in a grand show of power.

CALL FOR BACKUP[edit | edit 来源]

It’s all hands on deck as you encounter SEAF troops who will help join the fight to defend our Freedom. These small squads will fight 敌人 on their own, or they can be ordered to follow 绝地潜兵 and provide temporary backup as you navigate toward 目标. Salute and lead them on the path to victory, divers!

保护 CITIZENS[edit | edit 来源]

However, you must also be aware of the many civilians still roaming the streets, fearing for their lives. They may be panicked, 绝地潜兵, but it is your sworn duty to keep them safe and they look to you as the 终极 protectors of liberty. Remember: any 平民 lives lost from 友军误伤 will be punished.

PREPARE FOR THE FIGHT[edit | edit 来源]

Last week, we released a powerful 主武器 customization system and expanded surplus options to fight against the 光能者 as they emerged from the singularity. Now that you have learned the 战术 of these new squid troops–like the Crescent 监视者 and the hulking Fleshmob–you must master these tools to 继续 the fight on 超级地球. Use every available 资源, divers, and decimate their forces!
