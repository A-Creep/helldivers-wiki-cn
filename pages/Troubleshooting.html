稳定性与维护

As a 基本信息 rule of thumb it is 重要 to realize that keeping your system properly maintained is half the battle and can 有时 prevent time-consuming (and costly!) 故障排除 processes. Modern live-服务 games such as 绝地潜兵 2 rely heavily on your hardware and 软件 working together flawlessly. Especially in this day and age it's 容易 for gaming devices to become cluttered over time without us realizing it. Background apps, conflicting overlays, accumulated temporary files, etc. can compound to a point where they start to 喉缩 your system, especially in scenarios where performance is crucial.

Please be aware that this does not mean you are doing anything wrong: Such occurrences are the 自然 consequences of using a 电脑 or 控制台 over months and years. Even a simple task such as updating your drivers can potentially create leftovers by the old driver which then might accidentally interfere with a new game 补丁. In this sense, doing basic 维护 and 故障排除 is less about fixing a broken game and more about a routine check-up to keep your systems performance at the best it can be.

Before you dive deeper into the more 复杂 topics, there's a list of basic steps you can do first:

  1. 安装系统更新
    • Make sure to keep your Operating System or 控制台 firmware fully up to date.
    • A proper setup of your OS can also help in other regards (See Virtual Memory).
  2. 更新你的驱动
    • PC 玩家 should always perform a clean 更新 of their GPU and chipset drivers. (Please read this: GPU Driver Recommendation)
    • A BIOS更新 can potentially 改进 稳定性 and stuttering problems.
  3. 外设更新
    • Ensure your controller or 耳机 firmware is updated to prevent issues.
  4. 清理杂乱
    • Periodically 清除着色器缓存 and game data to wipe out corrupted temporary files. This can help restore lost performance and 解决 崩溃.
  5. 检查损坏
    • DISM / SFC for Windows can 修复 受保护 system files and 解决 issues that would stem from them being corrupted.
    • Please note that this does not replace a full system re-installation. It can help 改进 system 稳定性 though when these files are causing issues.
  6. 除尘
    • Keeping your device clean helps in preventing heat-相关 problems, such as performance drops and loud fan noises.
  7. 追踪你的 Mod
    • New Game Updates can clash with installed Mods. If you encounter crashing, it might be necessary to disable / remove them.
  8. 小心超频
    • Disabling all overclocking (including RAM overclocking profiles like EXPO and XMP) can help 改进 稳定性 if you encounter crashing. For 基本信息 information on how to set up your RAM configuration, please check 本文.

Regular 维护 and resolving of existing problems can 解决 issues long before they occur down the line.

Whether you are 故障排除 a 问题 by yourself or seeking help from the community, having as much diagnostic information as possible is 总是 the single most 有效 way to save yourself a lot of time and 接收 proper aid. An 示例 of this can be game 崩溃.
