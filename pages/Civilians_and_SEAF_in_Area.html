Civilians and SEAF in Area 是一个 操作 修正 in 绝地潜兵 2. This 修正 impacts all operations taken on Megacity Biomes during 防御-campaigns.

During 解放-campaigns and Eradication and 防御-missions, the 修正 is 已降低 to "SEAF Units In Area" thus only having SEAF Soldiers and 否 civilians in the 任务-map.

详情

  • SEAF Soldiers as well as 平民 will be present throughout the 生物群系 through the entirety of the 任务.
    • If a 绝地潜兵 is directly responsible for the death of either group, this will result in the entire squad depleting 25 申购点 Slips per kill from the 任务 total.
  • Civilians are unarmed pedestrians that often appear in moderately sized groups and will 通常 run past 绝地潜兵 while escaping from 敌人 of 超级地球.
  • SEAF Soldiers are often seen in 小型 firegroups of 3-6, where they will be actively fighting or seeking enemy forces of 超级地球.
