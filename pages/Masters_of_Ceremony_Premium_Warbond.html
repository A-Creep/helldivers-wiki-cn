概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

玩家 can use 奖牌 to 声称 various items, including a 强化资源，一款 战略配备, 3 武器, 2 armors, 6 cosmetics, and 300 超级货币. These 奖励 are spread across three pages, 每个 requiring increasingly 更多 奖牌 (ranging from 2 to 110). 玩家 开始 on 页面 one, unlocking subsequent pages as they 花费 更多 奖牌, gradually expanding their customization options.
