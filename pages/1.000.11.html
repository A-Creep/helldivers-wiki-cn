1.000.11 is an 更新 that was released for 绝地潜兵 2.

🛠️ 补丁 1.000.11 ⚙️

🔧 修复 Fixed 多个 崩溃 triggered when joining other 玩家’ ships.
Fixed crash triggered when exiting ADS.
Fixed crash triggered when 玩家 leave a session while bombardments are active.
Fixed issue where online missions in 星系战争地图 were unselectable.
Fixed issue with GameGuard and Steam’s “verify 正直” step.
Fixed issue with GameGuard and the Windows firewall.
Fixed issue preventing access to 舰船管理 panel.
Fixed issue causing 玩家 to get stuck in the defrosting or in the ship intro cinematic
Fixed crash triggered when the 流程 of buying 超级货币 fails.
Fixed crash triggered after consecutive quickplay attempts.
Implemented a functionality that will kick 玩家 who remain idle for 15 分钟 back to the 标题 screen.
Continuous 改进 on client > backend communications.
