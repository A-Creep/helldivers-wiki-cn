    Deciduous Forest

    Deciduous Forest 是一片森林 生物群系 可以在……中的各种行星上找到。 绝地潜兵 2.

    描述

    The Deciduous Forest is a lush 生物群系 that features mostly flat 地形 covered by green grass and multi-colored flower patches. The 浮出地面 is also mostly covered by thickets of trees, with some rarely being pine trees. The 浮出地面 also contains some large rocky mountainous formations, found rarely between the trees. Another 特性 are the lakes and rivers on the 浮出地面, though these 特性 shin-deep water that poses little to 否 威胁 to a 绝地潜兵 looking to take a swim.

    天空和大气呈蓝色，日落时天空变为黄/橙色，夜间则变为深蓝色，可以看到星星和附近的天体。云层也非常蓬松，与大多数其他生态群落不同，它们呈现出近乎纯白的颜色。与大多数其他生态群落不同，夜间通常很明亮，视野良好。

    As for fauna, butterflies can be seen floating around the 浮出地面, as well as a call from a 可能 Avian 生物 偶尔 being able to be heard. Flocks of black birds can also be seen flying around after a tree is destroyed.

    Of all the biomes available, the Deciduous Forest is by far the most Earth-similar 生物群系.

    环境条件

    此生态区域没有任何环境条件。

    环境危害

    减速 Ferns, which slow any 绝地潜兵 passing through them down.

    天气

    • 清楚
      • 天空中要么点缀着少量大而蓬松的白云，要么覆盖着薄如面纱的云层。雾气极少。

    • 雨/太阳雨
      • 雨天总是以微风和太阳雨开始，不久之后天空才会被灰云覆盖。这种天气还伴有浓雾。
