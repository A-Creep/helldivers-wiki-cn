重型蹂躏者

重型 蹂躏者 是一种中型 机器人 和……的一个变种 蹂躏者, equipped with a Fusion Repeater and a large 防弹盾牌.

生成

重型 Devastators can be first found on 简单 作为……的任务目标 消除 Devastators 任务.

Starting on 挑战, 重型 Devastators, alongside Rocket Devastators, will have a chance of replacing Devastators. This chance 增加 the higher the 难度. 重型 Devastators can spawn from 机器人空投, within patrols, and guarding 机器人 哨站, 目标，以及 Minor Places of Interest.

如果星球被……入侵   Incineration Corps, 重型 Devastators are replaced by 烈火蹂躏者 从……开始 简单, and additionally by 燃烧机枪蹂躏者 从……开始 中型.

行为

重型 Devastators will slowly but constantly push if a 绝地潜兵 is within their line of sight, firing a deadly 齐射 from their Fusion Repeater for three 秒, before 冷却 down for two 秒.

重型 Devastators can bash 绝地潜兵 with their shield if they're too close, knocking them down. They can also stomp on 绝地潜兵 if they are prone.

战术信息

重型蹂躏者

  • The 重型 蹂躏者 can be a nuisance even at a significant 射程, as their Repeater 齐射 can potentially hit and cause 绝地潜兵 to flinch 多个 times.
    • This can most notably prevent 绝地潜兵 from 使用针剂, making them 极其 危险 when 绝地潜兵 are surrounded by various 机器人.
    • They are also able to 火焰 through their 护盾 if walking towards 绝地潜兵 at a certain angle, 含义 that staying on their left 侧面 does not guarantee safety.
  • 多个 重型 Devastators can easily cover each others' Fusion Repeater cooldowns, leading to a nigh-endless amount of projectiles being fired.
  • 重型 Devastators are 易受 flanking, especially with the assistance of another 绝地潜兵. Their backpack can be damaged by any weapon, and destroying it will also 摧毁 the 重型 蹂躏者.
  • Explosives can 硬直 重型 Devastators, causing them to lower their shield and leaving themselves open, in addition to dealing some 爆炸伤害.
    • 用爆炸武器射击它们的护盾也能达到同样效果。
  • Shooting the 重型 蹂躏者's shield with 弹道 武器库 will deal 否 伤害 to the 重型 蹂躏者.

蹂躏者

  • 大量武器可以一发爆头摧毁蹂躏者，例如 P-113 裁决 和 R-63 Diligence, so precision is highly rewarded; this is especially so with the 重型 蹂躏者 and its  Incineration Corps 变体. Their 胃 and pelvis is still 易受 any weapon, but it is 通常 unfeasible for 绝地潜兵 to aim at due to the 防弹盾牌.
  • 与其他蹂躏者类似， 重型 蹂躏者's weapon can be shot off, which may be preferable if a 绝地潜兵 is unable to land a 爆头.
