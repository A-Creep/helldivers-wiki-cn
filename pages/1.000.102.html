1.000.102 was an 更新 released for 绝地潜兵 2

补丁 1.000.102

🌍 概述
Today's 补丁 is 应对 the 生成速率 of heavily 有护甲 终结族 敌人 as well as the possible play against them. It also contains some fixes to UI elements and crash fixes.

⚖️ Balancing
敌人:

  • The amount of heavily 有护甲 targets that spawn on higher difficulties, especially for 终结族, have been a big discussion point online and internally. The intent is for groups to have to bring some form of 反坦克 能力 but not to the degree previously needed.

To that end we have 已降低 the 生成速率 of Chargers and Bile Titans on difficulties 7 and up. In addition we have 已降低 the risk of spawn spikes of Chargers and Bile Titans.
Please note that we have 已更改 the 分布 of enemy 类型, not 已降低 难度. Expect other enemy 类型 to appear in 更大 numbers instead.

  • We are humbled by the community's 能力 to find things like Chargers “leg meta” in our game, however spending your 重型 anti tank 武器 on legs instead of the 明显 weak point seems 反制 to expectation.

We are not changing anything regarding the 强袭虫’s legs, we are however lowering the 生命值 of the 强袭虫’s head. It should now be at a point where a well placed shot from a 无后坐力炮 or EAT-17 instantly kills a 强袭虫.
Together with the unfortunately undocumented change of last 补丁 that 已增加 the 护甲穿透 能力 of less well placed shots for EAT-17s and 无后坐力炮 shots, Chargers should now be easier to handle by well equipped groups.

🎮玩法

  • "电子对抗措施" 操作 修正, which had a chance of giving you a random 战略配备 instead of the one you input, has been 已移除 in order to be reworked, and will be reintroduced in a future iteration. We found that this 修正 wasn’t communicated clearly enough and overall caused more frustration than excitement with the way it was currently implemented. This change was made in 1.000.100 but was unintentionally omitted from the 更新日志.

🔧 Fixes

  • 修复了多个抬头显示/用户界面元素上的缺失文字。
  • 修复了新闻视频中多个字幕/语音不匹配的问题。
  • Fixed various 崩溃 that occurred mid-玩法 and when deploying to missions.

🧠 已知问题
These are issues that were either introduced by this 补丁 and are being worked on, or are from a 上一个 version and have not yet been fixed.

  • Game 崩溃 when attempting to use a stim while inside an 外骨骼机甲.
  • 引发大型爆炸时，天空中可能出现粉色伪影。
  • 机器人 运输舰 seemingly disappears and slides in after being shot down.
  • 电弧武器的射击可能不会计入任务后统计中的击杀数。
  • 玩家无法移除通过好友代码添加的好友。
  • 跨平台好友邀请可能不会出现在好友请求标签页中，跨平台玩家可能无法接受好友请求。
  • 通过PS5活动卡加入多人游戏会话时，玩家可能无法选择装备或返回舰船。
  • The 外骨骼机甲 can 摧毁 itself with rockets if it fires while turning.
  • 撤离期间，文字聊天框显示被电影式黑边遮挡。
