G/40-K Melta Mine 是一个 标配投掷武器 that deploys a proximity mine which, when detonated, releases a large column of superheated thermal 能量 before exploding.

战术信息

  • The Melta Mine does not have a 标准 fuse - it will remain where it is thrown instead, waiting until 敌人 get close enough for it to 爆炸. It is armed when 绝地潜兵 twist the handle, this is done before the mine is thrown.
    • The Melta Mine does not trigger on 轻型 敌人, and can be safely walked over by 绝地潜兵. Instead, they are only triggered by 中型 or 重型单位.
  • When detonated, the Melta Mine first deploys a large column of superheated thermal 能量, dealing 庞大 伤害 to 敌人, capable of killing large 敌人 such as Hulks, then 引爆 again with a larger 爆炸, capable of killing stragglers and leaving 火焰 on the ground.
  • The Melta Mine has a significantly shorter throwing 射程 than other throwables, only being able to be thrown a few meters away. It is highly recommended to aim upwards when throwing to ensure 绝地潜兵 don't die when it lands and arms.
    • Alternatively, 绝地潜兵 may utilise the 沙漠突击者 or 伺服辅助 护甲 passives to 增加 the throwing 射程.
  • An 有效 策略 using the Melta Mine is to place it on or 下一个 to a Damaged Hellbomb, potentially bringing entire patrols to their doom.
  • While Melta Mine doesn't get triggered by 轻型 敌人, it will still 爆炸 upon taking 伤害
    • It can be manually detonated by shooting at it from a safe 距离. Grounded Warp Ships and 机器人 Fabricators are prone to being destroyed this way.
  • The mine can stick to most 浮出地面, including 背包 of the 超级地球武装部队 troops.
