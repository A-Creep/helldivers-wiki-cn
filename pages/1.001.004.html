修复[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

崩溃修复

  • 已修复 a 崩溃 when dropping into a 游戏 joined by invite while previously hosting.
  • 已修复 a 崩溃 that would occur after being kick for being AFK
  • 已修复 a 崩溃 that could happen when dropping down into a 任务.
  • 已修复 崩溃 that could occur if shutting down the 游戏 during boot.

基本信息 武器 and 战略配备 修复:

  • 已修复 an 问题 where 火焰喷射器 particles would ignore 敌人.
  • Mines can now be 射击 and blown up while near a 危险 object.

敌人 修复:

  • 已降低 the 爆炸半径 of Tentacles Stabbing 能力 and 轻型 Rockets. This should alleviate the excessive ragdolling caused by the 穿刺虫 and 机器人 敌人 using 轻型 rockets. We are still working on improving the ragdoll 行为 overall.
  • 已修复 a bug where the 穿刺虫's tentacles could chase you indefinitely in 一些 cases.

Miscellaneous 修复:

  • 已修复 an 问题 that could 原因 柔软-lock during 掉落-in after the host left.
