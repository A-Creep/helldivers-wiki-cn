补丁 01.001.004

概述

  • 崩溃修复
  • Weapon and 战略配备 fixes
  • Fixes to the 穿刺虫 & 爆炸半径
  • Soft lock fix

修复

崩溃修复

  • Fixed a crash when dropping into a game joined by invite while previously hosting.
  • Fixed a crash that would occur after being kick for being AFK
  • Fixed a crash that could happen when dropping down into a 任务.
  • Fixed crash that could occur if shutting down the game during boot.

基本信息 Weapon and 战略配备 fixes:

  • Fixed an issue where 火焰喷射器 particles would ignore 敌人.
  • Mines can now be shot and blown up while near a 危险 object.

Enemy fixes:

  • 已降低 the 爆炸半径 of Tentacles Stabbing 能力 and 轻型 Rockets. This should alleviate the excessive ragdolling caused by the 穿刺虫 and 机器人 敌人 using 轻型 rockets. We are still working on improving the ragdoll 行为 overall.
  • Fixed a bug where the 穿刺虫's tentacles could chase you indefinitely in some cases.

Miscellaneous fixes:

  • Fixed an issue that could cause soft-lock during 掉落-in after the host left.

已知问题

这些问题要么是由本次补丁引入的且正在修复中，要么来自之前的版本且尚未修复。

  • 玩家 may not 接收 Friend Requests sent from another platform.
  • "Invite only" games created by a friend can still be seen on the 星系战争地图 but cannot be joined.
  • “进行地质勘测” 任务 may become incompletable if a player calls in “Seismic Probe” and immediately leaves the game or disconnects.
  • 吐酸泰坦 有时 does not take 伤害 to the head.
  • 流血死亡的敌人不会推进个人指令和歼灭任务。
  • 大多数武器在瞄准时射击位置低于准星。
  • Chain-link fences block flamethrowers’ 火焰.
  • 战略配备光束可能会附着在敌人身上，但会部署到原始位置。
  • “Guard Dog Rover” does not overheat when firing continuously.
  • 玩家 are able to pick up empty expendable 武器 like EAT-17 or MLS-4X.
  • 增援 may not be available for 玩家 who join a game in progress.
  • 服务 技术员 on the ship cannot be interacted with - they complained they were bothered too much and could not do their work efficiently.
