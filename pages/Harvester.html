猎杀器
“

These spindly machines stalk the battlefield, reaping patriotic innocents with their cycloptic death beam. They see with only one eye, emblematic of the 有限, closed-minded perspective of the entire 光能者 species.

— 绝地潜兵 2 PlayStation Store Page

The 猎杀器 is a large, 机械 tripod created by the 光能者. It is equipped with a shield that will absorb incoming 伤害 from many 武器 and 战略配备 until it has been broken, and fires a powerful, continuous laser from its single eye-mounted Disintegration Beamer from 远距离 or an arcing 电力 电击 fired from its Tesla Ripper for short 射程.

生成

The 猎杀器 may be encountered in patrols, guarding Monoliths, and can be summoned as part of 光能者 增援. They do not spawn on planets occupied by Vote Snatchers.

行为

Harvesters do not deploy their 护盾 until alerted. In combat, their 主体 攻击 is a sweeping disintegration beam which they charge for a 小型 moment before firing, near-instantaneously killing 绝地潜兵. At 近距离, Harvesters will instead 放电 an equally deadly 弧度 of 电击 from their eye-mounted Tesla Ripper.

战术信息

  • Although they are not as healthy as other towering units such as Bile Titans and 工厂 Striders, Harvesters are extraordinarily 耐久 in their own way: they are 受保护 by a regenerating 能量护盾.
  • Their 能量护盾 is guaranteed to absorb any single instance of 伤害 and can regenerate after breaking. 高伤害 output is necessary to remove this shield and 伤害 the 猎杀器 directly.
    • 猎杀器眼睛上方和下方的叉状物是其护盾发生器；只需移除一个即可永久禁用猎杀器的护盾能力。
    • The eye and the hip joints are the 主体 practical 弱点. In 基本信息 武器 with higher 耐久伤害 will see better performance from aiming at the hip joints, whereas ones with lower 耐久伤害 can favor aiming for the eye, in particular 武器 with 重型 穿透 and especially ones with 散射/后坐力 since missing the 小型 eye will still yield 伤害 to the 主生命值 pool through the 甲壳 and chasis. Anti tank 武器库 such as the EAT-17 and GR-8 无后坐力炮 should 通常 目标 the eye/head as it is far easier to hit. The 突击兵, however, can 受益 from a hip joint 目标 since it requires only one 导弹, rather than two to the head.
    • 摧毁眼睛会使解体光束的伤害从1,400 每秒伤害提升至1,800 每秒伤害，并使其在光束持续时间结束时逐渐收窄为更细的光束。
      • However, since overkill 伤害 transfers 对主血量 the GR-8 无后坐力炮 or Variable in total-射击模式 can 1 shot a 猎杀器 to the eye. But even if you miss and hit the 甲壳 it'll kill the 猎杀器.
    • 爆炸武器 that 生产 shrapnel, such as the R-36 Eruptor and the AC-8 机炮 in flak mode, are especially 有效 at cheaply removing a 猎杀器's shield with only one or two shots.
  • It is 重要 to note the 完成 爆炸 免疫 that Harvesters have across their exterior body parts. This renders them highly 抵抗 to 战略配备 like the Eagle 500kg Bomb and immune to 伤害 from purely 爆炸武器 whose AP is below  重型, until their 甲壳 is broken.
  • 猎杀器拥有游戏中任何敌人都算得上最致命的武器；即使被解体光束擦到也能将任何绝地潜兵带到死亡边缘，其特斯拉撕裂者的电弧攻击能一击杀死多名队友。
    • 这种电弧攻击的索敌能力好得多，但只能在近距离使用，而猎杀器并不容易接近目标。
    • The M-102 Fast 侦察 载具 and 爱国者 or Emancipator 外骨骼机甲 are more 易受 Harvesters due to their large frame, though their large 生命池 is enough to survive a few 秒 of 正面 火焰.
    • 即使被消灭，尸体尤其是脱落的腿部砸到绝地潜兵也可能致命。
  • Continuous 弧度-3 电弧发射器 攻击 are capable of halting the fighting capabilities of a 猎杀器, provided the shield has been broken.
    • Same however cannot be said when using A/弧度-3 特斯拉塔: 猎杀器 will 继续 moving around and is also capable using its laser in 近距离. (NOTE: 支援 ticket pending to inquire if this intentional.)
  • The legs have 几个 致命 弱点 (hip joints, thighs, shins, feet) all which are only 受保护 by  中型 护甲. Destroying any of these limbs is enough to 消除 a 猎杀器.
    • Any 反坦克 weapon such as the EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器, MLS-4X 突击兵, or GR-8 无后坐力炮 will 摧毁 a joint with one rocket.
    • Both the APW-1 反器材步枪 and AC-8 机炮 in APHET mode will 摧毁 a joint with four shots.
    • 全自动 支援武器 such as the MG-43 机枪, MG-206 重机枪, and LAS-98 激光大炮 do not take long to 摧毁 a joint.
  • The 电路导管 护甲 被动 is 极其 valuable against Harvesters for its 能力 to resist 致命 amounts of 电弧伤害. This allows the user to remain near Harvesters, who will only use their Tesla Ripper's 弧度 攻击 for minor 伤害.
    • 电弧放电仍会对绝地潜兵施加“减速”效果；该效果无法被抵消。
  • 40的硬直力量可以从任何位置硬直猎杀器，但眼睛和甲壳弱点只有30的抗性。
    • As a result, you can interrupt their 攻击 by using 武器 such as the AR-23C 解放者 Concussive, SG-8S Slugger, R-6 Deadeye and P-69 Veto.

琐事

  • The 猎杀器 appears to be 继任者 to Tripod and its 更强 version Strider from 绝地潜兵 1.
    • However, unlike the versions from the 第一次银河战争, the 猎杀器 takes the role of an enemy elite, not 前线 infantry
  • Currently, it is 未知 whether the name refers to actual 收获 流程 of something. Or if the name is referencing to their uncanny resemblance to Opiliones aka. "收获 spiders / daddy longlegs". Or alternatively to Pholcidae aka. "gyrating (long-legged) spiders".
  • The 猎杀器 has a resembling 外观 to "Fighting Machines/Tripods" from the H.G Wells Novel; War of the Worlds. The design is more or less derived from the Tripods seen in Steven Spielberg's adaptation of War of the Worlds (2005) as it has a more squid-like design and it shows a more recent version of its 签名 heat ray/laser 攻击 and the 能量 护盾. 玩家 are also calling it Tripods for that reason as well.
    • Despite its 基本信息 resemblance to the 2005 movie, the 猎杀器 also shares many design aspects of the 2019 BBC miniseries adaptation of War of The World's depiction of the Tripod
  • 猎杀器似乎是生化有机体而非机器人，因为玩家在它死亡时可以观察到类似被摧毁大脑的东西。
