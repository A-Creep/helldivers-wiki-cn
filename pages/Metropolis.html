Metropolises are a 类型 of urban megastructure constructed by the  超级地球 联邦, unique to the Regions found on 部分星球.

概述

Metropolises encompass the entirety of the playable 任务 area when present, compared to the 更小 Colonies that only occupy a portion of it.

Within a Metropolis, a web of roads form city blocks littered with skyscrapers and other large structures. These blocks may 偶尔 be of a lower density, providing space to parks, playgrounds, and 更小 buildings.

The Metropolis can inherit certain 变更 based on the 行星's 生物群系, such as color scheme and 天气.

Parks and local flora appear differently based on the 当前 行星's 生物群系. For instance, trees and grass are colored blue on an Ionic Jungle 行星, and grass is replaced entirely with sandy soil on Desert Dune worlds.

Environmental Conditions are carried over from the parent 生物群系 as well, such as 火山活动 or 极暑.

Platoons of SEAF Soldiers spawn naturally throughout Metropolises.

Metropolises change drastically in 外观 based on whether the 当前 行星 is under a 解放 or 保卫战役:

  • On 防御 campaigns, billboards, street signs, and 轻型 fixtures 功能 properly. Structures remain largely unscathed prior to combat, and buildings outside the 任务 area are completely untouched. Roaming mobs of Civilians may spawn throughout the city in addition to SEAF soldiers.
  • On 解放 campaigns, the loss of 电击 plunges cities into darkness. Buildings generate pre-destroyed, including those outside the 任务 area, and are often overtaken by the incumbent 阵营. SEAF soldiers can still appear, but roaming Civilians will never naturally spawn. Lower density blocks (parks, etc.) are also less likely to generate.

战术信息

  • Skyscrapers significantly hamper visibility from a bird's eye view, affecting a large amount of 战略配备:
    • 攻击 Super Destroyers may be forced to reposition in order to 目标 the desired 位置, which may take 多个 秒 to 完成.
    • Eagle 攻击 vectors may change 方向 in order to avoid buildings and 目标 战略配备 beacons.
    • Pelicans delivering 外骨骼机甲/FRVs may need to find a nearby 开阔区域 to safely 掉落 their cargo, instead of attempting to leave it directly beside the 战略配备 beacon.
    • However, 战略配备 delivered in Hellpods, such as 支援武器, 哨兵，以及 物资, are largely unaffected.
  • Metropolises 通常 contain less useful 兴趣点 than Colonies, making it more 困难 to obtain field-found ammunition and other 物资.
  • Bunkers can only spawn in lower density city blocks (i.e. parks), but often do so in groups. On the map, they take the 外观 of a 小型 square with the shape of an "L" on it.
    • If specifically hunting for Bunkers, it is best to do so on planets under a 保卫战役, as lower density blocks are much less likely to spawn on 解放战役.
  • During the missions in metropolis, minor 目标 may 偶尔 exhibit anomalies. These anomalous 目标 do not appear during 玩法, even if the 雷达站 侧面 任务 has been completed. They may appear during settling.
    • For 示例, during the settling of a 终结族 任务, nonexistent SEAF SAM Site or the 取回变异幼虫 任务 may appear.
    • Similarly, any SEAF 大炮 目标 that did not spawn on the 前线 may also be included.
