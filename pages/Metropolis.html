概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Metropolises encompass the entirety of the playable 任务 范围 when present, compared to the 更小 Colonies that only occupy a portion of it.

Within a Metropolis, a web of roads 形成 城市 blocks littered with skyscrapers and other 大 structures. These blocks 5月 偶尔 be of a 更低 density, providing 太空 to parks, playgrounds, and 更小 buildings.

The Metropolis can inherit certain changes based on the 行星's 生物群系, such as 颜色 scheme and 天气.

Parks and local 植物群 appear differently based on the 当前 行星's 生物群系. For instance, trees and grass are colored blue on an Ionic 丛林 行星, and grass is replaced entirely with sandy 土壤 on 沙漠 沙丘 worlds.

Environmental Conditions are carried over from the 父母 生物群系 as well, such as 火山 Activity or 极暑.

Platoons of 超级地球武装部队 Soldiers spawn naturally throughout Metropolises.

Metropolises 变化 drastically in 外观 based on whether the 当前 行星 is under a 解放 or 保卫战役:

  • On 防御 campaigns, billboards, 街道 signs, and 轻型 fixtures 功能 properly. Structures remain largely unscathed prior to combat, and buildings outside the 任务 范围 are completely untouched. Roaming mobs of Civilians 5月 spawn throughout the 城市 in addition to 超级地球武装部队 soldiers.
  • On 解放 campaigns, the 亏损 of 电击 plunges cities into darkness. Buildings generate pre-destroyed, including those outside the 任务 范围, and are 经常 overtaken by the incumbent 阵营. 超级地球武装部队 soldiers can still appear, but roaming Civilians will 从不显示 naturally spawn. 更低 density blocks (parks, etc.) are also 更少 likely to generate.
