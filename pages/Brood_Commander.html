虫族指挥官
“

Directing its foul brethren with a mixture of 信息素-like spores and authoritarian shrieks, the bloodthirsty 虫族指挥官 is a rare and deadly strain known for its undeniable hatred of the right to vote.

— 绝地潜兵 2 PlayStation Store Page

The 虫族指挥官 is a lightly 有护甲, large 终结族 that can summon a trio of 武斗虫 to assist it in combat.

生成

Brood 指挥官 can be first found on 简单 as the 任务 目标 of the 消灭虫族指挥官 任务.

Starting on 中型, they can spawn from Bug 突破, within patrols, and guarding 目标 and Bug Nests.

Starting on 挑战, they can be found guarding Minor Places of Interest.

Starting on 不可能, Brood 指挥官 are replaced by their 变体, the 阿尔法指挥官.

行为

Brood 指挥官 will approach 绝地潜兵, climbing over 地形 and 障碍 in an attempt to 攻击 with their claws at 近距离. They can strafe sideways, and will attempt to cut off 绝地潜兵 trying to run past them. Similarly to 武斗虫, when their head is destroyed, Brood 指挥官 will enter a "狂暴" state, during which they gain 已增加 移动速度 but continuously 流血 out. Brood 指挥官 will also 偶尔 summon a trio of 武斗虫.

Brood 指挥官 are able to charge at 绝地潜兵, 通常 when a 绝地潜兵 is 15-20m away from them. The charge will deal 伤害 upon 联络 and can knock down 绝地潜兵, potentially inflicting a concussive 效果 onto them. The 虫族指挥官's charge can be dodged or can be stopped by colliding with 地形 or a larger entity, like a 强袭虫.

Brood 指挥官 are able to release a cloud of 信息素 into the air that causes a 虫族入侵.

战术信息

  • 虫族指挥官即使没有头部也能呼叫虫族入侵。
  • While any weapon is capable of killing a 虫族指挥官, explosives and  中型 护甲-penetrating 武器 are more 克制 them.
  • 摧毁它们的头部可能不如对付战士品系时有效，因为指挥官可以在狂暴状态下存活更长时间，从而有更高几率在死亡前追上并杀死绝地潜兵。
  • Despite their low 护甲, they can absorb large amounts of 弹药 before dying. Due to this, 高伤害 武器库 and explosives are recommended.
    • Their head, claws, and body have slightly thicker 护甲, reducing 伤害 from  轻型 穿透 武器库.
    • Their legs are not 有护甲, making them 易受  轻型 穿透 武器库. Destroying a leg will significantly reduce its movement and charge 速度, being 已降低 further with any additional legs destroyed.
    • 即使少了一条腿，虫族指挥官仍然可以“冲锋”，造成伤害及其效果；尽管看起来并不像是在真正冲锋。
