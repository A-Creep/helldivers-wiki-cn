碎裂坦克 是一种非常大型的 机器人 载具 and 变体 of the Annihilator Tank that has its 主体 armament replaced with a Quad-linked, 重型 Fusion Cycler turret.

生成

Shredder Tanks first appear on 中型 生成几率较低，可能出现在特定的中型到大型 机器人哨站.

当它们开始正常出现，从 挑战，它们可以从机器人空投中生成，出现在巡逻队中，守卫机器人哨站、要塞和 Minor Places of Interest。它们偶尔也会独自在地图上巡逻。

Shredder Tanks share a 生成几率 with 湮灭坦克，但从……开始 自杀任务，它们偶尔都会被……取代 弹幕坦克.

行为

When encountered outside of a 机器人空投, Shredder Tanks can be found still or moving with patrols. When it notices a 绝地潜兵, it will continuously 火焰 its Fusion Cyclers in one of 2 patterns, With one being a brief, 1-second precision burst and another being a longer 2-second burst where it will sweep its turret across the immediate area whilst firing.

Destroying its tracks will render it immobile. Destroying its back engine or turret will disable the Shredder Tank, followed by it exploding. Significant amounts of 伤害 will immediately cause it to 爆炸.

  • The Shredder Tank's turret is deadly, even at 近距离, due to its high 射速, relatively short 换弹, and fast rotation 速度 that can easily track and kill 绝地潜兵.

  • The Shredder Tank's turret 通风口 is 易受  中型 护甲-穿刺 武器

  • The 后部 底盘 of a Tank is also 易受 武器库 such as the APW-1 反器材步枪, AC-8 机炮，以及 R-36 Eruptor.
  • 可以用……摧毁坦克履带使其瘫痪  中型 护甲-穿刺 武器.
