the 霸王虫 is a colossal, worm-like 终结族. Currently, it is the largest and most 耐久 enemy in the game.

生成

A 霸王虫 may spawn during any 任务 rated 自杀任务 or higher 无论 any ongoing 目标 or enemy 增援 on Hive Worlds。 民主官 will 宣布 its presence after it 出现, should a player face it for the first time.

Although the conditions for a 霸王虫 出现中 from the ground are 未知, it seems they are most likely to 回应 to Bug 突破 when they occur. When one happens, it is most probable that the 霸王虫 will show up soon after. If the 霸王虫 "retreats" and stop attacking or appearing, it will retain the 伤害 it has taken if it returns to the 浮出地面.

There is only one 霸王虫 每次任务. Once it has been slain, another will not replace it.

行为

By 默认, Hive Lords will stay 地下, appearing as a 庞大 wave of trembling earth that kicks up clouds of dust and slowly approaches 绝地潜兵. Hive Lords will often follow a specific 绝地潜兵 until they are within 射程 of an 攻击. When 大约 100 meters from a 目标, it will emerge from the ground before performing an 攻击. Hive Lords can 攻击 多个 times before re-钻地中.

If it takes enough 伤害, the 霸王虫 will attempt to 撤退, sinking back into the ground. It randomly opts to forgo 攻击 to roar instead.

攻击

  • 浮出地面: The 霸王虫's emergence inflicts a slowing 效果 within its vicinity due to the 地震 it creates, launches any entities if they are too close, and boulders into the air that deal 伤害 upon impact. 绝地潜兵 standing too close to the 出现中 霸王虫 are likely to be launched high up into the air.
  • Burrow: Partially 出现中, the 霸王虫 will 继续 钻地中 forwards instead of fully surfacing upright, This move can still injure and knock away 绝地潜兵 if they are caught in the motion.
  • Body Slam: The 霸王虫 will throw itself forwards, attempting to crush its 目标 under its body. It will remain in a lying position for a moment afterwards before 钻地中 back into the ground. This 攻击 can 摧毁 or clip through 地形.
    • A variation of this 攻击 can be performed by the 霸王虫 by immediately slamming down upon 出现中, and momentarily afterward 钻地中 back. When it slams it lands on it's back, allowing for a brief moment of exposure to 火焰.
  • Bile Rain: The 霸王虫 will lean forwards, before shaking, shuddering, and releasing an 巨大 数量 of bile in three sweeps. The 范围效果 this 攻击 creates covers a wide area and slows 绝地潜兵, but there are gaps between each sweep that allows a careful 绝地潜兵 to survive.
    • There is a variation of this 攻击 where the 霸王虫 will do one large stream of bile straight in a line, a bit easier to avoid but higher potential for large 伤害 on uncareful 绝地潜兵, mechs or 炮台. It has a longer 射程 than its counterpart.

Upon taking 致命伤害, the 霸王虫 will 浮出地面 if previously burrowed, spew large amounts of 终结族 blood before collapsing to the ground in spectacular fashion.

After successfully killing a 霸王虫, 绝地潜兵 are rewarded with a completed 目标 and 222 经验值.

战术信息

  • the 霸王虫 has the largest 主生命值 pool in the game by a significant margin, at 150,000. But all body parts are 0% 耐久 so it takes full 标准伤害 from all sources.
    • Due to its 极其 large 生命池 and wide sweeping 致命 攻击, it is advised that the entire squad either avoids the 霸王虫 entirely, or 坐标 to take it down.
  • the 霸王虫's body is completely encased in  Tank I 护甲, with the exception being  Tank II 护甲 on the head and it's  轻型 有护甲 open mouth.
    • Its 身体护甲 can be broken with sustained 反坦克 火焰, revealing the  轻型-有护甲 flesh beneath. When a plate of 护甲 is close to being destroyed, it will visibly show cracks and breaks.
      • The 内圈 flesh is completely immune to 爆炸伤害, so 爆炸武器 like the MS-11 Solo Silo should be avoided.
    • Most of the toughness of the 霸王虫's plate 护甲 comes from its 庞大, non-decaying bleedout pool. It 作为额外 生命值, for a total of 50,000 有效 生命值 per 章节.
  • 绝地潜兵 who wish to 击倒 a 霸王虫 should consider bringing as much  反坦克 I firepower as possible in the form of 战略配备 和 支援武器.
    • “飞鹰”机枪扫射 和 轨道加特林火力网 are both highly 克制 the 霸王虫, as they can 伤害 多个 spinal 护甲 plates due to the 霸王虫's 庞大 size.
    • the GR-8 无后坐力炮, FAF-14 Spear，以及 EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器 allow for focusing on breaking 护甲 plates between 轨道武器 and Eagle cooldowns.
    • the EXO-45 爱国者 外骨骼机甲 is one of the most 有效 tools against a 霸王虫. Its 导弹 arm can help strip an 护甲 plate with rockets, dealing up to a total of 17,500 伤害 to an 护甲 plate, allowing it's 加特林 arm to quickly inflict 庞大 伤害 upon the stripped 章节. With a full 弹匣, the chaingun can deal up to 121,500 伤害 in this manner.
      • However, due to their 有限 mobility and large frame, 外骨骼机甲 are 易受 the 霸王虫's large 攻击.
    • the Bastion is likewise 克制 a 霸王虫 due to its high HP pool allowing it to withstand 多个 bile 攻击 and its large cannon and HMG 弹药 reserves can 摧毁 a plate and deal enough 伤害 to 干掉 the 霸王虫 with ease. However, the driver must maneuver the tank away from 钻地中 Hive Lords to prevent the 霸王虫 from flipping the tank.
    • the B-100 Portable Hellbomb can inflict an 极难 amount of 伤害 if 绝地潜兵 can position it correctly. best time to use it是通常 when the 霸王虫 attempts to spew bile, as this 攻击 does not hit at point-blank 射程 and keeps it in place long enough for the Hellbomb's 倒计时 to finish.
  • Once the 护甲 is broken, 高每秒伤害 武器 can be used to shred its 主生命值 pool by firing into the 暴露  轻型 有护甲 flesh. 武器 such as the AR-61 Tenderizer, M-105 盟友，以及 M-1000 Maxigun perform well at this.
    • the LIFT-850 喷射背包 or LIFT-182 Warp Pack can be used to avoid the 霸王虫's 攻击. 轻型护甲 and the 试验性注射剂 增益 further 增加 移动速度 on foot, and combining these options together can 改进 odds of survival.
  • the 霸王虫's movements will cause 地震 in the ground that slow all nearby units. Close proximity should be avoided when considering the presence of other 终结族
    • the 霸王虫 is 免疫 to all 效果 that would 眩晕弹 or 困惑 it, whilst still being prone to 正面 毒气伤害 and ignition by 火焰.
  • When engaging or withdrawing from the 霸王虫, 绝地潜兵 should 维护 距离 from one another. The size of the 霸王虫's 攻击 makes it 危险 for them to remain close to each other, as one strike could potentially kill all of them.
  • Most of the 造成伤害 by the bile of a 霸王虫 is 爆炸, so bringing 护甲 with the 加固 被动 can help save 绝地潜兵 from these 攻击 or even make their 伤害 negligible.

  • the 霸王虫 has its own 章节 of the 虫窝世界 theme that plays when it is engaged in combat, overriding all other sections of the theme until it retreats or dies.
  • Despite being 机密 as "庞大" in the game's enemy data, the 霸王虫 is referred to as a Titan-级别 终结族 in-宇宙, which also refers to to other 重型 终结族 敌人 like the 强袭虫, 吐酸泰坦，以及 穿刺虫.[1]
