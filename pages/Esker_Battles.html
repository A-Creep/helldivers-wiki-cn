First Battle of Esker - The First Battle of Falstaff 分区

Casualties Around 660k 绝地潜兵 K.I.A.

Summary Esker captured by 终结族, Falstaff 分区 was invaded by 终结族 for the first time during the 第二次银河战争.


Background

The 绝地潜兵 pushed deeper into 终结族 领土 as the 主要命令 ordered them to liberate all of Draco 分区 and half of Mirin 分区.

The 终结族 apparently tried to escape their inevitable fate by developing a survival adaptation. This adaptation caused the 终结族 to emit much more spores than before.

策略 and importance

Esker, simmiliarly to Acamar IV (that is the only 行星 that connects to Jin Xi 分区) is the only 行星 that allows access to Falstaff 分区. But, it wasn't as 重要 now as 绝地潜兵 were more focused on completing the 重要指令.

战斗

The battle went on for 24 hours, with the 终结族 completely outnumbering the SEAF forces. The 绝地潜兵 never stood a chance against hordes of 终结族 desperately trying to escape from their destroyed by The 绝地潜兵 worlds.

Summary


Background

The 绝地潜兵 fought 2 防御型 campaigns on Bore Rock. All the planets in Jin Xi 分区 that 绝地潜兵 were tasked with liberating, were already recaptured. It was the time to strike back in the Falstaff 分区.

策略 and importance

Esker was the only 行星 that connected to Falstaff 分区 and Erson Sands, capturing it would allow 绝地潜兵 to 攻击 Erson Sands.

战斗

The battle began on June 30th, when the most 绝地潜兵 in the 历史 of the 行星 gathered on Esker.

The Battle is still ongoing!
