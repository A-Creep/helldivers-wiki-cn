琐事[edit | edit 来源]

This 战争债券 is mostly themed after the 卡舍津 featuring some of their their armors and 武器

  • The name of the 战争债券 is a 参考 to the 标题 of Lord Castellan of Cadia, as well as two persons known to bear it:
    • Ursarkar E. Creed, who was the Lord 基本信息 of the Astra Militarum and Lord Castellan of Cadia during the Thirteenth Black Crusade
    • Ursula J. Creed, the 女儿 of Ursarkar E. Creed, and the 当前 Lord Castellan of the surviving Cadian 电击 Troopers
  • The player 标题, "Still Standing", is a 参考 to "Cadia Stands". The phrase came to be during a speech by Ursarkar E. Creed, where his mic cut off and all the gathered Cadians heard was "Cadia Stands"[1]. After the fall of Cadia this phrase became a common battle cry/cheer used by surviving Cadian Guardsman, and a way to honour their 母星.
