战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • Warp Ships come in three variations: Grounded, Airborne, and Landing.
    • 停靠的跃迁舰, which act as enemy spawners, have their own dedicated page.
    • Airborne Warp Ships 功能 similarly to 机器人 运输舰, flying in once 增援 have been called in by a 守望者, and projecting a tractor beam from their 圆形 underside that directly teleports 光能者 forces to the ground.
    • Landing Warp Ships are similar to Airborne Warp Ships, but are invincible until they land, at which point they reveal a door weak-spot similar to Grounded Warp Ship. They are only found in Repel 入侵 Fleet.
  • 增援 Warp Ships are significantly weaker, and can be neutralized in manners similar to Dropships.
    • One rocket from the FAF-14 Spear or GR-8 无后坐力炮 will immediately 摧毁 an unshielded airborne Warp Ship. (The latter is 有限 to direct hits to the fuselage)
    • Warp Ships have 护甲 on the sides which can be stripped away after sustaining enough 伤害. Hitting the sides of an airborne Ship is highly ill-advised.
    • An airborne Warp Ship's 护盾 are identical to its grounded counterpart. They must be destroyed before the main body can be damaged.
  • 激活 SEAF SAM Site is one possible method of destroying a 穿梭舰.
    • Prior to the 1.002.200 更新, SEAF SAM Sites were capable of destroying a Warp Ship with a single 导弹 hit, regardless of the impact 位置. After this 更新, Warp Ships in flight were granted protective 护盾 similar to those deployed during landing, which prevents most single-shot kills. As a result, SAM Sites 通常 要求 one 导弹 to disable the shield and another to inflict 致命伤害.
    • In the 当前 version, a single-hit 摧毁 of a Warp Ship is still technically possible under specific conditions:
      • The 导弹 must impact the shield 章节 that spatially overlaps with the 反重力 ring (The ring identified in the 结构解析 table as the Fuselage).
      • The direct hit 伤害 will first deplete the 光能者 shield. Since the Warp Ship does not 经验值 a stun 效果 after shield break, it continues advancing.
      • The 导弹’s 爆炸 point then resolves inside the model of the Warp Ship. Because the 反重力 ring possesses an ExDR of 100%, only internal explosions are 有效. The overlap results in the 爆炸 damaging the 反重力 ring, instantly destroying the Warp Ship.
    • Note: When SAM Sites are positioned too far away to reliably hit the 反重力 ring, it is recommended that 绝地潜兵 employ handheld 反坦克武器. These can capitalize on the brief 弱点 window created after the SAM breaks the Warp Ship’s shield to 击倒 Warp Ships.
