Disclaimer: 每个带数量的高亮部位 a 数量有各自的 生命值. 未标注数量的部位共享 生命值 pools.

1) 生命值标注为"主体"的部位没有各自的独立生命值。对这些部位造成的所有伤害都会转移到敌人的主生命值池。
2) 护甲值与敌人耐久度请参阅伤害 以获取更多信息。
3) 对部位造成的伤害也会部分或全部转移到主生命值。若设置为 0%，则不会转移任何伤害。
4) 若设置为"是"，转移到主生命值的伤害将以部位的生命值与构造之和为上限。
5) 构造充当次级生命池，一旦主体或肢体的生命值耗尽，可能会开始衰减。第一个数字是构造总量，第二个是衰减速率。若设置为 0/秒，则不会衰减，构造将作为额外生命值。
6) 爆炸抗性指爆炸伤害抗性，范围可为 0 至 100%。若设置为 100%，肢体无法承受爆炸伤害，爆炸伤害将转而攻击主体。请参阅伤害以获取更多信息。
  • 注：此机制称为\"受爆炸影响\"，每次爆炸只能触发一次，无论命中多少个 100% 爆炸抗性的部位；且爆炸的护甲穿透必须达到或超过主体护甲才能对主血量造成伤害。但如果同一次爆炸已经伤害过主体，此机制将不会触发。


战术信息

  • 穿梭舰有三种变体：地面、空中和降落。
    • Grounded Warp Ships, which act as enemy spawners, have their own dedicated page.
    • Airborne Warp Ships 功能 similarly to 机器人 Dropships, flying in once 增援 have been called in by a 守望者, and projecting a tractor beam from their 圆形 underside that directly teleports 光能者 forces to the ground.
    • Landing Warp Ships are similar to Airborne Warp Ships, but are invincible until they land, at which point they reveal a door weak-spot similar to Grounded Warp Ship. They are only found in 击退入侵舰队.
  • 增援穿梭舰明显更弱，可以用类似运输舰的方式予以清除。
    • One rocket from the FAF-14 Spear or GR-8 无后坐力炮 will immediately 摧毁 an unshielded airborne Warp Ship. (latter是有限 to 正面 hits to the fuselage)
    • Warp Ships have 护甲 on the sides which can be stripped away after sustaining enough 伤害. Hitting the sides of an airborne Ship is highly ill-advised.
    • An airborne Warp Ship's 护盾 are identical to its grounded counterpart. They must be destroyed before the 主体 body can be damaged.
  • Activating a SEAF SAM Site is one possible method of destroying a Warp Ship.
    • Prior to the 1.002.200 更新, SEAF SAM Sites were capable of destroying a Warp Ship with a single 导弹 hit, 无论 the impact 位置. After this 更新, Warp Ships in flight were granted protective 护盾 similar to those deployed during landing, which prevents most single-shot kills. As a result, SAM Sites 通常 要求 one 导弹 to disable the shield and another to inflict 致命伤害.
    • In the 当前 version, a single-hit 摧毁 of a Warp Ship is still technically possible under specific conditions:
      • The 导弹 must impact the shield 章节 that spatially overlaps with the 反重力 ring (The ring identified in the 结构解析 table as the Fuselage).
      • The 正面 hit 伤害 will first deplete the 光能者 shield. Since the Warp Ship does not 经验值 a stun 效果 after shield break, it continues advancing.
      • The 导弹’s 爆炸 point then resolves inside the model of the Warp Ship. Because the 反重力 ring possesses an 爆炸抗性 of 100%, only internal explosions are 有效. The overlap results in the 爆炸 damaging the 反重力 ring, instantly destroying the Warp Ship.
    • Note: When SAM Sites are positioned too far away to reliably hit the 反重力 ring, it is recommended that 绝地潜兵 employ handheld 反坦克武器. These can capitalize on the brief 弱点 window created after the SAM breaks the Warp Ship’s shield to 击倒 Warp Ships.
