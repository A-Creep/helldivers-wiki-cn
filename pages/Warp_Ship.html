战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Warp Ships come in three variations: Grounded, Airborne, and Landing.
    • 停靠的跃迁舰, which 法案 as 敌人 spawners, have their own dedicated 页面.
    • Airborne Warp Ships 功能 similarly to 机器人 运输舰, 飞行 in 一次 增援 have been called in by a 守望者, and projecting a tractor 光束 from their 圆形 underside that directly teleports 光能者 forces to the 地面.
    • Landing Warp Ships are similar to Airborne Warp Ships, but are invincible until they 陆地, at which point they reveal a 门 弱-spot similar to Grounded Warp 舰船. They are only found in Repel 入侵 Fleet.
  • 增援 Warp Ships are significantly 更弱, and can be neutralized in manners similar to Dropships.
    • One 火箭 from the FAF-14 飞矛 or GR-8 无后坐力炮 will immediately 摧毁 an unshielded airborne Warp 舰船. (The latter is 有限 to 指挥 hits to the fuselage)
    • Warp Ships have 护甲 on the sides which can be stripped away after sustaining enough 伤害. Hitting the sides of an airborne 舰船 is highly ill-advised.
    • An airborne Warp 舰船's 护盾 are identical to its grounded counterpart. They must be destroyed before the main 身体 can be damaged.
  • 激活 超级地球武装部队 SAM Site is one possible method of destroying a 穿梭舰.
    • Prior to the 1.002.200 更新, 超级地球武装部队 SAM Sites were capable of destroying a Warp 舰船 with a 单个 导弹 hit, regardless of the 冲击 位置. After this 更新, Warp Ships in flight were granted protective 护盾 similar to those deployed during landing, which prevents 最多 单个-射击 kills. As a 结果, SAM Sites 通常 要求 one 导弹 to disable the 之盾 and another to inflict 致命伤害.
    • In the 当前 version, a 单个-hit 摧毁 of a Warp 舰船 is still technically possible under specific conditions:
      • The 导弹 must 冲击 the 之盾 章节 that spatially overlaps with the 反重力 ring (The ring identified in the 结构解析 表格 as the Fuselage).
      • The 指挥 hit 伤害 will first deplete the 光能者 之盾. Since the Warp 舰船 does not 经验值 a stun 效果 after 之盾 打破, it continues advancing.
      • The 导弹’s 爆炸 point then resolves inside the 型号 of the Warp 舰船. Because the 反重力 ring possesses an ExDR of 100%, only internal explosions are 有效. The overlap results in the 爆炸 damaging the 反重力 ring, instantly destroying the Warp 舰船.
    • Note: When SAM Sites are positioned too far away to reliably hit the 反重力 ring, it is recommended that 绝地潜兵 employ handheld 反坦克武器. These can capitalize on the brief 弱点 window created after the SAM breaks the Warp 舰船’s 之盾 to 击倒 Warp Ships.
