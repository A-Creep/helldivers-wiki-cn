Blitz: 摧毁 光能者 Warp Ships 是一个 光能者 任务 in 绝地潜兵2. To 完成 it 绝地潜兵 must 摧毁 a specific amount of grounded 光能者 Warp Ships which 变更 with 难度.

目标 Steps

  • Find and 摧毁 any active Warp Ships
    • A random number of 光能者 Warp Ships must be destroyed.
  • Reward: Before multipliers, between 375 and 1250 申购点 Slips, and between 75 and 250 经验值

战术信息

  • Warp Ships have a protective shield around them, you can break the shield for a 小型 window of time to throw an 爆炸 into the open door
  • Warp Ships may have 闪电尖塔 stationed around them; to 摧毁 these towers it is recommended to bring an 爆炸 Weapon such as the GP-31 榴弹手枪 or CB-9 Exploding Crossbow
    • The 闪电 Spires and the conduit-torch towers are considered as structures which 要求 爆破力 for the 摧毁. This means 武器 like PLAS-1 Scorcher won't be able to 摧毁 these towers.
  • All 爆炸武器 can one-shot grounded warp ships from the door if the shield has been taken down.
  • An 轨道武器 毒气 Strike, 轨道激光炮, 轨道精准攻击, 轨道炮攻击, Eagle 500kg Bomb, a hit or 爆炸 from a 120mm/380mm/Walking 弹幕 shot or SEAF 大炮 will 摧毁 the Warp Ship even if its 护盾 are activated.

琐事

  • This 任务 is normally only available starting on 中型 难度, however in 特殊 Avert Void operations only available on planets with Exostorms present, both 简单 和 容易 难度 missions can be found.
