    终结族遏制体系+
    “

    With the arrival of Freedom to this newfound world, the bugs have been driven into a fascist frenzy. Their 破坏性 urge is denying our citizens the prosperous future they were always meant to inherit on this soil.

    A new 世代 of TCS+ towers stands ready to neutralize this 威胁 and ensure total 行星 safety through controlled fumigation. See that they are activated and fully operational.

    — 操作 描述 of a 启动TCS+ 操作

    the 终结族控制系统+ (also known as the TCS+) is a  超级地球 联邦 galactic 位置. It is the second iteration of the 终结族控制系统, which was designed to kill 终结族 and prevent them from living on 特定星球.

    背景故事

    “

    该地点的TCS+站点装载了精炼的终结杀虫剂2.0，现在没有可信的致突变副作用风险。启动后，它将用对终结族高度致命但对人类完全无害的烟雾对该区域进行灭菌。

    启动TCS+站点，让整片区域沐浴在净化之雾般的Termicide中。

    — 任务 简报 for the Activate TCS+ 车站 任务

    Following the failure of the original 终结族控制系统 in early 2184, the Ministry of Science began development of a new Termicide formula named "Termicide 2.0."

    In mid 2186, the formula for Termicide 2.0 was completed as a new 终结族控制系统, now named TCS+, began to be installed across planets bordering the 终结族 Gloom 正面. These new towers are meant to dispense the new Termicide 2.0, which is meant to be more 有效 at preventing further 终结族 advances into 超级地球 领土.

    行动：天穹围栏

    On June 30th, 2186, 行动：天穹围栏 commenced, with the 绝地潜兵 tasked with defending 多种星球 in order to 保护 the new sites, allowing for the 庞大 towers to be constructed and be fully operational. The 绝地潜兵 also began activating individual TCS+ stations on Brilliance following its 入侵.

    While the 材质 gathering phase of 操作: Celestial Fence failed, enough materials would be gathered to construct 50% of the planned Termicide 2.0-dispersing wall. New TCS+ Cells finalized construction on planets Terrek 和 Erata Prime. Shortly after construction finished, Terrek and Erata Prime were invaded by the 终结族 as the 绝地潜兵 began activating TCS+ stations.

    With the successful conclusion of 操作: Celestial Fence, new TCS+ arrays were constructed on planets Crimsica 和 Fori Prime. The 上一个 TCS+ arrays on Brilliance, Terrek, and Erata Prime were also fully activated at the same time the new arrays were revealed.

    行动：永久围歼

    On July 28th, 2186, 行动：永久围歼 commenced, which began the construction of the second half of the "TCS wall," 强化 by the newly constructed TCS+ Arrays. The 绝地潜兵 were tasked with securing the initial construction and deployment on Fronteria, Turing，以及 Achird III.

    Shortly after the 操作 began, SE High 指挥 informed the 绝地潜兵 that a cyberattack was taking place, spreading misinformation about the effectiveness of the Termicide 2.0 formula. These lab reports, which SE claims to be maliciously altered by the   Cyborg Legion, claim that the new formula is ineffective. While the report is likely dubious, SE did claim that a recent 爆发 on a 保卫终结族遏制体系+星球 was caused by dissidents, which was privately noticed by a couple of recently deceased 绝地潜兵.

    On August 5th 2186 while the 绝地潜兵 worked to liberate Meissa from the Cyborg Legion, the TCS+ array on Erata Prime was sabotaged by dissidents which SE once again claimed were mind-controlled by the 机器人's 宣传机器. Strategic Opportunity A3-8-2 was dispatched, ordering the 绝地潜兵 to liberate Erata Prime after dissidents allowed the 行星 to fall to the   Spore Burst Strain, and to do so before the 终结族 could 加强 their hold and pierce the wall. Though with the 绝地潜兵 liberating Meissa, the Strategic Opportunity failed and the 终结族 launched a successful 特攻 on Hellmire. Erata Prime was liberated a few hours later as SEAF troops began to siege Hellmire.

    On August 8th, 2186, constructions of the 剩余 TCS+ arrays begun on Darius II, Achernar Secundus, Grand Errant，以及 Pherkad Secundus. The 绝地潜兵 were ordered to defend these planets against anticipated fierce 终结族 assaults until the constructions were done. During the 下一个 few days, the TCS+ arrays on planets Fori Prime 和 Crimsica were sabotaged as the 终结族 began occupying those planets.

    On August 11th, 2186, 超级地球 High 指挥 已公布 the successful completion of this 操作. Whilst they touted the TCS+ system as an 'unbroken line of planets', providing 'total, permanent 安全 from the 威胁 of 终结族 入侵', the system had one unaddressed 弱点 - it had a gap in it. Heeth, located in-between Brilliance 和 Fronteria, was not included in the TCS+ system. This may allow the 终结族 威胁 to bypass the TCS+ wall, starting at Omicron, via the similarly-undefended planets of Oshaune, Hellmire, Fenrir III, Heeth itself, and then taking Cirrus; at which point they would have definitively breached the wall.

    玩法

    If a 行星 harboring the 终结族控制系统+ is invaded, it will host exclusive 绝地潜兵 Operations which will always contain one Activate TCS+ 车站 主体 任务 during its 防御.

    If a 行星 has an active 解放 campaign, however, normal Peacekeeping Operations will be present on the 行星, and 否 Activate TCS+ 车站 主体 missions will be available for the 解放.
