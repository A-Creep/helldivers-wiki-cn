背景故事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

“

A TCS+ 车站 at this site is loaded with refined Termicide 2.0, now with 否 credible 风险 of mutagenic 侧面 效果. 一次 initiated, it will sterilize the 范围 with fumes highly 致命 to 终结族, yet entirely 安全 to humans.

Activate the TCS+ 车站, and bathe the region in a purifying 霾 of Termicide.

Following the failure of the original 终结族控制系统 in early 2184, the Ministry of Science began development of a new Termicide formula named "Termicide 2.0."

In mid 2186, the formula for Termicide 2.0 was completed as a new 终结族控制系统, now named TCS+, began to be installed across planets bordering the 终结族 Gloom 正面. These new towers are meant to dispense the new Termicide 2.0, which is meant to be 更多 有效 at preventing further 终结族 advances into 超级地球 领土.

操作: Celestial Fence[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

On 6月 30th, 2186, 操作: Celestial Fence commenced, with the 绝地潜兵 tasked with defending 多种星球 in 命令 to 保护 the new sites, allowing for the 庞大 towers to be constructed and be fully operational. The 绝地潜兵 also began activating individual TCS+ stations on Brilliance following its 入侵.

While the 材质 gathering phase of 操作: Celestial Fence failed, enough materials would be gathered to construct 50% of the planned Termicide 2.0-dispersing 墙. New TCS+ Cells finalized construction on planets Terrek and Erata Prime. Shortly after construction finished, Terrek and Erata Prime were invaded by the 终结族 as the 绝地潜兵 began activating TCS+ stations.

With the successful conclusion of 操作: Celestial Fence, new TCS+ arrays were constructed on planets Crimsica and Fori Prime. The 上一个 TCS+ arrays on Brilliance, Terrek, and Erata Prime were also fully activated at the same 时间 the new arrays were revealed.

操作: Permanent Enclosure[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

On 7月 28th, 2186, 操作: Permanent Enclosure commenced, which began the construction of the second half of the "TCS 墙," 加固 by the newly constructed TCS+ Arrays. The 绝地潜兵 were tasked with securing the initial construction and deployment on Fronteria, Turing, and Achird III.

Shortly after the 操作 began, SE 高 指挥 informed the 绝地潜兵 that a cyberattack was taking place, spreading misinformation 关于 the effectiveness of the Termicide 2.0 formula. These lab reports, which SE claims to be maliciously altered by the   生化人 Legion, 声称 that the new formula is ineffective. While the 报道 is likely dubious, SE did 声称 that a recent 爆发 on a 安全 TCS+ 行星 was caused by dissidents, which was privately noticed by a couple of recently deceased 绝地潜兵.

On 8月 5th 2186 while the 绝地潜兵 worked to liberate Meissa from the 生化人 Legion, the TCS+ array on Erata Prime was sabotaged by dissidents which SE 一次 再次 claimed were 心智-controlled by the 机器人's 宣传 Machine. Strategic Opportunity A3-8-2 was dispatched, ordering the 绝地潜兵 to liberate Erata Prime after dissidents allowed the 行星 to fall to the  , and to do so before the 终结族 could 加强 their 按住 and pierce the 墙. Though with the 绝地潜兵 liberating Meissa, the Strategic Opportunity failed and the 终结族 launched a successful 特攻 on Hellmire. Erata Prime was liberated a 少数 hours later as 超级地球武装部队 军队 began to 围城 Hellmire.

On 8月 8th, 2186, constructions of the 剩余 TCS+ arrays begun on Darius II, Achernar Secundus, Grand Errant, and Pherkad Secundus. The 绝地潜兵 were ordered to 防御 these planets against anticipated fierce 终结族 assaults until the constructions were done. During the 下一个 少数 days, the TCS+ arrays on planets Fori Prime and Crimsica were sabotaged as the 终结族 began occupying those planets.

On 8月 11th, 2186, 超级地球 高 指挥 announced the successful completion of this 操作. Whilst they touted the TCS+ 星系 as an 'unbroken line of planets', providing '全射, permanent 安全 from the 威胁 of 终结族 入侵', the 星系 had one unaddressed 弱点 - it had a gap in it. Heeth, located in-between Brilliance and Fronteria, was not included in the TCS+ 星系. This 5月 允许 the 终结族 威胁 to bypass the TCS+ 墙, starting at Omicron, via the similarly-undefended planets of Oshaune, Hellmire, Fenrir III, Heeth itself, and then taking Cirrus; at which point they would have definitively breached the 墙.

玩法[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

If a 行星 harboring the 终结族控制系统+ is invaded, it will host exclusive 绝地潜兵 Operations which will 总是显示 contain one Activate TCS+ 车站 main 任务 during its 防御.

If a 行星 has an active 解放 campaign, however, normal Peacekeeping Operations will be present on the 行星, and 否 Activate TCS+ 车站 main missions will be available for the 解放.
