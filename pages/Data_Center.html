背景故事[edit | edit 来源]

The 机器人 hold large amounts of encrypted information in Data Centers. Thanks to this, the 行星 is heavily 加固 as 抗性 rises.

On 1月 22nd 2186, 超级地球 sent 绝地潜兵 to conduct 突击兵 Operations to assist the Cyberassault teams in completing a covert file 传输 to the 机器人 数据库. The 任务 was a success.

Although Clasa would be liberated on 1月 30th 2186, the Data Center would regain 操作 following the successful 入侵 of the 行星 on April 24th 2186.
