  • 暴风雪
  • 极寒
  • 烈焰龙卷

  • 离子风暴
  • 流星风暴
  • 暴雨
  • 沙尘暴
  • 浓雾
  • 地震
  • 火山活动
  • 重度“阴霾”笼罩
  • 中等 Gloom Blanket: The Gloom covers this 行星 completely, obstructing a variety of logistical operations. 撤离 times are 已增加 by 30 秒 and 飞鹰 Re-arm 时间 is 已增加 by 20%.
  • 轻型 Gloom 掩护: Gloom particles interfere with landing Pelicans, increasing 撤离 times by 20 秒.
  • “阴霾”源头: This 行星 continually produces a 厚 atmospheric Gloom 掩护, rendering logistical 支援 极其 挑战. 全射 增援 are 已降低 by 20%, 飞鹰 Re-arm 时间 is 已增加 by 30%, and 弹药 from pickups is 已降低 by 50%.

Operational 效果[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

有时, during certain events or 主要命令, 一些, if not 所有星球 5月 获得 an extra 效果/修正, available on 全部 difficulties.

  • 轨道武器 Fluctuations: 所有 轨道武器 战略配备 have an 已增加 冷却. (not to be confused with the 操作 修正 with the same 名称, this 效果 only affects 轨道武器 战略配备)
  • Medical 供给 Strain: 数字 of stims 每个 绝地潜兵 deploys with 已减少 by 1.
  • 驾驶员 Shortage: 撤离 takes 30 additional 秒.
  • 更慢 重新武装“飞鹰”: 鹰式重新装弹 时间 is 25% 更慢.

行动限制条件[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

操作 Modifiers are 已添加 障碍 that 绝地潜兵 must 处理 on the battlefield. A 单个 修正 will appear on 困难, increasing to two at 不可能. 操作 Modifiers do not appear on planets undergoing a 防御 Campaign.

  • 复杂 战略配备 Plotting – 战略配备 呼叫时间 已增加 by 50%.

Other 效果[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

护甲被动[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

护甲 Passives are unique, 玩家-specific abilities or enhancements that are tied to their corresponding 身体 armors. Depending on the 被动技能, different 属性(s) will be affected, altering the way 绝地潜兵 can operate in the 任务.

增益[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

每个 绝地潜兵 has the option to equip a 增益 before deployment, which 施加 增强 to the entire 小队. Depending on the 强化资源, it can 改变 a 绝地潜兵's 属性, certain pieces of 装备, or 任务 机制.

物理 Conditions[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

物理 Conditions refer to adverse 效果 that a 绝地潜兵 5月 经验值 on the battlefield. These conditions can 射程 from minor debuffs to being 致命 if left untreated. Depending on the 条件, it can be self-inflicted, caused by the 环境, or applied by an 敌人 攻击.
