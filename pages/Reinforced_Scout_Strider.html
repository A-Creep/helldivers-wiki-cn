Reinforced Scout Striders are first found on 不可能, replacing Scout Striders. Reinforced Scout Striders can spawn from 机器人空投, within patrols, and guarding 机器人 哨站, 目标，以及 Minor Places of Interest.

Reinforced Scout Striders will quickly approach 绝地潜兵 once alerted to their presence. It will 火焰 its 重型 fusion repeater three times before 冷却 down for four 秒. It will also 偶尔 火焰 one of its four rockets, 通常 after being alerted to a 绝地潜兵's presence; before firing a rocket, it will briefly remain still, with the prepared rocket releasing exhaust before it is fired.

the Reinforced 侦察纵步者 can stomp on a 绝地潜兵 if they get too close.

战术信息

  • Without its pilot as a weakpoint, the Reinforced 侦察纵步者 is harder to kill than its open-backed counterpart. However, it is still very 易受 攻击 on its  轻型 有护甲 rockets, which will instantly kill the Strider upon 引爆. (This is currently bugged and won't kill the strider, but will greatly weaken the legs).
    • These rockets have both the lowest amount of 生命值 of any 致命 body part as well as being 0% 耐用. This means that the rockets should be the primary 目标 for the majority of 武器 when facing this 机器人.
  • The waist should also be considered a 目标 for 绝地潜兵 possessing  中型 护甲 penetrating 武器库 due to its large size, 中等 生命值, and lack of 耐久度. This is a good option for 绝地潜兵 equipped with 主武器 such as the R-63CS Diligence 反制 Sniper or BR-14 审判者 that can 干掉 the waist quickly without having to aim at the 更小 rockets.
  • Even with all of the rockets expended,  轻型 penetrating 武器库 can still 应对 the Reinforced 侦察纵步者, although slower than 武器 with more penetrative power. This is due to the  轻型 有护甲 legs having more 生命值 than the waist while possessing 75% 耐久度.
  • Offensively, the Reinforced Strider is a much larger 威胁 than its regular counterpart due to the 侧面 rockets. These will instantly kill any diver hit by one, with the sole exception of 重型 强化 护甲 unless the 绝地潜兵 is prone. Near misses are almost as deadly due to the 9-meter 冲击波 carrying a 推力 of 100. This will send any 绝地潜兵 flying off at high speeds (even with the Rock Solid 护甲 被动) to slam into 地形 for often 致命 伤害.
    • 绝地潜兵 bringing 载具 should take extra care of the rockets due to their 极其 high 耐久伤害 making them do 高伤害 to these 载具.
  • The rocket launches are telegraphed by the strider standing still and the 燃烧 of the end of the rocket being launched. These rockets aren't very fast and can be easily avoided when fired from long distances away. At closer ranges, a 绝地潜兵 should dive out of the way as soon as they see the rocket launch. The additional 爆炸抗性 of going prone will be the only chance of survival.
  • Given that its 装甲兵 pilot is sealed inside, there is 否 risk of being ambushed after destroying the Strider.
