机制[edit | edit 来源]

The Democracy Space 车站's menu can be accessed from the 银河地图 interface; from there 绝地潜兵 can see a summary of the DSS, donate resources to activate powerful Tactical Actions and also 投票 on its 下一个 destination.

Moving And Voting[edit | edit 来源]

The movement of the DSS is dictated by a majority 投票. Every 4 hours, the 绝地潜兵 are given a list of possible planets to vote on. Once the 4-hour period is over the DSS immediately warps to the winning 行星's orbit and a new 选举 begins.

  • If the 当前 campaign ends and there is no longer an active campaign on the orbited 行星, the DSS will immediately move to orbit over the 下一个 行星 with majority of votes. In case of majority vote being "Stay", the DSS will immediately move to the second most voted 行星.
    • In these scenarios the 4-hour-timer however does not reset although 绝地潜兵 are given another vote-ticket to use.
    • In the case of a failed 防御 Campaign the DSS will not immediately move if the subsequent 解放 Campaign is accessible.

The list of votable planets is updated / refreshed during each movement of the DSS.

Tactical Actions[edit | edit 来源]

绝地潜兵 can donate 申购点, as well as 普通样本 and 稀有样本 in order to charge and activate Tactical Actions. Once fully charged, actions will immediately activate and provide its benefits to all operations occurring on the 星球 the DSS is orbiting. Each 绝地潜兵 can donate resources up to a cap (分离 for each 资源) within a 24-hour cycle; this period begins after the first 捐赠 since the end of the last participated cycle for that specific 资源.

The caps for each 24-hour 捐赠 cycle are:

  • 常见样本
  • 申购点
  • 稀有样本


Only one 战术行动 can be active at a time. If an action reaches its full charge, it will freeze progress for all other actions until the 当前 one is finished. After a 战术行动 concludes its 24-hour active period, it will enter 冷却 and be ineligible for donations.

  • 偶尔, Tactical Actions may not be active for the hosting 绝地潜兵 during their first 任务/玩法 session after booting up the game; this can be double-checked under the "效果"-信息-panel. The aforementioned situation can be rectified by either participating in an ongoing 任务 or completing a quick and 容易/简单 任务 first before hosting missions during active Tactical Actions.

背景故事[edit | edit 来源]

Retrieval[edit | edit 来源]

The plans for the Democracy Space 车站 were originally stored inside an Advanced 武器 Lab on X-45 during the 第一次银河战争, which was abandoned due to 危险 levels of 辐射 engulfing the 行星. Once the 辐射 levels had 已减少 during the 第二次银河战争, the 绝地潜兵 were tasked with defending the 行星 from 机器人 入侵 while the lab was excavated by 超级地球 forces in order to 恢复 the plans for the 车站, as well as an intact cache containing the once lost MLS-4X Commandos. Upon successful retrieval of the plans, the lab it was formerly contained in was destroyed by 超级地球 in order to prevent it from falling into 机器人 控制.

Construction[edit | edit 来源]

After a little over two months of 研究, construction of the Democracy Space 车站 was ready to begin. For the first stage of its construction, the 绝地潜兵 were tasked with choosing the 位置 of the 轨道武器 construction site between either Clasa or Gaellivare. Both of these planets were considered suitable due to their large magnetospheres, but both were also under 机器人 控制, requiring the 绝地潜兵 to liberate their 行星 of choice to secure the site. The majority of 绝地潜兵 descended on Gaellivare and ultimately liberated it for the construction of the DSS.

第1阶段[edit | edit 来源]

On 9月 25th, 2184, Upon 解放, a DSS Logistics Hub on Tarsh was built to 支援 the 质量 scale of the construction; 超级地球 High 指挥 determined that zero-g materials, FTL propulsion, and gigastructure staffing needed significant advancements to make the DSS a 现实. High 指挥 also reminded 绝地潜兵 that they would be crucial in the 防御 of the DSS should it come 遭受攻击, and any failures to do so would delay its construction.

Soon after establishing the DSS, High 指挥 began tasking the 绝地潜兵 to liberate planets to 建立 facilities for the advancements needed for the DSS. Turing was selected to build the Ministry of Science's new Xenoentomology Center for high-效率 Alcubierre drives. 绝地潜兵 quickly liberated the 行星 from the 终结族 swarm while also defending Tarsh's Logistics Hub. High 指挥 noted that the Center would still need to be defended from any potential 攻击 or 研究 will be lost.

To create FTL-抵抗 alloy for the DSS, a deep-mantle forge would be 必需 due to the 极难 heat and 压力. Luckily, High 指挥 found that the 机器人 on Claorell already had a deep-mantle mining 复杂. Seeking to repurpose it for their own ends, High 指挥 ordered the 绝地潜兵 to liberate Claorell and seize the 复杂. They would be successful, filling the mines with 超级地球's own in the new Deep Mantle Forge 复杂. However, High 指挥 found intel that revealed that the 机器人 were creating exceptionally 轻型 metal for 护甲板. Not long after, the 机器人 attempted to take back Claorell, accompanied by cyberattacks on DSS-相关 servers. The 绝地潜兵 would be successful in defending Claorell; the cyberattacks revealed the DSS project to the 机器人, but the DSS's exact 位置 remained a 谜团 to them for the time being.

第2阶段[edit | edit 来源]

On 9月 28th, 2184, with the Xenoentomology Center and Deep Mantle Forge 复杂 built, the DSS moved into Phase 2. Due to the DSS's scale, it would 要求 significant amounts of E-710, leading to the establishment of the 终结族 研究 Preserve on Acamar IV. It would provide 研究 capabilities and another 来源 of E-710. The 绝地潜兵 would need to liberate and hold the nearby planets of Crimsica, Turing, Darius II, Pandion-XXIV, and Gacrux to 建立 a 行星 quarantine zone around Acamar IV. The 绝地潜兵 would successfully create the quarantine needed for the construction of the 终结族 研究 Preserve, furthering E-710 研究.

Shortly after, the 机器人 would launch an 特攻 师, the Jet Brigade, onto numerous planets in search of the DSS. Though the Jet Brigade managed to 占领 Matar Bay and Martale, they were eventually stopped on Marfark. Due to the 绝地潜兵' efforts, the 机器人 were unable to find the DSS and its 相关 facilities.

To further 保护 the DSS, High 指挥 determined that liberating Lesath would provide a 防御型 buffer for the DSS against a potential Jet Brigade 特攻. However, a sudden 终结族 爆发 on Gacrux threatened the newly-built 终结族 研究 Preserve. The 绝地潜兵 were then left to decide which to prioritize, in which they chose to liberate Lesath. Another 终结族 爆发 would occur on Pandion-XXIV, eventually leading to its fall alongside Gacrux. The DSS would 继续 to stay 受保护, but the 终结族 研究 Preserve quarantine zone was now compromised.

第3阶段[edit | edit 来源]

On 10月 26th, 2184, the continued 保护 of the DSS allowed it to advance to its final construction phase before its long-awaited completion.

The DSS had to be staffed with a crew of nearly 1.3 million skilled workers. In order to get that many workers, the Ministry of 人类 authorized the relocation of colonists, 绝地潜兵 were ordered to hold Gloom-adjacent planets while citizens were relocated to serve within the DSS.

Final Phase and Activation[edit | edit 来源]

On 11月 10th, 2184, construction of DSS was completed and the project reached its final phase. The entire construction took 43 days.

In order to activate the DSS and fuel it, 绝地潜兵 were ordered to kill 500 million 终结族 and hold Gaellivare. However the Jet Brigade returned, and attacked the DSS Logistics Hub on Tarsh. The 占领 of Tarsh led to the 摧毁 of the Logistics Hub. High 指挥 reported that its loss was negligible, as it had fulfilled its 目的 and had an acceptable amount of 公民 evacuations. However, they noted that the DSS was finally located by the 机器人, who were now preparing to 攻击 Mastia to reach Gaellivare.

Upon the culling of 500 million 终结族, the DSS was immediately fueled and activated. It proceeded to travel between 多种星球 as the Jet Brigade attacked, achieving non-zero progress in each case. The DSS Eagle Storm would ultimately buy the citizens of Gaellivare enough time to evacuate before the 行星 fell into 机器人 控制. Despite losing the 行星, this resulted in a "successful" 重要指令 win.

Apparent 破坏 and Deactivation[edit | edit 来源]

The DSS would patrol the Galaxy for the 下一个 几个 weeks, resulting in a quantifiable 效果 on the 星系战争. It cannot be argued that the DSS had zero 效果, or that it was in fact 负面 for the forces on the ground.

With the return of the 光能者 和它们的 攻击 on Calypso. The Democracy Space 车站 would undergo 多个 catastrophic system failures, with the 伤害 being severe enough to render it inoperable. The DSS was locked down for repairs. 安全 screening measures had been drastically 已增加. This 破坏, manifested in failure to follow 安全 protocols, would later be attributed to 光能者 mind 控制.

The DSS would remain marooned in orbit of Vog-Sojoth for over a month, unable to aid in the 星系战争 against the return of the 光能者.

Reactivation and Operational Adjustments[edit | edit 来源]

Necessary repairs would eventually bring the 车站 online without further complications. Notably, its drives would 接收 a comprehensive recalibration, allowing the 车站 to move every 4 hours rather than daily. This change was made to help better confront the swift pace of 光能者 攻击, and are under review for long term 应用程序. The DSS would exit 机器人 space and begin coursing threatened planets at 6 times its 上一个 速度.

Beyond restoring basic functionality, these repairs would see tweaks to some core systems. In particular, the 行星 Bombardment tactical action would be temporarily retired so as to adjust certain 致命 externalities caused by the ceaseless bombardment from the 77 PRESIDENTIAL-级别 cannons installed on the DSS. A new tactical action with similar 效果 would take its place, with 重型 Ordnance 分布 providing the surplus 轨道武器 380mm HE 弹幕 战略配备 to all 绝地潜兵 in the field.

During 重要指令 A2-3-7 after the 解放 of Ingmar, it was also revealed the warp drives had been given enhancements to immediately deploy 下一个 行星 after each completed campaign rather having to wait to 冷却 充能 between each jump.

升级[edit | edit 来源]

Following the successful 防御 of Malevelon Creek, 升级 to the DSS became a priority due to its pivotal role in holding Pöpli IX. 绝地潜兵 were ordered to secure resources for the 升级, by liberating either Wezen to enable the construction of an 外骨骼机甲 支援 Bay, or Trandor for an Expanded Convocation Bay. Upon the 解放 of the 机器人 factories on Wezen, the 外骨骼机甲 支援 Bay was installed immediately, reducing the 冷却 of 外骨骼机甲 战略配备.

Second Deactivation[edit | edit 来源]

During the true 光能者 入侵 on Widow's Harbor, 超级地球 High 指挥 reported in a dispatch that the DSS, which was orbiting the 行星, received 伤害 from the 光能者. High 指挥 warned that further warps to 光能者-invaded planets could potentially deal additional 伤害.

Despite their 警告, the 绝地潜兵 would vote for the DSS to warp to Pilen V soon after the fall of Widow's Harbor. The 光能者 immediately attacked the DSS upon its arrival to Pilen V, inflicting significant 伤害 to the 车站 and killing many of its crew. The DSS would 撤退 to Claorell for repairs, though 超级地球 High 指挥 noted that it is undetermined of when the DSS would be fully operational.

Second Reactivation & Surge into Action[edit | edit 来源]

After receiving sufficient repairs due to 绝地潜兵 collecting Automation scrap, as ordered by High 指挥, the DSS immediately left for 超级地球 without delay. Upon reaching 超级地球, it began supporting the struggling 防御 against the 光能者 入侵. In order to avoid a repeat of its 上一个 near-摧毁, high 指挥 initialized a Fleet Positioning Protocol, creating a 屏障 of ships around the DSS to defend it against enemy fighter 攻击.

Star of Peace[edit | edit 来源]

Following the construction of the Center for the Confinement of Dissidence, 超级地球 High 指挥 ordered the construction of Pandora Base within Afoyay Bay. The new top-secret base would utilize the Dissident's labor in order to accomplish one of 操作 Free Space's main goals: a major augmentation of the DSS called Star of Peace.

Such 升级's centerpiece was the installation of an ionized[1] Dark Fluid beam capable of collapsing an entire 行星 into a black hole. [2][3]

On 1月 14th, 2186, after barely collecting enough E-711 from Omicron and successfully collecting platinum and scrapping 机器人 components, the Star of Peace would be able to be fired once in order to turn an entire 行星 into a black hole using synthesized Dark Fluid.

The choices were between Penta, Zagon Prime, and Valmox.

When the Star of Peace reached operational status, the 绝地潜兵 elected for Penta to be used as the demonstration for test-firing.

On 1月 18th, 2186, day 711 of the 星系战争, at 21:28 SEST, Penta would be destroyed and turned into a Conventional Black Hole, a normal, stable black hole much unlike the 默里迪恩奇点.

操作 Valid Pretext[edit | edit 来源]

On 2月 10th, 2186, the DSS would be deployed on Cyberstan as part of 操作 Valid Pretext, providing 支援 to 绝地潜兵 and SEAF forces on the 行星.

On 2月 15th, 2186, the DSS would be sent to Fort 正义 briefly to 恢复 35,000,000 绝地潜兵 in cryostasis stored in warehouses. The DSS remained on the 行星 for 12 hours before returning with the 35,000,000 绝地潜兵.

On 2月 19th, 2186, seeing 操作 Valid Pretext's failure was imminent, High 指挥 activated the 终端 指令, initiating the DSS' 行星 Bombardment tactical action for 48 hours.
