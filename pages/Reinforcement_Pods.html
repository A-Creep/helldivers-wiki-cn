“

Hellpods will be deployed to this 位置 upon 任务 launch. Due to the 超级驱逐舰's absence, normal 增援 will be unavailable. Securing this site will give access to additional 增援, who will deploy directly from this 位置.

— 任务 Briefing

增援 Pods is a 突击兵 任务 exclusive 可选目标 in 绝地潜兵 2. 绝地潜兵 are tasked with activating them, granting additional 增援 to use while the 超级驱逐舰 is away.

目标 Steps[edit | edit 来源]

  • 建立 连接 to 超级驱逐舰 to secure 增援.
    • Entering the 战略配备代码 will start to 建立 the link. After 10 秒, the link will be completed, granting 3 additional 增援.
  • Reward: 200 申购点 Slips, 50 经验值, 3 增援

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • When 增援 Pods have been successfully connected to the 驱逐舰, their glowing ring will change from a blinking yellow to a solid green.
  • When a 绝地潜兵 dies and is reinforced, they will deploy from the group of pods nearest to whoever called the 增援.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • At the start of the 任务, it is possible to see the 增援 Pods being launched should the player choose to deploy near the 可选目标.
  • When a 绝地潜兵 is deployed from a 增援 Pod, there is still 可见 frosting from cryo-stasis, this fades after a short time.
  • Similar to the Upload Escape Pod Data 任务, this 次要目标 is bugged to reset 机器人 增援 cooldowns upon entering the 战略配备代码. This means a 机器人空投 can happen again much quicker than is normal while completing this 任务.
