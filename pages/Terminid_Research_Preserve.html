the 终结族研究保护区 was a 超级地球 行星 wide quarantine 设施 located on Acamar IV. It allowed researchers at the Xenoentomology Center to obtain unlimited quantities of 终结族 研究 specimens. It was later destroyed from a 安全 breach, causing 质量 outbreaks to nearby planets.

背景故事

机构

The 终结族研究保护区 was established after 绝地潜兵 successfully completed the 为终结族研究保护区建立隔离区 重要指令 by establishing a buffer zone across Crimsica, Turing, Darius II, Pandion-XXIV，以及 Gacrux. To prevent the 终结族 from breaching the preserve as with the E-710 farms, the preserve would be continuously monitored by the 超级地球武装部队 'Bug Wardens' unit, as well as aboveground and belowground 电 fencing. A 弹幕 of nuclear warheads would be automatically launched upon a catastrophic containment breach.

Humorously, a 小型 part of the preserve was partially commercialized as "暴政 Park", where wealthy 超级地球 civilians could observe the 终结族 in their 自然 环境, and even participate in trophy hunts against some of the larger 终结族.

During the 启动并保护民主空间站 重要指令, the 终结族研究保护区 suffered a catastrophic 安全 breach, and the 终结族 attacked all neighboring planets, including Turing and its Xenoentomology Center. 绝地潜兵 could only successfully defend Turing and all other planets used to 建立 the quarantine were lost.

Acamar IV would be liberated immediately following the MO's completion, however the 终结族研究保护区 was 已移除 from the 行星 afterwards on 2024-11-20.
