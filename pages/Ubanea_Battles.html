Summary Draupnir and Ubanea were lost. Quickly recaptured but the 重要指令 didn't succeed.


Background

操作 Swift Disassembly was proceeding well; the first phase was a success and now it was time for the second phase.

Phase II of 操作 Swift Disassembly tasked 绝地潜兵 to 占领 Tibit, the most significant 制作人 of 非法 机器人 combatants. To accomplish this, 绝地潜兵 had to 占领 Ubanea.

As the 解放 of Ubanea progressed smoothly, Draupnir came 遭受攻击 (the Last Battle of Draupnir). 绝地潜兵 had to make a decision: defend Draupnir and risk the fall of Ubanea, or attempt to 占领 Ubanea before Draupnir fell, and then 继续 directly to Tibit.

绝地潜兵 chose the second option.

策略 and importance

The 策略 was straightforward: 占领 Ubanea and launch an 攻击 on Tibit. This battle was of utmost importance, as it would determine the success or failure of the 重要指令.

战斗

Ubanea Gambit started on 29th of March, when Draupnir was attacked by 机器人.

Ubanea was lost when it was at 95% 解放. 重要指令 Failed. Phase II of 操作 Swift Disassembly was unsuccessful.
