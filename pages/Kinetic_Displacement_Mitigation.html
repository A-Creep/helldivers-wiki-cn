描述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Kinetic Displacement Mitigation
提供 50% 伤害抗性 to 火焰, allowing bearer to rest assured in their inflammability.
Gives the wearer a 50% 几率 to avoid grievous 肢体 受伤.
降低 受到伤害 from 冲击 and collisions by 30%.

解释[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

1. The wearer takes half as 许多 伤害 from 火焰.

2. If the 效果 triggers, hits that would ordinarily 打破 a 肢体 still deal full 伤害 to your 生命值, but the 肢体 遗骸 at 1 HP instead of breaking.

3. The wearer takes moderately 更少 伤害 from impacts (such as being ragdolled into 地形 at 高 速度, or falling to the 地面) and collisions (such as being run over by a FRV or 强袭虫).
