Construction[edit | edit 来源]

On April 28th, 2185, the 负面 能量 实验室 was constructed on Pilen V in the Altus 分区. It was likely constructed to replace the Moradesh 研究设施, which was consumed by the 默里迪恩奇点 on March 14th, 2185.

摧毁[edit | edit 来源]

Just over 24 hours later, it was destroyed on April 28th, 2185.

负面 能量 & Dark 能量 研究[edit | edit 来源]

the 负面 能量 实验室 specializes in the 研究 of Dark 能量 and 负面 能量. While it is known that Dark 能量 is used to 加速 the motion of the Meridian Singularity and is gathered during 光能者 invasions of Planets, the exact 目的 of 负面 能量 遗骸 未知. The only known aspects of 负面 能量 is that it appears to be created by 光能者 Monoliths, and that it is 极其 危险 in this state, instantly killing 绝地潜兵 that come in 联络 with it.
