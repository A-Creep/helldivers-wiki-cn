概述[edit | edit 来源]

  • Fix for SH-32 防护罩生成包
  • Crash fix

修复[edit | edit 来源]

  • Fix for a crash during host migration when a new player joins.
  • 绝地潜兵 were accidentally granted the SH-32 防护罩生成器 Packs from 基本信息 Brasch´s personal stash. These are reserved for 10 star generals only. The workers at the 分布 center have been sent to re-education camps and regular 护盾 will resume 分布.
    • (It previously made the 绝地潜兵 immune to 近战伤害)

已知问题[edit | edit 来源]

These are the highest priority issues that were either introduced by this 补丁 and are being worked on, or are from a 上一个 version and have not yet been fixed. This is not a comprehensive list.

  • Friends "Invite only" games can still be seen on the 星系战争地图 but cannot be joined

  • Some 敌人 that bleed out do not progress 个人命令 and 歼灭 missions
