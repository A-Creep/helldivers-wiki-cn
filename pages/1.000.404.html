补丁 01.000.404[edit | edit 来源]

🌍 概述

This hotfix aims to 解决 崩溃 and to reduce the number of people stuck in the 绝地喷射舱 掉落-in loading screen. Tier 5 舰船模块 升级 were released alongside this 补丁.

🔧 修复

  • Fixed the '卓越封装方法' 舰船模块 升级 (technically this was fixed on the server side and has been working since July 1st).
  • Fixed 崩溃 and glitches occurring when dropping into a 任务.
  • Fixed instances of 玩家 becoming stuck in the 绝地喷射舱 loading screen.
  • Fixed a crash that occurs after changing settings (this should be fixed for real this time, the last fix didn’t stick the landing and we have sent the responsible 工程师 to a 超级地球 re-education camp).
