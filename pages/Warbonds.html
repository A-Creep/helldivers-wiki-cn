概述[edit | edit 来源]

战争债券 allow 绝地潜兵 to spend 奖牌 to unlock new gear, such as 武器, Armors, 战略配备, Boosters, and Cosmetics.

They are structured into unlockable pages, requiring a set number of Medals to be spent on the 战争债券 to progress through each page.

The 标准战争债券 is free, whereas all other Premium and 传奇 战争债券 must be unlocked with 超级货币.

战争债券 do not expire, allowing 绝地潜兵 to progress through one or 多个 at any desired pace.

背景故事[edit | edit 来源]

战争债券 are primarily designed and deployed by the Ministry of 防御, with some occasional 合作 from other 超级地球 Ministries when needed.[1]

FAQ[edit | edit 来源]

The following FAQ is sourced from the 绝地潜兵™ 官员 Discord [2]

问题 回答
How do the 战争债券 work? Is it a pay-to-win model? The goal with our 战争债券 is to provide 玩家 with different 玩法 experiences, but not unfair advantages when comparing the 内容.

We designed 战争债券 as an optional way to 支援 超级地球's war effort in the game and get access to additional gear for your 绝地潜兵. 战争债券 are unlocked with 超级货币, which can either be discovered at 兴趣点 during missions or purchased through the in-game store. Once you own a 战争债券, you can then use Medals you’ve been awarded in-game to unlock more 内容.

How often will new 战争债券 be released? Can we preview the 内容 in them? For right now, 战争债券 will be released every second 星期四 of the month.
If I only want one item from the 战争债券, do I still have to pay to get that item? Yes, you must unlock the 战争债券 itself before you can spend medals on the items in it.
Can you progress two 战争债券 at the same time? Yes you can progress your 战争债券 any way you choose. If you have 2 战争债券 unlocked you can spend your first 50 medals in 战争债券 1 and then switch and spend 50 medals in 战争债券 2.
Can old 战争债券 be purchased a year from now? 战争债券 are evergreen, so 玩家 are welcome to take their time in unlocking, and we'll be keeping things fresh with future updates containing new 战争债券 to choose from.
Are all the items in the 战争债券 strictly 装饰 No, Some of the items in 战争债券 have 被动 效果 attached to them.
Will progressing through 战争债券 分离 me from other 玩家 Nope! You will always be able to play all our 内容 updates with other 玩家 who have only bought the base game. All other future updates, for 示例 敌人, environments and missions will be free for everyone who's bought the base game.
Will 战争债券 contain 战略配备 No. New 战略配备 will be added to the 战略配备 menu and be unlockable through in-game play. We may add them to 战争债券 or 超级商店 in the future.
Can you still earn medals without an active 战争债券 You will always earn medals, no matter if you have 杰出 战争债券, completed 战争债券 or no 战争债券.
Is it possible to buy the DLC packs directly? Like buying a 战争债券 with money and not requiring you to buy the premium 货币 You will not be able to buy the 战争债券 directly with real money. You would need to 购买 a pack of 超级货币 and then buy the 战争债券.
Will all future 战争债券 contain some amount of 超级货币 Right now, the 战争债券 we have designed contain SC. However, not every future 战争债券 will contain 超级货币 but a lot of them will, and the SC inclusion may depend on the theme and size of the 战争债券.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • the 铁血老兵 尊享战争债券 was previously included in the Super 公民 Edition DLC. This was later replaced with a 尊享战争债券 Token which entitles the player to 1 free 尊享战争债券 of their choice. 传奇 战争债券 are not eligible
  • Most, but not all, 战争债券 have a corresponding 章节 in 超级商店:
    • The only 标准 绝地潜兵 Mobilize 战争债券 has three pages
    • the 铁血老兵 尊享战争债券 also has three pages
    • All other Premium 战争债券, except the Siege Breakers 尊享战争债券 have a single 超级商店 page
    • 传奇 战争债券 don't have corresponding sections in 超级商店
