the Supercolony is a Primordial 类型 生物群系 that planets may only have under 特殊 circumstances.

The only known Supercolony is Meridia, which mutated into this 生物群系 following the failure of the 终结族控制系统, which mandated it's eventual 摧毁.

描述[edit | edit 来源]

“

灭虫终结剂引发毫无预警的突变，导致虫族极速繁殖，将这颗星球变为了超级拓殖地。星球已经腐化得无可救药，虫族侵染了上面的每一寸土地，连地幔也没有幸免于难，每天都繁殖出数万亿孢子。

The Supercolony is a 变体 of the 火山 Jungle.

The 生物群系's 浮出地面 mostly consists of 终结族 matter, creating shores, hills, and rocks. Across the 浮出地面, 庞大 终结族 spires stand, made up of the same 终结族 matter. The 浮出地面 is also filled with yellow puddles and 小型 rivers everywhere. 偶尔, sections of the 浮出地面 will show the original 行星 beneath: green grass, gravel, tall grass, grey rocks, and the 典型 trees found in all of the Primordial Biomes.

The skies are yellow during the day, with them becoming a green hue at night.

The original 火山 Jungle beneath, as it's name suggests, had volcanoes across the 浮出地面. This is still case for the Supercolony as the volcanoes are seen in the backdrop along with ash coming out of them. The environmental 效果 linked to them however, was not present.

Environmental Conditions[edit | edit 来源]

This 生物群系 has no environmental conditions specific to it.

环境危害[edit | edit 来源]

  • Spike Plants are present in the patches of the 浮出地面 that still consist of the original 火山 Jungle.
  • Tall grass and slowing ferns are also present in those areas.
  • Bug Mines are scattered across the 浮出地面, growing from the 终结族 matter.
