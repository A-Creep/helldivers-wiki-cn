the Pump Fuel To ICBM is a 主要目标 in 绝地潜兵2 that can appear on both 终结族 and 机器人 controlled planets.

目标 Steps[edit | edit 来源]

  • 启动终端
    • Upon reaching the fueling 车站, 绝地潜兵 will encounter a small amount of 敌人 guarding the fueling 车站.
    • After clearing the area, 绝地潜兵 must activate the 终端 by interacting with it. Then, 绝地潜兵 must adjust the fueling route to move fuel from the Local 存储 to the 导弹 Silo; the up and down directional inputs will swap the 当前 pipe in the highlighted column.
  • 登录终端
    • The 绝地潜兵 must input the directional code as seen on the 终端.
  • 更改燃料流向，将其输送至新目标
    • On the 终端 screen, the 绝地潜兵 must create a 管道 from the fuel output (left) to the new 目标 (top right).
    • To do so, they must press "" or "" to move the pipes up and down. And press "" and "" to move from side to side.

  • 按照终端的提示，调整所有阀门
    • 3 valves will be located in the 基本信息 vicinity. The 绝地潜兵 must now adjust the three valves located around the fueling 车站 to the right by holding "" until they glow; the 终端 will also make a loud beep sound to 确认 a valve has been adjusted correctly.
    • After successfully adjusting all the valves, 绝地潜兵 will need to return to the 终端 and press "" to activate the pumps. Upon activation, the 任务 is successfully completed.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • 敌人 will continuously spawn and patrol near the fueling 车站 if the 任务 has yet to be completed. It is recommended that 绝地潜兵 隐藏 themselves as they 完成 the 目标, or to quickly dispatch nearby patrols if necessary.
