战术信息

  • Due to its low 冷却, two 使用次数 per call-in, expendable nature, and high sustain, the Bullet Storm is 极其 柔韧 as a 支援武器.
  • The Bullet Storm boasts an 极其 high 射速 of 1300rpm, comparatively low 后坐力, and an 出色 100 伤害 每发, higher than its counterparts, the MG-43 机枪 and the M-105 盟友.
    • This makes it particularly 克制 the 光能者 和 终结族, given the abundance of lightly 有护甲, high 生命值 targets.
    • It is not especially 克制 the 机器人 due to their propensity to engage at 射程, their 护甲, and their 小型 weakpoints that 要求 precise aiming, something the Bullet Storm is not especially good at.
    • The high 射速 means the Bullet Storm expends its 300 round 容量 in ~14 秒 of continuous firing. It is recommended to only use sustained 火焰 against groups of 敌人 and large 无护甲 targets to save ammunition.
    • The low 后坐力 makes the Bullet Storm relatively 准确 even in sustained 火焰 or while moving, especially in first person view.
  • The Bullet Storm can be used as a supplement to a slower, 反坦克 支援武器, being called down upon encountering hordes of targets, such as during a 虫族入侵.
    • When calling down the Bullet Storm against hordes, "reloading" is as quick as picking up the second gun from the 绝地喷射舱.
    • If used in tandem with the EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器, 绝地潜兵 can 呼叫 600 bullets and two 反坦克 projectiles every 70 秒, even without any 舰船升级.
  • The Bullet Storm has poor bullet drag and 速率 for a 机枪, and no reticle or sight in first person view. It is less 有效 at longer 射程 and 困难 to aim precisely at individual targets and weakpoints.
    • The Bullet Storm can easily be aimed using its tracer bullets, but the weapon's 人体工学, though better than most machine guns, hamper the 能力 to adjust aim against distant targets.
    • In first person view, the Bullet Storm's large horizontal muzzle flash can make it 困难 to see distant 敌人 and bullet impacts.
  • The Bullet Storm’s quick 冷却 means it is almost always available upon 增援 of additional 绝地潜兵. Even if a fresh 绝地潜兵 is called in far from their 装备, in a matter of 秒 they can have another Bullet Storm at hand.
    • Because Bullet Storms arrive in pairs, they can be easily distributed to teammates who might need a 支援武器.
    • The Bullet Storm’s quick 冷却 also allows the 绝地喷射舱 itself to be used as a weapon, dealing 2500  反坦克 VI 伤害 upon impact.
