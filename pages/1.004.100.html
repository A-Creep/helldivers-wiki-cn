概述

自由问候你们，绝地潜兵！

本次更新聚焦于当下最重要的事项：让绝地潜兵 2玩起来感觉更好。

We’ve overhauled how we approach patching to better 目标 the pain points you’ve shared with us. That means more focus on 稳定性, 平衡, and the issues that affect your 经验值 the most.

Over 200 bugs have been fixed, along with key 平衡 updates and quality-of-life improvements. (For a deeper look, check out Lennart and Niklas’ video breakdown.) While new features are coming, this 补丁 marks a big step forward: both in the game itself and in how we work to 改进 it. More refinements are already underway.

平衡性调整

基本信息 变更

Primaries, Sidearms, Throwables, and 战略配备
已进行改进，以提升主武器、随身武器、投掷物和战略配备的整体效能

轻型 vs. 中型 穿透 武器
轻型和中型穿透攻击武器现在提供更明显的优势。 轻型穿透武器通常对耐久敌人造成更高比例的总伤害，使其与中型穿透选项区分更明显

SMGs and Pistols
SMGs and pistols have been adjusted to emphasize their role as close-quarters 武器. Close-射程 伤害 has been 已增加, and 伤害 falloff has been 已增加 to 增援 their short-射程 combat identity

近战 武器 and Throwables
近战武器和投掷物已得到改进，使每种武器在功能上更具冲击力和独特性

重新补给 Rack
重新补给架不再可以攀爬
