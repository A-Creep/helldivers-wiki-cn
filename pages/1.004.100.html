概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Freedom's greetings, 绝地潜兵!

This 更新 focuses on what matters 最多 正确 now: making 绝地潜兵 2 feel 更好 to 游玩.

We’ve overhauled how we approach patching to 更好 目标 the 疼痛 points you’ve shared with us. That means 更多 focus on 稳定性, 平衡, and the issues that affect your 经验值 the 最多.

Over 200 bugs have been 已修复, along with key 平衡 updates and quality-of-life improvements. (For a deeper look, 检查 out Lennart and Niklas’ video breakdown.) While new features are coming, this 补丁 marks a big step forward: 两者 in the 游戏 itself and in how we work to 改进 it. 更多 refinements are already underway.

Balancing[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

常规变更

Primaries, Sidearms, Throwables, and 战略配备
Improvements have been made to 增加 the overall effectiveness of primary 武器, sidearms, throwables, and 战略配备

轻型 vs. 中型 穿透 武器
轻型 and 中型 穿透 武器 now 优惠 更多 distinct advantages. 轻型 穿透 武器 通常 deal a 更高 百分比 of their 总伤害 against 耐久 敌人, differentiating them 更多 clearly from 中型 穿透 options
