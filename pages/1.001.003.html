1.001.003 was a hotfix 更新 released for 绝地潜兵 2.

补丁 01.001.003

概述

  • 性能
  • 崩溃修复
  • 基本信息 Weapon and 战略配备 fixes
  • Social menu
  • Miscellaneous fixes

修复

崩溃修复：

  • Fixed crash that would occur when you or another player selects a new weapon while on the ship
  • Fixed crash when booting the game after saved 监控 was disconnected

武器与战略配备修复

  • 铝热弹 grenades 否 longer make the 轨道加农炮 目标 incompletable.
  • Mines can now be shot and blown up while near a 危险 object.
  • Fixed a bug where 火焰喷射器 武器 bounce off 能量 护盾, making them 无用 when firing within 防护罩生成中继器 domes.

社交菜单修复

  • Even more social menu fixes
  • 已改进 usability

其他修复：

  • 基本信息 performance improvements.
  • Some pertaining to fighting large swarms of bots.
  • 修复 bug where "撤离高价值资产" 任务 stops working then the host leaves.
  • Fixed infinitely loading thumbnails on low 纹理 Quality setting.

已知问题

这些问题要么是由本次补丁引入的且正在修复中，要么来自之前的版本且尚未修复。

  • Game may 显示 an infinite loading screen if a player disconnects during the deployment cutscene.
  • 玩家 may not 接收 Friend Requests sent from another platform.
  • "Invite only" games created by a friend can still be seen on the 星系战争地图 but cannot be joined.
  • “进行地质勘测” 任务 may become incompletable if a player calls in “地震探测仪” and immediately leaves the game or disconnects.
  • 吐酸泰坦 有时 does not take 伤害 to the head.
  • 流血死亡的敌人不会推进个人指令和歼灭任务。
  • Most 武器 shoot below the crosshair when aiming down the sights..
  • Chain-link fences block flamethrowers’ 火焰.
  • 战略配备光束可能会附着在敌人身上，但会部署到原始位置。
  • “Guard Dog Rover” does not overheat when firing continuously.
  • 玩家 are able to pick up empty expendable 武器 like EAT-17 or MLS-4X
  • 增援 may not be available for 玩家 who join a game in progress.
  • Wrong decal displayed for the B-27 强化 突击兵
  • We have a 修复 and plan to implement it soon
  • 服务 技术员 on the ship cannot be interacted with - they complained they were bothered too much and could not do their work efficiently
