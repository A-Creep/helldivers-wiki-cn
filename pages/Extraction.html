目标 Steps[edit | edit 来源]

  • 撤离飞船就位。
    • 绝地潜兵 must be within 50m of the 撤离 beacon for the 下一个 step to appear.
  • 激活撤离终端。
    • 绝地潜兵 must now activate the 撤离 终端 by interacting with it, then inputting the x 方向 long code.
  • 运输船 incoming. Defend 撤离 zone. [xx:xx]
    • During the deployment period (2 分钟 or 3 分钟 with the 复杂 战略配备 Plotting 操作 修正), at least one 绝地潜兵 must stay in the perimeter at all times (50m radius around the blue beacon), or else the arrival of the 撤离 运输船 will be aborted.
    • the 专家撤离飞行员 强化资源 can be used to reduce 撤离 time by 30%.
      • 绝地潜兵 have 20 秒 until 撤离 is aborted to return within 51m of the 撤离 Point.
    • If the 撤离 运输船 arrives but the 撤离 Point is inaccessible due to 敌人, the 撤离 运输船 will not land until the 障碍 are cleared.
    • If the 撤离 运输船 arrives and at least one 绝地潜兵 is present at the 撤离 Point, the 撤离 运输船 will begin descent and land, providing covering 火焰 until it has landed.
      • 绝地潜兵 are advised to clear the landing area; else they will meet a swift end of Democratic origin.
      • It will stay there until a 绝地潜兵 enters or until the 任务 timer reaches 0. After which, a 20 second 倒计时 will begin. Then the 撤离 运输船 with leave. 呼叫中 增援 will be disabled once one 绝地潜兵 enters the 运输船.
      • 所有 Hellpods within 10-meters on the same 高地 of the landing area will retract once the 撤离 运输船 lands. Supply-Hellpods will eject their 目录 onto the ground for 绝地潜兵 to pick up if needed to.
    • Once the 撤离 运输船 has arrived while no 绝地潜兵 is present at the 撤离 Zone, 鹈鹕-1 will 继续 to hover in circling 涂装 around the landing zone. While in this state, 鹈鹕 1 also continues attempting to shoot any enemy units within the line of sight of frontal 200-degree-angle until running out of ammunition; this amount being 3,000 rounds shot in 齐射 of three rounds which 通常 is depleted within 8-10 分钟 of continuous barrages.
      • The gun-turret can be damaged and disabled E.G. with GR-8 无后坐力炮.
      • 鹈鹕-1 also continues to hover above the landing zone if all 绝地潜兵 are inside a 载具 such as M-102 Fast 侦察 载具 when entering the 51-metre radius (NOTE: Currently bugged?).
  • If the 任务 timer runs out, the 超级驱逐舰 will leave low orbit, and the 撤离 运输船 will automatically be called. No 绝地潜兵 must be present at the 撤离 Point to prevent abortion.
    • After the 撤离 运输船 has landed, a 20 second 倒计时 will begin. Then the 撤离 运输船 will leave.
  • Any 绝地潜兵 that fail to 撤离 in time will be left behind. In 玩法 terms, no player receives the Samples in the 库存 of 绝地潜兵 who failed to 撤离.
