🌍 概述

This hotfix aims to reduce the number of people stuck in the 绝地喷射舱 掉落-in loading screen.

  • 已降低 instances of being stuck waiting in infinite 掉落-in

  • Various issues to do with unintended Ballstics Shield 行为.
  • 玩家 may be unable to be joined or invited to the game
  • 玩家 added to the Recent 玩家 list will appear in the middle of the list
  • 玩家 may 经验值 delays in 奖章 and 超级货币 payouts

  • 吐酸泰坦 有时 does not take 伤害 to the head
