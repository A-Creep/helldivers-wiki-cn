护甲 被动 变化[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

After 困难 fought battles on 超级地球 our 典礼官 have 成为 更多 grizzled and 更强. With their limbs not suffering 任何 breakages, 绝地潜兵 adorned with these armors are finding themselves able to 换弹 更快 and punch harder.

We have been monitoring your feedback in regards to armors and their passives. We have made 一些 adjustments to the Masters of 仪式 armors, adding 2 extra perks:

  • 已增加 换弹 速度 of primary 武器 by 30%
  • 已增加 近战伤害 by 50%


We will 监控 sentiment around this and assess going forward
