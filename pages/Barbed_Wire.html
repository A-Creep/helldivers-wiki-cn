倒刺铁丝网 is a 危害 constructed by the  机器人 Legion. It is considered an Environmental 危害.

效果

When a 绝地潜兵 moves through Barbed Wire, it inflicts slight continuous 伤害 while also slowing them down significantly.

Locations

Barbed Wire can only be found in 机器人 领土, but can be found on 任意星球 in said 领土. They are 通常 located in or around 机器人 哨站.
