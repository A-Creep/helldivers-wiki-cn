值得注意 Behaviors[edit | edit 来源]

When a Minor Place of Interest is first discovered, it will appear on the map as a image icon. It will also send a message in the in-game chat indicating that a player has discovered a Minor Place of Interest accompanied by a matching voice line. It will then become a image icon once 绝地潜兵 have taken all the Samples.

Radar Stations[edit | edit 来源]

the 雷达站, once reactivated, will mark all 终结族 Nests/机器人 哨站, 可选目标, and 次要兴趣点 on the map.
