巨型构筑者

巨型构筑者 是一个 机器人 megastructure found exclusively at 机器人哨站 in cities. They periodically deploy 重型 敌人 such as 巨型者 until they are destroyed.

Disclaimer: 每个带数量的高亮部位 a 数量有各自的 生命值. 未标注数量的部位共享 生命值 pools.

1) 生命值标注为 标注为 "主体" 没有各自的 individual 生命值. All 造成伤害 to these parts is 转移到敌人的 主生命值 pool.
2) 请参阅 护甲 values and enemy 耐久度 as defined in 伤害 for more information.
3) 造成伤害 to a part will also be 部分或全部转移 in full to 主生命值. 若设置为 0%, 否 伤害 is transferred.
4) 若设置为 "是", 伤害 transferred 对主血量 is capped by the combined 生命值 and 构造 of the part.
5) 构造 充当 a secondary 生命池 that, 一旦主体或肢体 生命值 has been depleted, 可能会衰减. The first number is the total 构造 and the second is the 衰退速率. 若设置为 0/s 不会衰减 and the 构造 作为额外 生命值.
6) 爆炸抗性 means 爆炸 伤害抗性, and can 射程 from 0 to 100%. 若设置为 100%, the limb cannot take 爆炸伤害; The 爆炸伤害 will instead 目标 主体. 请参阅 伤害 for more information.
  • Note: This 机械师, known as Affected By 爆炸 can only occur once per 爆炸, 无论 100% 爆炸抗性 parts hit, and the 爆炸's 护甲穿透 must match or exceed 主体护甲 to deal 伤害 对主血量. However, 如果相同 爆炸 has already damaged 主体, this 机械师 will not trigger.


战术信息

  • Bulk Fabricators can be taken down with 几个 methods:
    • Demolished via the vents using explosives with a 最小 拆除力 of 20. E.G. a G-4 毒气 手榴弹.
      • A 单发 of the R-36 Eruptor to the vents will 摧毁 it.
      • A G-123 铝热弹 手榴弹 can 摧毁 a Bulk 构筑者 if it manages to enter through one of the vents, although this can be 困难 to accomplish as the 手榴弹 can stick to the sides of a 通风口.
    • Applying 最小 爆破力 of 50 for the 外圈 shell. E.G. 正面 hit of an 轨道武器 毒气 Strike.
    • Causing enough 护甲穿透 of  反坦克 I - 伤害 until the 主生命值 has been depleted.
      • the FAF-14 Spear can 摧毁 a Bulk 构筑者 in 1 shot; the GR-8 无后坐力炮, EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器，以及 LAS-99 类星体加农炮 in 2 shots; the E/AT-12 反坦克炮台, EXO-45 爱国者 外骨骼机甲，以及 MLS-4X 突击兵 rockets 要求 4 shots.
      • Due to their large 浮出地面 area, an “飞鹰”机枪扫射 can easily 摧毁 a Bulk 构筑者, provided most of the shells 连接.
  • It is possible to use a Cannon Turret, Annihilator Tank，或 移动工厂 to 摧毁 Bulk 构筑者.
  • Upon 摧毁 the 构筑者 will 爆炸 with a large amount of force, ragdolling, damaging, or killing any 敌人 或附近的绝地潜兵。
    • A 绝地潜兵 getting out of a 绝地喷射舱 will be 受保护 from the 构筑者 爆炸 during the deployment 动画.
      • Alternatively calling down a 绝地喷射舱-战略配备 will also 摧毁 a Bulk 构筑者 with the 内容 surviving the following 爆炸.
