the 巨型构筑者 是一个 机器人 megastructure found exclusively at 机器人哨站 in cities. They periodically deploy 重型 敌人 such as 巨型者 until they are destroyed.

5) 构造 acts as a secondary 生命池 that, once Main or limb 生命值 has been depleted, may decay. The first number is the total 构造 and the second is the 衰退速率. If set to 0/s there is no decay and the 构造 behaves as extra 生命值.
6) ExDR means 爆炸 伤害抗性, and can 射程 from 0 to 100%. If set to 100%, the limb cannot take 爆炸伤害; The 爆炸伤害 will instead 目标 Main. Refer to 伤害 for more information.
  • Note: This 机械师, known as Affected By 爆炸 can only occur once per 爆炸, regardless of 100% ExDR parts hit, and the 爆炸's 护甲穿透 must match or exceed 主体护甲 to deal 伤害 对主血量. However, if the same 爆炸 has already damaged Main, this 机械师 will not trigger.


战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • Bulk Fabricators can be taken down with 几个 methods:
    • Demolished via the vents using explosives with a 最小 拆除力 of 20. E.G. a G-4 毒气 手榴弹.
      • A 单发 of the R-36 Eruptor to the vents will 摧毁 it.
      • A G-123 铝热弹 手榴弹 can 摧毁 a Bulk 构筑者 if it manages to enter through one of the vents, although this can be 困难 to accomplish as the 手榴弹 can stick to the sides of a vent.
    • Applying 最小 爆破 force of 50 for the outer shell. E.G. Direct hit of an 轨道武器 毒气 Strike.
    • Causing enough 护甲穿透 of  反坦克 I - 伤害 until the 主生命值 has been depleted.
      • the FAF-14 Spear can 摧毁 a Bulk 构筑者 in 1 shot; the GR-8 无后坐力炮, EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器，以及 LAS-99 类星体加农炮 in 2 shots; the E/AT-12 反坦克炮台, EXO-45 爱国者 外骨骼机甲，以及 MLS-4X 突击兵 rockets 要求 4 shots.
      • Due to their large 浮出地面 area, an “飞鹰”机枪扫射 can easily 摧毁 a Bulk 构筑者, provided most of the shells 连接.
  • It is possible to use a Cannon Turret, Annihilator Tank，或 移动工厂 to 摧毁 Bulk 构筑者.
  • Upon 摧毁 the 构筑者 will 爆炸 with a large amount of force, ragdolling, damaging, or killing any 敌人 or 绝地潜兵 nearby.
    • A 绝地潜兵 getting out of a 绝地喷射舱 will be 受保护 from the 构筑者 爆炸 during the deployment 动画.
      • Alternatively calling down a 绝地喷射舱-战略配备 will also 摧毁 a Bulk 构筑者 with the 内容 surviving the following 爆炸.
