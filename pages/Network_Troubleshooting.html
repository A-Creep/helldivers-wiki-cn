Basics

绝地潜兵 2 is a multiplayer game on a hybridized P2P (Peer-to-Peer) 网络 architecture.[1][2] In simple terms this means you are connecting both to a centralized server 网络 as well as to other 玩家 directly in order to play, 含义 your 电脑 will keep up two completely 分离 类型 of 网络 connections simultaneously:

  • 集中式数据库（专用服务器）: This constant 连接 is used to communicate with Arrowhead's 主体 servers. These handle your persistent player profile data (such as Player Level, unlocked 内容, 超级货币, etc.).
  • 游戏会话（P2P 网络）: A 正面 连接 that is used for connecting to other 玩家 themselves. In such connections your 电脑 (or 控制台) acts both as server and client. The 小队长是游戏主机 in such connections, while other 玩家 are clients.

This hybrid approach can explain a very common 故障排除 paradox:

  • If you are able to 连接 to the game but can't join other 玩家, then that indicates a possible issue with your P2P 连接性.
  • If you are not able to even log into the game, your 网络 issues might be more fundamental.

The first of those two is more common and might feel paradoxical because you might be able to play other multiplayer games with 否 problems - but if those do not rely on P2P, you will not notice that something is amiss.

VPNs & other 软件

The 使用 of Virtual 列兵 Networks (VPNs) can be a double-edged sword when it comes to 绝地潜兵 2. Due to the vast differences between 互联网 服务 providers (ISPs), routing paths, local router settings, etc., there's 否 true one-size-fits-all 解决方案. A VPN configuration that breaks the game for one player might be the exact 修复 another player needs to 连接 at all.

If your ISP utilizes strict 航母-Grade NAT (CGNAT), a so-called "Dual NAT", or similar problematic configurations, your router can potentially struggle to 建立 正面 P2P-connections with other 玩家. In such scenarios, a VPN can act as a bridge by routing your traffic and bypass your provider's local routing restrictions. However, many 标准 VPN protocols inject significant latency (ping) or actively block unprompted inbound P2P traffic for 安全 reasons, which then cause disconnects. One such 示例 would be CloudFlare WARP: While its 安全 protocol technically interferes with P2P data streams, there have been reports of 玩家 being able to bypass their ISP's restrictions by enabling iit.

As a rule of thumb it's 重要 that your VPN provider of choice explicitly supports and allows Peer-to-Peer (P2P) traffic. If P2P is blocked at the server level, you won't be able to join up with others. Please also be aware that Free VPN services almost always 限制 P2P traffic entirely or heavily throttle bandwidth and latency, making them poor choices for multiplayer games that 要求 low latency.

Deep Packet 检查

If you are using Deep Packet 检查 (DPI)-软件 (such as Zapret) as bypass tools or 网络 限制 workarounds, this may interfere with the game's 网络 protocol. Because they manipulate TCP/IP packet structures and HTTP headers on a low system level to bypass regional blocks, they directly 冲突 with the game's 网络 正直 checks.

Using these tools simultaneously with the game will likely result in:

  • Total loss of server 连接性.
  • Frequent "连接 Timed Out" errors.
  • Inability to authenticate or log in.
  • Anticheat error 10002039

DNS

One of the most 有效 ways to 解决 连接性 problems is to check your DNS setup as a lot of 网络-相关 problems originate here.

"DNS" stands for "Domain Name System", which is a hierarchical, decentralized 目录 that 充当 the phonebook of the 互联网. It translates human-readable domain names (such as 绝地潜兵.wiki.gg) into numerical IP addresses that machines 要求 to locate and communicate with servers.[3]

By 默认, your router automatically 使用次数 the DNS servers provided by your 互联网 服务 Provider (ISP). However, those ISP-run DNS servers are notoriously slow to 更新 their DNS records and quite 频繁 suffer from configuration errors. If a recent 绝地潜兵 2 server 更新 alters its 目标 IP addresses, an outdated ISP DNS will fail to 解决 the 连接, leading to all manners of 连接 problems.

If flushing your local DNS cache did not 解决 your 连接性 issues, the underlying 问题 is likely at the server level. In this scenario, the best course of action is to manually configure your 网络 settings to use a fast, reliable, third-party public DNS.

NAT

One of the most basic things to check and 排除故障 should be your 网络地址转换 (NAT). To make a long story short: NAT is a protocol running on your local router which translates the 列兵 IP addresses of your home devices into a single, global public IP 地址 which is then used to communicate with the 互联网 as a whole. So your PC, consoles, smartphones, etc. share one 互联网 连接 instead of all of them having their own public addresses.

航母-Grade NAT

One of the biggest hurdles that can interfere with 绝地潜兵 2's P2P networking is the presence of a so-called 航母 Grade NAT (CGNAT). It's used by 互联网 服务 Providers (ISPs) in order to mitigate IPv4 地址 exhaustion[5].

While it exists as a necessary lifesaver for 互联网 基础设施[6], this very technology creates a 屏障 for multiplayer games. As hundreds of households are 基本上 forced to share the exact same public IP 地址, incoming 正面 连接 requests are then blocked at the ISP level before they even reach your home router. This interferes with games that 要求 P2P connections and can also cause problems with Remote Access or 标准 Port Forwarding.

The issues below can also appear with Dual Stack Lite connections.

Solutions for NATs

If your diagnostic tests 确认 the presence of a 航母-Grade NAT (CGNAT) or a Double NAT,there are some options you can do in order to 解决 the situation.

  1. 联系您的互联网服务提供商： In most cases, you can 解决 CGNAT entirely at the 来源. Call your ISP's 顾客 支援 hotline or open a ticket stating that you “要求 a public IPv4 地址 for remote work VPNs and peer-to-peer online gaming.” Many major providers will switch your 账户 from a shared CGNAT profile to a dedicated, dynamic public IPv4 地址, often for an additional fee or while changing your plan to a business plan. Be prepared for the additional one-time or recurring 费用 this may incur.
  2. 使用高级 VPN： If your ISP refuses to grant a public IP, using a high-quality VPN with dedicated P2P 支援 is your best alternative. As detailed above, a paid VPN tunnels your traffic past the provider's restrictive 翻译 layers. Alternatively, you can rent a VPS or a root server (make sure it comes with a dedicated IPv4 地址) and build your own port forwarding VPN using Wireguard.
  3. 消除内部双重 NAT： If your traceroute showed two hops but your external IPs matched, the 瓶颈 is inside your own house. This can happen in scenarios where you daisy-chain a gaming router into a modem/router combo supplied by your ISP. You can 解决 such setups by either disconnecting the second router or logging into it and change its 操作 mode from "Router" to "Access Point (AP Mode)". Alternatively you might have to enable "Bridge Mode / Passthrough" on your ISP's modem.
  4. 更改你的连接类型： If 否 other options are available, switching to a different 网络 基础设施 that offers native public routing (such as transitioning from a restrictive 5G home cube to a stable DSL 连接) will permanently 消除 the issue. Most cable (i.e. Vodafone) and fiber connections as well as mobile (i.e. 5G) and 卫星 connections (i.e. Starlink) make use of CGNAT or Dual Stack Lite.

These issues result in random link drops with rapid reconnects. Often these can occur so quickly that Windows does not even notify the user, however the link drops are disruptive enough to cause a 断开 from the rest of the lobby. Please note that this specific issue does not only affect 绝地潜兵 2 but also other games.

Diagnosing the Link Drops

To 确认 if your 网络 adapter is actively dropping connections, you can check the Windows background logs:

  1. Press the Windows键, 类型 "Event Viewer", and press 进入.
  2. Expand "Windows Logs" on the left 侧边栏 and select "System".
  3. Look for 警告 entries labeled with the 来源 "e1dexpress" (Intel) or "Rt640" (Realtek).
  4. If you see logs stating 网络连接已断开 (Event ID 27) your hardware requires an immediate driver 更新.

Recommended Driver Fixes

Both Intel and Realtek released new drivers in 2月 2025 that are intended to 地址 these specific link drops. These driver packages are compatible with 许多 Intel and Realtek 网络 adapters. If you have an Intel or Realtek 网络 adapter that is 1Gb, 2.5Gb, 5Gb or 10Gb, you can use these packages to 更新 your 网络 drivers as they are fully backward compatible with most of those adapters from both brands.

  • 官员 Intel Ethernet Driver 包 for Windows 11

Login Problems

PlayFab Error 35

If you are unable to login and 接收 "PlayFab Error 35", then this means CURLE_SSL_连接_ERROR. This error indicates that your device failed to 建立 a secure cryptographic handshake when attempting to authenticate against Arrowhead's PlayFab 数据库.

Due to the nature of these SSL/TLS certificates and their precise expiration timestamps, this failure is almost always triggered because your system's clock has drifted out of sync with the global atomic time servers. Luckily it can be fixed by resynchronizing your system clock.

WindowsLinux

  1. 按下 WIN + R 打开"运行"对话框，输入 services.msc，然后点击"确定"。
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the "services" list, right-click "Windows Time", and select "Start" (or "重新开始" if it is already running). Close the window.
  3. 打开 Windows 搜索栏，输入 cmd，然后按 CTRL + SHIFT + ENTER 以管理员身份启动命令提示符。
  4. 类型 the following 指挥 and press Enter to force an atomic clock synchronization: w32tm /resync
  • 成功： 您现在应该能看到确认消息： 命令已成功完成。
  • 失败： 如果您收到服务未运行或超时的错误，请重新运行 w32tm /resync 再次执行命令直到确认。如果未发生，您可能需要先处理设置，例如阻止NTP port 123的激进防火墙。

第一步，请检查您的时间是否不同步：

  1. Open your 终端 (CTRL + ALT + T).
  2. Check your 当前 synchronization metrics by running timedatectl status
  3. Look at the line "System clock synchronized". If it says "否", your system time is drifted and blocking 安全 certificates.

如果您的系统时间确实不同步，您可以强制系统立即从公共服务器获取干净的时间戳。请依次执行以下命令：

  1. Enable the 网络 time protocol daemon: sudo timedatectl set-ntp true
  2. 重新开始 the time synchronization 服务 to force an 瞬间 更新: sudo systemctl 重新开始 systemd-timesyncd.服务
  3. Verify the 修复 by running timedatectl status 再次运行。"System clock synchronized"值现在应显示为"是"。

Status Code 400 / Error Code 1600

The error is caused by 不寻常 characters in your Steam username.

Switching it for an alphanumerical (a-Z and 0-9) username should solve the issue.

Starlink

There are known 连接性 issues with wired connections that 连接 to Starlink UTR-211 调制解调器. This 问题 seems to be rather old and has never been truly addressed, leading to IPv6 addresses randomly not being properly passed through the switch of the modem.

You can attempt different methods to 解决 these issues:

  1. If you are wired into a router that is then connected to the Starlink mode, set your router's IPv6 mode to "autoconfigure" instead of "autodetect".
  2. Play on wireless with 否 other 变更.
  3. Disable IPv6 on your 网络 adapter (Windows -> Uncheck "互联网 Protocol Version 6") and see if that fixes it. If it does, you can just do this before you play the game, but it 通常 fixes other 连接 issues on other applications as well.

有时 it may also work with IPv6 enabled.
