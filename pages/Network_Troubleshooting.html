Basics[edit | edit 来源]

绝地潜兵 2 is a multiplayer game on a hybridized P2P (Peer-to-Peer) 网络 architecture.[1][2] In simple terms this means you are connecting both to a centralized server 网络 as well as to other 玩家 directly in order to play, 含义 your 电脑 will keep up two completely 分离 类型 of 网络 connections simultaneously:

  • Centralized 数据库 (Dedicated Server): This constant 连接 is used to communicate with Arrowhead's main servers. These handle your persistent player profile data (such as Player Level, unlocked 内容, 超级货币, etc.).
  • 玩法 Session (P2P 网络): A direct 连接 that is used for connecting to other 玩家 themselves. In such connections your 电脑 (or 控制台) acts both as server and client. The Squadleader is the host of the game in such connections, while other 玩家 are clients.

This hybrid approach can explain a very common 故障排除 paradox:

  • If you are able to 连接 to the game but can't join other 玩家, then that indicates a possible issue with your P2P 连接性.
  • If you are not able to even log into the game, your 网络 issues might be more fundamental.

The first of those two is more common and might feel paradoxical because you might be able to play other multiplayer games with no problems - but if those do not rely on P2P, you will not notice that something is amiss.

VPNs & other 软件[edit | edit 来源]

The 使用 of Virtual 列兵 Networks (VPNs) can be a double-edged sword when it comes to 绝地潜兵 2. Due to the vast differences between 互联网 服务 providers (ISPs), routing paths, local router settings, etc., there's no true one-size-fits-all 解决方案. A VPN configuration that breaks the game for one player might be the exact fix another player needs to 连接 at all.

If your ISP utilizes strict 航母-Grade NAT (CGNAT), a so-called "Dual NAT", or similar problematic configurations, your router can potentially struggle to 建立 direct P2P-connections with other 玩家. In such scenarios, a VPN can act as a bridge by routing your traffic and bypass your provider's local routing restrictions. However, many 标准 VPN protocols inject significant latency (ping) or actively block unprompted inbound P2P traffic for 安全 reasons, which then cause disconnects. One such 示例 would be CloudFlare WARP: While its 安全 protocol technically interferes with P2P data streams, there have been reports of 玩家 being able to bypass their ISP's restrictions by enabling iit.

As a rule of thumb it's 重要 that your VPN provider of choice explicitly supports and allows Peer-to-Peer (P2P) traffic. If P2P is blocked at the server level, you won't be able to join up with others. Please also be aware that Free VPN services almost always 限制 P2P traffic entirely or heavily throttle bandwidth and latency, making them poor choices for multiplayer games that 要求 low latency.

NAT[edit | edit 来源]

One of the most basic things to check and 排除故障 should be your 网络 地址 翻译 (NAT). To make a long story short: NAT is a protocol running on your local router which translates the 列兵 IP addresses of your home devices into a single, global public IP 地址 which is then used to communicate with the 互联网 as a whole. So your PC, consoles, smartphones, etc. share one 互联网 连接 instead of all of them having their own public addresses.

航母-Grade NAT[edit | edit 来源]

One of the biggest hurdles that can interfere with 绝地潜兵 2's P2P networking is the presence of a so-called 航母 Grade NAT (CGNAT). It's used by 互联网 服务 Providers (ISPs) in order to mitigate IPv4 地址 exhaustion[5].

While it exists as a necessary lifesaver for 互联网 基础设施[6], this very technology creates a 屏障 for multiplayer games. As hundreds of households are 基本上 forced to share the exact same public IP 地址, incoming direct 连接 requests are then blocked at the ISP level before they even reach your home router. This interferes with games that 要求 P2P connections and can also cause problems with Remote Access or 标准 Port Forwarding.

The issues below can also appear with Dual Stack Lite connections.

  3. Look at the line "System clock synchronized". If it says "no", your system time is drifted and blocking 安全 certificates.

If your time is indeed out of sync, you can force your system to immediately fetch a clean timestamp from public servers. Follow these commands one after the other:

  1. Enable the 网络 time protocol daemon: sudo timedatectl set-ntp true
  2. 重新开始 the time synchronization 服务 to force an 瞬间 更新: sudo systemctl 重新开始 systemd-timesyncd.服务
  3. Verify the fix by running timedatectl status again. The "System clock synchronized" value should now 显示 as "yes".

Status Code 400 / Error Code 1600[edit | edit 来源]

The error is caused by 不寻常 characters in your Steam username.

Switching it for an alphanumerical (a-Z and 0-9) username should solve the issue.

Starlink[edit | edit 来源]

There are known 连接性 issues with wired connections that 连接 to Starlink UTR-211 modems. This 问题 seems to be rather old and has never been truly addressed, leading to IPv6 addresses randomly not being properly passed through the switch of the modem.

You can attempt different methods to 解决 these issues:

  1. If you are wired into a router that is then connected to the Starlink mode, set your router's IPv6 mode to "autoconfigure" instead of "autodetect".
  2. Play on wireless with no other changes.
  3. Disable IPv6 on your 网络 adapter (Windows -> Uncheck "互联网 Protocol Version 6") and see if that fixes it. If it does, you can just do this before you play the game, but it 通常 fixes other 连接 issues on other applications as well.

有时 it may also work with IPv6 enabled.
