概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

玩家 can use 奖牌 to 声称 various items, including a 强化资源, 3 武器, 2 armors, 6 cosmetics, and 300 超级货币. These 奖励 are spread across three pages, 每个 requiring increasingly 更多 奖牌 (ranging from 7 to 65). 玩家 开始 on 页面 one, unlocking subsequent pages as they 花费 更多 奖牌, gradually expanding their customization options.

目录[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • the SG-20 Halt 与……相似 SG-8 Punisher, but can alternate between stun 发 and 护甲-penetrating flechettes.
  • the 冲锋枪-32 Reprimand is a two-handed 冲锋枪, capable of firing 中型护甲-penetrating 发.
  • the PLAS-15 Loyalist is a semi-auto 等离子 手枪 that can be charged to deal 更多 伤害, similar to the PLAS-101 Purifier.
  • 不动摇 (盔甲被动加成) 降低 staggering when hit, in addition to increasing 护甲值 by 25 and providing 标记 雷达 scans.
  • 死亡冲刺 (强化资源) allows 绝地潜兵 to 继续 running with depleted 耐力, at the 费用 of draining their 生命值.
