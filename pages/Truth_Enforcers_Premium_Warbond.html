概述[edit | edit 来源]

玩家 can use Medals to claim various items, including a 强化资源, 3 武器, 2 armors, 6 cosmetics, and 300 超级货币. These 奖励 are spread across three pages, each requiring increasingly more Medals (ranging from 7 to 65). 玩家 start on page one, unlocking subsequent pages as they spend more Medals, gradually expanding their customization options.

目录[edit | edit 来源]

  • the SG-20 Halt 与……相似 SG-8 Punisher, but can alternate between stun rounds and 护甲-penetrating flechettes.
  • the SMG-32 Reprimand is a two-handed 冲锋枪, capable of firing 中型护甲-penetrating rounds.
  • the PLAS-15 Loyalist is a semi-auto plasma pistol that can be charged to deal more 伤害, similar to the PLAS-101 Purifier.
  • 不动摇 (盔甲被动加成) 降低 staggering when hit, in addition to increasing 护甲值 by 25 and providing marker radar scans.
  • 死亡冲刺 (强化资源) allows 绝地潜兵 to 继续 running with depleted 耐力, at the 费用 of draining their 生命值.
