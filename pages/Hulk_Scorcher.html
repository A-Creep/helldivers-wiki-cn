巨型炙炎者[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Barring 防火 and 应变自如-equipped 绝地潜兵, the 巨型者 Scorcher's 火焰喷射器 has a far reach and can quickly 击杀 绝地潜兵. 绝地潜兵 should react to burns by diving to put themselves out.

巨型者[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Heavily armed and 有护甲, Hulks are powerful 敌人 that demand 特殊 attention due to their 耐久度 and 重型 武器 载荷, as they are capable of killing 绝地潜兵 in 短 命令.
  • Hulks are covered in  重型 护甲, but they do have two 弱 spots: the glowing 高温 sink on their backs which are  无护甲 II, and their 头部, which only has 250 生命值 and will 击杀 a 巨型者 if destroyed.
    • Hulks vents can be damaged by 任何 武器, but are rather 耐久. It is advised to use a 更高-伤害 支援武器 to 更多 easily 摧毁 the vents. Destroying its vents will 原因 the 巨型者 to go into a bleedout 陈述, where they can be seen hobbling and flailing its 武器.

    • the RS-422 磁轨炮, APW-1 反器材步枪, and S-11 Speargun are capable of destroying its 头部 with a 单发.
  • The 巨型者's slow 移动速度 make them particularly 易受 dedicated 反坦克武器 such as the EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器, LAS-99 类星体加农炮, and GR-8 无后坐力炮 and are capable of destroying a 巨型者 in a 单发.
    • Due to their poor mobility, Hulks can also be easily destroyed by 最多 进攻型 爆炸 战略配备 such as the 飞鹰 500kg 炸弹, “飞鹰”机枪扫射, and 飞鹰 110mm 火箭巢.
    • 单个 G-123 铝热弹 will inflict enough 伤害 to 摧毁 a 巨型者 否 matter where on its 身体 it is placed.
  • While 更多 耐久 than its 弱 spots, the 巨型者's limbs can 经常 be 有效 targets for 摧毁 depending on the situation.
    • The 巨型者's arm joints can be 射击 and destroyed, causing them to 掉落 that arm's respective 武器. 一次 两者 手臂 are destroyed, Hulks attempt to 践踏 on 绝地潜兵 instead.
    • The 巨型者's 腿部 can also be 射击 and destroyed; destroying one leg cripples its movement, and destroying 两者 腿部 will 击杀 the 巨型者.
      • The 巨型者's 腿部 are 经常 许多 更多 convenient targets than its 头部 or 高温 sinks. Destroying a leg will 硬直 it for 几个 秒 and significantly slow its 移动速度.
