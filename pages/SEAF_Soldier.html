    超级地球武装部队战士

    The SEAF 战士 is a  超级地球 联邦 infantry unit seen on the streets of Mega Cities and deployed from 鹈鹕 Shuttles on planets with '重型 SEAF Presence' 修正 present. Equipped with various 武器库, they act as supporting troops for the 绝地潜兵.

    背景故事

    主页: 超级地球 武装力量

    The 超级地球 武装力量 (S.E.A.F/SEAF) are the primary 军事 forces of 超级地球. SEAF consists of 几个 branches, all under the 权威 of the Ministry of 防御. SEAF Soldiers fight in the 防御 of 超级地球 across 多种星球 throughout the galaxy.

    行为

    • SEAF Soldiers are often seen in squads of 5-7, often near Civilians, where they will be actively fighting or seeking out 敌对 forces.
      • 每个生成的超级地球武装部队士兵都会以绿色小点标记在绝地潜兵的小地图上。
    • SEAF Soldiers may also be deployed via 鹈鹕 to a 绝地潜兵's position during the "重型 SEAF Presence" 行星 修正.
    • 超级地球武装部队士兵装备各种武器，在战场上履行各自的职责：
      • SEAF Squad Leaders are equipped with modified AR-23 解放者 特攻 Rifles and G-12 High 爆炸 Grenades. They have a shoulder pad on their right shoulder and a large Radio Pack. Their 头盔 features a 麦克风 extension used for Radio 通讯 and Squad Coordination.
        • Despite being visually armed with the AR-23 解放者, they 火焰 AR-23P 解放者 Penetrator 发, allowing them to engage  中型 有护甲 targets.
        • A Squad Leader will lead a squad of SEAF Infantry around the map. He does not possess any kind of 特殊能力, like coordinating the Squad to 找掩护 or focus 火焰.[1]
        • 如果绝地潜兵命令小队队长跟随他们，该绝地潜兵将获得整支小队的控制权。
      • SEAF Riflemen have the same 武装配置 as Squad Leaders, but without the shoulder pad, large Radio Pack, or 头盔 mic. 偶尔 they can be seen with a backpack.
        • 一旦进入接战距离（通常35米内），超级地球武装部队步枪兵会用步枪攻击敌方目标，并偶尔投掷手雷。
          • These grenades can harm 绝地潜兵, so it is recommended to be aware of when and where they are thrown. It is also possible to recognise them from the glowing yellow flash they emit.
      • SEAF Specialists are equipped with MG-43 Machine Guns and an EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器 Launchers. They are the most visually 有护甲, having shoulder pads, wrist guards, shin guards, and a neck guard on their chest 护甲. Their 头盔 遗骸 标准 issue.
        • 一旦进入接战距离，超级地球武装部队专家会进入蹲姿，持续用机枪射击。
          • It is advisable to stay out of their field of 火焰 while they are in this state; they will shoot through 绝地潜兵 to hit targets. Treat them as mobile A/MG-43 机枪 Sentry.
        • When encountering high-生命值 or 有护甲 targets, they will 火焰 their 消耗性反坦克武器 launchers at those targets.
      • SEAF Medics are equipped with SMG-37 捍卫者 Sub-Machine Guns, P-11 Stim Pistols and Stims. These troopers can also be identified by the stripes on their 护甲 and a red backpack containing medical 物资. Their helmets are unique, the sides protruding forwards to cover their cheeks.
        • 如果任何超级地球武装部队士兵或绝地潜兵受伤，超级地球武装部队军医会尝试用治疗剂手枪远程治疗他们，或近距离注射治疗剂。
        • Stims administered by SEAF Medics, 无论 whether they are applied manually or via a 治疗剂手枪, will not be affected by 绝地潜兵 equipping the 试验性注射剂 强化资源.
    • With 更新 1.007.000 the SEAF Soldiers have received improvements to their AI.
      • 超级地球武装部队士兵现在能够更可靠地摧毁敌方哨站、巢穴和营地。
      • SEAF Soldiers will avoid Eagle 战略配备 thrown by the 绝地潜兵, attempting to run away from them. They will also avoid 火焰 on the ground. They are, however, still oblivious to 轨道武器 战略配备, Mines and 绝地潜兵 armed Hellbombs.
      • If there are 否 敌人 nearby SEAF will start making their way towards the nearest 目标 位置 or enemy presence. If all have been completed or destroyed they will instead make their way towards 撤离.
    • Up to 8 SEAF Soldiers can be recruited by each 绝地潜兵 by using the "FOLLOW ME" 指挥 in the comms wheel when in close proximity. SEAF Soldiers can spawn max 8 units on Mega City Maps, and the 生成 循环 will 继续 when a SEAF 战士 has died or is unloaded to spawn again.
      • On planets with the '重型 SEAF Presence' 修正 the max is higher. 3 鹈鹕 Shuttles can each deploy one Squad of 6 SEAF Soldiers simultaneously, deploying a total of 18 SEAF Soldiers, going beyond the Mega City limit. Groups as high as 35 have been encountered.
      • Using the Casual Salute or Lockstep emote near them, while they are not actively fighting, will 达成 the same 效果. They will also 回应 with a salute in kind.
        • 值得注意的是，超级地球武装部队士兵用左手敬礼，而绝地潜兵总是用右手敬礼。
      • Recruited SEAF Soldiers will 继续 to follow until the 绝地潜兵 dies, extracts, or 使用次数 the "WAIT" 指挥 in the comms wheel. Using the “WAIT” 指挥 twice will disperse them to stop following you and will cause them to act on their own again.
      • 跟随一名绝地潜兵的超级地球武装部队士兵仍可被另一名绝地潜兵招募，从而允许通过合作战术带领他们穿越战场。
    • 超级地球武装部队士兵在诸如击倒重型单位或附近绝地潜兵死亡等事件时拥有特殊语音台词。
    • When SEAF Soldiers are heavily injured, they will enter a downed state, unable to move or fight until they are healed by using Stim or P-11 治疗剂手枪. If they are not treated in time, they will 流血 out and die.
    • 非战斗时，超级地球武装部队士兵可以以多种方式与绝地潜兵的表情互动并作出回应。
      • Some emotes, like the 显示 of Brawn, can illicit cheers from the SEAF.
      • The SEAF Soldiers can interact with the Hug and various handshake emotes.
        • The Scout Handshake will cause the SEAF 战士 to either look at their hand dejectedly, or to perform a gesture as though they are surveying the area.
        • The High-Five will cause the 绝地潜兵 to pull the SEAF in for a head bump, resulting in the 装甲兵 being knocked back and holding their head for a moment.
        • The 爆炸 Handshake will make the SEAF 战士 mimic the hand gesture of the 绝地潜兵.

    战术信息

    • SEAF Soldiers spawn in Mega Cities and on planets with the 重型 SEAF Presence 修正. They are deployed via 鹈鹕 shuttles.
      • 在超级城市中，降落时现场就会有一支超级地球武装部队士兵小队，让绝地潜兵可以快速招募他们为己所用。
      • In 撤离高价值资产 missions that take place in Mega Cities, there will always be a single squad of SEAF Soldiers present at the 任务 site in the beginning, and more will be deployed as the 任务 progresses through 鹈鹕 shuttles.
      • SEAF Soldiers deployed from 鹈鹕 Shuttles follow a 基本信息 Squad composition consisting of 1 Squad Leader, 2 Riflemen, 2 Specialists, and 1 Medic. The SEAF Soldiers will follow their Squad Leader.
    • Each SEAF 战士 killed by 正面 actions of a 绝地潜兵 immediately deducts 25 申购点 Slips from the 当前 amount for the 任务. This includes Soldiers killed by automated 战略配备 such as the AX/AR-23 "Guard Dog" and A/G-16 加特林哨戒炮.
      • SEAF soldiers following the 绝地潜兵 属性 their kills to the 绝地潜兵. As such, any civilians or SEAF they kill detracts from the 绝地潜兵 leading them. This will not be represented in player death screen messages.
      • If a 绝地潜兵 is caught 9 times recklessly killing SEAF Soldiers or Civilians, they will be branded as a 叛徒. As such, the 超级驱逐舰 will begin firing 380mm HE Shells at the 绝地潜兵 until they are killed. Mines will not add to the 叛徒 tally but will still deduct 申购点 Slips.
    • The A/弧度-3 特斯拉塔 does not react to SEAF Soldiers in its proximity[2]. However, if an 弧度 is triggered by someone else, it will still kill the SEAF 战士.

    琐事

    • With the release of Devoid of Liberty, SEAF soldiers' preexisting and added 主武器 and MG-43 Machine Guns were given new color schemes, with the 标准 variation featuring the same blue and white found on their 护甲. For some reason, the colors for EAT-17 Expendable Anti-Tanks equipped by SEAF soldiers were not 已更改, and still sport the traditional 绝地潜兵 black and yellow.
    • SEAF soldiers' color schemes for their 护甲, uniforms, and 武器 change depending on the 环境 in which they are deployed:[3]
      • In Mega Cities and on planets within the Void, SEAF units are equipped with their 标准 colors of 轻型 blue 护甲 and 武器, and white uniforms.
      • 在寒冷星球上，超级地球武装部队单位装备海军蓝盔甲、白色武器，以及蓝灰色迷彩制服。
      • 在干旱星球上，超级地球武装部队单位装备棕褐色盔甲和武器，以及棕褐色迷彩制服。
      • On forested planets, SEAF Medics specifically are equipped with white 护甲, while the other 类型 of troopers are given dull green 护甲. All units are equipped with dull green 武器 and uniforms with a brown and green 迷彩 涂装.
    • According to Arrowhead Community Manager miitchimus, the SEAF Medic’s name is Stimmothy.[4]
      • This name is a wordplay on the word "Stim" and the name Timmothy.
    • A 绝地潜兵 2 Xbox News post shared the names of the other three SEAF Soldiers.[5]
      • Squad Leader - C. Zhang
      • 步枪手 - A. Aaronson
      • 专家 - B. Devaux
    • 社区通常用两个“昵称”称呼超级地球武装部队士兵：SEAFie和蓝莓，后者指他们的蓝色配色。
