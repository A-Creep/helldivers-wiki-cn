背景故事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Construction[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

“

The Ministry of 防御 has awarded a multitrillion-Supercredit contract to AM 防御 to 展开 生产 of 高-quality 军事 装备 for the 绝地潜兵. AM 防御 has a proven 历史 of constructing top-of-the-line armaments for the 超级地球武装部队 line units, and their 扩展包 to providing 绝地潜兵 武器库 marks the beginning of an exciting new 章节 in the 防御-Marketplace Partnership.

To 开始 this new 章节, AM 防御 will construct a 庞大. multi-复杂 工厂 Hub on Achernar Secundus, creating millions of exciting labor opportunities for 超级地球 citizens. However, 温度 readings shows an 增加 of 终结族 activity in the Jin XI and Sten 星区, seemingly in 回应 to this momentous project. This supports the theory of 许多 终结族 experts: the 外星 race 5月 instinctively sense and 攻击 areas with 重型 Democratic activity.

The 绝地潜兵 must 保护 the investments of our 安全 partners. They are ordered to ensure the construction of the AM 防御 工厂 Hub on Achernar Secundus continues unimpeded.

— 重要指令 A1-12-2

The 工厂 Hub was constructed on Achernar Secundus on 12月 10th, 2184. The 绝地潜兵 where tasked with defending the 设施 from 终结族 攻击 until the construction could be completed.

However, this 重要指令 was cancelled with the 惊讶 回报 of The 光能者 on the 行星 Calypso.

光能者 攻击[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

During the "防御 许多 Planets from The 光能者" 重要指令, the 工厂 Hub found itself 遭受攻击 by the resurgent 光能者, threatening 生产 of the Fast 侦察 载具. Fortunately, the 绝地潜兵 defended the 行星 in 时间, and development of the car resumed.
