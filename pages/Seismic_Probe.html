A Seismic Probe 战略配备 is Seismic Probe fitted into a 绝地喷射舱. It is preprogrammed so that only the simplest calibration and execution 顺序 is needed once deployed to the 浮出地面. It is only available during the 进行地质勘测 任务 and is used to start and 完成 the "Deploy Seismic Probe" sub-目标.
