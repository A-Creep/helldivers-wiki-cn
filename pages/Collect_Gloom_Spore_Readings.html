“

Gloom spores suffuse the 行星's 大气层 from the 地面 to the exosphere. It is possible that this is key to its 能力 to interfere with the 光能者 machinations. Utilize the scattered LiDAR towers normally used to 采集 topographical 数据 and use them to measure the density and composition of Gloom spores.

Activate the towers, 下载 their 数据, and transmit it off-行星 using the local relay 车站.

— 任务 Briefing

收集 Gloom 孢子 Readings is a 任务 in 绝地潜兵 2. 绝地潜兵 are tasked with activating one or 更多 Lidar Stations and a 发电机, then reactivating the 卫星 Uplink 车站 and uploading the 数据. Despite being called a 数据 合集 任务, transporting an 固态硬盘 Drive is not 必需 to 完成 the final 目标.
