虫窝护卫

The 虫窝护卫 is a 中型-sized 终结族 with frontal  中型 护甲. They are able to enter a crouching stance to 保护 their 脆弱 body.

生成

Hive Guards can be first found on 容易. They can spawn from Bug 突破, within patrols, and guarding 目标 and Bug Nests.

Starting on 挑战, they can be found guarding Minor Places of Interest.

行为

Hive Guards will approach 绝地潜兵, climbing over 地形 and 障碍 in an attempt to 攻击 with their claws at 近距离. They can strafe sideways, and will attempt to cut off 绝地潜兵 trying to run past them. Upon being shot a few times, be that nearby or directly, they will hunker down into a crouching stance, bringing their 前腿 closer to their body for about three 秒. If a 绝地潜兵 continuously fires their weapon, Hive Guards will 继续 to crouch until the 绝地潜兵 stops firing.

Similar to 武斗虫, Hive Guards can go into a "狂暴" state after their heads begin to 流血 out (specifically, when it releases green fluids from its head). This will briefly 增加 its 移动速度 before they die.

虫窝护卫能够向空气中释放信息素云，引发虫族入侵。

战术信息

  • Naturally,  中型 中型护甲 penetrating 武器库 and explosives are the best for dispatching Hive Guards.
  • If hitting its  Very 轻型 body is not a viable option in a situation,  中型 护甲 penetrating 武器库 and explosives are the best for dispatching Hive Guards.
  • Two 绝地潜兵 with  轻型 护甲 penetrating 武器库 can easily exploit the 虫窝护卫's defenses by firing at its head to 压制 it while another 绝地潜兵 flanks it.
  • 虫窝护卫蹲伏时正面仍然脆弱；其前腿和头部之间有一个小缝隙，可以击中主体。
    • 摧毁虫窝护卫的2只利爪也可以杀死它们。
  • 它的“狂暴”状态很少见，因为虫窝护卫在被多次击中头部后会优先蹲伏。
  • 不蹲伏时，可以通过射击护甲后方无护甲的部位来截断它的腿，降低其防护。
  • 高每秒伤害  中型 护甲 penetrating 武器库 such as the M-1000 Maxigun, MG-43 机枪, or SMG-32 Reprimand, can pierce the 头部护甲 and kill the 虫窝护卫 before it can become a 威胁.
  • 由于它们相对安静，如果绝地潜兵被其他虫子追着分散注意力，有时会被它们偷袭。
  • 火焰 is an 有效 反制 to Hive Guards due to its  重型 护甲穿透. This makes flamethrowers like the FLAM-40 火焰喷射器, and grenades like the G-10 燃烧弹 and G-13 燃烧弹 Impact particularly 克制 these 敌人, alongside other 武器 that can apply this 效果, like the SG-451 Cookout and the SG-225IE Breaker 燃烧弹.
