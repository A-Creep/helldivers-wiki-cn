the 碉堡 炮塔 is an 机器人 武器 星系 present at 机器人 structures.

生成[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

It is 最多 commonly seen atop 指挥碉堡, but have a 生成几率 at 机器人 occupied Minor Place of Interests, which 开始 appearing at 自杀任务 难度.

On 超级绝地潜兵难度 难度, it can be seen defending 机器人 Fortresses.

行为[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

The 碉堡 炮塔 slowly fires two times before cooling down for roughly five 秒, and has 非常 少量 警告 of when it is going to 火焰 at 绝地潜兵, besides it aiming at them.

战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The 正面 of the 碉堡 炮塔 is covered in  重型 护甲, while having a  中型 弱点 on its 返回. 任何  重型 护甲-penetrating 武器, such as the AC-8 机炮 and LAS-98 激光大炮, can 摧毁 them effectively.
    • 任何 反坦克 武器 can 1-射击 the 炮塔.
      • Notably, the FAF-14 飞矛 is incapable of locking onto a 碉堡 炮塔, as they are considered a 小 敌人.
    • the RS-422 磁轨炮 requires an 80% 冲锋 射击 to 摧毁.
    • the PLAS-45 Epoch requires a fully-charged 射击.
  • Damaging 榴弹 that have  重型 or above can 摧毁 a 碉堡 炮塔.
    • 铝热弹 can 1-射击 碉堡 炮塔 if it sticks to it.
    • 两者 G-12 高 爆炸 and TED-63 Dynamite can also 1-射击 碉堡 炮塔, but requires careful cooking of the 手榴弹.
  • Completing the 妥协 机器人 Defenses 可选目标 disables the 碉堡 炮塔's 能力 to 目标 绝地潜兵, making it practically harmless.
  • 碉堡 炮塔 can also be destroyed by 任何 of the 进攻型 战略配备 or 阵地 战略配备.
  • 碉堡 炮塔 have a 有限 射程 of around 150m. Using 长-远程 武器 such as the APW-1 反器材步枪 can 摧毁 them from afar.
  • The 炮塔's 激光 can severely 伤害 or outright 击杀 a 绝地潜兵 depending on their 护甲值, and can 伤害 the M-102 Fast 侦察 载具, making this 炮塔 a 高-priority 威胁.
  • The 炮塔's barrel depression can unintentionally clip through its 基地, causing it to 有时 火焰 even 更低 than expected.
  • 碉堡 炮塔 can be 困难 to spot at 夜 or during 天气 events, especially within 机器人 Fortresses.
    • As they are considered a 小 敌人, they appear as a 小 dot on the 雷达, making them 困难 to 定位 during an 攻击 on an 机器人 堡垒.

琐事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The 炮塔 has 否 官员 in-游戏 名称; it is simply referred to as "敌人 单位", and dying to it reads "Killed by 未知". However, Arrowhead has recognized it as the "碉堡 炮塔" or "碉堡 阵地", as seen in 01.003.00's 更新日志 video, and in 1.005.000's 更新日志，分别。
  • Before the Escalation of Freedom 更新, 碉堡 炮塔 at Minor Place of Interests did not 功能, but could still be destroyed.
