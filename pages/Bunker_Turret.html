生成

It is most commonly seen atop 指挥碉堡, but have a 生成几率 at 机器人 occupied Minor Place of Interests, which start appearing at 自杀任务 难度.

On 超级绝地潜兵难度 难度, it can be seen defending 机器人 Fortresses.

行为

The Bunker Turret slowly fires two times before 冷却 down for roughly five 秒, and has very little 警告 of when it is going to 火焰 at 绝地潜兵, besides it aiming at them.

战术信息

  • The 正面 of the Bunker Turret is covered in  重型 护甲, while having a  中型 弱点 on its back. Any  重型 护甲-penetrating weapon, such as the AC-8 机炮 和 LAS-98 激光大炮, can 摧毁 them effectively.
    • Any 反坦克 weapon can 1-shot the turret.
      • Notably, the FAF-14 Spear is incapable of locking onto a Bunker Turret, as they are considered a 小型 enemy.
    • the RS-422 磁轨炮 requires an 80% charge shot to 摧毁.
    • the PLAS-45 Epoch requires a fully-charged shot.
  • Damaging 榴弹 that have  重型 or above can 摧毁 a Bunker Turret.
    • 铝热弹 can 1-shot Bunker Turret if it sticks to it.
    • 两者 G-12 High 爆炸 和 TED-63 Dynamite can also 1-shot Bunker Turret, but requires careful cooking of the 手榴弹.
  • Completing the 妥协 机器人 Defenses 可选目标 disables the Bunker Turret's 能力 to 目标 绝地潜兵, making it practically harmless.
  • Bunker 炮塔 can also be destroyed by any of the 进攻型 战略配备 or 阵地 战略配备.
  • Bunker 炮塔 have a 有限 射程 of around 150m. Using long-远程 武器 such as the APW-1 反器材步枪 can 摧毁 them from afar.
  • The Turret's laser can severely 伤害 or outright kill a 绝地潜兵 depending on their 护甲值, and can 伤害 the M-102 Fast 侦察 载具, making this turret a high-priority 威胁.
  • The Turret's barrel depression can unintentionally clip through its base, causing it to 有时 火焰 even lower than expected.
  • Bunker 炮塔 can be 困难 to spot at night or during 天气 events, especially within 机器人 Fortresses.
    • As they are considered a 小型 enemy, they appear as a 小型 dot on the radar, making them 困难 to locate during an 攻击 on an 机器人 堡垒.

琐事

  • The Turret has 否 官员 in-game name; it is simply referred to as "Enemy Unit", and dying to it reads "Killed by 未知". However, Arrowhead has recognized it as the "Bunker Turret" or "Bunker 阵地", as seen in 01.003.00's 更新日志 video, and in 1.005.000's 更新日志，分别。
  • Before the Escalation of Freedom 更新, Bunker 炮塔 at Minor Place of Interests did not 功能, but could still be destroyed.
