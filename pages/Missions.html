Tactical 目标[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Tactical 目标 (also known as 可选目标) are 小 目标 to 完成 within a 任务, with up to five 生成 on a map. Depending on the 难度 and 阵营, completing them can unlock in-任务奖励, or 移除 map 效果 or hazards.

Completing the Tactical 目标 will 奖励 绝地潜兵 with Syntax Error 申购点 and Syntax Error 经验值, with an exception for the "摧毁 高-数值 目标" 目标, which awards Syntax Error 经验值 and 否 申购点. Aside from reactivating a 雷达站, here are 一些 other methods to finding Tactical 目标.

主要命令[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

主要命令 (abberviated as MO) are community-宽阔 goals given 全部 绝地潜兵 for them to 完成. These 指挥 the community for larger narrative plotlines, which will 冲击 the 背景 and even the 银河地图 itself. There is 通常 only one 重要指令 active at a 时间, 有时 with 一些 时间 in-between, though it is possible to have 多个 active at 一次.

全部 active 绝地潜兵 贡献 to the completion of the 重要指令, directly or indirectly. Depending on the planets and/or fronts being issued in the 重要指令, their participation 5月 帮助 or hinder its combat effectiveness.

Upon completion, 奖牌 will be rewarded, though it is possible to be rewarded with other items, such as 战略配备 or 装饰 items. In 命令 to be rewarded, 玩家 must be active within that 时间 时期, otherwise the 奖励 will not be given to them.

Minor 命令 / Strategic Opportunities / Strategic Threats[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

绝地潜兵 5月 also 接收 Minor 命令, Strategic Opportunities, and Strategic Threats during on-going 主要命令, typcially on a different 阵营 being the 目标. It's completion can 奖励 surplus 战略配备 使用 instead of the usual 奖牌, though it is possible for them to directly affect the 当前 MO by altering its 赢得 parameters.

Strategic Threats are instances of a 少校 效果 causing 大-鳞片 issues, affecting 绝地潜兵 galactic-宽阔. Due to their 大 效果, resolving them is highly advised, as it could 已移除 various debuffs 相关 to them.

Failure to 完成 them 通常 does not directly 原因 任何 负面 impacts, though the 时间 lost attempting to 完成 them can be seen as such.

个人命令[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

全部 绝地潜兵 daily are given optional 个人命令 （缩写为 PO) to 完成, which involves completing certain goals on a 行星. These 5月 involve killing a 数字 of 敌人 (in 基本信息 or with a certain 武器), collecting certain samples, or various 任务-相关 目标 (extracting, sub-目标, etc.). Depending on the PO, fellow 绝地潜兵 can 贡献, increasing its completion success 速率.

个人命令 last 24 hours and rest at 9:00 AM UTC, with 每个 绝地潜兵 completes their 个人指令 individually. Upon completion, they are rewarded 15 奖牌.
