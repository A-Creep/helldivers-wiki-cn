🌍 概述[edit | edit 来源]

Attention 绝地潜兵,

High 指挥 has authorized a significant tactical 更新 with both 内容 and quality of life 升级. We are deploying 绝地潜兵 in new Galactic Campaigns, making 升级 to the new diver training 程序, and making essential fixes and 升级 across the board.

We have also made changes to weapon, 战略配备, and enemy balancing based on feedback from 绝地潜兵 on the frontlines, including reductions to enemy 耐久伤害 and a 已减少 外骨骼机甲 冷却.

绝地潜兵, we are committed to making the necessary 升级 to keep you in the fight. Because you have never given up on 管理式民主, we will never give up on making it easier for you to spread.

Introducing the 控制 CENTER 终端[edit | edit 来源]

超级地球 High 指挥 has completed fleet-wide 升级 to the previously unused 终端 opposite the 军械库. Now 绝地潜兵 will be able to get more context to the ongoing Campaigns and their subsequent 主要命令. Each Campaign will come with its own potential reward, should the 绝地潜兵 be victorious in a majority of the 主要命令 within it. The 终端 will also 特性 an archive (to be populated as the first campaign concludes) so that new and returning 绝地潜兵 can read up on the twists and turns of 上一个 campaigns.

⚖️ 平衡性调整[edit | edit 来源]

基本信息 平衡 changes[edit | edit 来源]

载具 护甲 and 敌人 AP rebalance

  • 超级地球 载具 now have more 准确 护甲 values (Bastion gets 重型护甲, 外骨骼机甲 a mix of 重型 and 中型护甲, 防弹盾牌 重型护甲).
  • Some 敌人 have received 已增加 护甲穿透 values so they can 应对 the 载具, but not all. There will be a lot of 更小 敌人 and explosions that won't be able to hurt something like the Bastion or the Exo Suits (scavengers, 机器人 小型 arms 火焰, 追猎虫, 小型 to 中型 障碍 explosions, cyborg 小型 arms etc).
  • But there are still a lot in the 敌人 arsenal that can hurt you (and also 重要 is that a lot of YOUR arsenal now has a harder time hurting your own 载具).
  • Some 敌人 have had their 耐久伤害 已降低
  • There are too many changes to properly list, so we ask you to play and tell us how it feels. The intent is the following
    • The Bastion tank should feel "tankier" and ignore 小型 攻击, withstand more 中型 攻击 and its survivability against 重型 攻击 should be unchanged
    • The Exo Suits have a similar change as the Bastion tank in terms of ignoring more 小型 攻击 and withstanding 中型 攻击 better. The 增加 in 生命值 should overall make it feel a bit more tanky even against 重型 攻击.
    • the FRV has received an 增加 in 生命值 that will make it handle 中型 攻击 similar to before, but be able to withstand 重型 攻击 better granting better survivability.
    • the 防弹盾牌 should feel like it can ignore 小型 攻击 and handle 中型 攻击 better.

Sentry 生命值 rebalance

  • 已增加 the 生命值 of all 哨戒炮 by 100% to compensate for the 基本信息 伤害 增加 of 敌人 vs 载具.
  • We have previously 已增加 耐久伤害 on a lot of 敌人 and since 哨戒炮 are 耐久 they have lost some of their 功能型. We didn't want them to become squishier, this change should 改进 their versatility on the battlefield.

Reloading improvements

  • Improvements to make reloading more responsive, part of this is allowing reloading while dodge diving, also remembering the 换弹 state better so if you're ragdolled while reloading you will 继续 it when regaining 控制.
  • Introduced the concept of delayed reloads. If you try to trigger a 换弹 while the player is not allowed to do so, the 换弹 will be delayed until the player is able to 换弹 with a 最大 of 1.2s delay from when the 换弹 input was pressed.

Bastion 涂装 Customization

  • If you own the 载具 patterns from a 尊享战争债券, they can now also be applied to the Bastion Tank! It does not apply to 传奇 战争债券 retroactively at this time.

已减少 Exo Suit 冷却

  • Reduce the 冷却 of mechs from 10 分钟 down to 8. The intent is to find a better 平衡 with mechs and allow more time with fun 战略配备

Throwables amounts rebalance

  • Intent is to make some of the less used throwables more available and viable options

Orbitals 伤害 rebalance

  • Intent is to make the larger orbitals feel more like the large projectiles from orbit as they are so the 投射物 and 爆炸伤害 should better match that.
  • Larger projectiles explosions like the 380mm and 120mm are 已增加 to make close hits more 有效 vs larger 敌人 like Chargers.
  • Compared to explosions like the Ultimatum these will have less 伤害 at the center, but also less 伤害 fall off so it will have a more consistent 伤害 over its total area.
  • The Railcannon also got more 伤害 to be more 有效 vs some of the largest 敌人 like the 噪轰引擎. It will still not one shot the largest 敌人 like 移动工厂, 霸王虫 and similar.
