科学中心

The 科学中心 is a 超级地球 研究设施 located on Fenrir III. In order to 研究 the recent events 相关 to the 光能者 and Meridia, 超级地球's best astrophysicists gathered here to 研究 it.

背景故事

建造

  • 12月 24th, 2184 - The 科学中心 was constructed on Fenrir III following the 重要指令.

默里迪恩黑洞与暗能量研究

While researching the nature of Dark Fluid and its 关系 to Meridia, the scientists at the 科学中心 made the alarming discovery that Meridia was not a Black Hole, but a Wormhole. While this revelation 已更改 the way we viewed the object, its secrets were only beginning to show.

After further 研究 into Dark 能量, the 能量 that was linked to 光能者 activity and Meridia, the scientists were successful in modelling spacetime fluctuation, and managed to recreate them by simulating sudden Dark 能量 drops. In tandem with the Ministry of Science, SE High 指挥 added these activity readings to 绝地潜兵 Super Destroyers in order to better inform 绝地潜兵 of the potential 威胁.

遭遇掠食变种

When Fenrir III was attacked by the 终结族, the 捕食者 Strain was first encountered by the 绝地潜兵. Due to the importance of the 科学中心, all efforts were made to secure the 行星. The 绝地潜兵 were able to repel the 威胁 and successfully defended the Center.
