the Gazer Spire 是一个 光能者 exclusive 建筑 found exclusively on 摧毁 Gazer Spire Missions.

战术信息

  • the Gazer Spire requires a 爆破力 of 60, which means it can be destroyed only with a NUX-223 Hellbomb，一款 B-100 Portable Hellbomb or SEAF 大炮 Mini-Nuke shell.
  • After spotting a 绝地潜兵 within their ~250 meter 射程, the Gazer Spire begins to visibly charge up before firing its laser. Evasive action is recommended as the beam is highly 致命.
    • 载具, especially 外骨骼机甲, are very 易受 to being destroyed from the beam's 远距离.
    • Similar to the Gazer, the Gazer Spire's beam can easily be outmaneuvered on foot by walking to the 侧面 or 找掩护中.
  • Warp Gateways are constructed around the Gazer Spire's 建筑, which will constantly spawn enemy units unless destroyed.
