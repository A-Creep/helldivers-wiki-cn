the 虫穴 is a  终结族 Horde exclusive 建筑 found primarily in Bug Nests. These holes will continuously deploy new 终结族 units until they are destroyed. They are 通常 indicated by yellow-orangish spores in-game.

战术信息

  • 虫穴 can only be destroyed in two ways:
    • Damaging the inside of the 虫穴:
      • With a 最小 爆破 force of 20, which requires an 爆炸伤害 类型.
    • Damaging the outside of the 虫穴:
      • With a 爆破 force of 40 or 更大 (which does NOT have to be 爆炸).
  • There are various 武器 and 战略配备 that can be used by 绝地潜兵 to 摧毁 虫穴:
    • Throwing an 爆炸 类型 手榴弹 inside the 虫穴 will 摧毁 it. Smoke Grenades, 眩晕手榴弹，以及 Throwing Knives in particular do not have a high enough 爆破 force to close 虫穴.
    • Shooting a 手榴弹 inside the 虫穴 with the GL-21 榴弹发射器 or GP-31 榴弹手枪 will 摧毁 it. The GL-52 De-Escalator in particular does not do not have a high enough 爆破 force to close 虫穴.
    • Shooting 爆炸 武器库 into the 虫穴 will 摧毁 it. The R-36 Eruptor, AC-8 机炮, CB-9 Exploding Crossbow，以及 GR-8 无后坐力炮 are among 几个 武器 that can close 虫穴.
    • Certain 战略配备 or 武器 with a demo force of 40 or 更大 to 摧毁 the hole from the outside such as 鹰式空袭, Eagle Napalm Airstrikes, Eagle Smoke Strikes, Eagle 500kg 炸弹, 轨道激光炮, 轨道武器 毒气 Strike, 轨道精准攻击, 120mm, 380mm, and Walking Barrages, FAF-14 Spear, B-100 Portable Hellbomb or the GP-20 Ultimatum.
    • 绝地喷射舱 战略配备 like the 重新补给, or any 支援武器 绝地喷射舱 can be used to close the hole, given the 绝地喷射舱 lands on the 虫穴. 绝地潜兵 are advised that they may not have access to what the 绝地喷射舱 would've supplied if they do this.
    • 绝地潜兵 are advised that deploying Eagle/轨道武器/防御型 战略配备 while other 绝地潜兵 are present may result in accidents.

中型 虫穴

  • 中型 虫穴 will 通常 spawn Brood 指挥官, Bile 喷吐者 and Nursing 喷吐者. Starting on 不可能, Brood 指挥官 are replaced by its 变体, the 阿尔法指挥官.
    • If the 中型 Bug is part of a 追踪虫巢穴, it will spawn Stalkers.

吐酸泰坦 虫穴

  • 吐酸泰坦 虫穴 will 通常 spawn Bile Titans.
    • If the Dragonroach is active on the 行星, 吐酸泰坦 虫穴 will spawn Chargers.

人造 虫穴

The 人造 虫穴 变体 can only be found in the tutorial. It is 不可能 to jump into one. It will close once it detects a 手榴弹 爆炸 inside itself.

  • The 人造 虫穴, closed
  • The 人造 虫穴, opened

吐酸泰坦 虫穴

the 吐酸泰坦 虫穴 can be found on 极难 missions and above, in specific 重型 Nests and mega Nests. Instead of 生成 小型 终结族, it will spawn Bile Titans. It is much bigger than 标准 虫穴 and requires at least 爆破 力度 40 to 摧毁.

  • Worth noting is that Hellpods have a 爆破 force of 40. Therefore, calling down a 绝地喷射舱 战略配备 onto the 吐酸泰坦 Hole will close it, though as mentioned earlier on this page, the called-down 战略配备 itself might become inaccessible.
  • It is also possible to have a 吐酸泰坦 carcass crash down onto the hole and close it. This can be achieved by 击倒中 a guarding 吐酸泰坦 without alerting it first, or eliminating one as it 出现 from the 虫穴.
  • Close up of a 吐酸泰坦 Hole on the edge of a Mega Nest.
  • Ditto

中型 虫穴

The 中型 虫穴 can be found on 挑战 or above as part of 追踪虫 Lairs, in 重型 Nests, or Mega Nests

The 中型 虫穴 shares the same 属性 as a 小型 虫穴.

  • 中型 虫穴 used for a 追踪虫巢穴
  • 中型 虫穴, 绝地潜兵 for scale
