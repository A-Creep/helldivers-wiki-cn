The Jet Brigade 装甲兵 is a 变体 of the 装甲兵 affiliated with the   Jet Brigade that has been modified to mount the iconic 喷射背包.

生成

Jet Brigade Troopers can be found on 中型 and above. They can spawn from 机器人空投, within patrols, guarding 机器人 哨站 and 目标, or from 机器人 Fabricators. They are more likely to be replaced by more 危险 轻型 机器人, such as theJet Brigade MG Raider on higher difficulties.

Starting on 挑战, they can be found guarding Minor Places of Interest.

行为

Jet Brigade Troopers are 本质上 unchanged from their 标准 counterparts, but are equipped with a jump-pack that covers their back and allows them to jump long distances.

When initially detected, Jet Brigade Troopers at 中型 to 远距离 will use their jump-pack to close the 距离 to 绝地潜兵, proceeding to open 火焰 with their Fusion SMGs or throwing Grenades should the 绝地潜兵 rush into cover.. 当绝地潜兵脱离射程时，它们会使用喷射背包重新接战。

战术信息

喷气旅专属

  • Jet Brigade Troopers can cross large areas and quickly get into ranges where their 武器 are far deadlier. They will prioritize getting into close quarters at all costs, limiting your 能力 to 消除 them at 远距离.
  • The 喷气背包 of the Jet Brigade Troopers is more 脆弱 than the Jet Brigade 装甲兵 itself, exploding and igniting 敌人, itself and potentially you upon 摧毁. The 喷气背包 will 引爆 posthumously if enough 伤害 reaches the 喷气背包 through the Jet Brigade 装甲兵's chest or 胃.
    • 武器 such as the Purifier, Plasma Punisher or the Eruptor can be used to 引爆 the 喷射背包 from afar, dealing 范围伤害 around the Jet Brigade Troopers and disabling its jump 能力, often killing the enemy as well.

轻型机器人

  • 全身轻型 机器人 are equipped with a hidden flare 发射器 in their left arm, which can be used to 呼叫 机器人空投.
    • 在它们完成发射信号弹之前将其摧毁，可以防止机器人空投被呼叫。
  • 手榴弹和爆头可以稳定一击秒杀所有轻型机器人。

琐事

  • According to early concept art, the 装甲兵 was originally named the "Conscript". Its first iteration had a much blocker 外观 and a dark green color scheme. The second iteration has a more human like 外观 and is armed with a 步枪 that resembles the FG 42.
    • The 更新日志, such as 1.001.104, 偶尔 参考 全身轻型 机器人 as Conscripts.
