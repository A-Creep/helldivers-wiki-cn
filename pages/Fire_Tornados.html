烈焰龙卷 are an environmental 条件 that 创建 occasional storms of 火焰 Tornados on Scorched Moor planets. which 移动 slowly and (semi)randomly throughout the 游玩 范围. An active 火焰 龙卷风 风暴 will activate the 极暑 environmental 条件 for its 持续时间, after which the 条件 will deactivate.

概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Three 火焰 Tornadoes on 撤离.

When spawned, 每个 火焰 龙卷风 will 移动 around the map similar to an 敌人 巡逻, with slightly 更多 randomization. Wherever the 火焰 龙卷风 goes, it will leave an immolating (30-米 长, 10-米 宽阔) trail of flames with the immolation-lifetime of 30-秒. 联络 with the 火焰 龙卷风 or it's 之火 trail will 结果 in 任何 living entity making 联络 to be lit on 火焰; 建筑-entities such as A/MLS-4X 火箭哨戒炮 won't get immolated but will suffer and won't survive the 基地-火焰-伤害 without 生命值 升级 from 高级建造. Immolated 绝地潜兵 can extinguish themselves by performing a 出击.

While 火焰 Tornadoes are active on the map, the 极暑 environmental 条件 will be active as well.

While 最多 certainly 非常 致命 and 破坏性 to 绝地潜兵 and their 战略配备, the 火焰 龙卷风 itself lacks 爆破 之势, leaving structures in their pristine conditions (E.G. fences). In fact, 火焰 Tornados 通常 tend to avoid non-超级地球武装部队 structures to 开始 with (E.G. 尖啸虫巢穴们）。[引用 needed They 移动 randomly...]

应对 火焰 Tornadoes[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 火焰 Tornadoes can go anywhere an 敌人 NOT chasing after a 绝地潜兵 can walk. Climbing on a rocky 悬崖 should keep a 玩家 安全 from said tornadoes.
    • This can 最好 be seen within Cities. As patrols tend to stick to the roads, so will the 火焰 Tornadoes. 绝地潜兵 will find themselves to be 安全 on the buildings.
  • Should a 绝地潜兵 be unable to avoid the trail of flames, they should quickly 出击 to extinguish themselves.

琐事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The 名称 for the in-游戏 效果 使用次数 Tornados, the 更少 common plural spelling of 龙卷风. This spelling originates from Spanish and has 总是显示 been 更少 popular than Tornadoes.
