敌人[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Stealth Detection 更新:
    • 敌人 will 否 longer automatically detect you when you're near 终结族 spawners or spawners that generate 飞行 敌人. Instead, these spawners will now only activate if an 敌人 has already identified you as a 目标.
    • Previously, simply being within 200 米 of certain spawner 类型—such as 飞行 敌人 spawners, 追踪虫 lairs, or inside 终结族 locations—would trigger immediate 敌人 spawns and reveal your 位置. This 更新 shifts detection to a 更多 logical, line-of-sight or alert-based 星系, reducing arbitrary auto-detection 机制 and improving stealth 玩法
    • As a 结果 of these changes, 机器人 Fabricators now spawn 敌人 更多 consistently, ensuring smoother pacing and 交战 平衡
