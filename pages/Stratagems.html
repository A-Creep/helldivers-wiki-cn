战略配备

战略配备 are powerful ordnance, utilities and 装备 available to The 绝地潜兵, provided to them by their Super Destroyers. They are considered to be the 最多 powerful tool in a 绝地潜兵's arsenal.

概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

使用[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

战略配备 are deployed by 绝地潜兵 by entering a unique 法典. By opening the 战略配备 菜单 (CTRL on PC) and correctly entering the 法典, 绝地潜兵 will, in 最多 cases, need to throw a 战略配备 ball to 指挥 the 目标 范围 for the 超级驱逐舰. Incorrectly entering a 章节 of the 法典, or closing the 战略配备 菜单, will 要求 绝地潜兵 to re-enter the 法典 until it is 正确.

获取[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

战略配备 are unlocked in three ways: by purchasing them in the 爱国行政中心, by purchasing them from 战争债券, and 偶尔 as 奖励 from 主要命令 and Campaigns.

一些 战略配备 5月 also 偶尔 be temporarily provided in certain missions, as 部分 of a 任务 目标, or through temporary permits issued by 高 指挥 in the 形成 of a "武器 Augmentation."

战略配备类型[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

战略配备 have a variety of purposes, but can be separated into four primary categories (denoted by their 许可类型 and 图标 颜色): 进攻型 (Red), 供给 (Blue), 防御型 (Green), and 任务 (Yellow).

  • 进攻型 战略配备 are primarily used to 摧毁 大 amounts of 敌人 or 非常 大 敌人. Different 战略配备 are 更多 有效 than one another in these different roles. For 示例, the 轨道加特林火力网 is 更多 suited to 应对 大 groups of 更小 敌人 (such as Troopers, Scavengers, and 无票者), while the 轨道炮攻击 is 最好 used against 单个, 庞大 targets (such as 胆汁 Titans, Vox Engines, and Harvesters). 最多 进攻型 战略配备 can also be used to 摧毁 敌人 structures, such as Fabricators, 孢子 喷吐者, and 叛变研究站.
    • 进攻型 战略配备 come in two primary categories: 轨道武器 Strikes and 飞鹰 Strikes.
  • 供给 战略配备 are 装备 that is used to 提升 an individual 绝地潜兵's capabilities against 敌人. This is 经常 in the 形成 of portable 重型 武器, supporting 装备, and 载具.
    • 供给 战略配备 come in three primary categories: 支援武器, Backpacks, and 载具.
  • 防御型 战略配备 are 更多 suited towards creating an 有效 防御型 posture against oncoming 敌人. 防御型 战略配备 are 通常 used to supplement a 绝地潜兵's firepower against approaching 敌人, whether coming from a 增援 呼叫, or by protecting a 主要目标.
    • 防御型 战略配备 come in two primary categories: Sentries and Emplacements.
  • 任务所需战略配备 are unique in that they are not 经常 used to supplement 绝地潜兵 or 摧毁 敌人, but rather to 完成 任务 目标. 一些 任务 战略配备 will 总是显示 be available to 绝地潜兵 (such as Reinforce & 重新补给) while others will be 任务-specific, 经常 requiring 绝地潜兵 to be in a certain 范围 before they are able to be called-in.

进攻许可[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

轨道武器 Strikes 轨道武器 strikes take longer to 呼叫-in, but are 经常 更多 powerful and reach a wider 范围 than 飞鹰 strikes. 轨道武器 strikes are 最好 used against a slow and predictable 之势, such as an unaware 敌人 巡逻, or a 虫族入侵. 轨道武器 strikes will go on 冷却 immediately after they make 联络 with the 浮出地面. In Cities and Factories, a 超级驱逐舰 5月 need to reposition to accurately 火焰 an 轨道武器 打击, this can further 增加 呼叫时间.

飞鹰 Strikes 飞鹰 strikes are 更快 to 呼叫 and have 多个 使用次数 before going on 冷却, but 经常 掩护 a 更小 范围 than 轨道武器 strikes. 飞鹰 strikes 功能 as a 许多 更多 on-demand 回应 to apparent threats. When 全部 of its 载荷 is expended, an 飞鹰 will automatically go on 冷却 to rearm. This can also be done at 任何 时间 by 绝地潜兵 when an 飞鹰 has 更少 than 100% of its 载荷 by entering the 重新武装“飞鹰” 法典.

补给许可[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

支援武器 支援武器 are 重型 武器 that are designed to augment an individual 绝地潜兵's firepower against 敌人. 点赞 进攻型 战略配备, 支援武器 have different utilities and are intended for facing different 敌人. 一些 支援武器 (such as the MG-43 机枪) 允许 绝地潜兵 to 更好 应对 groups of 轻型-中型 敌人, while others (such as the GR-8 无后坐力炮) 允许 绝地潜兵 to 摧毁 大-庞大 敌人. Certain 支援武器 (such as the AC-8 机炮) 要求 绝地潜兵 to 携带 an ammunition backpack to 换弹 the 武器. If an 盟友 is nearby, they can interact with the backpack to provide 船员-loading, massively increasing the 换弹 速度 of the 武器.

载具 载具 consist of 快速侦察载具 (FRVs), 外骨骼机甲, and the Bastion. FRVs are 侦察 载具 that come equipped with mounted 武器 and are able to 携带 多个 绝地潜兵 at a 时间, used to quickly maneuver around a 任务 范围. Exousits are 单个-piloted mechsuits that have 已增加 护甲 and a variety of mounted 武器, meant to 摧毁 a 大 数量 of 敌人 while clearing a path for allies. The Bastion is a four-man 有护甲 坦克 驱逐舰.

Sentries Sentries are automated 武器 炮塔 that come with a 有限 供给 of 弹药 and will automatically engage 敌人 when in 射程. Sentries are outfitted with a variety of different 武器库 for different purposes, including machine guns, autocannons, mortars, and even rockets. A 绝地潜兵's choice of 哨戒炮 should 变化 depending on their playstyle, 当前 武装配置, as well as the expected 敌人 之势. 哨戒炮 炮塔 are 最好 used to fill the gap in a 绝地潜兵's 防御, covering one or 更多 areas that would be 不可能 for a 绝地潜兵 to 掩护 by themselves.

Emplacements Emplacements are other 防御型 战略配备 that can 射程 from 可部署 minefields to mounted 武器 platforms. Minefields (and the A/弧度-3 特斯拉塔) fill a similar 角色 to sentries, providing 掩护 to an 范围 without requiring 绝地潜兵 attention. Alternatively, mounted 武器 platforms are emplacements operated by 绝地潜兵, providing a significant 提升 in firepower in exchange for sacrificing 全部 mobility.
