战略配备 are powerful ordnance, utilities and 装备 available to The 绝地潜兵, provided to them by their Super Destroyers. They are considered to be the most powerful tool in a 绝地潜兵's arsenal.

概述[edit | edit 来源]

使用[edit | edit 来源]

战略配备 are deployed by 绝地潜兵 by entering a unique code. By opening the 战略配备 menu (CTRL on PC) and correctly entering the code, 绝地潜兵 will, in most cases, need to throw a 战略配备 ball to direct the 目标 area for the 超级驱逐舰. Incorrectly entering a 章节 of the code, or closing the 战略配备 menu, will 要求 绝地潜兵 to re-enter the code until it is 正确.

获取[edit | edit 来源]

战略配备 are unlocked in three ways: by purchasing them in the 爱国行政中心, by purchasing them from 战争债券, and 偶尔 as 奖励 from 主要命令 和 Campaigns.

Some 战略配备 may also 偶尔 be temporarily provided in certain missions, as part of a 任务 目标, or through temporary permits issued by High 指挥 in the form of a "武器 Augmentation."

战略配备类型[edit | edit 来源]

战略配备 have a variety of purposes, but can be separated into four primary categories (denoted by their 许可类型 and icon color): 进攻型 (Red), 补给（蓝色）, 防御型 (Green), and 任务 (Yellow).

  • 进攻型 战略配备 are primarily used to 摧毁 large amounts of 敌人 or very large 敌人. Different 战略配备 are more 有效 than one another in these different roles. For 示例, the 轨道加特林火力网 is more suited to 应对 large groups of 更小 敌人 (such as Troopers, Scavengers, and 无票者), while the 轨道炮攻击 is best used against single, 庞大 targets (such as Bile Titans, Vox Engines, and Harvesters). Most 进攻型 战略配备 can also be used to 摧毁 enemy structures, such as Fabricators, Spore 喷吐者, and 叛变研究站.
    • 进攻型 战略配备 come in two primary categories: 轨道武器 Strikes and Eagle Strikes.
  • Supply 战略配备 are 装备 that is used to boost an individual 绝地潜兵's capabilities against 敌人. This is often in the form of portable 重型 武器, supporting gear, and 载具.
    • Supply 战略配备 come in three primary categories: 支援武器, 背包, and 载具.
  • 防御型 战略配备 are more suited towards creating an 有效 防御型 posture against oncoming 敌人. 防御型 战略配备 are 通常 used to supplement a 绝地潜兵's firepower against approaching 敌人, whether coming from a 增援 call, or by protecting a 主要目标.
    • 防御型 战略配备 come in two primary categories: 哨戒炮 and 炮台.
  • 任务所需战略配备 are unique in that they are not often used to supplement 绝地潜兵 or 摧毁 敌人, but rather to 完成 任务 目标. Some 任务 战略配备 will always be available to 绝地潜兵 (such as 增援 & 重新补给) while others will be 任务-specific, often requiring 绝地潜兵 to be in a certain area before they are able to be called-in.

支援武器 支援武器 are 重型 武器 that are designed to augment an individual 绝地潜兵's firepower against 敌人. Like 进攻型 战略配备, 支援武器 have different utilities and are intended for facing different 敌人. Some 支援武器 (such as the MG-43 机枪) allow 绝地潜兵 to better 应对 groups of 轻型-中型 敌人, while others (such as the GR-8 无后坐力炮) allow 绝地潜兵 to 摧毁 large-庞大 敌人. Certain 支援武器 (such as the AC-8 机炮) 要求 绝地潜兵 to carry an ammunition backpack to 换弹 the weapon. If an ally is nearby, they can interact with the backpack to provide crew-loading, massively increasing the 换弹 速度 of the weapon.
