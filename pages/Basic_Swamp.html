    Swamp Biomes
    基础沼泽

    The Basic Swamp is a Swamp 类型 生物群系 that can be found on 多种星球 in 绝地潜兵 2.

    描述

    “

    Tall, gnarled trees flourish across the 浮出地面 of this forested world. Sunlight filters past dangling vines, to the saturated undergrowth below.

    基础沼泽是一片繁茂的生态群系，以其巨树和粗壮的树根而闻名。

    The 浮出地面 of the Basic Swamp 生物群系 is very hill-like and uneven, going up and down. It is filled with grass, tall grass, flowers, roots and 庞大 thick trees. 有时, trees can become so large and so thick, they can create a 庞大 屏障 made of bark that can only be walked around, not through.

    The 浮出地面 is also filled with water, with 庞大 deep rivers/lakes dividing entire 任务 areas. As the 生物群系 is swampy, shin-deep green stagnant water can also be found across the 浮出地面, with both roots, trees, rocks, roots, and grass 偶尔 coming out of the waters.

    As for the skies, almost 50% of it is filled by both thin and puffy white clouds at all times. The background of the 行星 also features very tall distant rocky towers with trees at the very top.

    地表变体

    大都市

    In parks located in Metropolises, the usual grassy 地形 can be found. Although the 生物群系 has 庞大 trees in wilderness areas, Earth-like trees will generate with green leaves in this 生物群系.

    巨大的背景地形尖塔在大都市中依然存在，站在城市边缘时可以在背景中看到它们。

    阴霾

    Inside The Gloom, the 行星 loses it's 主体 environmental 条件 暴雨. The 大气层 and the clouds become fully puffy and noticeably more yellow than usual as a yellow/orange fog permanently appears in the 距离. The murky waters also reflect the now-yellowed skies, becoming a yellow color.

    异界风暴

    On Basic 沼泽星球 that harbor an Exostorm, the 行星's present fog will turn a slight shade of pink, which turns into an indigo shade at night. It will also permanently rain thanks to the 暴雨 修正.

    环境条件

    暴雨 – Torrential 暴雨 reduce visibility.

    环境危害

    • 火山 Plants 爆炸 into 火焰 when damaged.
    • Tall Grass and Ferns slowing any 绝地潜兵 walking through it down.
    • 会导致任何入水游泳的绝地潜兵溺亡的深水，以及经常贯穿任务区域的大河。

    天气

    • 清楚
      • 晴朗天气是基础沼泽的主要天气状态，天空中布满了大而蓬松的云朵以及轻薄如纱的云层，覆盖约50%的天空。

    • 小雨
      • 偶尔会出现太阳雨。

    • 暴雨
      • 当暴雨效果激活时，天空将被厚厚的灰色云层完全覆盖，大量雨滴落下，大雨倾盆而至。
