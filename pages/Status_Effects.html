硬直 and Ragdoll

Staggering can 射程 from mere flinching to being knocked off your feet entirely. It is caused by dealing or taking 伤害 with sufficient 硬直力度 to meet an enemy's 硬直 thresholds. Higher thresholds impart 更强 效果, such as completely interrupting animations. Unlike other 状态效果, 硬直力度 does not build up over 多个 攻击.

敌人

  • Staggering will cause an enemy to be pushed back and 通常 interrupts their movement, with the 距离 pushed scaling with 硬直力度.
  • Most 敌人 have abilities that can be interrupted upon being attacked with sufficient 硬直力度.
    • Some 值得注意 interruptable abilities include certain 攻击 and calling for 增援.
    • 硬直 thresholds listed on enemy pages will be the value 必需 to cause interrupting 硬直.
  • 硬直力度 必需 to interrupt an enemy can vary wildely, from the lowely 食腐虫's 10 to the unyielding Fleshmob's 45.
  • 武器 that 火焰 多个 projectiles (Such as the SG-8 Punisher) will have every 投射物 apply full 硬直力度, allowing just a single pellet to 硬直 an enemy.

绝地潜兵

  • 绝地潜兵 that are hit by any enemy 攻击 will flinch, causing their reticle to be thrown off the point of aim temporarily.
  • 绝地潜兵 that are hit by enemy 攻击 with a 硬直 value of 10 or 更大 will be staggered. This will interrupt certain animations, such as stimming.
  • 绝地潜兵 that are hit by enemy 攻击 with a 硬直 value of 25 or 更大 will be ragdolled. This throws the player, interrupts all animations, stops actions like typing on a 终端, and temporarily takes away all player 控制. This does, however, have a plus of providing 90% 爆炸伤害 reduction while in this state.
  • the Rock Solid 盔甲被动加成 降低 incoming 硬直 by 30%. This enables you to avoid being ragdolled by many 攻击, such as Rocket 蹂躏者 rockets, 蹂躏者 punches, and 监视者 staff swings, but not 更强 攻击 like Cannon Turret shots or Reinforced 侦察纵步者 rockets.
    • 攻击 with 硬直力度 values of 36 or above can ragdoll you with the Rock Solid 被动.

酸 溅射

  • 酸 covered targets will be slowed and may additionally take 伤害 over time. This 效果 lasts 4 秒.
  • Can be inflicted by the following 终结族 敌人： 胆汁吐沫虫, 追猎虫, 吐酸武斗虫, 胆汁喷涌虫, 抚育喷涌虫, 孢子强袭虫, 吐酸泰坦, Bug Mines, 掠食追猎虫, and all Spore Burst Strain 终结族.
