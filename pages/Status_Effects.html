踉跄 and Ragdoll

Staggering can 射程 from mere flinching to being knocked off your feet entirely. It is caused by dealing or taking 伤害 with sufficient 踉跄值 to meet an enemy's 踉跄 thresholds. Higher thresholds impart 更强 效果, such as completely interrupting animations. Unlike other 状态效果, 踉跄值 does not build up over 多个 攻击.

敌人

  • 硬直会使敌人被击退，通常打断其移动，击退距离随硬直力缩放。
  • 大多数敌人拥有可以用足够硬直力攻击打断的能力。
    • 一些值得注意的可打断能力包括某些攻击和呼叫增援。
    • 敌人页面上列出的硬直阈值将是造成打断性硬直所需的值。
  • 踉跄值 必需 to interrupt an enemy can vary wildely, from the lowely 食腐虫's 10 to the unyielding Fleshmob's 45.
  • 武器 that 火焰 多个 projectiles (Such as the SG-8 Punisher) will have every 投射物 apply full 踉跄值, allowing just a single pellet to 踉跄 an enemy.

绝地潜兵

  • 被任何敌人攻击击中的绝地潜兵会退缩，导致其准星暂时偏离瞄准点。
  • 绝地潜兵 that are hit by enemy 攻击 with a 踉跄 value of 10 or 更大 will be staggered. This will interrupt certain animations, such as stimming.
  • 绝地潜兵 that are hit by enemy 攻击 with a 踉跄 value of 25 or 更大 will be ragdolled. This throws the player, interrupts all animations, stops actions like typing on a 终端, and temporarily takes away all player 控制. This does, however, have a plus of providing 90% 爆炸伤害 reduction while in this state.
  • The Rock Solid 护甲 被动 降低 incoming 踉跄 by 30%. This enables you to avoid being ragdolled by many 攻击, such as Rocket 蹂躏者 rockets, 蹂躏者 punches, and 监视者 staff swings, but not 更强 攻击 like Cannon Turret shots or Reinforced 侦察纵步者 rockets.
    • 硬直力值为 36 或更高的攻击可以无视“坚如磐石”被动效果将你击飞。

EMS Stun

The 轨道武器 EMS Strike 战略配备, EMS 迫击哨戒炮 阵地, and SEAF 大炮 Static Field Shells inflict a 特殊 变体 of Stun which creates a cloud of static that renders most 敌人 unable to move. 在电磁冲击波眩晕云中，绝地潜兵会严重减速并失去耐力。
