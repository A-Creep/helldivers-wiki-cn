物理条件

物理 Conditions are debuffs or buffs given to a 绝地潜兵 in 绝地潜兵 2.

目录

  • 1 条件类型
  • 2 参考资料

条件类型

  • Right Arm Broken
    • Negatively affects 手榴弹 and 战略配备 throwing 距离.[1]
    • Negatively affects weapon 精度 and 操控性.
  • Left Arm Broken
    • Negatively affects weapon 精度 and 操控性.
  • Left/Right Leg Broken
    • Slows you down.
    • Disables Sprinting.
  • Torso Injury
    • Causes 流血.
  • 减速
    • Caused by some foliage.
    • Caused by barbed wire.
    • Caused by EMS fields.
  • 出血
    • Lose all 耐力 and blood until you die.
  • 淹溺
    • Happens after swimming in a deep enough body of water for four 秒.
  • 灼烧
    • Take 伤害 over time.
    • Being in the prone position removes 燃烧. You can dive to be prone faster.
