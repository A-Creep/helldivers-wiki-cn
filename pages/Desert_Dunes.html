描述[edit | edit 来源]

灼热的沙漠星球，除了坚硬的平顶山以外，只有数不尽的沙丘。

The Desert Dunes 生物群系 is composed of sandy dune-based 地形, more often than not being flat, however, it still features occasional hills, and rarely a set of misty springs and ponds.

小型 and 中型-sized rocks and rock formations cover the 浮出地面, providing sudden cover. In between the rocks and the sand, 有时 tall, dead shrubs can be found.

The skies are blue similar to Earth, showcasing sand-colored clouds and one or more large suns. A 庞大 approaching sandstorm can also be seen in the backdrop of the 行星, though it will not disappear regardless of whether a sandstorm event occurs.

Thanks to the sandy 浮出地面 of the 行星, lots of structures and assets (including the 绝地潜兵) will be covered by 一些 sand.

Exostorms[edit | edit 来源]

On Desert Dunes planets that have been engulfed by an Exostorm, all Environmental Conditions will be 已移除. A permanent, slightly less potent 沙尘暴 will engluf the 浮出地面, featuring dusty skies and thick "fog" which still 特性 已降低 visibility.

The purple streaks that have been radiated from the 行星 into orbit can be seen from the ground between the clouds both during daytime and nighttime and 偶尔 "blink", giving the 浮出地面 a more purple color before dimming.
