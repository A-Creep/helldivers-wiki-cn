    Desert Dunes

    Desert Dunes 是一种沙地类型。 生物群系 可以在……中的各种行星上找到。 绝地潜兵 2.

    描述

    “

    灼热的沙漠星球，除了坚硬的平顶山以外，只有数不尽的沙丘。

    The Desert Dunes 生物群系 is composed of sandy dune-based 地形, more often than not being flat, however, it still features occasional hills, and rarely a set of misty springs and ponds.

    小型 and 中型-sized rocks and rock formations cover the 浮出地面, providing sudden cover. In between the rocks and the sand, 有时 tall, dead shrubs can be found.

    The skies are blue similar to Earth, showcasing sand-colored clouds and one or more large suns. A 庞大 approaching sandstorm can also be seen in the backdrop of the 行星, though it will not disappear 无论 whether a sandstorm event occurs.

    Thanks to the sandy 浮出地面 of the 行星, lots of structures and assets (including the 绝地潜兵) will be covered by 一些 sand.

    大都市

    在位于……的公园中。 Metropolises, the usual sandy 地形 can be found. Although the 生物群系 seemingly has 否 trees, trees 通常 seen in Primordial biomes （例如 火山 Jungle) 将会生成，在该生物群系中长有绿叶。

    异界风暴

    On Desert Dunes planets that have been engulfed by an Exostorm, all Environmental Conditions will be 已移除. A permanent, slightly less potent 沙暴 will engluf the 浮出地面, featuring dusty skies and thick "fog" which still 特性 已降低 visibility.

    从地面望去，在白天和黑夜的云层之间都能看到从行星辐射到轨道的紫色条纹，它们偶尔还会"闪烁"，在变暗之前让地表呈现出更紫的色泽。

    环境条件

    极暑 is only active during the day, meanwhile 极寒 is only active at night.

    酷热 极寒 沙暴 – Dense 沙暴 reduce mobility and greatly reduce visibility for both enemy and friendly units.

    环境危害

    • Occasional misty springs, harmless, though they slow down any 绝地潜兵 deciding to take a bath
    • Tall dead Ferns, slowing any creatures walking through down

    天气

    • 清楚
      • Clear 天气 is the 标准 天气 for the Desert Dunes, featuring 轻型 distant fog and clouds that barely even cover the sky. The clouds can be either puffy or thin veil-like, though not both simultaneously.

    • Hazy
      • Hazy 天气 is a common 天气 效果 in the Desert Dunes, with thicker fog setting in (though not enough to significantly lower visibility). 轻型 wind and heat distortion 效果 also set in for the 持续时间 of the event.

    • Standstorm
      • 沙暴 are rare 天气 events that cause a 庞大 orange fog of lifted sand to suddenly appear around the 任务 area. Along with the fog and the sand particles rapidly flying in one 方向, 闪电 is also present.
      • They are rampant and sudden, with the visibility dropping to a radius of about 50 meters in less than 30 秒.
