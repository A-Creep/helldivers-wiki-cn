利维坦封锁屏障 is an 操作 修正 in 绝地潜兵 2. This 修正 impacts all operations taken on 超级地球 during the Battle for 超级地球 starting on 困难 难度. This was later 已更改 to 不可能 难度.

After the Battle for 超级地球, this 修正 was added to all City Missions that take place during 光能者 operations.

琐事

  • 利维坦封锁屏障 is the only 操作 修正 with a unique icon. All other modifiers (including ones like 漫游尖啸虫) use the 标准 helix-shaped icon.
