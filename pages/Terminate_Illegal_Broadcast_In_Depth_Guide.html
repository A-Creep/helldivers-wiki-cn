The easiest way to 完成 the Terminate 非法 广播/In 深度 引导 任务 is to 摧毁 the 广播 塔 directly. The 广播 塔 will look something 点赞 the following 图像:

The 飞鹰 and 轨道武器 战略配备 are 完美 for destroying the 塔. Simply activate the 战略配备 by inputting the 正确 法典, and throw the 索敌 光束 at the 基地 of the 塔. 一次 the 战略配备 hits the 任务 should 显示 as 完成 and the 能力 to 撤离 will 成为 available.

Disable 广播 塔[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

If destroying the 广播 塔 is not an option, the 塔 can instead be disabled utilizing a 终端 at the 基地 of it. Simply approach the 终端 and tap the "Interact" key to 转变 it on

After a 少数 秒, the 终端 will 完成 its boot-up 顺序 and the interaction prompt will 回报. Interact with the 终端 再次 to enter the 终端 查看. Similar to 呼叫中 战略配备, the 玩家 will need to 输入 a 系列 of directional codes to unlock the 控制 panel. As the 法典 is 输入, the arrows on the screen will 变化 from white to yellow.

If you need to exit the 终端 for 任何 理性, you can re-press the interact key to exit the 查看. 一次 the arrow 法典 is 完成, a loading 酒吧 will appear. This is a waiting step with 否 输入 必需 from the 玩家

一次 the waiting step is completed, a new interactive screen will appear on the 终端. This screen involves the user using their directional inputs to find the spot with the 最好 信号 力量. Using the UP and DOWN directional 输入 will 调整 the bars on the 正确 侧面 of the 终端. Using the LEFT and 正确 directional inputs will 调整 the circle on the left 侧面 of the 终端.

开始 by using the UP and DOWN inputs to get the 信号 力量 as 高 as possible. 一次 the highest 数值 is found, use the LEFT and 正确 inputs to push the 信号 力量 to 100%.

一次 the 力量 is at 100% 制止 全部 actions, the 终端 should 完成 after a 少数 moments, completing the 任务.

撤离[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

一次 the main 任务 is 完成, a blue beacon will appear in the sky, indicating the 撤离 位置 of the 任务.

This beacon will not 移动 or disappear, and can be used to navigate to the 撤离 终端. The 撤离 终端 will be found at the 基地 of this beacon

Interacting with this 终端 will give another string of directional inputs 必需. 完成 this 终端 step the same way as the first step of the 非法 广播 塔 终端.

一次 the inputs have been entered, the 玩家 will be kicked out of the 终端 and a 计时器 will 开始.

The 玩家 will need to stay 关闭 to the 撤离 终端 for the 下一个 two 分钟 while waves of 敌人 攻击. A 警告 will appear if the 玩家 get too far away from the 撤离 site, giving the 玩家 20 秒 to 回报 to the 撤离 zone. If the 玩家 do not 回报 in 时间, the 撤离 will be canceled and will need to be re-triggered.

一次 the 计时器 reaches 0, the 撤离 舰船 will arrive. It will 射击 敌人 as it lands, and 打开 its 后部 门 for the 玩家 to enter.

The 舰船 will remain there as 长 as needed, until a 玩家 enters it. 一次 one 玩家 enters the 舰船 it will remain until 任一 全部 玩家 enter or 20 秒 has passed. 玩家 within the 舰船 are fully invulnerable to 伤害. 一次 全部 玩家 are dead or have entered the 舰船, it will 飞 away and 完成 the 任务.
