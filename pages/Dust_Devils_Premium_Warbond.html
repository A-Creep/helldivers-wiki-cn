概述

玩家 can use 奖章 to claim various items, including 3 战略配备, 2 armors, 2 武器, 10 cosmetics, and 300 超级货币. These 奖励 are 散射 across three pages, each requiring increasingly more 奖章 (ranging from 2 to 110). 玩家 start on page one, unlocking subsequent pages as they spend more 奖章, gradually expanding their customization options.
