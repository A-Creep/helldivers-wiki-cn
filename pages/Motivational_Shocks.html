激励电击
“

Allows all 绝地潜兵 to 恢复 faster after being slowed by an 攻击, such as 酸. Does not mitigate “area 效果,” such as EMS strikes.

— 军械库 描述

激励电击 is a 增益 in 绝地潜兵 2. When equipped, all 绝地潜兵 are able to 恢复 faster after being slowed by certain 攻击. While it does mitigate most 攻击 that slow down movement, it does not mitigate all of them.

描述

激励电击 is a 强化资源 that allows for 更大 恢复 when being hit by certain 攻击 that will slow down a 绝地潜兵 for a 有限 time. When equipped, these 攻击 will reduce the mobility debuff, allowing 绝地潜兵 to regain their mobility and 继续 moving around without restrain. The 强化资源 is only 克制 certain 攻击, which can be inflicted by an enemy 攻击 (such as an 酸 spit), or 地形-相关 障碍.

获取途径

the 激励电击 强化资源 is unlocked on the 1st page of the 极地爱国者 尊享战争债券 for 15 Medals.

Known 恢复 Times

Stun 中型 效果 from G-23 Stun 和 CQC-30 Stun Baton is 已降低 by about 2.5 秒. All other stun 效果 are 已降低 by half, with the exception of 轨道武器 EMS Strike clouds.

The slowed 效果 from Tremors is 已降低 by 25%, resulting in the 标准 10 second de-buff being 已降低 to 7.5 秒. [1]

The slowed 效果 caused from triggering an Ice Plant is 已降低 by 25%, resulting in the 标准 ~5 second de-buff being 已降低 to ~3.77 秒. [1]

问题

“

Allows all 绝地潜兵 to 恢复 faster after being slowed by an 攻击, such as 酸.

At this time, the 激励电击 强化资源 doesn't make you 恢复 faster from the slowed 效果 caused by 酸. Whether you bring the 强化资源 or not, the 持续时间 of the slow 效果 is the same. It should be a bug according to AH 支援 team.[2]
