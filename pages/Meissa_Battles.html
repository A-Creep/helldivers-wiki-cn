Background[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

机器人 and 终结族 launched 许多 攻击 on 许多 无辜 planets. The 绝地潜兵 were tasked with defending on two fronts.

Meissa, 返回 then was the only way for the 机器人 to enter Ymir 分区. Holding it would halt 机器人 advance deeper into 超级地球's 领土.

Background[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

绝地潜兵 recently repelled 机器人 攻击 on X-45, now they focused on liberating Meissa.

策略 and Importance[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Meissa was the only 行星 that was able to launch a 指挥 攻击 on X-45, liberating it would 剪切 off 机器人 access to the 行星, making it 更多 安全 and harder to 占领.

战斗[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Meissa was liberated on 6th of 6月. It was immediately attacked by 机器人, but The 绝地潜兵 managed to 按住 Meissa, at the 费用 of losing Vega 海湾.
