Background

机器人 and 终结族 launched many 攻击 on many 无辜 planets. The 绝地潜兵 were tasked with defending on two fronts.

策略 and Importance

Meissa, back then was the only way for the 机器人 to enter Ymir 分区. Holding it would halt 机器人 advance deeper into 超级地球's 领土.

战斗

绝地潜兵 on Meissa were severly outnumbered, as most 绝地潜兵 focused on 终结族 front. The battle was lost 24 hours after the 攻击 was launched.

Summary Meissa liberated and later successfully defended.


Background

绝地潜兵 recently repelled 机器人 攻击 on X-45, now they focused on liberating Meissa.

策略 and Importance

Meissa was the only 行星 that was able to launch a direct 攻击 on X-45, liberating it would cut off 机器人 access to the 行星, making it more secure and harder to 占领.

战斗

Meissa was liberated on 6th of June. It was immediately attacked by 机器人, but The 绝地潜兵 managed to hold Meissa, at the 费用 of losing Vega Bay.
