生成

破裂 武斗虫 first appear on 极难 on planets with the   破裂 Strain present, replacing 武斗虫 completely. They can spawn from Bug 突破，出现在巡逻队中，守卫 Bug Nests 和 目标，或来自特定的 虫洞.

行为

the 破裂 武士 is capable of 钻地中 and 出现中 from the ground, often taking 绝地潜兵 by 惊讶 if they aren't paying attention.

战术信息

破裂 武士 Specific

  • 破裂 武斗虫 that are 钻地中 will emerge from the ground within moments of closing within ~3m of a 绝地潜兵, often directly under said diver's feet, and immediately deal 伤害 if the player is in 正面 of them when this happens.
    • If a 绝地潜兵 is within 射程 of the 破裂 武士 to start to 浮出地面 but then runs away, the 破裂 武士 will immediately lunge at them to try and close the 距离, making it harder to avoid 伤害 if said player 遗骸 roughly in the 方向 they were tunneling.
      • 破裂 武士 that are surfacing deal 2 分离 instances of 伤害, the first immediately upon protruding their 正面 claws out of the ground, the second 大约 upon pulling their 后腿 out. This later point does not visually 表明 an 攻击 and may cause 玩家 attempting to 近战 the unit to take unexpected 伤害.
    • It is possible to evade their 钻地中 攻击 if the following conditions are met:
      • They have fully burrowed and are 地下 for at least ~3-4s.
      • The player sprinted right above and past them.
      • Perform this too early, and they will stay burrowed and then will hit the player with rigorous tracking.
  • 绝地潜兵 can either trick or force 破裂 武斗虫 into surfacing by sprinting past them or by shooting 爆炸 ordnance on top of them, such as a CB-9 Exploding Crossbow.
  • 应当注意的是， 破裂 武斗虫 often burrow in groups, and it can be 困难 to differentiate a single 钻地中 unit from a clustered group, potentially causing unattentive or overly eager divers to take significant 伤害.
  • 破裂 武斗虫 are more 危险 towards 任务 hosts, 出现中 from the ground and attacking much closer to the hosting 绝地潜兵 than non-hosting 绝地潜兵. Therefore, it is recommended to bring either 重型护甲, Feet First 被动 护甲, or wear an SH-32 防护罩生成包.

将军

  • 破裂 武士, unlike their normal 武士 counterparts, have  中型 护甲 on their heads and claws in exchange for less 主生命值.
  • The 前腿 and tail (part of 主体 in the 结构解析 章节) of a 破裂 武士 protrude slightly above its head and are 易受  轻型 穿透.
    • Their 钻地中 动画 exposes their entire body/tail for a moment, offering a brief chance for 轻型 穿透 弹道武器 to disrupt or kill.
  • The fastest way to kill a 破裂 武士 is by destroying its head, though destroying it will cause it to enter a "狂暴" state and 增加 its 移动速度 before 流血 out. 绝地潜兵 can shoot the 武士 more to fully kill it, or they can avoid it until it fully bleeds out.
    • Destroying any of its legs will prevent its 移动速度 增加.
