漫游尖啸虫 is an 操作 修正 in 绝地潜兵 2. This 修正 impacts 全部 missions within the 操作.

详情[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

飞空尖啸虫群会穿过战场。

战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 尖啸虫 lack 任何 排序 of 护甲 and can be taken out by 最多 武器库.
    • 能量-based 激光 武器库, such as the LAS-5 Scythe and the LAS-98 激光大炮, have 极其 远距离 and 瞬间 伤害, allowing them to easily 追踪 and 伤害 尖啸虫.
    • Shotguns with spread, such as the SG-8 Punisher and the SG-225IE Breaker 燃烧弹, can 伤害 尖啸虫 at 关闭 to 中距离.
    • the AC-8 机炮's flak 弹药 can be used to 击杀 多个 尖啸虫 at 任何 距离.
  • the A/MG-43 机枪 哨戒炮, A/G-16 加特林哨戒炮 , and 弧度-3 电弧发射器 are 有效 at 干掉中 swarms of 尖啸虫.
  • Rocks or 物理 objects can be used to 打破 line-of-sight from 尖啸虫, preventing them from attacking in the 方向 of said obstruction.
  • Laying 卧倒 on the 地面 will make a 绝地潜兵 harder for 尖啸虫 to hit.
  • Killing a 尖啸虫 in the middle of its 出击 攻击 will 原因 it to plummet directly towards the 绝地潜兵 that killed it. 绝地潜兵 are advised to 移动 or 出击 to avoid a 尖啸虫's 尸体 from dealing unnecessary 伤害.
