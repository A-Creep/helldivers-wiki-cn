Summary 防御 failed. 机器人 advanced deeper into Trigon 分区.


背景

In that time, most 绝地潜兵 were focused on 终结族 正面. 机器人 have decided to take 优势 and 攻击 Ustotu.

战略与重要性

Ustotu was an 重要 行星 in the Trigon 分区, it allowed access to Vandalon IV, so 绝地潜兵 could 攻击 Vandalon even if Mantes was captured.

战斗

The battle lasted for around 24 hours, 绝地潜兵 were severely outnumbered and lost this battle. Around 6.5 million 绝地潜兵 died during this battle.

The Ustotu will be under 机器人 控制, until the Second Battle of Ustotu, almost a month later.

背景

The TCS has been activated and 操作 Swift Disassembly was 已公布. 绝地潜兵 were tasked with capturing Troost.

Ustotu wasn't a necessary 行星, 绝地潜兵 were able to 占领 Troost even if they didn't liberate Ustotu, by liberating Vandalon IV.

战斗

绝地潜兵 quickly recaptured the 行星, and continued their advance deeper into 机器人 领土.
