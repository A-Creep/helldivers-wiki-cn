Into the Unjust: 6.0.3

Attention 绝地潜兵, As we fight against the 生化人, 超级地球 is making sure all 绝地潜兵 have every chance of democratic Success!

We're sharpening our 武器 of 质量 解放. All our efforts must find their Clanker targets!

Below you'll see the latest fixes and updates for our brave divers marching forward...

补丁 Highlights

  • The C4 pack gets more boom for your credits
  • Missiles hit their 目标 even more!


Balancing

B/MD C4 Pack

  • 增加 爆破力 of C4 from 30 to 40

修复

崩溃修复

  • Fixed a rare 导弹 相关 crash
  • Fixed a crash that could occur when finishing 任务 with a Convoy 目标
  • Fixed a crash on "快速夺取" and "没收资产" Missions
  • Fixed a crash would could occur on startup or shutdown
  • Fixed a rare crash which could occur when entering the ship


武器 & 战略配备 Fixes

  • 已修复 SH-51 Directional Shield 有时 not making any noise
  • Fixed a bug where reloading the R-72 Censor caused the camera to attach inside of the barrel of the weapon
  • 已修复 LAS-16 Trident not displaying hit 效果 on 环境 objects
  • 炮舰 Facilities are now properly destroyed by the democratic might of the Hellbomb
  • 已移除 a hatch close to the Bastion Tank gunner door that could mistakenly feed 任务-致命 材质 into the ammunition 舱室 of the tank


Miscellaneous Fixes

  • Fixed two 超级驱逐舰 videos missing audio in Simplified Chinese
  • 已降低 the likelihood of 突击兵 Operations not awarding 奖章
