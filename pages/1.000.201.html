已知问题 (Unchanged from 补丁 1.000.200)

These are issues that were either introduced by this 补丁 and are being worked on, or are from a 上一个 version and have not yet been fixed.

  • Game might crash when picking up a snowball or throwing back a 手榴弹
  • Various issues involving friend invites and cross-play:
  • Cross-platform friend invites might not show up in the Friend Requests tab.
  • 玩家 cannot unfriend other 玩家 befriended via friend code.
  • 玩家 cannot unblock 玩家 that were not in their Friends list beforehand.
  • 玩家 cannot befriend 玩家 with Steam names shorter than 3 characters.
  • 爆炸 weapon 属性 include only direct hit 伤害 but not 爆炸伤害.
  • Explosions do not break your limbs (except for when you fly into a rock).
  • 行星 解放 reaches 100% at the end of every Defend 任务.
  • Drowning in deep water with a 活力增益 equipped puts 绝地潜兵 in a broken state.
  • 战略配备 beam might attach itself to an enemy but it will deploy to its original 位置.
  • Some player customizations (like 标题 or body 类型) may reset after restarting the game
