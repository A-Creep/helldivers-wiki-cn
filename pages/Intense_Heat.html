效果

极暑 is applied depending on the time of day and 天气 conditions. Planets with 烈焰龙卷 will activate the 极暑 修正 only for the 持续时间 of the 火焰 龙卷风 storm.

  • 最大 耐力 is halved. This means it takes half as long for a 绝地潜兵 to become winded from constant sprinting, but it also means they regain their 最大 耐力 in half the usual time (because the rate of 耐力 regen is unaffected).
  • Heat-based 武器 lose heat slowly, contrary to the in-game 描述 of increasing build-up. This means that once overheated, it takes longer for the weapon to cool down, 通常 by a factor of 33% 亲戚 to it's normal 冷却 持续时间.[1]

Affected Biomes

  • Desert Dunes
  • Desert Cliffs
  • Scorched Moor
  • Magma Desert

变更

  • The 极暑 修正 is 否 longer static throughout the entire 任务, and may now change based on time of day or different 天气 conditions.
  • Some 沙漠星球 will now only have the 极暑 修正 active during the day. This means that during night the 行星 will either have 否 修正, or in some cases have the 极寒 修正.
  • Arid planets (similar to the Hellmire 行星) will now only have the 极暑 修正 active during 火焰 龙卷风 storms.
