Pyro 装甲兵 是一种轻型 机器人 属于……的一部分 Incineration Corps subfaction. It is equipped with a Portable Flamer and specialised White Phosphorous Grenades.

生成

Pyro Troopers can be found on all difficulties on planets invaded by the Incineration Corps. Pyro Troopers can spawn from 机器人空投, within patrols, guarding 机器人 哨站 and 目标，或从 机器人 Fabricators.

Starting on 挑战, they can be found guarding Minor Places of Interest.

行为

Pyro Troopers will sprint towards 绝地潜兵 to get in 射程 with their 火焰喷射器, continuously using it in quick 2.4 second bursts 3 times before having to cool off the weapon for 3.4 秒. If within cover, they may 偶尔 throw a specialised White Phosphorus 燃烧弹 手榴弹 to flush out 绝地潜兵.

Their 燃料箱 can unintentionally 爆炸 if damaged, instantly killing the Pyro 装甲兵 but significantly damaging and 燃烧 nearby 机器人 or 绝地潜兵.

When alerted to a 绝地潜兵's presence, Pyro Troopers can 火焰 a flare to 呼叫 a 机器人空投.

战术信息

Pyro 装甲兵 Specific

  • As their 毒气 tanks are taller than them, they can be easily shot at to cause them to 爆炸. 绝地潜兵 should ensure they are at a safe 距离 away before doing so.
    • Diving backwards while shooting them is recommended to avoid potential 伤害 from the Industrial Flamer.
  • The 毒气 tanks can still potentially 爆炸 on their 遗骸, but they can still be shot to blow them up prematurely.

轻型机器人

  • 全身轻型 机器人 can be suppressed by shooting at them; this will briefly reduce their 精度.
  • 全身轻型 机器人 左臂中隐藏着信号弹发射器，可以用来呼叫 机器人空投.
    • Destroying them before it can finish launching the flare will prevent a 机器人空投 from being called in.
  • 手榴弹和爆头可以稳定一击秒杀所有轻型机器人。

Other data on Incineration Corps troopers

  • The grenades used by the incineration corps 引爆 much sooner and are 燃烧弹.
  • 火箭奇袭者 of this sub 阵营 have their rockets replaced with a strong 单发, similar to the bolt from bunker 炮塔
  • Incineration Corps may be found to be 通常 more 有护甲, with some troopers who did not have 护甲, to now have 护甲.
