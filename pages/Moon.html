描述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

“

崎岖不平的孤单卫星，地下埋着极其珍贵的矿产。

Similarly to the 沙漠 Dunes 生物群系, the 月球 生物群系 is composed of grey sandy 沙丘-based 地形, 更多 经常 than not being flat, however, it still features occasional hills, and 很少 a 套装 of misty springs and ponds. 非常 prominently, it features craters 全部 over the 浮出地面.

小 and 中型-sized grey rocks and 岩石 formations 掩护 the 浮出地面, providing sudden 掩护. In between the rocks and the sand, 有时 高, dead shrubs can be found.

Notably, the 大气层 of a 月球 行星 is 非常 薄 as 否 clouds ever 形成 whatsoever, and literal 太空 is clearly 可见 from the 浮出地面 whether during the day or at 夜. While unspecified, air seems to be present on the 行星 as cities and colonists seemingly thrive on the 浮出地面. A 庞大 approaching grey sandstorm can also be seen in the backdrop of the 行星, though it will not disappear regardless of whether a sandstorm 事件 occurs.
