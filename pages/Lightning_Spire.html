战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • The 闪电 Spire targets any live non-光能者 targets within 25 meters of its line of sight.
    • Any 光能者 unit caught between a 闪电 Spire and its non-光能者 目标 will be used as a conduit for lighting chains.
  • The 闪电 Spire takes about 1 second to charge up, after which it sends out a devastatingly powerful 电力 弧度 at its 目标. This 攻击 will instantly kill most 绝地潜兵 on hit. However, some armors may allow to survive (assuming head is not hit):
    • 绝地潜兵 with 导电 护甲 will only take minor 电弧伤害 of 12 or less.
    • 绝地潜兵 with Acclimated/Adreno-Defibrillator 中型 or 重型护甲, or with Desert Stormer 中型护甲 will be able to survive a hit at high enough 生命值.
    • Having Acclimated/Adreno-Defibrillator 轻型护甲, or 额外衬垫/弹道 Padding 重型护甲 is enough to survive at high 生命值 but only with Vitality Enhancement 强化资源 active.
    • Having Desert Stormer 轻型护甲, or any 护甲 with 175 or less 护甲值 and no 弧度/chest 抗性, is 通常 not enough to survive.
  • Like the A/弧度-3 特斯拉塔, the 闪电 Spire will not 目标 卧倒 绝地潜兵 if they move inside their 射程.
    • Like other 光能者 弧度 攻击, going prone after the player has been targeted and the 攻击 is already charging up will cause the 攻击 to cancel or miss the player.
    • However, 闪电 Spires will 目标 any guard dog drones in 射程, which can often chain into their owner even if they are 卧倒. It is advised to manually deactivate drones before engaging 闪电 Spires in 近距离.

外观[edit | edit 来源]

  • 与……相比 A/弧度-3 特斯拉塔, the 闪电 Spire has a more sinister, spiky 外观.
  • There is also a lack of any 可见 rotating assembly or other moving parts.
  • The large ball of 能量 at the top will grow in size when a 目标 enters its 射程, and a subtle charging up sound can be heard.
  • The 闪电 Spire is mounted on top of a sizable base, which is left intact when the Spire itself is destroyed.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • 与……相比 A/弧度-3 Tesla Towers of the 超级地球 武装力量, the 光能者 闪电 Spires deal roughly third the amount of 伤害.
  • The 闪电 Spire shows up on the 小地图 as a red blip when in 射程, just like enemy units.
