First version

The first version of the crossover, titled "绝地潜兵 2 x Killzone 2" was planned to include 超级商店 内容 released in two phases, with the first phase launched on 12月 18, 2024[1]. The second phase was originally scheduled for release on 12月 23, 2024. However, following significant community backlash over the exorbitant pricing of Phase One, ARROWHEAD 和 Guerrilla decided to make amends by altering the crossover.

  • The Phase One offer time in the 超级商店 was greatly 已增加, allowing for more 绝地潜兵 to farm 超级货币 in a reasonable amount of time.
  • The entire second phase was 已更改 to be a complimentary 节日 gift to all active 绝地潜兵, extending their season's greetings.

the W.A.S.P.发射器，一款 支援武器, was initially made temporarily available to all 绝地潜兵 on 星球 invaded by the then recently returned 光能者, before becoming permanently unlockable on the 24th.

Second version

Main 条款: 正义 Revenants 传奇 战争债券

On 12月 11, 2025 the return of the crossover, now titled "绝地潜兵 2 x Killzone", was announced[2]. Starting on 12月 18, 2025 all original Killzone items, with the addition of new 载具 patterns, were available in a new 传奇 战争债券 called 正义复仇者.

玩家 who have previously purchased any of the Killzone items got the 战争债券 unlocked for free, however those who received the gifted items in 12月 2024 but did not 购买 any Killzone items, would still have to unlock the 战争债券 using 1500 超级货币. Items previously purchased (or gifted) were already medal unlocked in the 战争债券.
