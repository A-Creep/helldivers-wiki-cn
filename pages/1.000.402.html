1.000.402 was a hotfix 更新 released for 绝地潜兵 2.

目录

  • 1 补丁 01.000.402

补丁 01.000.402

🌍 概述

对于本次补丁，我们对以下领域进行了改进和更改：

  • Quality of life improvements
  • 崩溃修复
  • 基本信息 bug fixes

🔧 修复

崩溃

  • Fixed various 崩溃 that occur when changing settings.
  • Fixed crash or corrupted font 纹理 when changing languages.
  • Fixed a crash that occurs after exiting a 任务.
  • Fixed a crash that occurs when opening the social menu with more than 100 friends. As a result we have also 有限 the friends list visibility to 100. You will remain friends with anyone over 100, but you may not be able to see them if they are 离线.
  • Fixed crash social menu crash 相关 to high number of blocked 玩家 and/or friend requests.
  • Fixed a crash when minimizing after changing fullscreen mode.
  • Fixed rare crash which could occur when picking up 装备.
  • Fixed a crash when a client interacts with a 终端 in a waiting state after host migration.
  • Fixed a crash 相关 to minefields.

杂项修复

  • Fixed issues with dodge and prone.
  • Fixed not being able to stand up while wielding the 防弹盾牌.
  • Fixed the Recoilless 换弹 速度.
  • Fixed issue where users couldn't write a 描述 on 控制台 when reporting a player.
  • Fixes 载具 preview not despawning in 战争债券 when quickly scrolling through the 目录.
  • 已修复 长矛 not 索敌 几个 entities (spawners, compound objects, etc).
  • 哨戒炮 now have a higher 摧毁 value so most explosions do not instantly 摧毁 them 无论 the 伤害 amount.
  • Fixed bug where 操作 progress would be lost when kicked due to inactivity.
  • The "Remove Friend" and "Block Player" buttons are now hold-to-确认 instead of a single click.
  • Fixed bug where dying would try to enter ADS.
  • Fixed a soft lock if you 接收 an interact emote while holding a 手榴弹.
  • Fixed 投射物 to crosshair inaccuracies in ADS when the player is in different stances
  • Fixed broken player model in career tab when on someone else's ship.
  • 审判者: fixed 不正确 后坐力 values.
  • Fixed 军械库 terminals losing their functionality if the last client player who interacted with them leaves the host's ship.
  • Fixed missing localization for flying patrols 行动限制条件.
  • 修复了以下问题： throwing knives could be left floating in the air.
  • 支援 for non-latin fonts in the game's 安装 folder path.
  • 载具 skins are now applied to all 载具 variations when equipping directly from 战争债券.
  • Fixed flag 目标 not properly tracking a 绝地潜兵’s position while on a Combat Walker.
  • Fixed missing 描述 for the PH-202 Twigsnapper 头盔.
  • Fixed the issue where the 航天飞机 would clip through Bile Titans and 地形 during landing sequences.
  • 已改进 performance on PS5 when CPU bound.
  • Fixes FX 剩余 when destroying 孢子喷涌虫.
  • Ensure 兴奋剂 vfx 遗骸 on screen for the entire 持续时间 of the stim 效果 when 急救包 bonus is present.
  • 修复 for emote receiver 玩家 being able to use their 武器 during emote 动画
  • Hellbombs now get called down facing the player, rather than away from the player.

🧠 已知问题

这些问题要么是由本次补丁引入的且正在修复中，要么来自之前的版本且尚未修复。

  • Sending friend requests via friend code in game currently does not work.
  • 玩家 may be unable to be joined or invited to the game.
  • 玩家 added to the 'Recent 玩家' list will appear in the middle of the list.
  • 玩家可能会遇到勋章和超级货币发放的延迟。
  • Available Operations are generated again after reconnecting after getting AFK kicked.
  • 流血死亡的敌人不会推进个人指令和歼灭任务。
  • 电弧武器有时表现不一致，有时会哑火。
  • 大多数武器在瞄准时射击位置低于准星。
  • Plasma Punisher is unable to shoot out of the shield generators.
  • 战略配备光束可能会附着在敌人身上，但会部署到原始位置。
  • "Hand Carts" 舰船模块 does not reduce 防护罩生成包's 冷却.
  • 吐酸泰坦 有时 does not take 伤害 to the head.
  • 强袭虫’s butt does not take 伤害 from explosions.
  • 玩家 may become stuck in the 武装配置 when joining a game in progress.
  • 增援 may not be available for 玩家 who join a game in progress.
  • 每个防御任务结束时，星球解放度达到100%。
  • "Raise Flag of 超级地球" 目标 does not show a progress bar.
  • 任务 count in the Career tab is being reset to zero after every game 重新开始.
  • Some 武器’ descriptions are out-of-date and don’t reflect their 当前 design.
