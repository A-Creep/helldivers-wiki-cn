概述

玩家 can use Medals, a form of in-game 货币, to claim various items, including 6 boosters, 14 武器, 10 armors, 20 cosmetics, and 750 超级货币. These 奖励 are spread across 10 pages, each requiring increasingly more Medals (ranging from 1 to 150). Cadets start on page one, unlocking subsequent pages as they spend more Medals, gradually expanding their customization options.
