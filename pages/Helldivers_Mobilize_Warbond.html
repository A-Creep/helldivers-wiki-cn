概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

玩家 can use 奖牌, a 形成 of in-游戏 货币, to 声称 various items, including 6 boosters, 14 武器, 10 armors, 20 cosmetics, and 750 超级货币. These 奖励 are spread across 10 pages, 每个 requiring increasingly 更多 奖牌 (ranging from 1 to 150). Cadets 开始 on 页面 one, unlocking subsequent pages as they 花费 更多 奖牌, gradually expanding their customization options.
