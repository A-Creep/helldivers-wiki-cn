战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The Stoker is equipped with an under-barrel 火焰喷射器, allowing for 高伤害 in a 宽阔 范围 at point-blank 射程.
    • The 火焰喷射器 is 非常 similar to that of the P-72 Crisper, sharing the same 伤害, 射程 (20m, first person), and 容量 (50).
    • 绝地潜兵 can map the left 武器 action to an easily accessible key to quickly 切换 between the 火焰喷射器 and 枪.
      • One use of this is lighting a 重型 敌人 on 火焰 (点赞 a Fleshmob), then switching to the 子弹 模式 to lay down full auto 火焰, and switching to the 火焰喷射器 再次 to reignite the 敌人 before going 返回 to 子弹 火焰 再次 to 完成 the 击杀.
      • The 火焰喷射器 can also 法案 as a powerful 关闭-射程 backup option when the 子弹 弹匣 is depleted.
    • 绝地潜兵 should be cautious when firing the 火焰喷射器 in 关闭 quarters to prevent accidental self-immolation.
      • In 命令 to 减少 the 效果 of self-伤害, equipping 护甲 with the Inflammable 被动 is recommended.
  • The under-barrel 火焰喷射器 excels at damaging and confusing 大 敌人 such as Chargers, Fleshmobs, and 阿尔法 指挥官
    • With the mild 困惑 效果 已添加 in 1.006.100, the 火焰喷射器 does an 极佳 job of disrupting these 敌人, even while charging, allowing 绝地潜兵 to 获得 the upper hand.
  • Having a 基地 数值 of 55 (compared to the 冲锋枪-37 捍卫者's 80), the Stoker has a 低 人体工学 数值, 含义 that it is 更慢 to 移动 around while aiming.
  • At 420 转速, the Stoker has the lowest 射速 out of 全部 submachine guns. 绝地潜兵 should be cautious with their 目标 when using this 武器.

琐事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The Stoker is the second primary to have an underbarrel secondary 火焰. The first being the AR/GL-21 One-Two with its under-barrel 榴弹发射器.
  • The Stoker's 外观 seems to be loosely based on early 20th 世纪 submachine guns such as the MP18 and Sten MkV, while maintaining 一些 parts of the 冲锋枪-37 捍卫者.
