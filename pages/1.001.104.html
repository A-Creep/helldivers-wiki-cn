Plasma 投射物 行为

We’ve standardized the 行为 of Plasma projectiles, which previously had varying characteristics in how they traveled through the air. Now, all Plasma projectiles 经验值 high drag, causing them to slow down faster. However, each 等离子武器 still fires projectiles at different speeds and calibers, so their 有效 ranges will remain distinct. For most 武器, this change won’t be very noticeable. With the SG-8P Punisher Plasma you’ll see a less pronounced 弧度 in its 投射物 path.
