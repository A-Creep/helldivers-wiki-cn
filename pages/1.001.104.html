等离子 投射物 行为

We’ve standardized the 行为 of 等离子 projectiles, which previously had varying characteristics in how they traveled through the air. Now, 全部 等离子 projectiles 经验值 高 drag, causing them to slow down 更快. However, 每个 等离子武器 still fires projectiles at different speeds and calibers, so their 有效 ranges will remain distinct. For 最多 武器, this 变化 won’t be 非常 noticeable. With the SG-8P Punisher 等离子 you’ll see a 更少 pronounced 弧度 in its 投射物 path. Instead, it will 开始 off 更快 and lose 速度 更多 rapidly, keeping its 有效 射程 关于 the same.
