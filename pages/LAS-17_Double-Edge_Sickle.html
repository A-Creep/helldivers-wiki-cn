“

The LAS-17 Double-Edge Sickle is a Primary 能量型 weapon that operates like an 突击步枪, shooting targets with a 弹幕 of laser pulses but will 伤害 the user after prolonged firing.

功能

The LAS-17 Double-Edge Sickle is a LAS-16 Sickle with all overheating protections conveniently 已移除, expelling excess heat to the user. This allows it to 火焰 for much longer before reaching 最大 heat, and the 散热器 does not need to be replaced upon reaching 最大 heat.

随着武器升温，它发射的激光脉冲威力增强，但射击时对使用者造成的伤害也会增加：

  • 0-25% heat: Laser pulses are  轻型 护甲 penetrating and deal 60 伤害. Does not deal 伤害 to the user.
  • 26-50% heat: Laser pulses are  中型 护甲 penetrating and deal 55 伤害. Deals 10 heat 伤害 每秒 to the user.
    • Visually, the weapon starts to emit sparks from the sides as it fires, as well as bursts of 火焰. This can cause visual noise in first-person mode, but otherwise, visual clarity is unaffected.
  • 51-90% heat: Laser pulses are  中型 护甲 penetrating and deal 70 伤害. Deals 20 heat 伤害 a second to the user.
    • Visually, the weapon starts smoking and emitting even more sparks and flames. Visual clarity in first-person mode is moderately affected, but still allows for some use.
  • 91%+ heat: Laser pulses are  重型 护甲 penetrating and deal 70 伤害. Sets the user on 火焰 and deals 50 heat 伤害 a second (in addition to 伤害 from being set on 火焰.) Without proper resistances, this stage is incredibly 致命 and is very likely to kill the user.
    • Visually, the weapon fully catches on 火焰, billowing smoke, and emitting an 极难 amount of sparks in every 方向. First-person mode is nigh 无用 at this point, and the sheer number of sparks can even make it 困难 to tell if your shots are landing in third-person mode.

The DE Sickle is not affected by 极暑, but cools down 50% faster on planets with 极寒.

战术信息

这把武器拥有巨大的压制伤害潜力，但代价高昂——自伤最终可能杀死使用者，而且只能减轻，无法完全消除。

对于想充分利用这把武器的绝地潜兵，有几件物品可以减轻灼烧伤害的负担；

  • Wearing an 易燃易爆 护甲套装 降低 burn 伤害 to a quarter of its normal value. 应变自如 and 沙漠突击者 also 减少 burn 伤害 by 50% and 40% respectively in addition to their other 效果. Heavier 变体 of these armors allow for longer firing periods before taking 伤害 compared to lighter options.
  • The Vitality Enhancement 强化资源 reduce all ticks of 伤害 by a flat amount.
    • 如果搭配适应环境或沙漠风暴，灼烧伤害会被降低到足以让双刃镰在超过51%热量前无法伤害使用者。
    • 如果搭配防火，灼烧伤害会被降低到足以让双刃镰在超过91%热量前无法伤害使用者。
    • When passing the safe level of heat, further 伤害 can be mitigated by burst firing the DE Sickle for under a second. However, doing this will impact the 伤害 每秒.
  • When the weapon passes 91% heat, going prone prevents the user from being set on 火焰. This can greatly 改进 its use time at this level of heat.
  • Using a Stim when the 燃烧 is too much to bear can keep the shooter alive as long as they have spare stims. This can be supported by the 急救包 被动 and/or 试验性注射剂 强化资源. The P-11 治疗剂手枪 can provide healing to DE Sickle users, and benefits from the aforementioned perks.

While it has the longest firing 容量 of any LAS weapon (theoretically infinite), it has the longest 冷却 time of its family. Reloading a new ICE will reset the heat quickly, but also remove the bonus 伤害 that comes with it. Regardless, it can still be prudent to simply 换弹 the weapon after reaching 危险 heat thresholds during extended engagements; expending an ICE is often more economical than continuing to 火焰 at high temperatures and using up valuable stims.

  • 双刃镰实际上无法超过100%热量，因此无论在该等级使用多久，冷却时间都保持不变。
  • Only the third round shot from a 91%+ heat DE Sickle will 伤害 the user. This allows the user to 火焰  重型 laser pulses indefinitely, but greatly impacts 伤害 每秒. Useful for gravely injured 绝地潜兵 that are in desperate encounters.
    • As the DE Sickle accumulates heat, sparks, smoke, and flames will fly toward the 绝地潜兵 firing it. This 增加 in intensity as heat rises. At high levels of heat, this obscures the 绝地潜兵's vision when firing in first-person mode, and can even make third-person aiming more 困难.
