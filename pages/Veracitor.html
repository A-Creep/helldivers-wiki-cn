生成

Veracitors are part of the  Appropriators sub-阵营 of the 光能者 and can only spawn on worlds where the Appropriators are present. They begin 生成 on level 4 难度 from 穿梭舰 or patrols.

行为

Veracitors will pursue 绝地潜兵 through charging and approaching them with their leg-like appendages. Once close enough, they will swipe with their claws to deal 伤害. At 射程, they will cross their arms in order to 保护 the piloting 监视者. The pilot will also 有时 do a charge if far enough from the 绝地潜兵.

战术信息

  • The 驾驶舱 is 受保护 by a rechargeable 能量护盾, when destroyed, the pilot is 易受 gunfire and when killed will also 摧毁 the mech.
    • Unlike normal 监视者, the pilot is a unified  轻型 hitbox, so there is no need to aim for particular weakpoints when shooting the pilot.
  • Veracitors, like their 御门者 sibling enemy, are highly 抵抗 to pure 爆炸伤害, as all limbs are immune to 爆炸伤害.
  • The arms of a Veracitor are  中型 护甲, leaving them 易受 the M-1000 Maxigun, MG-43 机枪, or other 武器 capable of penetrating 中型护甲. The RL-77 空爆火箭弹发射器 with its  中型 penetrating flak is 极其 有效 when aimed at the 驾驶舱, due to that area's close proximity and line-of-sight to all of the limbs.
    • Destroying the head of the Veracitor 外骨骼机甲 will permanently disable the shield covering its pilot.
    • Destroying the 护甲 surrounding the "head" of this enemy will reveal an  Very 轻型 弱点 that can be destroyed to 摧毁 the entire enemy. Holding the highground allows you to dispatch the Veracitor with this method even easier.
  • The Veracitor features 几个  Very 轻型 弱点 blue vents on its body. Destroying these 摧毁 the body part they are attached to. The 驾驶舱 弱点 however is 致命, instantly eliminating the Veracitor.
  • 反坦克 武器库 such as the EAT-17 和 GR-8 无后坐力炮 should 通常 目标 the unshielded parts of the 驾驶舱 as it is far easier to hit and 消除 目标.
  • Unlike other 敌人, a patrolling Veracitor can detect and engage 绝地潜兵 from 100 meters.

琐事

  • If a Veracitor is stunned with an EMP 攻击 such as a G-23 Stun or A/M-23 EMS 迫击哨戒炮, then the 监视者 pilot can be seen angrily smacking at the controls to try and boot it back up.
  • The Veracitor is likely a 参考 to the Yale Veracitor forklift. This suggests that the primary 目的 of the Veracitor is excavation, a theory further supported by it’s 外观 alongside 光能者 ruins
    • This is further reinforced by its 描述, which states that it is "an overly 复杂 forklift".
