补丁 01.000.200[edit | edit 来源]

🌎 概述

This 更新 includes

  • 平衡 changes to missions, 战略配备, 武器, 敌人 and 绝地潜兵.
  • 基本信息 fixes and 稳定性 improvements.

📍 玩法

行星 危害 additions

  • 暴风雪
  • 沙尘暴

最大 level cap raised to 150

⚖️ Balancing 任务

  • 回收 Essential Personnel
    • Moved the enemy spawn points further away from the 目标 to give 玩家 a fairer chance of defending the 位置.
    • There are fewer civilians 必需 to 完成 the 任务 on higher difficulties.
  • 摧毁指挥碉堡
    • Now has more 目标 locations, the 任务 was too 容易 before compared to other missions.
    • It can now appear in operations from 难度 5.
  • Halved the 负面 效果 of 行动限制条件 that 增加 战略配备 cooldowns or 呼叫 times.


Primary, Secondary, & 支援武器

  • 电弧发射器: fixed charging inconsistencies; it will now always take 1s to charge a shot.
  • 电弧发射器: 已降低 距离 from 50m to 35m.
  • 电弧发射器: 已增加 硬直力度.
  • “护卫犬”: now restores full 弹药 from supply boxes.
  • 反器材步枪: 伤害提升30%。
  • Breaker 燃烧弹: 伤害 per bullet 已增加 from 15 per bullet to 20 per bullet.
  • 火焰伤害 per tick 已增加 by 50% (from all sources).
  • 解放者 Penetrator: now has a full auto mode.
  • Dominator: 已增加 伤害 from 200 to 300.
  • Dominator: 已增加 硬直.
  • Diligence 反制 Sniper: 已增加 护甲穿透 from 轻型 to 中型.
  • Slugger: 已降低 硬直.
  • Slugger: 已降低 伤害 from 280 to 250.
  • Slugger: 已降低 爆破 force.
  • Slugger: fixed 护甲穿透 tag in the menu.
  • Slugger, 解放者 Concussive, 参议员: fixed 不正确 护甲穿透 标签 in the menu.
  • 无后坐力炮: 已增加 the number of rockets you restore from supply boxes from 2 to 3.
  • Spear: 已增加 the number of missiles you restore from supply boxes from 1 to 2.
  • 重机枪: the highest 射速 mode 已降低 from 1200 rpm to a more 中等 950

战略配备

  • 爱国者 外骨骼机甲: rockets will now penetrate 护甲 only on direct hit.

敌人

  • Balancing adjustments have been made to
    • Chargers normal 近战攻击 now does less 伤害 against 外骨骼机甲.
    • 胆汁喷涌虫 and 抚育喷涌虫 do less 伤害 with their puke.
    • the 吐酸泰坦 can no longer be stunned.
    • 尖啸虫 no longer create bug 突破.
    • 尖啸虫 hitting you while they are dead now does significantly less 伤害.

绝地潜兵

  • Balancing adjustments have been made to
    • 重型 and 中型 护甲 protects better and you now take about 10% less 伤害 than before while wearing 重型 and about 5% less when wearing 中型护甲. 加固 突击兵 and 轻型护甲 is unchanged.

🔧 Fixes

  • Fixed issue where save settings for PS5 would be reset when the game is rebooted, causing things such as 武装配置 and hint settings to reset.
  • 敌人 now properly 目标 外骨骼机甲. Previously, many 敌人 effectively ignored 外骨骼机甲 if a 绝地潜兵 on foot was available for them to 目标.
  • Fixed 外骨骼机甲 being able to 火焰 their 武器 while opening the 小地图.
  • The 绝地潜兵 and the 外骨骼机甲 both had a bug that made them 有时 take 爆炸伤害 多个 times making things like 机器人 rockets be too deadly, this is now fixed.
  • 机器人 enemy constellations that preferred to spawn more of certain Devastators 类型 did not work and are now functioning as they should. This means that 有时 when playing against the 机器人 you will face more Devastators instead of other enemy 类型.
  • We have 已改进 the system that prevents 绝地喷射舱 steering close to large or 重要 objects.
    • We have solved issues where the 有效 area around objects was a lot larger than intended.
    • We have 已降低 the number of objects that prevent 绝地喷射舱 steering.
    • ⚠️Note: This system is intended to prevent softlocks where 玩家 can 掉落 on 重要 interaction points, or 掉落 into unintended places. We will 继续 to 监控 the state of the system after the 更新 to see if additional tweaks are necessary.
  • Fixed cases where the ground under some assets could be bombed causing them to float.
  • 防弹盾牌 changes:
    • Collision mesh has been slightly 已增加 in size for more forgiveness
    • 已更改 shield poses so that less of the 绝地潜兵 is 暴露
    • Addressed bug where parts of the 绝地潜兵 would become 脆弱 while using the shield in first person

🧠 已知问题

  • These are issues that were either introduced by this 补丁 and are being worked on, or are from a 上一个 version and have not yet been fixed.
  • Game might crash when picking up a snowball or throwing back a 手榴弹
  • Various issues involving friend invites and cross-play:
    • Cross-platform friend invites might not show up in the Friend Requests tab.
    • 玩家 cannot unfriend other 玩家 befriended via friend code.
    • 玩家 cannot unblock 玩家 that were not in their Friends list beforehand.
    • 玩家 cannot befriend 玩家 with Steam names shorter than 3 characters.
  • 爆炸 weapon 属性 include only direct hit 伤害 but not 爆炸伤害.
  • Explosions do not break your limbs (except for when you fly into a rock).
  • 行星 解放 reaches 100% at the end of every Defend 任务.
  • Drowning in deep water with a 活力增益 equipped puts 绝地潜兵 in a broken state.
  • 战略配备 beam might attach itself to an enemy but it will deploy to its original 位置.
  • Some player customizations (like 标题 or body 类型) may reset after restarting the game.
