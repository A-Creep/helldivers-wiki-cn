补丁 01.000.200[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

🌎 概述

This 更新 includes

  • 平衡 changes to missions, 战略配备, 武器, 敌人 and 绝地潜兵.
  • 基本信息 修复 and 稳定性 improvements.

📍 玩法

行星 危害 additions

  • 暴风雪
  • 沙尘暴

最大 level 便帽 raised to 150

⚖️ Balancing 任务

  • 回收 Essential Personnel
    • Moved the 敌人 spawn points further away from the 目标 to give 玩家 a fairer 几率 of defending the 位置.
    • There are fewer civilians 必需 to 完成 the 任务 on 更高 difficulties.
  • 摧毁指挥碉堡
    • Now has 更多 目标 locations, the 任务 was too 容易 before compared to other missions.
    • It can now appear in operations from 难度 5.
  • Halved the 负面 效果 of 行动限制条件 that 增加 战略配备 cooldowns or 呼叫 times.


Primary, Secondary, & 支援武器

  • 电弧发射器: 已修复 charging inconsistencies; it will now 总是显示 take 1s to 冲锋 a 射击.
  • 电弧发射器: 已降低 距离 from 50m to 35m.
  • 电弧发射器: 已增加 硬直力度.
  • “护卫犬”: now restores full 弹药 from 供给 boxes.
  • 反器材步枪: 伤害提升30%。
  • Breaker 燃烧弹: 伤害 per 子弹 已增加 from 15 per 子弹 to 20 per 子弹.
  • 火焰伤害 per tick 已增加 by 50% (from 全部 sources).
  • 解放者 Penetrator: now has a full auto 模式.
  • Dominator: 已增加 伤害 from 200 to 300.
  • Dominator: 已增加 硬直.
  • Diligence 反制 狙击手: 已增加 护甲穿透 from 轻型 to 中型.
  • Slugger: 已降低 硬直.
  • Slugger: 已降低 伤害 from 280 to 250.
  • Slugger: 已降低 爆破 之势.
  • Slugger: 已修复 护甲穿透 标签 in the 菜单.
  • Slugger, 解放者 Concussive, 参议员: 已修复 不正确 护甲穿透 标签 in the 菜单.
  • 无后坐力炮: 已增加 the 数字 of rockets you restore from 供给 boxes from 2 to 3.
  • 飞矛: 已增加 the 数字 of missiles you restore from 供给 boxes from 1 to 2.
  • 重机枪: the highest 射速 模式 已降低 from 1200 转速 to a 更多 中等 950

战略配备

  • 爱国者 外骨骼机甲: rockets will now penetrate 护甲 only on 指挥 hit.

敌人

  • Balancing adjustments have been made to
    • Chargers normal 近战攻击 now does 更少 伤害 against 外骨骼机甲.
    • 胆汁喷涌虫 and 抚育喷涌虫 do 更少 伤害 with their 呕吐物.
    • the 吐酸泰坦 can 否 longer be stunned.
    • 尖啸虫 否 longer 创建 bug 突破.
    • 尖啸虫 hitting you while they are dead now does significantly 更少 伤害.

绝地潜兵

  • Balancing adjustments have been made to
    • 重型 and 中型 护甲 protects 更好 and you now take 关于 10% 更少 伤害 than before while wearing 重型 and 关于 5% 更少 when wearing 中型护甲. 加固 突击兵 and 轻型护甲 is unchanged.

🔧 修复

  • 已修复 问题 where 拯救 settings for PS5 would be 重置 when the 游戏 is rebooted, causing things such as 武装配置 and hint settings to 重置.
  • 敌人 now properly 目标 外骨骼机甲. Previously, 许多 敌人 effectively ignored 外骨骼机甲 if a 绝地潜兵 on foot was available for them to 目标.
  • 已修复 外骨骼机甲 being able to 火焰 their 武器 while opening the 小地图.
  • The 绝地潜兵 and the 外骨骼机甲 两者 had a bug that made them 有时 take 爆炸伤害 多个 times making things 点赞 机器人 rockets be too 致命, this is now 已修复.
  • 机器人 敌人 constellations that preferred to spawn 更多 of certain Devastators 类型 did not work and are now functioning as they should. This means that 有时 when playing against the 机器人 you will face 更多 Devastators instead of other 敌人 类型.
  • We have 已改进 the 星系 that prevents 绝地喷射舱 steering 关闭 to 大 or 重要 objects.
    • We have solved issues where the 有效 范围 around objects was a lot larger than intended.
    • We have 已降低 the 数字 of objects that prevent 绝地喷射舱 steering.
    • ⚠️Note: This 星系 is intended to prevent softlocks where 玩家 can 掉落 on 重要 interaction points, or 掉落 into unintended places. We will 继续 to 监控 the 陈述 of the 星系 after the 更新 to see if additional tweaks are necessary.
  • 已修复 cases where the 地面 under 一些 assets could be bombed causing them to 漂浮.
  • 防弹盾牌 changes:
    • Collision mesh has been slightly 已增加 in 尺寸 for 更多 forgiveness
    • 已更改 之盾 poses so that 更少 of the 绝地潜兵 is 暴露
    • Addressed bug where parts of the 绝地潜兵 would 成为 脆弱 while using the 之盾 in first person
