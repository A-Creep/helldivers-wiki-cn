the DSS物流中心 was a 设施 located on Tarsh. It was a place where all parts necessary for constructing the DSS were shipped and assembled. The Jet Brigade destroyed it after occupying Tarsh on the 11th of 11月, 2024.

背景故事

Construction

DSS物流中心 was constructed after 绝地潜兵 successfully secured the DSS construction site on Gaellivare during the Secure DSS Construction Site 重要指令.

摧毁

On 11月 11th, 2024, the Jet Brigade attacked Tarsh. In a brutal campaign, the 绝地潜兵 attempted to defend Tarsh, but they failed and the Logistics Hub was destroyed. However, by then the DSS was already finished and the 设施 was 否 longer needed.
