the DSS Logistics Hub was a 设施 located on Tarsh. It was a place where 全部 parts necessary for constructing the DSS were shipped and assembled. The 喷气 Brigade destroyed it after occupying Tarsh on the 11th of 11月, 2024.

背景故事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Construction[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

DSS Logistics Hub was constructed after 绝地潜兵 successfully secured the DSS construction site on Gaellivare during the 安全 DSS Construction Site 重要指令.

摧毁[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

On 11月 11th, 2024, the 喷气 Brigade attacked Tarsh. In a brutal campaign, the 绝地潜兵 attempted to 防御 Tarsh, but they failed and the Logistics Hub was destroyed. However, by then the DSS was already finished and the 设施 was 否 longer needed.
