生成[edit | edit 来源]

捕食者 追猎虫 only spawn on planets where the   捕食者 Strain is presented and replace regular Hunter.

行为[edit | edit 来源]

捕食者 追猎虫 will attempt to cloak and 攻击 绝地潜兵 by flanking them and jumping at them. They are much more 激进 than their 标准 变体.

捕食者 Hunter们可以向空气中释放信息素云，引发虫族入侵。

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

捕食者 Hunter Specific[edit | edit 来源]

  • Unlike regular Hunter, 捕食者 追猎虫 are able to cloak just like Stalkers.
  • Similarly to Bile Spitters, 捕食者 追猎虫 can spew bile at the 绝地潜兵. The 总伤害 done, however, is significantly higher, so dodging the 攻击 is highly advised.
  • AX/TX-13 "Guard Dog" Dog Breath is especially useful against 捕食者 Hunter, as it will slow down their fast, agile, and 激进 moves.

将军[edit | edit 来源]

  • 追猎虫 are relatively weak when compared to 武士们和其他中型终结族。射击头部可以快速解决它们。
  • Shooting the legs of a Hunter will greatly slow them and prevent them from performing a pounce.
  • 追猎虫 are weak if encountered alone, but should be prioritized within swarms over other 中型-sized 终结族, due to their 能力 to quickly approach and pounce 绝地潜兵.
  • Using a 近战攻击 will momentarily stun the Hunter and knock them back. Though 困难, 绝地潜兵 can 近战 追猎虫 mid-pounce to prevent their 攻击.
