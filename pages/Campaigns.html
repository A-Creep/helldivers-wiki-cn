Campaigns (also known as 星系战争 Campaigns) are supplements to the 星系战争 星系 in 绝地潜兵 2 that narratively 链接 主要命令 together. They are organized via names, and revolve around events pertaining to a 阵营's activities. Upon completion, participants are rewarded with a 战略配备, 武器, or 护甲/装饰.

概述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

每个 Campaign contains at 最少 one 重要指令 and has a unique 名称, such as "Census Thunder". 点赞 主要命令, Campaigns 要求 the combined efforts of the majority of the 绝地潜兵 人口 to succeed. 每个 successful Campaign 赋予 at 最少 one 奖励 if conditions are met and are considered successful if a majority of 主要命令 within it are completed.

每个 Campaign so far has lasted 2 weeks, with 每个 having 3 different phases.

Campaign 奖励[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Campaign 奖励 are distributed to 全部 participating 绝地潜兵 who partake in the Campaign, similarly to how 主要命令 distribute Syntax Error 奖牌. Since they are 通常 three 主要命令 tied together, participating in at 最少 one MO is guaranteed to be rewarded with one at the 结束. Currently, Campaign 奖励 can consist of:

Campaign 奖励 will 回报 at a later 日期[1], however currently only the M-103 供给 FRV has been announced as returning via 购买 with 申购点 Slips[2] and it is still not known if other items will be returning in a similar fashion.

控制中心[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

当前 and past campaigns can be viewed at the 控制 Center, which can be scrolled through to see their results. It is located inside the 超级驱逐舰, across from the 军械库.
