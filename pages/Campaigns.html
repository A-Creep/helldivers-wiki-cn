战役 (also known as 银河战争战役) are supplements to the 星系战争 system in 绝地潜兵 2 that narratively link 主要命令 together. They are organized via names, and revolve around events pertaining to a 阵营's activities. Upon completion, participants are rewarded with a 战略配备, Weapon, or 护甲/装饰.

概述

Each Campaign contains at least one 重要指令 and has a unique name, such as "Census Thunder". Like 主要命令, Campaigns 要求 the combined efforts of the majority of the 绝地潜兵 人口 to succeed. Each successful Campaign grants at least one reward if conditions are met and are considered successful if a majority of 主要命令 within it are completed.

Each Campaign so far has lasted 2 weeks, with each having 3 different phases.

Campaign 奖励

Campaign 奖励 are distributed to all participating 绝地潜兵 who partake in the Campaign, similarly to how 主要命令 distribute 奖章. Since they are 通常 three 主要命令 tied together, participating in at least one MO is guaranteed to be rewarded with one at the end. Currently, Campaign 奖励 can consist of:

奖励类型 规格
战略配备

载具：

  • M-103 Supply FRV
武器

主武器：

  • R-4 Hyena
装饰

护甲套装：

  • KDM-500 Outrider

Campaign 奖励 will return at a later date[1], however currently only the M-103 Supply FRV has been 已公布 as returning via 购买 with 申购点 Slips[2] and it is still not known if other items will be returning in a similar fashion.

控制中心

当前 and past campaigns can be viewed at the 控制 Center, which can be scrolled through to see their results. It is located inside the 超级驱逐舰, across from the 军械库.
