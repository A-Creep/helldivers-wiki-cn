生成[edit | edit 来源]

Leviathans spawn beyond the 任务 area, similarly to the Stingray. Up to 5 Leviathans may appear at any time on 不可能 and above.

They will exclusively spawn within the Megacities of planets with the Leviathan Blockade 操作 修正.

Icon of the Leviathan Blockade 操作 修正

行为[edit | edit 来源]

The Leviathan hovers above the battlefield at a variable altitude. It 攻击 any 绝地潜兵, SEAF 战士，或 平民 with four ventral Disintegration Culverins and ten Plasma Bomb 发电机. Said Generators only 掉落 their 载荷 on 敌人 directly below or nearby the Leviathan.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • A Leviathan's Disintegration Culverin 炮塔 can be destroyed by any  重型 AP or higher 支援武器, drastically lowering the Leviathan's lethality.
    • the LAS-99 类星体加农炮 和 EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器 are very 有效 at this, destroying the 炮塔 in a 单发.
  • Given their tough 护甲 and 庞大 生命池, 反坦克 options such as the GR-8 无后坐力炮, FAF-14 Spear，以及 E/AT-12 反坦克炮台 are favorable against Leviathans.
    • Destroying the Fins or Tail of the Leviathan will expose its internals (marked by spewing blue flames), which are  重型 护甲, and can be damaged by 重型护甲 Penetrating 武器库 like the APW-1 反器材步枪 or LAS-98 激光大炮.
  • the Leviathan's Distintegtraion Culverins are 极其 致命, doing 400 伤害 每秒. A single one is capable of killing a 绝地潜兵 within half a second, and 多个 beams often focus on the same 目标. Therefore, it is advised to stay on the move when under 火焰 from it.
  • While 困难 and requiring precise positioning, it is possible to 摧毁 a Leviathan with a single “飞鹰”机枪扫射 or 轨道加特林火力网.
  • Smoke Strategems such as the “飞鹰”烟雾攻击 or 轨道烟雾攻击 can be very useful to block a Leviathan's line of sight, preventing them from 索敌 绝地潜兵.
    • If a beam is already 索敌 a 绝地潜兵, then it will 继续 doing so, even if the line of sight is blocked by smoke.
  • As with other large 敌人 such as the 吐酸泰坦, the falling wreckage of a Leviathan can 伤害 or kill 绝地潜兵 caught underneath it. 绝地潜兵 should be prepared to quickly move out of the way if shooting at one directly overhead.
  • 使用 外骨骼机甲, M-102 Fast 侦察 载具, and 阵地 战略配备 is ill-advised due to how easily they can be destroyed by a Leviathan, very likely killing the 绝地潜兵 in the 流程.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • Leviathans bear resemblance to certain Artiopods, also known as Trilobites (and close relatives), like for 示例 Aglaspis or Pseudosaukianda.
  • the Leviathan's namesake is derived from the Bible, in which it is described as a 火焰-breathing sea monster clad in impenetrable scales, in line with its in-game portrayal of having tremendous aerial 大炮 power and being heavily 有护甲.
  • It is possible, after strenuous effort, to climb onto the back of the Leviathan; the player won't 接收 any 伤害 when standing above it.
  • the Leviathan appears to be a cybernetic organism. When their head weakpoint is destroyed, 有机-looking appendages can be seen jutting out of it, rather than the hull cracking or catching 火焰. Additionally, rather than 机械 sounds, it appears to wince in pain whenever its weakpoints are destroyed, or it is killed. Additionally, when it dies, blue fluid similar to 光能者 blood will burst from the Leviathan.
