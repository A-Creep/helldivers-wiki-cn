机器人
移动工厂
“

The 机器人 have deployed a new 重型单位: the 移动工厂. Previously deployed in very 有限 quantities, likely prototypes, this 庞大 abomination is a walking 机器人制造厂. It is heavily armed and 有护甲, and should be engaged with coordinated firepower and unhesitating 攻击性.

— 超级地球 Dispatch

The 移动工厂 is a 庞大 机器人 载具 that periodically deploys four Devastators. Its armaments include a twin set of Fusion 加特林 Guns and a Fusion Repeater Cannon that is capable of unloading a burst of three 爆炸 projectiles.

生成

工厂 Striders can be first found in the 消除 机器人 移动工厂 任务 starting on 挑战. Starting on 自杀任务 (and on 极难 when the  Cyborg Legion is present), they begin 生成 from 机器人空投 or guarding 重型 机器人 哨站.

The secondary 目标 拦截车队 spawns five 工厂 Striders, patrolling from one end of the map to the other. One of them uniquely carries a 战略配备干扰器 that resembles a miniature 战略配备干扰装置.

On planets invaded by the  Cyborg Legion, they are replaced by Vox Engines starting on 自杀任务, including on convoy secondary 目标. The 目标 icon, however, does not change.

行为

Upon being alerted to a 绝地潜兵's presence, the 移动工厂 will audibly roar, and the 移动工厂's armaments will 火焰 independently at the 绝地潜兵 as long as they are in the 移动工厂's line of sight. As its cannon can rotate 360° and independently from the 移动工厂, it will often be the first to 火焰. It can 火焰 up to three 发 as long as a 绝地潜兵 is in its line of sight and not too close to the 移动工厂. Its Fusion 加特林 Guns have a 有限 rotation, mainly tied to the 移动工厂's head, but 火焰 at a deadly 1200 发/分钟. The 移动工厂 can also knock down and 伤害 绝地潜兵 as it walks.

The 移动工厂, pertaining to its name, will also spawn four Devastators every 50-55 秒, and makes a sound akin to a 机械 door opening when it is doing so.

Upon taking a 致命 blow, it will let out a shriek before violently exploding. As it is dying, its body can crush 绝地潜兵 near it.

战术信息

  • The 移动工厂's 庞大 生命池 allows it to take a significant amount of 伤害, even from 进攻型 战略配备. The Eagle 500kg Bomb, 轨道精准攻击, “飞鹰”机枪扫射, and 轨道武器 380mm HE 弹幕 are examples of 战略配备 that can 摧毁 or soften up the 移动工厂 for  反坦克 I 护甲 penetrating 支援武器.
  • Two 支援武器 that can easily 摧毁 a 移动工厂 无论 where it hits are the EAT-411 Leveller and MS-11 Solo Silo.
  • The 移动工厂's 加特林 guns are deadly at 近距离, especially if 绝地潜兵 are in 正面 of it. 绝地潜兵 can 摧毁 its 加特林 guns using  中型 护甲 penetrating or higher 支援武器 such as the APW-1 反器材步枪, AC-8 机炮, or the LAS-98 激光大炮.
    • 绝地潜兵可以用兴奋剂扛过它的聚变加特林炮，除非被命中头部。
    • Oddly, the 移动工厂's fusion 加特林 guns do not 火焰 at 外骨骼机甲. However, its cannon will still 火焰 at them.
  • The 移动工厂's "eye" is a 致命 zone, requiring  重型 护甲 penetrating 武器库. It is vital for 绝地潜兵 to 干掉 their fusion 加特林 guns first, as the 暴露 part of head是小型 and requires precise aim.
    • 绝地潜兵 can 摧毁 their 头部护甲 with a 单发 from an EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器, GR-8 无后坐力炮, LAS-99 类星体加农炮, or 2 shots from an E/AT-12 反坦克炮台 or MLS-4X 突击兵. The 护甲's 摧毁 exposes the entirety of the head to  重型 护甲-penetrating 支援武器, making it much easier to 摧毁.
  • The 移动工厂's undercarriage is another 弱点. Destroying it only requires  中型 护甲 penetrating 武器库, and it has slightly lower 生命值 than its eye. Note the 构造 has 0 decay, therefore it behaves as hp for a total of 2,400 有效 生命值. However, this requires the 绝地潜兵 to be under or near the 移动工厂, and may leave the 绝地潜兵 open to its newly-deployed Devastators. Similar to its eye, it is also 重要 for 绝地潜兵 to 摧毁 its fusion 加特林 guns first before going for its undercarriage.
    • 绝地潜兵应瞄准单个底盘舱门，因为它们各自有独立的生命池。
    • 使用机枪进行此方法时，建议将射速调到最高。
    • The 突击兵 is able to deal 2,316 伤害 with two missiles, allowing any  中型 or higher 护甲-penetrating 武器 to easily finish the panel off.
  • The 移动工厂's feet are also a 弱点. Note the 构造 has 0 decay, therefore it behaves as hp for a total of 3,500 有效 生命值. It can be destroyed in 3 shots from the E/AT-12 反坦克炮台, 2 shots from the 无后坐力炮, EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器 or LAS-99 类星体加农炮, and only requires one shot from the Bastion's 主体 cannon. The MD-17 反坦克地雷 are also a viable 策略, especially so against 工厂 Striders in a Convoy, as they follow a predictable path, only taking 2-3 mines to bring them down.
  • The 移动工厂's cannon requires  反坦克 I 武器库 to 干掉, such as the FAF-14 Spear and GR-8 无后坐力炮. As the 轨道炮攻击 or Eagle 110mm 火箭巢 automatically 目标 the center of an enemy, it will 有时 end up destroying the cannon first. If the 驱逐舰 is not directly above the 移动工厂, the Railcannon Strike will 通常 successfully strike the 主体 body instead.
  • The 移动工厂's cannon is powerful enough to 摧毁 demo 50 threshold structures. Smart 绝地潜兵 may use this to their 优势 to 摧毁 机器人 Fabricators, Bulk Fabricators, 探测器 Towers, 战略配备 Jammers, and Rogue 研究 Stations.
  • Despite their size, 工厂 Striders will die immediately if the 运输舰 carrying them is shot down by destroying the fuselage before the deployment window.
    • If a SEAF SAM Site is active, it will attempt to engage the 运输舰 carrying it. However, if the 导弹 approaches at an improper angle (like from underneath), it may 摧毁 the 移动工厂 directly instead of the 运输舰 carrying it.
  • E/AT-12反坦克阵地、EAT-17一次性反坦克和LAS-99类星体加农炮可以对暴露的头部两发快速击杀移动工厂，GR-8无后坐力步枪则只需一发。
  • Brave 绝地潜兵 can use the B-100 Portable Hellbomb to 摧毁 工厂 Striders within 射程.

琐事

  • 工厂 Striders were quietly introduced to the game on April 1st, 2024 in addition to the 炮舰 during Phase III of 操作 Swift Disassembly.
  • The 移动工厂 may be inspired by Star Wars's AT-M6 or AT-AT Walkers.
