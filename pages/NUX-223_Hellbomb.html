要求[edit | edit 来源]

the NUX-223 Hellbomb is a 标准-issue 战略配备, available to all 绝地潜兵 regardless of rank. However, 绝地潜兵 指挥 only 提供 this 战略配备 during missions that 要求 the 摧毁 of an enemy 建筑: 指挥碉堡, Fuel Reserves, 炮舰设施, 轨道加农炮, 战略配备干扰器, Monoliths, 尖啸虫巢穴, Spore 喷吐者，以及 叛变研究站.

A Portable version is available as a backpack 支援 战略配备.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • the 战略配备 Code may be easily remembered by splitting it into 3 sections; Down-Up-Left, Down-Up-Right, Down-Up
  • After the Hellbomb has been deployed, it must be manually armed by interacting with its panel and inputting the code shown. Then, a swift 撤退 is recommended due to its huge blast radius (about 25m).
  • The Hellbomb will 引爆 immediately if damaged after being armed. It is advised to place it in a covered position to ensure a safe 撤退 after activation.
    • A 防护罩生成器中继站 or Shield 投掷物 may be used to 保护 the bomb until 最小 safe 距离 is achieved. Be sure to deploy the shield after the bomb is called in, or the 绝地喷射舱 will 摧毁 the shield on 联络.
    • An unarmed Hellbomb will be harmlessly destroyed by enemy 攻击 if left unattended.
  • Most stationary targets requiring the approval of the Hellbomb 战略配备 may be destroyed more efficiently with other 战略配备. The Hellbomb is offered as a plan B in case the 绝地潜兵 are unable to 应对 the 目标 otherwise.
    • However, Fuel Reserves, 炮舰设施, 轨道加农炮，以及 Monoliths can only be destroyed with the inner blast radius of a Hellbomb (Portable Hellbombs or nearby Damaged Hellbombs can 达成 the same), or the inner blast radius 爆炸 from a SEAF 大炮 Mini-Nuke-shell; if one has been found and loaded.
      • In case there are 多个 targets located within short 距离 from each other when 使用 of Hellbomb is authored, the 小地图 offers 绝地潜兵 a helpful 摧毁 radius indicator in a form of an orange (for main 任务 目标) or blue-tinted circle (for Optional 目标): Calling down a Hellbomb into the middle of this circle should be the most ideal spot for fastest clearance of the 目标 zone.
  • Damaged Hellbombs may be randomly found during missions as a Minor Place of Interest. They may also be found without the usual characteristics of the Minor Place of Interest 类型, lacking an inscription, loot and other distinguishing features.
  • The Hellbomb has an 爆炸半径 of 25 meters. Its 伤害 is so 庞大 that there's no observable 伤害 fall off to 绝地潜兵.
    • However, only the first 17-meter inner radius has the 爆破 Force value of 60 必需 for the 摧毁 of the above-mentioned structures.
  • Marking a Hellbomb, called-in or damaged, will play a "Hellbomb" voice line, to alert your teammates of its presence.
