1.000.400 was a major 更新 released for 绝地潜兵 2

补丁 01.000.400

🌍 概述

对于本次补丁，一些主要关注领域是

  • 银河战争中显示补给线和攻击起源。
  • 战略配备、武器、行星和敌人的平衡更新。
  • 各种崩溃修复、稳定性改进和其他更新。
  • 仅邀请大厅创建。
    • The right most option in the Lobby settings. Currently only localized in English. Additional languages coming in the 下一个 补丁

⚖️ Balancing

Goal with 战略配备 平衡 变更 this 补丁:

With these 平衡 变更 we wanted to buff up some of the weaker 战略配备 to make them more viable and add more possibility for variety in the loadouts. We also 已更改 a few to make them more consistent, but the goal was to keep a similar or higher power level. We are looking into the 战略配备 more to see if there are any other 战略配备 that might need some buffs or 变更 to make them more viable. We also want to be better at explaining what our goals are with the 变更, please see the linked 博客文章 for more information.

  • 将投射伤害从200提升至300

A/MG-43 机枪 sentry

🎮 玩法

基本信息

  • 已移除 操作 修正 AA-Defenses: Reducing the 战略配备 slots by 1
    • We want to look over the 操作 modifiers in the future. Right now there are too few of them which become repetitive and the ones we have do not create variety or 推广 different playstyles. For now, we 已移除 this one because it only encourages the 玩家 to bring the best 战略配备 and does not 推广 variety.
  • Disabled the 找回重要人员 defend event 任务 for the time being
    • 这是为了让该任务得到应有的关注和打磨，因为它目前被认为不够有趣，表现也未达到我们的预期。
  • 超级样本
    • 超级样本 now spawn on 难度 6. The reason for this change is that we feel that their existence only on 难度 7+ forced some 玩家 to play the game on a harder 难度 than they wanted to comfortably play on. We do however still want a gating of them but a less punishing one.
  • The SEAF 大炮 战略配备 is 否 longer blocked by 战略配备 jammers or 离子风暴, and is available after the 任务 timer ends and the 驱逐舰 leaves close orbit.
  • 敌人 in 近战 射程 of the gates in the 撤离高价值资产 任务 will now 攻击 it more consistently.
  • 新增了从游戏内菜单和任务加载界面进行聊天的功能。
  • 更新了部分第一人称准星以提高可读性。

Social

  • 现已支持仅邀请大厅

Armors

  • 新增装甲被动：健壮体格（+50%近战伤害，+30%武器人体工学性能）

Planets & Hazards

  • Spike Plant
  • 出现在某些行星上的尖刺植物已重做。
  • 被植物爆炸或尖刺击中时不再造成流血或耐力消耗。
  • 现在会"爆裂"三次，向四周发射尖刺，造成更高的伤害。
  • 烈焰龙卷
    • 火焰龙卷风的行为已更改，它们不再会主动回应玩家的移动，而是会更随机地移动。
    • While a 火焰 龙卷风 storm takes place, enemy vision is 已降低. Player vision is unaffected.
    • 烈焰龙卷 being more random should result in more variance in situations 玩家 find themselves in. Tornados are significantly less likely to pile up and overlap on 撤离 points or 目标, and will 通常 be a bit easier to 应对.
  • 地震
    • 地震的生成已调整为略微更加随机。
    • 地震的震中大小和影响范围已增加。
    • 这些改动应该会导致更多远离玩家的敌人被眩晕的情况，同时减少玩家身边持续发生震颤的频率。
  • 视野
    • A lot of planets have had their fog amounts tweaked to be a bit less harsh and dense, to provide less fatigue from constantly fighting on planets with bad visibility. We still intend there to be foggy planets with worse visibility, but the 平衡 was a bit off.
    • 沙漠星球 such as Erata Prime, Chort Bay, Hellmire, and similar.
    • 高地星球 such as Varylia 5, Matar Bay, Oshaune, and similar.
    • 人造光源的强度已全面重新平衡和降低，以修复光源完全致盲玩家的情况。
  • Unexploded Hellbomb
    • The unexploded malfunctioning hellbombs that can 有时 be found on planets will now 爆炸 immediately if hit with strong explosions or 重型 武器库. They will still have the same delay if hit by 小型 arms 火焰 or weaker 攻击.
  • 植被
    • Vegetation that’s large enough to slow the player now has an extra 功能. When inside the vegetation 绝地潜兵 will be harder to detect, reducing their detection 射程 by 敌人. This 效果 stacks with other detection reducing 效果 such as nighttime, being crouched or prone, or things such as the scout 护甲 被动.
  • 离子风暴
    • 为离子风暴添加了额外VFX。

敌人

  • 腐蚀酸效果
    • The 酸 效果 applied by 追猎虫, bug mines, etc. now allows you to sprint while under the 效果 and slows you by 30% instead of 50%. 持续时间 has been 已增加 from 3 to 4 秒. These 变更 are intended to make it less punishing for 玩家 to be slowed while fighting the 终结族, This allows us to use it in more places without making the 经验值 very punishing, for 示例 the Bile 喷吐者.
  • 装甲敌人平衡性调整
  • We have toned down the amount of heavily 有护甲 敌人 like Bile Titans, Chargers on higher difficulties and instead spawn more hordes of 更小 敌人, the difference should be quite noticeable and the amount should be at least 30% less than before during bug 突破. We have also toned down slightly how many Hulks that spawn for the 机器人. Our intent is to ease up on the demand of 反坦克武器 and by having more of the other 敌人 give a better incentive for the group to bring 战略配备 and 武器 that take care of hordes.

Patrols

  • 巡逻队生成现在恢复到补丁01.000.300之前的工作方式，并进行了一些微调，以便当你远离有敌人存在的重要地点时，关卡不会显得过于空旷。

Stunning

  • 中型 and large sized 敌人 now don't get stunned as easily. This will not affect the 战略配备 that stun, and will mostly just affect how easily the Pummeler can stun larger 敌人.

🐜 终结族

  • Bile 喷吐者 & Nursing 喷吐者
    • 喷涌虫失去腿部后现在会被减速。
    • 喷涌虫呕吐物现在施加腐蚀酸效果。
    • Spewer puke now can only 伤害 绝地潜兵 up to 4 times 每秒 and the 绝地潜兵 cannot take 伤害 多个 times from the same 投射物. This should reduce instances where you are instantly killed by it.
  • 吐酸泰坦
    • 吐酸泰坦的头部对较弱的反坦克武器略微更不耐久。 It's not a large change, and will mostly matter for 武器 like the 磁轨炮.
    • The 吐酸泰坦’s puke can now only 伤害 the 绝地潜兵 4 times 每秒 and the 绝地潜兵 cannot take 伤害 多个 times from the same 投射物. The 伤害 is still very deadly so running through their puke is not recommended. Their puke now also has a bigger 散射.
  • 巨兽级强袭虫
    • The 巨兽级强袭虫 is now joining the battle on higher difficulties, it can now also take more 伤害 than before. This enables us to spawn fewer chargers but still retain the 难度.
  • 强袭虫
    • Will now only show bleedout 效果 from the body if the bleedout state has started. 它仍然可以在未开始流血至死状态的情况下显示来自嘴部的效果。

🤖 机器人

  • Hulk Scorcher
  • The Hulk’s 火焰喷射器 now does less 伤害 and cannot 伤害 绝地潜兵 more than 4 times 每秒. In addition, the 绝地潜兵 cannot be damaged 多个 times by the same flame 投射物. This should reduce instances where you are instantly killed by it.
  • 机器人 Tanks
    • The Tank 护甲 value on the 正面 has been lowered to be the same as the non-通风口 sections on the 后部 of the tank. The intent was initially that it was supposed to be more 有护甲 in the 正面, but the visual 语言 did not show that.
    • 坦克炮塔背面的通风口仍拥有与之前相同的装甲值，但现在将正确检测爆炸方向。
  • 机器人跳跃背包爆炸的伤害降低了50%，不过它仍然会使你着火。

🌍 星系战争

Introducing Supply Lines & Origin of 攻击:

Supply lines were previously not shown on the 星系战争地图 to reduce clutter and 改进 readability. However based on the feedback from our community we have made an implementation showing them on the map. This 解决方案 tries to 维护 the 基本信息 readability while still exposing the system to 玩家 in game.
你现在还能看到攻击来自哪个行星，这可能让社区能够在源头阻止攻击。
我们还更新了分区和行星信息弹出窗口的视觉效果。
