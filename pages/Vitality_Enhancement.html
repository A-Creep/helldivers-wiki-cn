描述

The Vitality Enhancement 强化资源 is a 支援 强化资源 that is designed to 增加 survivability when taking 伤害. When equipped, all 绝地潜兵 in the squad will become more 抵抗 to 伤害 (降低 受到伤害 by 10%), allowing a 绝地潜兵 to have more 有效 生命值 and reduce the occurrence of limb injuries.

采购

The Vitality Enhancement 强化资源 is unlocked on the 4th page of the 绝地潜兵 Mobilize 战争债券 for 20 奖章.

战术信息

  • Vitality Enhancement is a good 强化资源 for any 任务 类型, as 已增加 抗性 allows for a 绝地潜兵 to stay in the fight longer, as well as not needing to use as many Stims to 恢复 from limb injuries (by reducing the chance of injury outright).
  • This 强化资源 works well in more 防御型 missions, where squad是必需 to hunker down in an area, as the 有限 mobility means there will be more incoming 攻击. The 已增加 survivability 增加 the chance of survival, as well as less reliance on Resupplies.
  • When combined with 死亡冲刺, 生命值 drain caused by running is 已降低 from 大约 3.6% 生命值 to ~1%.
  • When using the LAS-17 Double-Edge Sickle, the amount of self-受到伤害 from firing weapon是已减少 by a flat amount. When combined with 易燃易爆 this prevents the weapon from dealing self-伤害 until 51% heat is reached.
    • The 易燃易爆 降低 all 火焰-伤害 by 75%, reducing the 10-火焰-伤害/second (26-50% heat) to 2.5 and 20 伤害/second (51-90% heat) to 5. 活力增益 降低 these damages by 1 and 2 respectively. 重型护甲 (150 AR) 降低 the 伤害 by 2.5 and 5 respectively.
  • When combined with 高级过滤 (AF) and 重型护甲 (HA), the 绝地潜兵 becomes virtually immune to lingering 毒气-伤害.
    • The 高级过滤 alone 降低 the 毒气-伤害/second from 25 to 5 (by 80%). By itself both 重型护甲 (150 AR) and 活力增益 (VB) would reduce the 毒气-伤害 by 6.25 and 2.50-points respectively.
