描述[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

the Vitality Enhancement 强化资源 is a 支援 强化资源 that is designed to 增加 survivability when taking 伤害. When equipped, 全部 绝地潜兵 in the 小队 will 成为 更多 抵抗 to 伤害 (降低 受到伤害 by 10%), allowing a 绝地潜兵 to have 更多 有效 生命值 and 减少 the occurrence of 肢体 injuries.

获取途径[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

the Vitality Enhancement 强化资源 is unlocked on the 4th 页面 of the 绝地潜兵 Mobilize 战争债券 for Syntax Error 奖牌.

战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Vitality Enhancement is a 好 强化资源 for 任何 任务 类型, as 已增加 抗性 allows for a 绝地潜兵 to stay in the 战斗 longer, as well as not needing to use as 许多 Stims to 恢复 from 肢体 injuries (by reducing the 几率 of 受伤 outright).
  • This 强化资源 works well in 更多 防御型 missions, where the 小队 is 必需 to hunker down in an 范围, as the 有限 mobility means there will be 更多 incoming 攻击. The 已增加 survivability 增加 the 几率 of survival, as well as 更少 reliance on Resupplies.
  • When combined with 死亡冲刺, 生命值 drain caused by running is 已降低 from 大约 3.6% 生命值 to ~1%.
  • When using the LAS-17 双重-Edge Sickle, the 数量 of self-受到伤害 from firing the 武器 is 已减少 by a flat 数量. When combined with Inflammable this prevents the 武器 from dealing self-伤害 until 51% 高温 is reached.
    • The inflammable 降低 全部 火焰-伤害 by 75%, reducing the 10-火焰-伤害/second (26-50% 高温) to 2.5 and 20 伤害/second (51-90% 高温) to 5. 活力增益 降低 these damages by 1 and 2 respectively. 重型护甲 (150 AR) 降低 the 伤害 by 2.5 and 5 respectively.
  • When combined with 高级过滤 (AF) and 重型护甲 (HA), the 绝地潜兵 becomes virtually 免疫 to lingering 毒气-伤害.
    • The 高级过滤 alone 降低 the 毒气-伤害/second from 25 to 5 (by 80%). By itself 两者 重型护甲 (150 AR) and 活力增益 (VB) would 减少 the 毒气-伤害 by 6.25 and 2.50-points respectively.
