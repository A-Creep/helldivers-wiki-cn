样本 Extricator
“

大 敌人 now have a 几率 of dropping Samples on death. Capped at 10 空投 每次任务.

— 军械库 描述

样本 Extricator is a 增益 in 绝地潜兵 2. When equipped, 全部 大 敌人 will have a 几率 of dropping 普通样本 upon death. The 数量 varies, but the 数量 dropped 每次任务 is capped at 10.

获取途径[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

the 样本 Extricator 强化资源 is unlocked on the 3rd 页面 of the Borderline 正义 尊享战争债券 for Syntax Error 奖牌.

战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 这个增益是 效果 that's active throughout a 任务, which allows a 绝地潜兵-小队 to 获得 更多 普通样本 than the 最大 生成 数字.
  • This 强化资源 is 最多 有用 on 撤离高价值资产, 歼灭 终结族 虫群 and 歼灭 机器人 Forces, where the map is 小 and/or spawns are at known locations. This makes finding the samples relatively 容易. It is beneficial if the 小队 has 绝地潜兵 who need to 农场 them for 超级驱逐舰 升级.
    • 撤离高价值资产 normally does not contain samples (2 at 最多) so extracting with 10 additional samples can be a big 帮助. The samples can 经常 be found in a 少数 分钟 by checking the 防御 大门 after the 任务 has been completed.
    • 两者 eradication missions for the 终结族 and 机器人 do come with a 少数 samples, but adding 10 can still 双重 the 数量 of samples found, finding them takes a bit 更多 effort but due to the 小 map 尺寸 these can also be found with 亲戚 ease.
  • This 强化资源 does not perform well on 更低 difficulties where 大 敌人 do not spawn at 全部, or in 低 quantities.
  • In terms of the 星系战争, it is beneficial for 样本 MOs, where an 增加 in their 合集 is 重要 in progressing it. An additional 10 普通样本 can 平均值 a three-任务 操作 can potentially 产出 the same 数量 as four* missions. (Depending on the 难度, the extra 数量 varies)
