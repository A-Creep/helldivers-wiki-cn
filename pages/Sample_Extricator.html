样本拯救者
“

Large 敌人 now have a chance of dropping Samples on death. Capped at 10 drops 每次任务.

— 军械库描述

Sample Extricator 是一个 增益 in 绝地潜兵 2. When equipped, all Large 敌人 will have a chance of dropping 普通样本 upon death. The amount varies, but the amount dropped 每次任务 is capped at 10.

采购

the Sample Extricator 强化资源 is unlocked on the 3rd page of the Borderline 正义 尊享战争债券 用于 65 奖章.

战术信息

  • 这个增益是 效果 that's active throughout a 任务, which allows a 绝地潜兵-squad to obtain more 普通样本 than the 最大 生成 number.
  • This 强化资源 is most useful on 撤离高价值资产, 歼灭 终结族 Swarm 和 歼灭 机器人 Forces, where map是小型 and/or spawns are at known locations. This makes finding the samples relatively 容易. It is beneficial if the squad has 绝地潜兵 who need to farm them for 超级驱逐舰升级.
    • 撤离高价值资产 normally does not contain samples (2 at most) so extracting with 10 additional samples can be a big help. The samples can often be found in a few 分钟 by checking the 防御大门 after the 任务 has been completed.
    • Both eradication missions for the 终结族 and 机器人 do come with a few samples, but adding 10 can still double the amount of samples found, finding them takes a bit more effort but due to the 小型 map size these can also be found with 亲戚 ease.
  • This 强化资源 does not perform well on lower difficulties where Large 敌人 do not spawn at all, or in low quantities.
  • In terms of the 星系战争, it is beneficial for 样本 MOs, where an 增加 in their 合集 is 重要 in progressing it. An additional 10 普通样本 can mean a three-任务 操作 can potentially yield the same amount as four* missions. (Depending on the 难度, the extra amount varies)
