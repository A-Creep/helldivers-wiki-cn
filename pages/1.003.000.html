Spread: Spread refers to how much a 投射物 veers from the point you're aiming at when you 火焰. This rebalance primarily targets SMGs and sidearms, which previously had noticeably higher spread values. We've reviewed all primary, 副武器, and 支援武器 with the intent to significantly reduce spread overall, as it didn’t make much sense for these specific 武器 to have such high inaccuracy.

Sidearms will naturally have more sway than primaries due to the lack of 支援 features like stocks. This 平衡 补丁 specifically focuses on adjusting sway for primaries and sidearms.
