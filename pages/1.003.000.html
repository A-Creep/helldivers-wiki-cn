Balancing[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

武器 balances:

  • 散射
  • Drag
  • 摆动
  • 近战 武器 耐力 费用
  • Shrapnel 生成
  • 火焰伤害

Spread: Spread refers to how 许多 a 投射物 veers from the point you're aiming at when you 火焰. This rebalance primarily targets SMGs and sidearms, which previously had noticeably 更高 spread values. We've reviewed 全部 primary, 副武器, and 支援武器 with the intent to significantly 减少 spread overall, as it didn’t make 许多 sense for these specific 武器 to have such 高 inaccuracy.

Drag: Drag determines how quickly a 投射物 loses 速度 over 距离, affecting its 伤害. We've 已增加 drag for 手枪-caliber ammunition (used in SMGs and 一些 sidearms) to 更好 represent its shorter, wider 投射物 compared to 步枪 发. As a 结果, these 武器 will be slightly 更少 有效 at longer ranges.

Sway: Sway refers to how 许多 the 武器's 目标 shifts, influenced by factors 点赞 movement and stance. It represents the 武器's inertia and 稳定性—so 武器 with 更低 人体工学 will 经验值 更少 sway, while those with 更高 人体工学 will have 更多. Sidearms will naturally have 更多 sway than primaries due to the lack of 支援 features 点赞 stocks. This 平衡 补丁 specifically focuses on adjusting sway for primaries and sidearms.

耐力 费用: 全部 近战 武器 now 消耗 更少 耐力 when attacking, reducing the 费用 from 0.1 to 0.05. This 变化 makes 近战 combat 更少 punishing and allows for easier repositioning between strikes. The affected 武器 include the CQC-30 Stun Baton, CQC-19 Stun Lance, CQC-5 Combat Hatchet, and the Entrenchment Tool.

Shrapnel 生成: With this 更新, shrapnel will now 总是显示 spawn in a full 360-degree spread, regardless of where the 爆炸 occurs. This means 武器 点赞 the R-36 Eruptor will now 更多 reliably hit the intended 目标 with their shrapnel. In addition, we’ve adjusted shrapnel performance against 护甲. Shrapnel now has 已降低 effectiveness at poor 冲击 angles, to reflect its lack of 设计 for 护甲穿透 in such situations.

These shrapnel 平衡 changes will 冲击 the following 武器:

  • R-36 Eruptor
  • G-6 Frag 手榴弹
  • AC-8 机炮
