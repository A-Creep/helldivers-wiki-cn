1.003.000

1.003.000 was a major 更新 released for 绝地潜兵 2.

概述

  • 新的光能者阵营敌人
  • 新武器自定义与进度。
  • 崩溃修复
  • 武器修复
  • 其他修复

武器自定义

装备起来，绝地潜兵——你们的军火库刚刚变得个性化了！ With the new Weapon Customization system, you can now tailor your 收藏 武器 to match your combat style and preferences once you’ve progressed and unlocked different 配件. Whether it's tweaking sights for precision, changing color patterns, adjusting magazines for 弹药 容量, muzzles to 优化 weapon performance characteristics or adjusting underbarrels for the 操控性 you want, you're in 指挥 of how your 武器库 performs on the battlefield. 在菜单中调整，自定义你的特定武器配置，并在任务武装配置阶段切换它们。 是时候用真正属于你的武器为超级地球而战了！

We are aware of a bug that prevents 玩家 from canceling customization with a controller. We have a 修复 identified and prepared for our 下一个 hotfix. In the meantime, you can save the customization and then go in and do it again.

新光能者敌人

Stingray: 从空中为光能者提供支援的喷气式战斗机，锁定绝地潜兵并排列成毁灭性的扫射队列。

Crescent 监视者: 有能力对掩体中的绝地潜兵倾泻弹幕。

Fleshmob: A failed 光能者 实验 made up of 无票者 parts turned into a brute battlefield force that the 绝地潜兵 must work 困难 to 摧毁.

平衡性调整

武器平衡：

  • 散射
  • Drag
  • 摆动
  • 近战武器耐力消耗
  • 破片生成
  • 火焰伤害

散布： 散射 refers to how much a 投射物 veers from the point you're aiming at when you 火焰. This rebalance primarily targets SMGs and sidearms, which previously had noticeably higher 散射 values. We've reviewed all primary, 副武器, and 支援武器 with the intent to significantly reduce 散射 overall, as it didn’t make much sense for these specific 武器 to have such high inaccuracy.

Drag: Drag determines how quickly a 投射物 loses 速度 over 距离, affecting its 伤害. We've 已增加 drag for pistol-caliber ammunition (used in SMGs and some sidearms) to better represent its shorter, wider 投射物 compared to 步枪 发. As a result, these 武器 will be slightly less 有效 at longer ranges.

晃动: 晃动 refers to how much the weapon's aim shifts, influenced by factors like movement and stance. It represents the weapon's inertia and 稳定性—so 武器 with lower 人体工学 will 经验值 less 晃动, while those with higher 人体工学 will have more. Sidearms will naturally have more 晃动 than primaries due to the lack of 支援 features like stocks. This 平衡 补丁 specifically focuses on adjusting 晃动 for primaries and sidearms.

耐力消耗： All 近战 武器 now 消耗 less 耐力 when attacking, reducing the 费用 from 0.1 to 0.05. This change makes 近战 combat less punishing and allows for easier repositioning between strikes. The affected 武器 include the CQC-30 Stun Baton, CQC-19 Stun Lance, CQC-5 Combat Hatchet, and the Entrenchment Tool.

破片生成： With this 更新, shrapnel will now always spawn in a full 360-degree 散射, 无论 where the 爆炸 occurs. This means 武器 like the R-36 Eruptor will now more reliably hit the intended 目标 with their shrapnel. In addition, we’ve adjusted shrapnel performance against 护甲. Shrapnel now has 已降低 effectiveness at poor impact angles, to reflect its lack of design for 护甲穿透 in such situations.

这些破片平衡性调整将影响以下武器：

  • R-36 Eruptor
  • G-6 Frag 手榴弹
  • AC-8 机炮

平衡性调整

榴霰弹

破片弹丸的护甲穿透从3-3-3-0降低至3-3-2-0。 这些数字中的每一个都代表一个角度阈值，这意味着在弹片投射物能够穿透敌人的最后一个范围内，我们将AP值从3降低到了2。

Frag 手榴弹

  • 破片生成数量从30增加到35

机炮 Flak

  • 破片生成数量从25增加到30

火焰与火焰喷射器

This 更新 aims to better 平衡 火焰伤害 between 正面 hits (like those from flamethrowers) and burn 效果 over time, while also introducing scaling of 火焰伤害 depending on the size of the enemy. Previously, burn 伤害 didn’t scale well against larger 敌人, making putting them on 火焰 feel less 有效 in those encounters.

Now, the larger the enemy, the more 伤害 they take while 燃烧. However, bigger 敌人 will also be slightly more 抵抗 to ignition. Additionally, 正面 火焰伤害 now scales with enemy size—so large 敌人 like Chargers will still take roughly the same 伤害 as before, while 更小 敌人 will take slightly less.

We have also 已增加 the 弹匣容量 of the FLAM-66 Torcher and the FLAM-40 火焰喷射器 because putting stuff on 火焰 is fun!

伤害

  • 燃烧伤害现在随敌人尺寸缩放，对更大型的敌人会随时间造成更多伤害。
  • 火焰 正面伤害 also scales with enemy size. The base 伤害 of 正面 火焰 hits has been lowered as compensation. This means that large 敌人 will take roughly the same amount of 伤害 as before, while 更小 敌人 will take slightly less

点燃时刻

  • 大型敌人现在需要更长时间才能点燃
  • 机器人敌人比有机敌人更难被点燃。
  • 燃烧弹和激光现在点燃敌人的效果降低，但一旦点燃，得益于新的缩放系统，产生的燃烧伤害更具影响力。

绝地潜兵

  • 绝地潜兵现在对被点燃的抵抗力略微提高。
  • 绝地潜兵受到的燃烧伤害保持不变。

  • 爆炸 内圈半径从4降低至2

这些改动的意图：

  • We want to 维护 the Ultimatum's overall power level while rebalancing how that power is delivered—shifting more of the 伤害 from the 投射物 itself to the 爆炸. Previously, the Ultimatum behaved more like a 庞大 kinetic 投射物 (similar to the 380mm shell) because of its high 投射物伤害 and relatively weaker 爆炸. This 更新 reinforces its intended identity as a powerful 爆炸 weapon, emphasizing high 爆炸伤害 over impact force
  • 大多数重型敌人现在会被近距离命中击杀而非直接命中，移动工厂除外。
  • Additionally, due to its 已降低 爆破 力量, the Ultimatum will 否 longer 摧毁 战略配备干扰装置 目标 or landed 光能者 Warp Ships through their 护盾. However, the dropships now have 生命值, allowing the Ultimatum to 摧毁 them once the shield is down
