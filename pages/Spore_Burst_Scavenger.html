孢裂食腐虫

The Spore Burst 食腐虫 小型 终结族 that is part of the   Spore Burst Strain subfaction, and is a 变体 of the 食腐虫.

生成

The Spore Burst 食腐虫 only spawns on planets where the Spore Burst Strain is presented and replaces the 食腐虫.

行为

The Spore Burst 食腐虫 is behaves identically to normal 食腐虫. However, upon death, it will 爆炸, dealing 伤害 to any 绝地潜兵 who is unfortunate enough to be near it when that happens.

战术信息

孢裂变种专属

  • 死亡时，孢裂终结族会释放一团孢子，伤害附近的绝地潜兵、扰乱视线，并为附近孢裂终结族提供增益。
    • When entering a cloud of spores (created by another Spore Burst 终结族’s death or through the Spore Burst 吐酸泰坦’s bile spew), Spore Burst 终结族 will gain a significant 增加 in 速度, allowing them to close the gap on 绝地潜兵.
  • 毒气 and 火焰武器 such as the AX/TX-13 Dog Breath, FLAM-40 火焰喷射器, and B/FLAM-80 Cremator can slow and deter Spore Burst 终结族 from a 绝地潜兵's position, allowing them to more easily avoid 攻击 and spore explosions.
    • Spore Burst 终结族 do not release their spores if they die while 燃烧. This makes continuous laser 武器 (such as the LAS-5 Scythe) and 火焰武器 useful to avoid enraging a horde of Spore Burst 终结族.
  • Using 近战 武器 against Spore Burst 终结族 is not recommended, as repeated spore explosions will 踉跄 and eventually kill an ignorant 绝地潜兵.
  • Despite its 被动 效果, 护甲 with the 应变自如 or 沙漠突击者 passives are not very 克制 Spore Burst 终结族, as they provide 有限 功能型 against the already 低伤害 spore explosions.

将军

  • 由于生命值低，任何武器都可以轻松消灭食腐虫。
  • 遇到巡逻队或终结族群体时，建议在食腐虫引发虫族入侵之前迅速杀死它们。
    • The AX/LAS-5 "Guard Dog" Rover and 爆炸 武器库 can be 克制 swarms of Scavengers or other 小型 终结族.
