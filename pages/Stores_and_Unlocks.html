货币

Main 条款: 货币

Collected currencies are fully shared with all team members, regardless of the collector. To secure Samples, they must be successfully 已提取, while other 类型 are immediately acquired upon pickup.

经验值

经验值 is earned after missions and contributes to your level progression, unlocking higher Ranks, Titles, and most 战略配备. 主武器 are also leveled up with 经验值, unlocking new attachments 和 weapon skins.

申购单

申购点 Slips are earned after missions and can also be collected at certain 次要兴趣点. They are used to 获取 most 战略配备, as part of the 费用 of tier 4 and 5 舰船模块, and they can be donated to activate Democracy Space 车站's 轨道武器 Blockade. Additionally, 主武器 Attachments 和 Patterns are purchased with 申购点 Slips once the weapon has been sufficiently leveled.

Medals

Medals are spent within 战争债券 and can be collected at certain Minor POIs. They are also awarded for completing missions, as well as 少校 和 个人命令.

超级货币

超级货币 are purchased with real-world money or collected at certain minor POIs. They are used to buy Premium and 传奇 战争债券 or 超级商店 items.

样本

Samples are scattered across the battlefield and must be extracted to be acquired. They can be used to 升级 舰船模块 or activate Democracy Space 车站's Tactical Actions.

军械库

Main 条款: 军械库

In the 军械库, Sergeants and higher can unlock and 购买 主武器 Attachments 和 Patterns.

采购中心

Main 条款: 采购中心

The 获取 Center is accessible aboard a 超级驱逐舰 and features three tabs: 战争债券, 超级商店, and 超级货币.

战争债券

Main 条款: 战争债券

战争债券 are reward tracks through which majority of in-game 武器 和 Cosmetics are unlocked using Medals; the 标准战争债券 is free, while Premium and Legenadry ones 费用 1,000 和 1,500 超级货币 respectively.

超级商店

Main 条款: 超级商店

The 超级商店 is a rotating item shop in which items can be bought using 超级货币.

超级货币

"超级货币" is the third tab of the 获取 Center. 超级货币 can be purchased with real money here.

Main 条款: 舰船模块
