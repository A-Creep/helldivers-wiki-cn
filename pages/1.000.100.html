📍 少校 Updates
行星 Hazards 已激活

  • 许多 planets now have additional environmental challenges that will appear at random while you are deployed, from 火焰 tornadoes to 流星雨 and 许多 更多.

⚖️ Balancing
歼灭 Missions

  • 歼灭 missions now 要求 更多 kills and 敌人 spawn 更多 经常. The 时间 to 完成 the 任务 was previously shorter than intended and should now take 两次 as 长 to 完成.

主要, 次要, & 支援武器s

  • SG-225 Breaker - 已减少 弹匣容量 from 16 to 13, 已增加 后坐力 from 30 to 55
  • SG-8 Punisher - 已增加 全射 弹药 容量 from 40 to 60, 已增加 硬直力度, 已增加 伤害 from 40 to 45 per 子弹
  • SG-225SP Breaker Spray & Pray - 已增加 护甲穿透, 已增加 射速 from 300 to 330, 已增加 pellets from 12 to 16 每发, 已减少 mag 尺寸 from to 32 to 26
  • RS-422 磁轨炮 - 已减少 护甲穿透 in 安全 模式, 已减少 伤害 against 耐久 敌人 parts
  • FLAM-40 火焰喷射器 - 已增加 伤害 每秒 by 50%
  • LAS-98 激光大炮 - 已增加 伤害 against 耐久 敌人 parts, 已增加 护甲穿透, 已改进 人体工学

战略配备

  • 防护罩生成包 - 已增加 延迟 before 充能中
  • 轨道武器 120MM HE 弹幕 - 已增加 持续时间 of the bombardment, 已减少 spread
  • 轨道武器 380MM HE 弹幕 - 已增加 持续时间 of the bombardment, 已减少 spread

🔧 修复

  • 已修复 护甲值 values not reducing 伤害 as intended.
  • 已修复 certain 虫穴 (including 追踪虫 Nests) that were unnecessarily 困难 to 摧毁.
  • 已修复 anti-aliasing 切换 not working on PS5.
  • Balanced lighting across 所有星球 to 解决 cases where the 游戏 was too dark.
  • 已改进 flashlight efficacy.
  • 已增加 visibility during “sand 雨” 天气 on Erata Prime.
  • Updated tutorial materials and lighting.
  • 已改进 cases where 一些 materials could look blurry if "Lighting" 图形 setting was 套装 to "低".
  • 已修复 timing issues that could occur in the “撤离 E-710” primary 目标.
  • 已更改 button interaction 行为 for buttons in 碉堡 POIs. 绝地潜兵 will now let go of the button after holding it for a 少数 秒.
  • 已修复 一些 cases of 大 assets floating if the 地面 beneath them was blown up.
  • 绝地潜兵 standing 下一个 to ICBMs during launch will get properly toasty with a 几率 of not-so-spontaneous 燃烧.
  • 已修复 unthrowable snowballs after ragdolling.
  • 已修复 being able to use grenades after drowning.
  • 摄像头 否 longer 已锁定 the 玩家's own 尸体 and blocking spectator 模式.
  • 绝地潜兵 now take 伤害 from 火焰, 毒气 etc. generated by other 玩家.
  • 护甲 否 longer stretches when dismembered.
