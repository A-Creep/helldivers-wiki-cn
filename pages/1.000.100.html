📍 Major Updates
行星 Hazards 已激活

  • 许多 planets now have additional environmental challenges that will appear at random while you are deployed, from 火焰 tornadoes to 流星雨 and many more.

⚖️ Balancing
歼灭 Missions

  • 歼灭 missions now 要求 more kills and 敌人 spawn more often. The time to 完成 the 任务 was previously shorter than intended and should now take twice as long to 完成.

主要, 次要, & 支援武器s

  • SG-225 Breaker - 已减少 弹匣容量 from 16 to 13, 已增加 后坐力 from 30 to 55

  • RS-422 磁轨炮 - 已减少 护甲穿透 in Safe Mode, 已减少 伤害 against 耐久 enemy parts
  • FLAM-40 火焰喷射器 - 已增加 伤害 每秒 by 50%
  • LAS-98 激光大炮 - 已增加 伤害 against 耐久 enemy parts, 已增加 护甲穿透, 已改进 人体工学

战略配备

  • 防护罩生成包 - 已增加 delay before 充能中
  • 轨道武器 120MM HE 弹幕 - 已增加 持续时间 of the bombardment, 已减少 spread
  • 轨道武器 380MM HE 弹幕 - 已增加 持续时间 of the bombardment, 已减少 spread
