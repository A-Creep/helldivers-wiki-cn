Concealed Insertion 是一个 增益 in 绝地潜兵 2. When equipped, 绝地喷射舱 emits smoke on impact.

描述

Concealed Insertion is a 强化资源 that causes all 绝地喷射舱 (manned or unmanned) to deploy a short burst of smoke on impact, obscuring the area around the 掉落 site and concealing 绝地潜兵 and 装备.

采购

the Concealed Insertion 强化资源 is unlocked on the third page of the Redacted 团 尊享战争债券 用于 40 奖章.

战术信息

When a player is in smoke, 敌人 can only see them if they are less than 10m away, the 最小 view 距离. The smoke from Concealed Insertion 施加 to all Hellpods. It is useful if reinforcing closeby to 敌人, as the player has time to escape before being spotted. The smoke helps to 保护 the 绝地潜兵 while using certain 任务 战略配备, such as when activating 地狱火炸弹, grabbing 重新补给, and saluting the 超级地球 Flag。 撤离信标, being called down immediately after the 主要目标 is completed, is also temporarily obscured by smoke. Nearby 敌人 are alerted to any 战略配备, so concealment when grabbing 支援武器 is also useful.
