战术信息

  • 破裂 Chargers can be dispatched with the same 对付强袭虫的战术.
  • 破裂 Chargers have 更强 护甲 values on their head and 前腿 than any other 强袭虫 变体;  Tank I instead of  重型.
  • 破裂 Chargers can burrow 地下 to approach a 绝地潜兵, 通常 surfacing within 3m of their 目标 before attacking.
  • 钻地中 disturbances caused by 破裂 Chargers are larger than other 破裂 strain units, making them 容易 to 识别 while 地下.
  • 绝地潜兵 can trick a 破裂 强袭虫 into surfacing at an inopportune angle of 攻击 by quickly sprinting toward/past its burrowed form when close enough.
  • 钻地中 units can be forced to 浮出地面 by any 爆炸 效果. This includes 主武器 with the '爆炸' trait. such as the CB-9 Exploding Crossbow, PLAS-1 Scorcher，或 PLAS-101 Purifier, 副武器 such as the GP-31 榴弹手枪 or PLAS-15 Loyalist，以及 最多 (but not all) 手榴弹 类型.
  • the 破裂 强袭虫 can be damaged while 钻地中, but only by grenades, 毒气, and 有时 火焰.
