战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • 破裂 Chargers can be dispatched with the same 对付强袭虫的战术.
  • 破裂 Chargers have 更强 护甲 values on their 头部 and 前腿 than 任何 other 强袭虫 变体;  坦克 I instead of  重型.
  • 破裂 Chargers can 钻地 地下 to approach a 绝地潜兵, 通常 surfacing within 3m of their 目标 before attacking.
  • 钻地中 disturbances caused by 破裂 Chargers are larger than other 破裂 strain units, making them 容易 to 识别 while 地下.
  • 绝地潜兵 can trick a 破裂 强袭虫 into surfacing at an inopportune angle of 攻击 by quickly sprinting toward/past its burrowed 形成 when 关闭 enough.
  • 钻地中 units can be forced to 浮出地面 by 任何 爆炸 效果. This includes primary 武器 with the '爆炸' 特质. such as the CB-9 Exploding Crossbow, PLAS-1 Scorcher，或 PLAS-101 Purifier, secondary 武器 such as the GP-31 榴弹手枪 or PLAS-15 Loyalist, and 最多 (but not 全部) 手榴弹 类型.
  • the 破裂 强袭虫 can be damaged while 钻地中, but only by grenades, 毒气, and 有时 火焰.
