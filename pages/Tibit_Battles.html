Summary The Battle was won on Apr. 7th, unfortunetly because of failed Ubanea Gambit it was too late for the Phase II to succeed, but the late part of the battle was a part of Phase III, which succeeded.


背景

Ubanea Gambit has failed, Phase II of 操作 Swift Disassembly was lost. Fortunetly, 绝地潜兵 quickly managed to recapture Draupnir during the Last Battle of Draupnir and Ubanea 在以下期间 Ubanea 第二次战役.

When Ubanea was liberated, Helldvivers received a 重要指令 that tasked them with liberating Malevelon Creek, because of this Tibit stayed under 机器人 控制 for longer.

The Phase III of 操作 Swift Disassembly also didn't order 绝地潜兵 to liberate Tibit.

Finally, The Phase IV of 操作 Swift Disassembly ordered 绝地潜兵 with liberating Maia, Durgen (Siege of Durgen - the largest battle during the 第二次银河战争 to this date) and Tibit.

战略与重要性

Tibit was the largest 制作人 of 机器人 Combatants. Capturing the 行星 would really harm the 生产 of 机器人.

战斗

The battle lasted for around a week. During the Phase IV of 操作 Swift Disassembly, 绝地潜兵 focused on liberating Maia, a 行星 located in the north (upper 章节) of the Severin 分区.

After completely obliterating 机器人 forces on Maia, 绝地潜兵 focus on TIbit 下一个. 解放 of Tibit was a matter of hours. 绝地潜兵 quickly captured Tibit and all the fabricators on this 行星 were deconstructed.

Around 44 million 绝地潜兵 died during this battle.
