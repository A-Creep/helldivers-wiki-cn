Background[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Ubanea Gambit has failed, Phase II of 操作 Swift Disassembly was lost. Fortunetly, 绝地潜兵 quickly managed to recapture Draupnir during the Last 战斗 of Draupnir and Ubanea during the Second 战斗 of Ubanea.

When Ubanea was liberated, Helldvivers received a 重要指令 that tasked them with liberating Malevelon Creek, because of this Tibit stayed under 机器人 控制 for longer.

The Phase III of 操作 Swift Disassembly also didn't 命令 绝地潜兵 to liberate Tibit.

Finally, The Phase IV of 操作 Swift Disassembly ordered 绝地潜兵 with liberating Maia, Durgen (围城 of Durgen - the largest 战斗 during the 第二次银河战争 to this 日期) and Tibit.

策略 and Importance[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Tibit was the largest 制作人 of 机器人 Combatants. Capturing the 行星 would 确实 伤害 the 生产 of 机器人.

战斗[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

The 战斗 lasted for around a week. During the Phase IV of 操作 Swift Disassembly, 绝地潜兵 focused on liberating Maia, a 行星 located in the north (upper 章节) of the Severin 分区.

After completely obliterating 机器人 forces on Maia, 绝地潜兵 focus on TIbit 下一个. 解放 of Tibit was a matter of hours. 绝地潜兵 quickly captured Tibit and 全部 the fabricators on this 行星 were deconstructed.

Around 44 million 绝地潜兵 died during this 战斗.
