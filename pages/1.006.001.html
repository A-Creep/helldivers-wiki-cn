⚖️ 平衡性调整

基本信息 变更 概述

  • 近战 武器 and 护甲 被动 with 已增加 近战伤害 re-平衡

Intent is to make the 护甲 被动 that 增加 近战伤害 less mandatory but still be useful to make 近战 武器 more 有效.

  • We 已增加 all the 近战 武器 伤害 and 已减少 the modifiers on the 护甲 passives that 增加 近战伤害.
  • 总伤害 with the 近战 护甲 被动 will be higher or the same as before.
  • Base 近战攻击 also got a 50% 伤害 增加.

冲锋枪与手枪射击晃动降低

  • The intent is that the 已增加 drag of SMG and Pistol which make it a close combat weapon and less 有效 at 射程 is the 主体 characteristic of these 武器.
  • We 已减少 the 晃动修正 to be the same as most other 武器

统一武器功能方向输入

  • The intent is to make the "weapon 功能 modes" that are similar to be on the same "weapon 功能 方向 input".
  • This is to make it easier to remember which 方向 similar 类型 of weapon functions are and also make it so you need to use less "quick weapon functions" inputs.
  • Weapon 功能 modes that affect Programmable 弹药, 弹药 类型, 齐射 modes, Safe modes, Guided modes will now be in the left 方向.
  • We have not moved the 下挂 weapon switching option to the left yet, but we are looking into moving it to the left 方向 so all 弹药 altering switching is in the left 方向 even if it is an 下挂 selection that 通常 is in the down 方向.

难度9及以上

  • Made 变更 across all 阵营 to 增加 the intensity on high difficulties, the biggest change should be noticed on the 机器人 正面 while 终结族 and the 光能者 are less affected.

🔧 修复

卡顿检测 As part of our ongoing war against bugs and instability we have introduced a “hang detection” 特性 in the PC version. If the game hangs (freezes) for more than 20 秒 then this new 特性 will crash the game instead of letting it stay hung. This generates a file called a “crash dump” which contains 重要 debugging information for our programmers to diagnose and 修复 the root cause of the hang.

If you 经验值 a hang, please wait at least 40 秒 before restarting your PC to allow the hang 探测器 to do its job.

If you 经验值 a 20+ second hang followed by a crash:

If the crash reporting window shows up then please submit the report as usual. If the crash reporting window doesn’t show up (it can fail), we humbly request that you share the crash dump file with our player 支援 team [(https://arrowhead.zendesk.com https://arrowhead.zendesk.com/]). They will be able to assist you in locating and sharing the crash dump file.

With your help, we can 消除 these issues once and for all!
