⚖️ Balancing[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

基本信息 changes 概述

  • 近战 武器 and 护甲 被动 with 已增加 近战伤害 re-平衡

Intent is to make the 护甲 被动 that 增加 近战伤害 更少 mandatory but still be 有用 to make 近战 武器 更多 有效.

  • We 已增加 全部 the 近战 武器 伤害 and 已减少 the modifiers on the 护甲 passives that 增加 近战伤害.
  • 总伤害 with the 近战 护甲 被动 will be 更高 or the same as before.
  • 基地 近战攻击 also got a 50% 伤害 增加.

冲锋枪 and 手枪 发 更少 Sway

  • The intent is that the 已增加 drag of 冲锋枪 and 手枪 which make it a 关闭 combat 武器 and 更少 有效 at 射程 is the main characteristic of these 武器.
  • We 已减少 the 晃动修正 to be the same as 最多 other 武器

Unify 武器 功能 directions inputs

  • The intent is to make the "武器 功能 modes" that are similar to be on the same "武器 功能 方向 输入".
  • This is to make it easier to remember which 方向 similar 类型 of 武器 functions are and also make it so you need to use 更少 "quick 武器 functions" inputs.
  • 武器 功能 modes that affect Programmable 弹药, 弹药 类型, 齐射 modes, 安全 modes, Guided modes will now be in the left 方向.
  • We have not moved the underbarrel 武器 switching option to the left yet, but we are looking into moving it to the left 方向 so 全部 弹药 altering switching is in the left 方向 even if it is an underbarrel selection that 通常 is in the down 方向.

难度 on 难度 9+

  • Made changes across 全部 阵营 to 增加 the intensity on 高 difficulties, the biggest 变化 should be noticed on the 机器人 正面 while 终结族 and the 光能者 are 更少 affected.

🔧 修复[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

Hang Detection As 部分 of our ongoing war against bugs and instability we have introduced a “hang detection” 特性 in the PC version. If the 游戏 hangs (freezes) for 更多 than 20 秒 then this new 特性 will 崩溃 the 游戏 instead of letting it stay hung. This generates a 文件 called a “崩溃 dump” which contains 重要 debugging information for our programmers to diagnose and 修复 the root 原因 of the hang.

If you 经验值 a hang, please wait at 最少 40 秒 before restarting your PC to 允许 the hang 探测器 to do its job.

If you 经验值 a 20+ second hang followed by a 崩溃:

If the 崩溃 reporting window shows up then please submit the 报道 as usual. If the 崩溃 reporting window doesn’t 显示 up (it can fail), we humbly request that you 分享 the 崩溃 dump 文件 with our 玩家 支援 团队 [(https://arrowhead.zendesk.com https://arrowhead.zendesk.com/]). They will be able to 协助 you in locating and sharing the 崩溃 dump 文件.

With your 帮助, we can 消除 these issues 一次 and for 全部!
