生成[edit | edit 来源]

Radicals can be found on all difficulties on planets where the Cyborg Legion is present. Radicals can spawn from 机器人空投, within patrols, and guarding 机器人 哨站 and 目标.

行为[edit | edit 来源]

Compared to 机器人, Radicals do not hesitate when detecting a 绝地潜兵, and will immediately 攻击 them. Before attacking, Radicals will yell and will immediately throw a smoke 手榴弹 between them and a 绝地潜兵, sprinting towards them while attempting to be concealed; their sprint 速度 can keep up with a sprinting 绝地潜兵, even in 轻型护甲. Radicals can throw another smoke 手榴弹 about every 30 秒 to 隐藏 and 继续 their pursuit. 偶尔 during combat, Radicals can perform a backstep.

At 近距离 (15m), Radicals will 火焰 their Scattergun while approaching or chasing after a 绝地潜兵. Radicals will often close the 距离 to perform techno-martial arts, which can be a frontal kick with either leg, a flying right knee strike, a roundhouse kick with their left leg, and a swing or backhand strike using either of their upper limbs. These 攻击 will deal a 中等 amount of 伤害 and will knock a 绝地潜兵 off their feet. If a 绝地潜兵 is on the ground, Radicals can stomp on them, or use their Scattergun to 处决 them.

Radicals have been seen destroying 防护罩生成中继器 strategems with their kicking 攻击, even while a 绝地潜兵 is nearby enough to 火焰 at.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • The Radical's head is 易受 high-伤害 武器 such as the APW-1 反器材步枪, R-63 Diligence，以及 JAR-5 Dominator, killing a Radical in one shot.
  • The Radical's main body has a large amount of 生命值, but 多个 parts can be hit by explosions.
  • The Radical tends to hyperfocus on closing the gap between it and the 绝地潜兵 it chose to pursue. 绝地潜兵 can dive out of the way of a Radical attempting to use its techno-martial arts, as Radicals will be 脆弱 while finishing their 近战 攻击.
    • Radicals cannot ragdoll 绝地潜兵 using the Rock Solid 护甲被动技能。
  • The smoke 手榴弹 thrown by the Radical can serve as a valuable 警告 that one is approaching. Note the 方向 from which an unexpected smoke 手榴弹 was thrown and prepare for the arrival of the Radical that threw it.
  • Keep in mind that the Radical’s smoke 手榴弹 also hinders the view of other 机器人, and functions very similarly to the 绝地潜兵 very own G-3 Smoke.
  • Radicals may also use their smoke grenades against sentry 战略配备, blinding them and allowing the Radicals to close the 距离 and 摧毁 them uncontested.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • 有时 when killed, Radicals will raise their left fist over their head in a final act of defiance. They will also twist the dial on their 机械 heart when their 生命值 is low.
  • the Radical is one of three 敌人 in the game to have a single part with a higher 生命值 value than its 主生命值, that being its torso. This is likely a bug.
  • Some of the 生化人 are female, having entirely different voice lines, and a slightly different 物理 外观 (longer hair, chest coverings)
