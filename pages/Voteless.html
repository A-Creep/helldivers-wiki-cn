生成[edit | edit 来源]

无票者 are the most prevalent 光能者 unit, and are present in virtually every 定居点, patrol, and enemy 增援. They additionally emerge 频繁 from Grounded Warp Ship.

在……的星球上   Appropriators subfaction is active, the 无票者 are completely absent.

行为[edit | edit 来源]

无票者 攻击 by running towards 绝地潜兵, attacking with a swipe of their claws when within 近战 射程. 无票者 are able to lunge a short 距离 towards targets that are slightly out of reach of their 默认 attacking 射程.

结构解析[edit | edit 来源]

无票者 appear to be alien mutated forms of 超级地球 citizens that have undergone severe 光能者 bio-modification and brainwashing, resulting in glowing solid blue eyes, a gray fleshy exterior, long arms ending in four tentacles akin to digits, and an organ/device resembling a breathing apparatus covering their mouth.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • The 无票者 individually pose little 威胁, only able to 攻击 at arm's length and weakly at that. Their real 目的 is to overwhelm 绝地潜兵 with sheer numbers and 速度 in order to soak up 火焰 for the more 危险 光能者 敌人.
  • 无票者 are slightly more 有韧性 than other chaff units like 食腐虫 和 装甲兵.
  • 武器 with a high volume of 火焰 or large 范围效果 are well-suited for cutting down 无票者 hordes.
    • 有效 examples include the CB-9 Exploding Crossbow, FLAM-66 Torcher, AR-23A 解放者 卡宾枪，以及 SG-8P Punisher Plasma.
  • 毒气 效果 are particularly 克制 庞大 hordes of 无票者 due to their 困惑 效果. The poisonous 毒气-伤害 itself will also neutralise a 无票者 within 4 to 7-秒 depending on their size.
  • Setting a 无票者 on 火焰 is sufficient to kill them outright even without other sources of 伤害, but does not 困惑 them.
    • Beam 武器 such as the LAS-5 Scythe and 燃烧弹 shotguns like the SG-451 Cookout are 值得注意 for being able to set 无票者 ablaze at ranges farther than they can run before 燃烧 down.
  • the A/弧度-3 特斯拉塔 和 A/FLAM-40 火焰喷射哨戒炮 are 克制 hordes of 无票者 because of their 范围效果 capabilities.
  • Some 无票者 may 继续 to crawl and 攻击 after losing their legs.
    • If they survive losing a leg, 无票者 will 折叠 to the ground and then spend a couple of 秒 examining their legs before they resume their advance at a 已降低 速度.
    • The majority of fully-全自动 武器 can 摧毁 a 无票者' leg in a 单发. Going prone at a safe 距离 and spraying towards a crowd of 无票者' legs can significantly reduce their mobility en masse. This tactic is most 有效 on level ground.
  • 无票者' heads are a weakpoint that can be targeted in order to terminate them in a much quicker fashion.
    • The 无票者' weak heads make AX/AR-23 "Guard Dog" an 有效 choice against them as the drone has precise 精度. Since it is automated, the drone will also allow you to focus on more 危险 光能者 threats.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • Upon death, blue particles and smoke seem to seep out of the 无票者, with their eyes being drained of their blue shine.
  • Death by 电弧伤害, or single, precise hits to their head may result in the 无票者 showing strange 行为 before dying:
    • Their body stiffening straight and performing a rough 超级地球 salute.
    • Looking around in a confused or clueless manner.
    • Frantic, fearful glances in different directions before kneeling down and perishing.
    • Catching a final glimpse of their unsightly form by looking at their now disfigured arms.
      • The aforementioned behaviors do not occur in crawling 无票者.
      • If a leg-shot is damaging enough to kill a 无票者 instead of sending them into their crawling state, the 无票者 may gesture incredulously at their injured leg before losing their 平衡 and dying.
