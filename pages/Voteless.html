生成[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

无票者 are the 最多 prevalent 光能者 单位, and are present in virtually 每个 定居点, 巡逻, and 敌人 增援. They additionally 出现 频繁 from Grounded Warp 舰船.

在……的星球上   Appropriators subfaction is active, the 无票者 are completely absent.

行为[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

无票者 攻击 by running towards 绝地潜兵, attacking with a swipe of their 螯爪 when within 近战 射程. 无票者 are able to lunge a 短 距离 towards targets that are slightly out of reach of their 默认 attacking 射程.

结构解析[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

无票者 appear to be 外星 mutated forms of 超级地球 citizens that have undergone severe 光能者 bio-modification and brainwashing, resulting in glowing 坚固 blue eyes, a gray fleshy exterior, 长 手臂 ending in four tentacles akin to digits, and an 器官/device resembling a breathing apparatus covering their 嘴部.

战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • The 无票者 individually pose 少量 威胁, only able to 攻击 at arm's 长度 and weakly at that. Their 真实 目的 is to overwhelm 绝地潜兵 with sheer numbers and 速度 in 命令 to soak up 火焰 for the 更多 危险 光能者 敌人.
  • 无票者 are slightly 更多 有韧性 than other chaff units 点赞 食腐虫 and 装甲兵.
  • 武器 with a 高 卷 of 火焰 or 大 范围效果 are well-suited for cutting down 无票者 hordes.
    • 有效 examples include the CB-9 Exploding Crossbow, FLAM-66 Torcher, AR-23A 解放者 卡宾枪, and SG-8P Punisher 等离子.
  • 毒气 效果 are particularly 克制 庞大 hordes of 无票者 due to their 困惑 效果. The poisonous 毒气-伤害 itself will also neutralise a 无票者 within 4 to 7-秒 depending on their 尺寸.
  • Setting a 无票者 on 火焰 is sufficient to 击杀 them outright even without other sources of 伤害, but does not 困惑 them.
    • 光束 武器 such as the LAS-5 Scythe and 燃烧弹 shotguns 点赞 the SG-451 Cookout are 值得注意 for being able to 套装 无票者 ablaze at ranges farther than they can run before 燃烧 down.
  • the A/弧度-3 特斯拉塔 and A/FLAM-40 火焰喷射哨戒炮 are 克制 hordes of 无票者 because of their 范围效果 capabilities.
  • 一些 无票者 5月 继续 to 爬行 and 攻击 after losing their 腿部.
    • If they survive losing a leg, 无票者 will 折叠 to the 地面 and then 花费 a couple of 秒 examining their 腿部 before they 继续 their advance at a 已降低 速度.
    • The majority of fully-全自动 武器 can 摧毁 a 无票者' leg in a 单发. Going 卧倒 at a 安全 距离 and spraying towards a crowd of 无票者' 腿部 can significantly 减少 their mobility en masse. This tactic is 最多 有效 on level 地面.
  • 无票者' heads are a weakpoint that can be targeted in 命令 to terminate them in a 许多 quicker fashion.
    • The 无票者' 弱 heads make AX/AR-23 "守卫 Dog" an 有效 choice against them as the 无人机 has precise 精度. Since it is automated, the 无人机 will also 允许 you to focus on 更多 危险 光能者 threats.

琐事[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Upon death, blue particles and 烟雾弹 seem to seep out of the 无票者, with their eyes being drained of their blue shine.
  • Death by 电弧伤害, or 单个, precise hits to their 头部 5月 结果 in the 无票者 showing strange 行为 before dying:
    • Their 身体 stiffening straight and performing a 粗糙 超级地球 salute.
    • Looking around in a confused or clueless manner.
    • Frantic, fearful glances in different directions before kneeling down and perishing.
    • Catching a final glimpse of their unsightly 形成 by looking at their now disfigured 手臂.
      • The aforementioned behaviors do not occur in crawling 无票者.
      • If a leg-射击 is damaging enough to 击杀 a 无票者 instead of sending them into their crawling 陈述, the 无票者 5月 gesture incredulously at their injured leg before losing their 平衡 and dying.
