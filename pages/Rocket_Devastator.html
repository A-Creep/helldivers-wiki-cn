Rocket 蹂躏者 是一种中型 机器人 和……的一个变种 蹂躏者, equipped with 火箭巢 and a double-barreled Fusion 特攻 Cannon.

生成

Rocket Devastators can be first found on 简单 作为……的任务目标 消除 Devastators 任务.

Starting on 挑战, Rocket Devastators, alongside 重型 Devastators, will have a chance of replacing Devastators. This chance 增加 the higher the 难度. Rocket Devastators can spawn from 机器人空投, within patrols, and guarding 机器人 哨站, 目标，以及 Minor Places of Interest.

行为

Rocket Devastators will often 火焰 their rockets first when it spots a 绝地潜兵. Before firing its rockets, it takes one second to put itself into a firing position, then sends a four rocket 齐射 towards a 绝地潜兵. After this, it takes four to five 秒 before firing another 齐射. Between 齐射, it will 偶尔 火焰 its Fusion 特攻 Cannon twice. After firing all twelve of its rockets, it will 换弹 its 火箭巢 once. Once it exhausts these rockets, it will begin using only its Fusion 特攻 Cannon.

Devastators are able to punch 绝地潜兵 if they're too close, knocking them down. They can also stomp on 绝地潜兵 if they are prone.

战术信息

火箭蹂躏者

  • Rocket Devastators are 准确 and deadly even at 射程, and its rockets can leave 绝地潜兵 易受 other 机器人 if they're knocked off their feet. They should be considered a high-priority 目标 at all times. In a pinch, any weapon can be used to 摧毁 their 火箭巢, 本质上 reducing it to a 蹂躏者.
    • Alternatively, 绝地潜兵 can peek in and out of thick cover to waste a Rocket 蹂躏者's 弹药.

蹂躏者

  • 蹂躏者的躯干覆盖着  中型 护甲, but their head, pelvis, and 胃 are  Very 轻型 护甲。大量武器可以一发爆头摧毁蹂躏者，例如 P-113 裁决 和 R-63 Diligence，因此精准射击收益极高。但这也存在风险，因为未命中的爆头会被躯干护甲弹开。射击腹部可以提供更稳定的击杀方式。
  • If 绝地潜兵 are unable to quickly kill a 蹂躏者, staggering them while dealing 伤害 is a reliable 策略. The most 值得注意 candidates for this are 变体 of the SG-8 Punisher。通过持续射击，可以让蹂躏者保持失衡状态，使其无法精准开火，直到造成足够伤害或命中更精准的爆头。
  • the CB-9 Exploding Crossbow is 极佳 at destroying Devastators, as 2 shots on their torso will 通常 kill them and the 爆炸 will 硬直 other Devastators nearby.
  • The use of 爆炸武器 on the hips/legs can rapidly deconstruct most Devastators.
  • the LAS-16 Sickle 和 LAS-17 Double-Edge Sickle 可以通过射击头部和腿部高效摧毁蹂躏者，但其低精度在激烈战斗中可能不太可靠。

琐事

  • Rocket Devastators can appear on planets invaded by the  Incineration Corps. However, they 功能 the same, differing only by the red and white color scheme and 徽章 of the Corps.
