补丁 01.000.304[edit | edit 来源]

🌍 概述

For this 补丁, we have made improvements and changes to the following areas:

  • 崩溃修复
  • 杂项修复

🔧 Fixes

崩溃

  • Fixed a crash triggered when the host left after a player hot joined.
  • Fixed an occasional crash in 武装配置 menu.
  • Fix for a rare crash when transitioning to or from the ship.

杂项修复

  • Fixed 玩家 getting stuck on the 任务 summary screen when the host leaves.
  • Fixed the issue of non-purchased 超级货币 not being 可见 in the 战争债券 概述 screen (only relevant for Japanese release).
  • Optional 账户 linking now available in the 账户 章节 of the in-game settings.
  • Fixed 'Steam AppId supplied 无效 or not allowed' being shown due to a different error.
  • Fix attachments on 武器 showing up as purple 问题 marks in the tutorial in some cases.
  • Airburst proximity 爆炸 is now triggered only by 敌人 & 绝地潜兵.
  • the AR-61 Tenderizer now has the 正确 color scheme.

🧠 已知问题

These are issues that were either introduced by this 补丁 and are being worked on, or are from a 上一个 version and have not yet been fixed.

  • 玩家 may not have the option to send, accept or decline friend requests.
  • 玩家 may not have the option to Block other 玩家.
  • Blocked 玩家 are not added to the Blocked Player list or prevented from joining games.
  • Game may crash if the player changes the text 语言 while on a 任务.
  • 玩家 may 经验值 delays in Medals and 超级货币 payouts.
  • 玩家 may 偶尔 接收 excessive XP.
  • 敌人 that bleed out do not progress 个人命令 and 歼灭 missions.
  • 电弧武器 有时 behave inconsistently and 有时 misfire.
  • Most 武器 shoot below the crosshair when aiming down the sights.
  • AR-61 Tenderizer deals too little 伤害 and is not in the final state we are intending.
  • Spear’s 索敌 is inconsistent, making it 困难 to 锁定 to larger 敌人.
  • 战略配备 beam might attach itself to an enemy but it will deploy to its original 位置.
  • 绝地潜兵 may be unable to stand up from crouching when surrounded by 敌人.
  • "Hand Carts" 舰船模块 does not reduce 防护罩生成包's 冷却.
  • Some 玩家 might become stuck in the 武装配置 when joining a game in progress.
  • 增援 may not be available for some 玩家 who join a game in progress.
  • 行星 解放 reaches 100% at the end of every Defend 任务.
