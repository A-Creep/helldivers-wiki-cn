描述

高级过滤
提供 80% 抗性 to 毒气伤害 and 效果.

解释

You become near-抵抗 to the DoT and slowing 效果 of 毒气.

Interestingly, the 重型 variety of this 护甲, when combined with the Vitality Enhancement 强化资源, renders the 绝地潜兵 completely immune to 毒气.

值得注意 毒气 threats include the following:

  • AX/TX-13 “Guard Dog” Dog Breath
  • G-4 毒气 榴弹
  • MD-8 毒气 Mines
  • 轨道武器 毒气 Strikes
  • TX-41 Sterilizer
  • S-11 Speargun
