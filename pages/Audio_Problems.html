Setting your audio devices

If you are on Windows the easiest method to set your 麦克风 and headphones for 绝地潜兵 2 is to use the Xbox Gamebar. You can set them as 默认 Communications Input/Output on the "Voice"-tab by pressing WIN + G.

Audio Latency

If you 经验值 audio popping / cutting out, then this can have 几个 reasons.

  1. Disable any and all mods to ensure they are not interfering.
  2. 安装 Latencymon to check if your system might be struggling. This 软件 has been 目的 built for these exact issues. It's recommended to follow the userguide to diagnose your system.

In the case that Latencymon can't find problems, you might want to look at 故障排除 Stutters/Short Freezes.

Bluetooth Audio is broken

It's not uncommon to encounter problems when using Bluetooth headsets, resulting in bad audio quality. Bluetooth itself is 有限 to two 频道 audio. That means if you turn on the 麦克风, you get one 频道 for audio (which downgrades your audio to mono, so you cannot tell left from right), and the second 频道 is dedicated to your 麦克风. This is a very widely 已知问题 with bluetooth, and affects everything, not just 绝地潜兵 2.

This is also why if you look at all the "gaming wireless" headphones that are bluetooth they ALWAYS also come with a usb plug, which allows them to switch to NON bluetooth 2.4ghz and get all three channels.

Hands Free Telephony Mode

The issue of bad audio stems from the "Hands Free Telephony Mode". This is part of a lot of systems like Windows and can drastically impact audio quality.

Windows:

  1. Open Settings & Click On Bluetooth & Devices.
  2. Click on View More Devices.
  3. Scroll Down To Find Option More Devices & Printers Settings.
  4. Click On More devices and printer settings.
  5. Double Click on the audio device that you are using.
  6. Click on the Services-Tab.
  7. Uncheck the Hands-free Telephony
  8. Click on apply, then Turn Off and Turn On Bluetooth.

Bluetooth Audio Gateway 服务

There are cases where the hands-free telephony mode gets re-enabled after restarting the 电脑. If that is the case, it can be 已解决 by disabling the "Bluetooth Audio Gateway 服务". The Bluetooth Audio Gateway 服务 (BTAGService) is a Windows 服务 which supports the audio gateway role of the Bluetooth Handsfree Profile.

If you are on PC and use the Hellbomb Script you can go to "Audio Options" and then choose "Bluetooth Telephony 服务", which will disable Windows' telephony 服务.

If you want to turn it off manually, you can do it this way:

  1. Open the search bar.
  2. 类型 services in and open the "Services" app.
  3. Find "Bluetooth Audio Gateway 服务".
  4. Stop the 服务.
  5. Right click and go to "Properties".
  6. Change the "Startup 类型" to "Disabled".

Now the 服务 will remain disabled when restarting the PC. This will however, turn off all Handsfree Telephony for the PC.

Bluetooth 麦克风 receives no input

  5. If a 分离 麦克风 is present, set that as the 默认 in the recording tab.

Other Bluetooth interference

There have been reports where Xbox controllers that were connected via bluetooth caused choppy audio. When 故障排除 audio problems it is therefore advised to turn other Bluetooth hardware off to avoid any interference. If you are unsure if your Controller causes problems, you can also 购买 a WiFi-Dongle for it to use 2.4GHz instead.

Static Noise

If you 经验值 static noise, there's a number of things you can try:

  1. Make sure to use the latest audio drivers.
  2. 更新 any third party 软件 that relates to audio to the latest version.
  3. Test if sound improves by disabling or removing said third party 软件.

Some headphones also 显示 hissing sounds on higher audio levels when no sound is playing. This is only abnormal when it's still audible while playing sounds. In such scenarios you can try and mute the 麦克风 in case the 耳机 is configured to play back what the 麦克风 is picking up, which therefore creates a constant background noise. You can check input and output in your task bar.

USB wired peripherals:

  • If you have a wired USB 麦克风 it's worth trying out a different USB port in case the currently used one has a grounding 问题.
  • If all USB ports 显示 buzzing sound, an AC to USB converter may be necessary.
  • Laptops have a slightly louder buzzing sound due to the internal 连接 of USB ports.
  • If other USB devices are connected to the PC it’s recommended to 断开 them and see if the issue persists.

System Input Volume goes down

Some systems show that during 绝地潜兵 2 any audio input automatically causes the audio input volume to lower itself.

You can try and disable Audio Enhancements first which should get rid of any auto gain 控制:

  1. Open your "Sound Settings" in Windows.
  2. Go to "More Sound Settings".
  3. Choose "Recording".
  4. Double click on your input device (the 麦克风 you are using).
  5. Go to "Advanced".
  6. Uncheck "Enable Audio Enhancements".
  7. Click "Apply".

Aux vs. USB

According to this Steam post it seems that this 问题 is rooted in the USB driver, which automatically 降低 input gain for USB microphones. You can circumvent the 问题 by connecting an AUX 麦克风 and setting it as your 默认 input device, preventing the USB driver from automatically lowering system input volume. Some 软件, such as "Virtual Audio Cables" can create virtual AUX inputs and therefore 达成 the same 效果 by tricking Windows into assuming an analog 麦克风 is present.

Third-Party 软件

Logitech & Blue

If you are using peripherals made by Logitech or Blue (which was purchased by Logitech) you might need to 安装 G-Hub or Blue 控制 to properly set them up. The main issue is caused by a limiter option that is selected in the 控制 软件. However if that 软件 is not installed, the 耳机 might behave as if these options are enabled.

You can try to uncheck the options for "Exclusive Mode" in mmsys.cpl to stop this 行为 but that does not always work. In such cases you can only 安装 the 软件 and disable the 特性.

Steelseries

If you use peripherals made by SteelSeries, you have to set them up using the SteelSeries GG app. 突出 problems are microphones not picking up audio input.

  1. 安装 Steelseries GG app or 更新 it if necessary.
  2. Enable Sonar. You open the "Mixer" tab and select the 正确 devices there (such as Headphones or 麦克风).
