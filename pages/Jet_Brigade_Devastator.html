Jet Brigade 蹂躏者 是……的变种  机器人 蹂躏者 unit. Now affiliated with the   Jet Brigade, this 蹂躏者 has been repainted and refitted with a 喷射背包.

生成

Jet Brigade Devastators can first be found on 容易 在被……入侵的星球上 Jet Brigade. They can be spawned from 机器人空投 and within patrols.

the 移动工厂 can spawn four Jet Brigade Devastators from its undercarriage.

行为

Jet Brigade Devastators will slowly approach 绝地潜兵 while firing their Fusion 特攻 Cannon. They'll rapidly 火焰 six 2-shot bursts, before pausing for about 3 秒 to cool their 武器.

Like regular Devastators, Jet Brigade Devastators are able to punch 绝地潜兵 if they're too close, knocking them down. They can also stomp on them if they are prone.

In the case that there is a large amount of 距离 between a Jet Brigade 蹂躏者 and the 绝地潜兵 it is 索敌, they will use their 喷射背包 to rapidly close the 距离. Their 喷射背包 can 爆炸 if damaged, significantly damaging and 燃烧 themselves and nearby 机器人 或绝地潜兵。

战术信息

  • Jet Brigade Devastators can cross large areas and quickly get into ranges where their 武器 are far deadlier. They will prioritize getting into close quarters at all costs, limiting the player's 能力 to 消除 them at 远距离.
  • The 喷气背包 of the Jet Brigade 蹂躏者 is more 脆弱 than the 蹂躏者 itself, exploding and igniting 敌人, itself, and potentially the player upon 摧毁. The 喷气背包 will 引爆 posthumously if enough 伤害 reaches the 喷气背包 through the 蹂躏者's chest or 胃.
    • 武器 such as the PLAS-101 Purifier, SG-8P Punisher Plasma，或 R-36 Eruptor can be used to 引爆 the 喷射背包 from afar, dealing 范围伤害 around the 蹂躏者 and disabling its jump 能力.

琐事

  • Jet Brigade Devastators have an alternate color palette with considerably fewer red accents than 标准 机器人 colors. Notably, their heads are plain black with stark white eyes.
  • Jet Brigade Devastators are referred to as 'Devastators' by the game, lacking a unique name.
