战术信息

  • Impact zones on the ground can be seen with a red-hue during 白天 and during night the impact zones are white-hued until a moment before the impact.
  • On impact, the 燃烧 rocks leave behind patches of 火焰.
  • The haphazard nature of 火山 Activity while deadly to 绝地潜兵 has the possible perk of significantly hastening the progress of the 任务 by destroying 目标 hundreds of meters away from the 当前 位置 of the 绝地潜兵 due to being a map-wide event rather than being a radius tied to a 绝地潜兵.
