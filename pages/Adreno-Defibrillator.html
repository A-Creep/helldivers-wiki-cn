描述[edit | edit 来源]

Adreno-Defibrillator
提供 one-time, short-lived resuscitation upon death, given that the 绝地潜兵's body is still intact.
增加 stim 效果 持续时间 by 2.0秒.
提供 50% 抗性 to 电弧伤害.


功能[edit | edit 来源]

Upon dying, unless gibbed, the player will ragdoll and 生命值 is immediately restored to full. While not under the 效果 of a stim, vision will turn black and white and audio will be muffled. Revived 玩家 suffer a permanent bleed penalty, where their 耐力 will always be fully depleted and their 生命值 constantly drains. 生命值 drains at a rate of 大约 20% 每秒, and the player can still take 伤害 from 敌人 as normal. This last stand state ends when the player's 生命值 is fully depleted, whether from the 生命值 drain or from enemy 攻击.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • This 护甲 被动 grants 玩家 the opportunity to perform a final stand after their 生命值 reaches zero.
  • In this last stand, you are intended to do as much 伤害 as possible before you fully die, such as with a B-100 Portable Hellbomb, or an Eagle 500kg Bomb thrown at your feet.[1]
  • Using a Stim can prolong the 持续时间 of the last stand, however the 生命值 drain is permanent and can never be stopped.
  • Boarding the 鹈鹕 in a last stand state will count as a successful 撤离, as your 绝地潜兵 will still pose victoriously upon returning to the 驱逐舰 instead of standing to the side.
  • Each stim used by the wearer, whether on themselves or on a teammate, lasts 2.0 秒 longer.
    • The 已增加 stim 持续时间 can allow the patient to endure 伤害 for longer periods of time before the 效果 expire. Principally, this means that 玩家 under the 效果 of the stim have a bigger window during which they will emerge from the stim's 效果 with full 生命值.
    • The stim will also 增加 the 持续时间 of the last stand state, allowing the player to 继续 dealing 伤害 over a longer period of time.
  • The 护甲 also 提供 50% 抗性 to 电力 攻击 from certain 类型 of Illuminates or 友军误伤 arcs from other 绝地潜兵.
