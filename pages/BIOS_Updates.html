BIOS 更新

Motherboard BIOS / UEFI Updates are among the most impactful system 维护 routines you can perform. This 流程 updates your motherboard's core firmware, which dictates how your CPU, RAM, graphics card, and 存储 devices interact on a low hardware level.

⚠ 致命 警告: READ CAREFULLY BEFORE PROCEEDING ⚠ Flashing your BIOS is a low-level hardware modification that carries inherent risks. Unlike 标准 软件 installations, interrupting a BIOS 更新 (due to a power outage, system freeze, or accidentally pulling the USB drive) can permanently brick your motherboard., rendering your PC completely unbootable.

Please only 继续 if you are confident in taking this risk. Any and all damages that may occur to your hardware or data during this 流程 are entirely your own responsibility. This guide is provided strictly for informational purposes; 否 liability or responsibility is assumed for any errors, hardware failures, or data loss encountered. If you feel inexperienced or uncomfortable with this 程序, please consult a certified hardware 技术员.

何时以及为何应该更新

As a 基本信息 rule of thumb, it is often best to only 更新 your BIOS if you encounter persistent 稳定性 problems that 标准 故障排除 methods cannot 解决. As per Intel's 官员 guidelines: "BIOS updates aren’t a routine 流程, and 通常 shouldn’t be performed on PCs that are working normally." Or in simpler terms: If it ain't broke, don't 修复 it.

However, with modern hardware architectures, this paradigm has significantly shifted. 致命 稳定性 issues in existing hardware, voltage safety corrections for newer processors, memory compatibility fixes, and severe 安全 flaws often make these updates an absolute necessity rather than an optional choice. BEWARE: BIOS updates 通常 won't unlock any extra performance (not that it cannot be, it's just a very rare occurrence). For more information on this, you may want to read up on XDA Developers.

如果你决定继续进行BIOS更新，强烈建议事先仔细阅读主板（或笔记本电脑）制造商提供的官方文档，确保你了解精确的、特定于型号的步骤。

已知硬件缺陷

在某些系统上，BIOS/UEFI更新极为关键：

  • Intel's 13th and 14th 世代 CPUs are known to suffer from hardware instability issues known as "vMin Shift", caused by 高处 operating voltages which lead to rapid 物理 degradation of the 处理器. To mitigate this 问题, motherboard manufacturers released 致命 UEFI updates containing microcode 0x12F to prevent further degradation.
    • ⚠ Please be aware: If your CPU has already suffered 物理 degradation and is actively crashing, a BIOS 更新 cannot undo the hardware 伤害. In such cases, the 处理器 must be replaced via RMA.

But AMD also has their fair share of problems. While this doesn't mean that you will inevitably run into problems with your configuration, it's good practice to keep your system updated to avoid hardware failure.

  • AMD Ryzen 7000 series CPUs: Particularly the 3D V-Cache models like the 7800X3D, suffered fom a devastating hardware flaw tied to outdated motherboard firmware.[1]
    • 当玩家启用内存超频配置文件（如AMD EXPO或Intel XMP）以最大化性能时，许多早期AM5主板BIOS配置会自动将"CPU SOC电压(VCC_SOC)"推至不安全限值以上——经常超过1.35V至1.4V。
    • 这种过度过压随后引发了剧烈的内部高温退化，导致处理器的3D V-Cache硅片物理膨胀、熔化，并最终烧毁CPU和主板插槽，使两个组件永久报废。
    • AMD紧急发布了关键AGESA微码更新，将最大SOC电压严格限制在安全的1.3V。
  • AMD Ryzen 9000 series CPUs: These CPU face severe 稳定性 and hardware risks if their motherboard firmware is outdated.[2]
    • The issue stems from the initial AM5 BIOS versions provided by 多个 motherboard vendors (such as ASRock), which use an overly 激进 工厂 默认 for Precision Boost Overdrive (PBO), delivering excessive voltage and 当前 limits.[3]
    • Under 重型, sustained multi-threaded CPU workloads (which can be applied by applications or games), these 极难 power settings caused severe thermal strain, leading to premature 处理器 deaths and, in rare catastrophic cases, physically burnt silicon or scorched pads.[4]

Updating your BIOS to the latest stable version is therefore recommended to 受益 from the fixes. Once your mainboard's BIOS / UEFI has been updated, it will automatically load the updated microcode which will then 保护 your CPU from such occurrences.

准备的黄金法则

To minimize the risks of a failed flash and 消除 common post-更新 frustrations, always 完成 the following preparatory steps before starting the 更新 管道. Always keep your handbook ready and if you are unsure about specifics for your motherboard, please 联络 the 官员 支援 of your motherboard 制造商.

查找你的确切主板型号

Motherboard firmware is hyper-specific. Flashing a BIOS meant for a "Version 1.0" board onto a "Version 2.0" board, or confusing an ASUS Prime Z790-A with a Z790-P, can immediately lock up the system. Check your exact model beforehand.

  1. 您主板的包装上应印有制造商名称和型号。
  2. 主板型号也印在主板PCB上。
  3. If you are on Windows, you can open the Windows Start Menu, 类型 in msinfo32 (System Information), and look at the "BaseBoard 产品" line.

Please be aware: If your board received a Revision, it's 重要 to 下载 BIOS updates for your exact revision and not for older / newer revisions.

对于预装电脑，建议检查该电脑的制造商是否提供其自有UEFI版本。

BitLocker 数据陷阱

If Windows BitLocker device encryption is active on your 存储 drives, updating your BIOS will reset your Trusted Platform Module (TPM). Windows will interpret this as a 物理 安全 breach and trap you in a permanent boot loop demanding a BitLocker 恢复 Key.

  • Temporarily suspend BitLocker encryption in Windows or locate your 官员 Microsoft 账户 恢复 Key BEFORE you reboot your PC to flash the firmware.

准备一个可靠的USB驱动器

请使用高质量、稳定的U盘。

  1. Format it strictly to the FAT32 file system, as most motherboard BIOS flash utilities cannot read NTFS or exFAT partitions.
  2. 直接从制造商官方支持页面下载固件文件（而非任何第三方页面！）
  3. 解压ZIP压缩包，并将纯".CAP"或".ROM"文件放入该驱动器的根目录。

清理环境

Never attempt to flash your BIOS if your area is currently experiencing severe 天气 (storms, 重型 winds, other 极难 天气 phenomenon) that could cause a localized power failure. If you own an Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS), ensure your PC and 监控 are plugged into it.

Keeping your power uninterrupted is doubly 重要 if you are playing on a Laptop! Only 更新 your BIOS when you are connected to a wall socket. NEVER 更新 YOUR BIOS WITH 电池 ONLY.

记录你当前的自定义设置

Flashing the BIOS will completely erase all your custom profiles and restore 工厂 defaults. If you previously enabled "XMP/EXPO" for your RAM, adjusted custom Fan Curves for an AIO liquid cooler, or configured "Resizable BAR", you will need to manually re-enable them after the system reboots successfully.

Nvidia Legacy 黑屏循环

Certain Graphics cards - most commonly the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5000 series - suffer from a firmware-level incompatibility with specific motherboard BIOS versions if the system relies on legacy / CSM boot modes instead of the modern UEFI. This can result in a permanent black screen upon system reboot.

请在更新UEFI之前检查你的特定GPU，以确认是否需要提前准备任何事项，或者是否可以在更新主板BIOS/UEFI之前更新GPU的vBIOS。

Ensure that your motherboard's boot state is strictly set to "native UEFI mode (CSM Disabled)" before updating. If you are already caught in a black screen loop with these specific cards, you will need to utilize the 官员 NVIDIA GPU UEFI Firmware 更新 Tool v2.0.

避免使用基于Windows的更新工具

如今许多主板制造商捆绑了桌面软件套件（如"ASUS 军械库 Crate"、"MSI Center"等），宣称能够直接从Windows桌面一键刷新BIOS。

通常 it is recommended to not use these 更新 utilities. Windows is a highly 复杂 环境 with a lot of active services and background processes that run simultaneously. If a Windows-based flash tool encounters issues (in the worst of circumstances triggering a Blue Screen of Death) mid-flash, the 更新 might fail, resulting in a permanently corrupted firmware.

通过格式化USB驱动器并在主板原生工具中直接执行来更新BIOS，可以避免另一个潜在故障点，且该方式不那么容易被中断。

避免使用测试版

有时 manufacturers offer beta versions of upcoming BIOS updates. As a 基本信息 rule of thumb these should be avoided, unless you have a very specific 问题 that cannot be 已解决 with older stable versions. Be aware though that while manufacturers 通常 create new versions with care, there's always a chance of something not working as it should.

芯片组驱动程序（AMD）

For AMD users it's 重要 to also 更新 their chipset drivers to the latest version after updating the BIOS. Otherwise you might encounter instability problems.

主板快速刷新功能（USB FlashBack / Q-Flash Plus）

Most of modern mid-射程 to high-end motherboards come equipped with a specialized, hardware-level 恢复 特性. Among them are:

  • BIOS FlashBack (ASRock)
  • USB BIOS FlashBack (ASUS)
  • M-Flash Button (MSI)
  • Q-Flash Plus (GigaByte)

该技术允许您使用U盘和后置I/O面板上的专用按钮来刷写或恢复主板固件。

In the event of a failed BIOS 更新 (due to a sudden power outage or system freeze) which would render your motherboard completely, this tool can fully restore and revive the board. As this 流程 runs via a dedicated microchip on the motherboard, it functions without needing a working CPU, installed RAM or even a graphics card. It only requires power from your Power Supply Unit (PSU).

Because this 功能型 executes without an on-screen 显示 or graphical interface, it is 极其 strict regarding file preparation. Deviation from these steps will cause the flash 流程 to fail. Before you attempt it, make sure to read your manufacturers 文档 carefully and follow the steps provided.
