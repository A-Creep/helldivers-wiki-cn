生成[edit | edit 来源]

  • 绝地潜兵 deploy from Hellpods during the start of a 任务, when a 增援 is called by an ally, or when an entire 绝地潜兵 squad is wiped out.
    • During 突击兵 Missions, 绝地潜兵 will deploy from 特殊 增援 Pods that have been pre-placed in the surrounding area.
  • Aside from the first deployment during a 任务, 绝地潜兵 have a 有限 number of respawns, amounting to 5 per squad member, pooling together for a total of 20 if there are 4 绝地潜兵 present.
    • This can be 已增加 to 6 per squad member if one 绝地潜兵 is using the 已增加 增援预算 增益。
    • If all 增援 are depleted, then they will slowly 充能 to a 最大 of one on a timer of 120 秒, 已降低 to 90 秒 with the 灵活增援预算 增益。

行为[edit | edit 来源]

  • 绝地潜兵 will often work together in groups of up to 4, focusing on achieving their 当前 任务 目标 by any means necessary.
  • 绝地潜兵 are player-controlled and can be unpredictable, caution is advised when approaching.
  • 通常, 绝地潜兵 are 通常 cooperative with one another, and will relay 兴趣点 in order to 完成 their 任务 as efficiently as possible.
  • 绝地潜兵 will 通常 focus on collecting Samples and finding Minor Places of Interest when not engaged in combat or completing an 目标.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • When any limb reaches 0 生命值, it will break, causing the following 效果:
    • Head: Death
    • Chest: 10 每秒伤害 of bleed 伤害 until death or healed by a 兴奋剂
    • Arm: 已增加 Sway and 已降低 精度
      • If the right arm breaks, 已降低 throwing 射程
    • Leg: 已降低 移动速度, Sets 耐力 to 0, Prevents 耐力 regeneration
  • Excluding any 护甲 Passives, 绝地潜兵 take 130% 毒气 伤害, 50% 爆炸 and 50% 火焰伤害
    • 爆炸伤害 taken is 已降低 by 90% when a 绝地潜兵 is prone or in ragdoll state.
  • 绝地潜兵 are very 脆皮 compared to most 敌人, having no 护甲 and only 125 生命值. They can die quickly to just about anything.
    • This is counteracted by the unique 属性 of 护甲 to resist 伤害 and availability of Stims to heal, granting them fully restored 生命值 and 耐力 along with a temporary 10% 伤害抗性 if using the 试验性注射剂 强化资源 in Stim state or 被动 additional 10% 伤害抗性 with the Vitality Enhancement 强化资源.
  • 绝地潜兵 have a vast arsenal at their disposal, consisting of 几个 different 武器 和 战略配备 designed to supplement their operations and 摧毁 their 敌人.
  • 绝地潜兵 can be easily knocked over with enough force, throwing them to the ground, rendering them unable to move, heal, or 攻击 for a short time.
