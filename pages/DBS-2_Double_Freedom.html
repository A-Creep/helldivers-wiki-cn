战术信息[编辑 | 编辑 来源]

  • Due to the 有限 容量, it is not recommended that 绝地潜兵 engage 大 groups of 敌人 with only the 双重 Freedom.
  • 绝地潜兵 can 套装 the 双重 Freedom to 齐射 模式 to 火焰 两者 barrels at 一次, providing a 许多 larger 连射 of 伤害.
    • The 双重 Freedom's 齐射 模式 is 优秀 for hitting the 弱 spots of 重型 敌人 at 关闭 距离, 点赞 强袭虫 tails, 巨型者 vents, 穿刺虫 heads or 猎杀器 joints. It doesn't have 许多 use elsewhere, as the 双重 Freedom's 庞大 伤害 of 840 is already enough to one-射击 nearly 任何 敌人 in the 游戏.
    • Using 齐射 模式 on 关闭 距离, 双重 Freedom is also capable to swiftly 摧毁 structures such as 孢子喷涌虫's or 生物处理器 and breaking Grounded Warp 舰船 护盾.
  • Using an 护甲 被动 that 增加 换弹 速度 such as 围城-Ready or Reinforced Epaulettes is highly recommended due to how frequent the reloads are.
