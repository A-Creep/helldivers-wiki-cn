FAF-14 飞矛（常简称为飞矛）是一种可装填的反坦克支援武器，发射威力强大的追踪导弹，可有效对付重型敌人。

采购

The Spear is unlocked at the 爱国行政中心 for 9000 申购点 Slips at Level 20.

功能

The Spear cannot be dumb-fired, unlike other rocket launchers. The wielder must aim it at a 目标 which is inside of the large white reticle, causing a 小型 yellow 三角形 to appear. This 三角形 is centered on the 目标 and turns green with an accompanying "click" sound when the Spear has successfully 已锁定, which takes one second. The Spear can then be fired to launch a seeking 导弹 which does 高伤害. Locks cannot be attained past 300 meters.

战术信息

  • The Spear only locks onto "large" targets (larger blip on the 小地图) and non-SEAF structures. A list of valid targets can be found below this 章节.
  • The Spear requires a clear line of sight to the 目标 to successfully 锁定. 障碍 like foliage and fences can prevent locks, even if it looks like they shouldn't.
    • This is most egregious with the Bulk 构筑者 as its 索敌 point is very low to the ground 亲戚 to its height.
    • The Spear can still attain locks through smoke and fog, making it useful at 反制中 Spore Chargers.
  • The 导弹's upwards 弹道 may cause it to miss 敌人 at 近距离. Staying at least 30 meters away from the 目标 is advised.
    • If forced into 近距离, 绝地潜兵 can go prone to lower the 导弹's 弹道. Aiming downwards will lower it further.
  • 一旦 HUD 显示导弹已装入发射器，即可取消换弹动画。
  • The Spear performs best in relatively flat and open biomes, where 敌人 can be attacked at farther distances.
    • Sandy and moor biomes are good examples, while the trees in primordial and swamp biomes can block missiles.
    • 城市环境中的建筑会遮挡视线。
  • The 导弹's 弹道 is affected by where the 绝地潜兵 aims. Aiming left and right can curve the 导弹 to hit weakspots at an angle, while aiming above and below affects the vertical 弹道 of the 导弹.
    • 在发射导弹前俯冲会将绝地潜兵的动量传递给导弹，从而实现高级弹道操控。
  • The Spear's higher 爆破力 allows it to 摧毁 吐酸泰坦 虫穴.
  • The Spear is able to 摧毁 a 运输舰 with a single hit to the fuselage, killing all of its occupants.
    • This is also able to 摧毁 Vox Engines and 工厂 Striders.
    • 攻击引擎并不可取，因为这通常无法保证乘客死亡。
    • 绝地潜兵应等到运输舰开始减速后再开火，以防止脱靶。
  • The Spear is able to lock onto Stalkers even while they are invisible. This can be used to find them if one is aware of their presence, but not their precise 位置.
  • Spear是有效 反制 to Dragonroaches, as one 导弹 is sufficient to 摧毁 a wing or the abdomen.
  • The Stingray and Dragonroach can outrun the Spear's missiles, causing them to expire and be wasted.
    • 刺魟在准备扫射时很脆弱。

琐事

  • The weapon and how it operates appears to be inspired by the real world FGM-148 Javelin man-portable 反坦克 system.
    • A javelin is a throwing spear, built lighter and with a 更小 head, but in shape and 能力 to pierce 护甲 is very similar to spears, hence the name.
  • The weapon's designation "FAF" stands for 火焰 And Forget, referring to the weapon's 能力 to guide itself to its 目标 upon firing without additional input from the wielder.
