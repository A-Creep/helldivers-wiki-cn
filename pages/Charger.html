生成

Starting on 挑战, Chargers can spawn from Bug 突破, patrolling the map with other 终结族, and guarding 目标. Higher difficulties will 增加 how often they can spawn. In addition, they can be found guarding Minor Places of Interest，它们会

Starting on 自杀任务, Chargers can be found guarding 重型 Bug Nests and 超级绝地潜兵难度's mega Nests. In addition, Chargers have a chance of being replaced by its 变体, the 巨兽级强袭虫 and the Spore 强袭虫. Higher difficulties will 增加 this chance.

当游戏尝试生成 吐酸泰坦 在带有……的任务中 Dragonroach效果激活时，例如通过 吐酸泰坦 虫穴, a normal 强袭虫 is spawned instead. This makes it possible to encounter regular Chargers on 超级绝地潜兵难度, where their spawns are otherwise replaced entirely by their tougher 变体.

行为

在虫族入侵之外遇到时，强袭虫通常会保持不动。注意到绝地潜兵的动作或声音后，它们会转身面向绝地潜兵或声音来源。强袭虫会用咆哮预示即将攻击，然后往往立刻向绝地潜兵冲锋。在巡逻队中，它们也会停下脚步，转向附近任何移动或声音。从虫族入侵生成时，它们会立即进入攻击状态并搜寻附近绝地潜兵加以践踏。

一旦开始冲锋，强袭虫就不会停下，直到冲过绝地潜兵、撞上坚固地形甚至其他强袭虫。被冲锋击中的绝地潜兵会被击倒/击飞，并经常受到震荡效果。强袭虫还会尝试用前腿近距离攻击绝地潜兵。

绝地潜兵 that 摧毁 a 强袭虫's 后部 or 炸掉 its 躯干护甲 will cripple it, slowing its movement and causing it to 流血 out.

战术信息

  • The 强袭虫 can be dodged by moving or diving perpendicular to its movement 方向 or diagonally towards it as it approaches. Beware of moving too early, as the 强袭虫 can make tight turns.
  • The most straightforward way to dispatch a 强袭虫 is firing an 反坦克 weapon, such as the EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器, GR-8 无后坐力炮 和 LAS-99 类星体加农炮, at its head, which will instantly kill it. However, a 正面 shot to anywhere on its torso with the FAF-14 Spear or GR-8 无后坐力炮 will crack open its 护甲 and either kill it (butt shot) or cause it to slowly 流血 out.
    • The 暴露 flesh from this will also allow any weapon to deal 伤害 to the 强袭虫; 造成伤害 to the 内圈 flesh has a 300% 倍率 对主血量.
    • Alternatively, shooting a leg will reveal the 强袭虫's weak flesh. Any weapon can 伤害 the leg flesh, dispatching the 强袭虫 fairly quickly.
  • By positioning behind the 强袭虫 and aiming in the 方向 of its 内圈 flesh, 武器 such as the RS-422 磁轨炮, APW-1 反器材步枪，以及 MG-206 重机枪 can 火焰 发 that penetrate the butt region and inflict 正面伤害 to the 内圈 flesh. This method enables rapid elimination of the 强袭虫.
  • 摧毁强袭虫的  无护甲 后方会导致它在几秒内流血至死。大多数武器都能伤害它，但它有80%的耐久度和很高的生命值。射击臀部的最佳时机是等它们向你冲锋，然后在它们的恢复动画期间向臀部倾泻火力。
    • Due to the high 耐久度 the 最佳武器 are explosives.
    • If one cannot bait the 强袭虫 to charge one can use the GL-21 榴弹发射器 and "bounce" the grenades underneath it to hit the 后部 from the 正面.
  • 利用强袭虫的  重型 护甲 covering most of its body, 支援武器 such as the AC-8 机炮, APW-1 反器材步枪 和 LAS-98 激光大炮 can penetrate it for 已降低 伤害. While this is a rather slow way to dispatch a 强袭虫, it can work if 否 other options are available.

  • the G-123 铝热弹 手榴弹, if stuck to a 强袭虫's head or torso, will instantly kill it upon 引爆.
  • 使用……时的推荐战术 MG-206 重机枪 is to aim at the 强袭虫's 腿部护甲 as it takes fewer total shots to both break the 护甲 and 摧毁 the leg flesh than to aim at the head.
  • FLAM-40 火焰喷射器 是 有效 yet risky method of dispatching a 强袭虫, as it can penetrate the 强袭虫's 护甲 as well as the 强袭虫 having a 150% 火焰伤害倍率.
  • Dropping Hellpods of any kind can strip the 护甲 off a 强袭虫's back, and a 正面 hit will kill it outright, though this requires some luck or sticking 战略配备 beacons onto it.
  • Chargers tend to hone in on one member of the team at a time. Consider coordinating with other 绝地潜兵 to bait a 强袭虫 into exposing its head to a squad mate with an 反坦克 weapon, or to focus on a specific leg to open up its 弱点.
  • 引导强袭虫撞上墙壁或大岩石会使其短暂眩晕，为绝地潜兵提供攻击或撤退的时间。不过它们可以翻越较小的岩石和墙壁，所以要小心。经过足够多的冲撞后，护甲可能会受损脱落。

  • Intimidating as they are, it's 容易 to treat Chargers as the highest 威胁 on the field. However, they 攻击 infrequently, and with enough skill, are 容易 to dodge.
