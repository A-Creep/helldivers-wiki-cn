“

光能者 Harvesters patrol this colony, enforcing the zealous rule of their masters. The unnatural, spindly constructs must be destroyed, so that Freedom can return.

— 任务 概述

“

Invading 光能者 forces have unleashed Harvesters upon the city. These intolerant machines patrol the streets of this area, devastating once-peaceful neighbourhoods and murdering 无辜 civilians. 摧毁 them, and reclaim 控制 of this 分区.

— 任务 概述, city 任务

摧毁猎杀器 is a 任务 目标 that can only appear on 光能者 controlled planets. To 完成 the 目标, 绝地潜兵 must 摧毁 all marked Harvesters.

— 目标 概述

“

光能者 Harvesters are patrolling this city block. Seek and 摧毁 the roaming menace.

— 目标 概述, city 任务

  • 前往地图上的目标地点。
    • The exact 位置 of the 目标 may be ambiguous, so search nearby enemy encampments. (marked red on the map)
  • Kill the 猎杀器 found in the area.

琐事

  • 此任务的任务简报文本异常简短，只说“摧毁区域内的所有猎杀器”，而任务概览文本却异常冗长，暗示两者可能被错误地对调了。
  • 这是唯一一个说“摧毁”而不是“消灭”的“消灭”任务。
