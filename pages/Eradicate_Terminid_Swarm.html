“

A 庞大 终结族 swarm is gathering in this region. Left unchecked, it threatens to 爆炸 out of 控制.

Kill as many 终结族 as you can to thin their numbers.

“

A 庞大 终结族 swarm has gathered in this area. We cannot leave this 威胁 unchecked if we are to take the city back.

— 任务简报，城市任务

歼灭 终结族 Swarm is a 主体 任务 in 绝地潜兵 2. 绝地潜兵 are dropped into a 小型 area, where they must kill a set number of 敌人.

目标步骤

消灭终结族

“

击杀终结族，削减它们的数量。

— 任务简报

  • 击杀虫族敌人
    • 绝地潜兵 must kill x amount of 终结族, the exact number of which depends on the 难度.
  • 奖励： 200 申购点 Slips 和 40 经验值

战术信息

  • 终结族 will stop 生成 after the 目标 has been completed.
  • Enemy 类型 presence and kill 要求 at each 难度:
    • 容易: Scavengers, Pouncers, 武斗虫. 200 kills.

琐事

  • In one of the two maps, a random Vending Machine can be seen out standing alone at the edge of the map.
