🌍 概述

Attention 绝地潜兵,

This hotfix addresses a number of issues reported after the release of Machinery of 压迫 (6.2.5).

We’re continuing to track feedback around upscaling quality and performance, and Nixxes and Arrowhead are actively working on further optimizations and improvements.

📍 补丁 Highlights

  • [PC] 已改进 image quality when using NVIDIA DLSS, AMD FSR or Intel XeSS upscaling.
  • [Xbox] 已改进 AMD FSR upscaling image quality
  • [PS5] 已改进 AMD FSR upscaling image quality
  • [PS5 Pro] 已改进 Sony PSSR upscaling image quality

🔧 修复

  • [Xbox] Fixed an out-of-memory crash on Xbox Series S.
  • [PC] Fixed 崩溃 相关 to Intel XeSS upscaling.
  • [PC] Dynamic 分辨率 Scaling is now turned off by 默认.
    • We’ve seen your reports on Dynamic 分辨率 Scaling causing graphical issues on some graphics cards. While working on a proper fix, we’ve disabled DRS by 默认 on PC. You can still manually enable DRS if desired.
