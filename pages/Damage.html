伤害 Calculation

[1]Calculating 伤害 in 绝地潜兵 2 takes a large number of variables into consideration. All 伤害 calculation information in this 章节 of the 文档 has been confirmed to be 准确 as of 游戏版本 1.001.001

Enemy Parts

An enemy's hitbox is not as simple as a single value of 生命值 and universal 护甲 value. An enemy is made up of many different enemy parts, each part with its own 护甲 value, 生命值, 耐久度, and a 主生命值 pool. Parts can also be considered "耐久" parts, which take 伤害 in a completely different way.

Enemy parts can be destroyed, but that does not always mean the enemy dies. An enemy will die if one of the following conditions are satisfied:

  • Enemy 主生命值 is 已降低 to 0.
  • A 致命 enemy part is destroyed.

百分比 (%) 对主血量

Enemy parts that take 伤害 通常 have a 百分比 of the 造成伤害 to them also be applied to their 主生命值. Most enemy parts will cap the 伤害 对主血量 at their full HP value, however some parts allow excess 伤害 to overflow into 主体.

  • Regular 示例: A 蹂躏者's 主生命值 is 750, with its arms having 260 生命值 and 50% 伤害 对主血量. An AR-23 解放者 deals 90 伤害 to the arms leaving them at 170 生命值. 45 伤害 is also dealt to the 主生命值 leaving it at 705.
  • Uncapped 伤害 示例: A 装甲兵's 主生命值 is 125, with its arms having 65 生命值 and 50% 伤害 对主血量. A 单发 from an R-6 Deadeye will deal its full 300 伤害 to the arms, dealing 150 伤害 to the 装甲兵's 主生命值 which will kill it.

生命值 构造 (流血)

构造 is a second 生命池 that begins decaying once the 主生命值 is depleted. The enemy is now in the "downed" state and will limp until they bleedout and die. Bleedout is a second 生命池 for limbs that determines how long they live for once they are destroyed, at which point, its 构造 will begin to decay, or "流血 out," at a set rate. For some limbs, the 衰退速率 may be 0, 含义 they will not decay over time and bleedout must be depleted with further 伤害 to fully 摧毁 the limb.

  • 构造 示例: 强袭虫 have 750 构造 with a 衰退速率 of 100hp/s. Destroying the butt or depleting 主生命值 will trigger the "downed" state and the 强袭虫 will 流血 out until it depletes its 构造 and dies. Bleedout is 通常 致命.
  • Bleedout without Decay 示例: 移动工厂 have 1,500 腿部生命值 and 2,000 构造 but 0 decay therefore the 构造 effectively 作为额外 生命值 so the legs have 3,500 有效 生命值.

Breakable 护甲板

Some 敌人 have protective 护甲板 shielding 脆弱 and 致命 parts underneath it. The 敌人 that have this are 新月监视者, 崇高监视者, 监视者, 猎杀器, 利维坦, 吐酸泰坦, 强袭虫, 巨兽级强袭虫, 爆裂强袭虫, 孢子强袭虫, 穿刺虫, Hive Lords, 和 移动工厂.

  • Breakable 护甲板 has a 分离 生命池 from the parts underneath it that 不会 传输 excess 伤害 to the parts underneath. The 护甲板 必须 be destroyed in order to deal 伤害 to the 脆弱 and 通常 致命 part underneath.
    • For 示例: Destroying a 强袭虫's 腿部护甲 with a rocket from the MLS-4X 突击兵 and finishing off the 暴露 脆弱 flesh off with a 主武器 such as the BR-14 审判者.

爆炸伤害

爆炸伤害 deals 100% 耐久伤害 and due to the 能力 to hit 多个 limbs, This allows explosives to deal multiplied 伤害 to 主生命值.

  • 爆炸伤害 transferred 对主血量 via 爆炸 脆弱 parts "ignore" 主体 护甲值 and its 爆炸抗性.

For 示例: An HE shell 爆炸 from the GR-8 无后坐力炮 which is  重型 护甲穿透 fired at an Annihilator Tank's 通风口 will ignore the 主体 护甲值 when transferring 伤害 对主血量 which is  Tank I 护甲。

  • Explosions 伤害 主体 via a 机械师 called 受爆炸影响. This allows 爆炸伤害 to 伤害 主体 via a 100% 爆炸抗性 Part because instead of the limb taking the 伤害, it will be redirected 对主血量. Note: This can only occur 一次, 无论 parts hit and 爆炸 护甲穿透 must 比赛 or 超过 主体护甲 to deal 伤害.
    • For 示例: An Eagle 500kg Bomb exploding underneath a 吐酸泰坦 and dealing 伤害 to all four of its legs, bile sacs, underside, 躯干护甲 and head.

爆炸 伤害抗性 (爆炸抗性)

Enemy limbs commonly have 爆炸抗性 and/or 免疫 to 保护 against explosions dealing 多个 instances of 伤害 by hitting 多个 parts.

  • A limb with an 爆炸抗性 value of 100% means it is 爆炸免疫 and that part itself does not take 爆炸伤害. 主体 takes the 爆炸伤害 instead.
  • An 爆炸抗性 value of a 百分比 更低 than 100% means that the 目标 can negate a 百分比 of 爆炸伤害 done to that part to reduce their effectiveness (e.g. Tank hulls have an 爆炸抗性 value of 35%).

Explosions have 3 different radii from the center of impact where the following rules apply:

  • 内圈半径: 100% 伤害, 100% 硬直, Full AP, 100% 爆破力.
  • 外圈半径: 99-0% 伤害 100% 硬直, -1 AP (最小 2 AP), 99-0% 爆破力.
    • Explosions have linear 伤害, 硬直 & 爆破力 fall-off.
  • 冲击波 (硬直): 0% 伤害, 99-0% 硬直, Full AP, 0% 爆破力.
  • 绝地潜兵 will only 经验值 硬直 if they are hit by the 内圈 or 外圈半径.
  • Going prone 降低 爆炸伤害 by 90% only in the 外圈半径 as well as being in ragdoll state.
  • 内圈半径 will bypass 障碍 and obstructions, while 外圈半径 will only let 冲击波 and 硬直 效果 through 障碍.

爆炸 Verification Mode (ExVM)

Explosions use raycasts; ExVM dictates whether these raycasts 要求 Line of Sight (LoS) or not. ExVM is part-dependent on 敌人. Note: 多个 limbs with ExVM: 外圈半径 or 无 can have explosions "clip" through hitboxes as the raycast can ignore the LoS 要求.

  • 所有: 内圈 & 外圈半径 要求 LoS.
  • 外半径: 内圈半径 ignores LoS, 外圈半径 requires LoS.
  • 无: 内圈 & 外圈半径 both ignore LoS.

护甲穿透 & 护甲 Values

Every part of every 敌人 has a specific 护甲 Value, with a 射程 of 0 to 10. This number indicates how 有护甲 that part is, and how much 护甲穿透 or AP a 武器 needs to be to deal 伤害 to it.

  • 护甲穿透 is abbreviated as AP0-10.
  • 护甲 Values are abbreviated as 护甲值0-10.

Similar to 敌人, every weapon has a set of 护甲穿透 Values that 表明 how much 护甲 the weapon can pierce.

  • If the 护甲穿透 Value is 大于 the enemy part's 护甲 Value, the weapon deals 100% 伤害 (Red Hitmarker)
  • If the 护甲穿透 Value is 等于 the enemy part's 护甲 Value, the weapon deals 65% 伤害 (White Hitmarker)
  • If the 护甲穿透 Value is 小于 the enemy part's 护甲 Value, the weapon deals 否 伤害 (Ricochet)

Each weapon has a total of four 穿透 values:

  • One 穿透 value for a 正面 hit, looking straight at the enemy part with (almost) 否 angle of 攻击: less than 25°.
  • One 穿透 value for hits at a 小角度: 26 to 60°
  • One 穿透 value for hits at a 大角度: 61 to 80°
  • One 穿透 value for hits at an 极难 value: 81 to 90°

The first three 穿透 values are often identical, resulting in a 基本信息 穿透 value and a bouncing angle.

For explosions, only one 护甲穿透 value is used. It 减少 by 1 in the 外圈半径.

Overpenetration (Cleave)

Overpenetration is when a 投射物 pierces 多个 parts or 敌人. Overpenetration occurs when the 护甲穿透 of the 投射物 exceeds the 护甲 value of the struck part by 2 or more. With each hitbox cleaved, the 投射物 has its 护甲穿透 已降低 by 1.

Some 敌人 sport breakable 护甲, overpenetration of which requires a 投射物 with at least three times the 护甲穿透 as the 护甲 value of the struck part.

  • 示例 1: The M-105 盟友 cleaving through a horde of 无票者 as it has  轻型 护甲穿透 vs  无护甲 护甲。
  • 示例 2: The APW-1 反器材步枪 can cleave through the butt of a 强袭虫 and hit its 内圈 flesh as its 护甲穿透 is three times 更大 than the butt, but with 1 additional AP as it loses 1 AP after cleaving the butt.

穿透减速

  • 穿透减速 is the 百分比 of 速率 lost when a 投射物 cleaves through an enemy's 伤害 zone.
    • 示例: A 投射物 has a 速率 of 500m/s with 否 速率 loss and 25% 穿透减速. That means 25% of 速率 is lost, so the 投射物 will exit the 目标 at a rate of 速度 of 375m/s.

Weapon 伤害

Each weapon, 战略配备, etc. has two different 伤害 values assigned to them

  • 标准伤害 is the normal 伤害 the weapon does to anything it shoots.
  • 耐久伤害, is the 伤害 done to 耐久 enemy parts[2] and large volume bodies[3], both of which have a 耐久度 更大 than zero.

Ballistics

  • Caliber: Drastically 增加 伤害 fall-off.
  • 速度: The bullet's initial magnitude to its 速率, higher 速度 增加 伤害 fall-off slightly.
  • 质量: 降低 伤害 falloff.
  • Drag: 增加 伤害 falloff.
  • 重力 倍率: How much the bullet drops in travel.

伤害 Fall-Off

Many 伤害 sources can suffer from reduction independently of their 目标. 伤害 fall-off is affected by Caliber, 速度, 质量, and Drag. There are two 类型; 速率-based fall-off (ballistics) and 射程-based fall-off (explosions).

  • If a 投射物 is slower than its muzzle 速率, 伤害 is lost.
  • If a 投射物 is faster than its muzzle 速率, 伤害 is gained.
    • This can be achieved via diving, jumping with the LIFT-850 喷射背包 or traveling at high speeds in the M-102 Fast 侦察 载具.
  • Projectiles lose 速率 due to 阻力系数. 伤害 增加 can occur when firing at a 目标 at a lower 高地 using, for instance, JAR-5 Dominator which has 0% Drag but is affected by 重力. Reduction in 速度 and 重力 is otherwise 典型 for most 武器 否 matter the 高地.
    • An exception to this rule are 武器 with guided projectiles. Guided projectiles lose 速度 but are programmed to 加速 to match their 初始速度, simultaneously resulting in 否 伤害 lost. These 武器 are the FAF-14 Spear, MLS-4X 突击兵, P-33 制导手枪, P-92 Warrant，以及 StA-X3 W.A.S.P. 发射器.
    • 武器 with 0% drag do not suffer 伤害 fall-off.
  • The 爆炸 from projectiles is not affected by this system, only the 投射物.

One 重要 thing to note, these 伤害 values are the 伤害 done at point-blank 射程. 伤害 减少 as the 距离 traveled 增加.

Enemy 耐久度 & 耐久伤害

Enemy parts may also have 耐久度. This is a variable between 0 and 100%.

  • A value of 0% means the enemy part is not 耐久 at all, and will take full 标准伤害
  • A value of 100% means the enemy part is 100% 耐久 and will take full 耐久伤害.
  • The ratio of 耐久度 & 标准伤害 always adds up to 100%
    • For 示例: An enemy part is 30% 耐久, therefore 70% 标准伤害 is also taken into 账户.

Each weapon has a specific value defined for the amount of 伤害 it can do to 标准 enemy parts, and a 分离 value defined for the amount of "耐久" 伤害 it can do to enemy parts. The part's 耐久度 value defines the ratio of 标准伤害 对耐久伤害 applied.

Examples of 伤害 to a 耐久 Part

Formula to calculate 伤害 vs 耐久 targets:

标准伤害 × (1 - 耐久 %) + (耐久伤害 × 耐久 %) = 造成的伤害

  • Note: Round 下 to the nearest whole number for the 造成伤害.


For this 示例, we'll be using the BR-14 审判者 which has a 标准伤害 of 95 and 耐久伤害 of 23 firing at a 30% 耐久 目标 with an 护甲 value of  轻型 with 否 伤害 fall-off.


示例: 95 × (1-30%) + (23 × 30%) = 73.4 → 73 造成伤害 to the 目标.


For this 示例, it will be assumed that the weapon used has 1,000 标准伤害 and 500 耐久伤害 with 否 伤害 fall-off. Assume a 单发 is being fired at a specific Enemy Part, and that the shot pierces the 护甲 of the enemy completely (AP > 护甲值).

  • If the enemy part has a 耐久度 value of 0 (0% 耐久), the part will take 1,000 伤害.
    • 100% of 标准伤害 = 1000
    • 0% of 耐久伤害 = 0
  • If the enemy part has a 耐久度 value of 100 (100% 耐久), the part will take 500 伤害.
    • 0% of 标准伤害 = 0
    • 100% of 耐久伤害 = 500
  • If the enemy part has a 耐久度 value of 50 (50% 耐久), the part will take 750 伤害.
    • 50% of 标准伤害 = 500
    • 50% of 耐久伤害 = 250
  • If the enemy part shot by this weapon has a 耐久度 value of 75 (75% 耐久), the part will take 625 伤害.
    • 25% of 标准伤害 = 250
    • 75% of 耐久伤害 = 375

脆皮 Enemy Parts

脆皮 parts have an 护甲 Value of 0 ( 无护甲) but have 极其 high 耐久度, high 生命值 and normally 0% 爆炸抗性 (e.g. 胆汁喷涌虫, 强袭虫 & 抚育喷涌虫 butts).

  • 示例: 强袭虫 butts are  无护甲, 950 hp and 80% 耐久.
  • This means that all 武器 can 伤害 the part, but the 伤害 done is almost entirely 相关 to the guns 耐久伤害, not its 标准伤害.
  • 武器, 战略配备, etc. with 爆炸伤害 such as the GL-21 榴弹发射器 are most 克制 脆皮 enemy parts because explosives deal 100% 耐久伤害.

爆破力 & Structures

另见: 爆破

All destructible structures in the game have a 爆破 Threshold value between 0 and 60. In order to 摧毁 most structures, the user needs to be using a weapon/战略配备 that has enough 爆破力. Depending on the 建筑, the weapon/战略配备 may need to be 爆炸 as well if it has BaDR (弹道 爆破 抗性) set to 是. Currently only spawners such as 虫洞, 机器人 Fabricators，以及 停靠的跃迁舰 have BaDR set as 是. 护甲穿透 is irrelevant to this check, as is 伤害. If the threshold is met, the 建筑 is destroyed.

Status 力量 & Thresholds

This 章节 needs 扩展包. 
You can help by expanding it.

武器 that have 状态效果 are not so simple that a single pellet can hit an enemy and proc the status 效果; they have status 力量 or "buildup" where once the threshold is met the status 效果 will have a chance to trigger on the enemy, with the 效果 guaranteed to trigger at the 最大 threshold. Status 力量 also has decay so ensure to continuously apply the "buildup" to trigger the 效果.

  • For 示例: The SG-225IE Breaker 燃烧弹 has 0.6 status 力量 per pellet and a 武士 has a 最小 trigger threshold of 2.1 & 3.3 最大 for 火焰 DoT. This means that at least 4 pellets need to hit the 武士 to have a chance to trigger the 燃烧 DoT, with the chance to trigger guaranteed with 6 pellets (since 最大 threshold is reached)

持续伤害

伤害 over time, commonly known as its 缩写, DoT is a status 效果 that deals 伤害 仅 to 主生命值. The status 效果 must be able to penetrate the 护甲 Value or 护甲值 of the enemy in order to deal 伤害. DoT is also 主题 to the 伤害减免 from 护甲穿透 being identical to the 护甲 value.

  • For 示例: 火焰 DoT which is  重型 护甲穿透 deals 35% less 伤害 to a 强袭虫's 主生命值 which is  重型 护甲。
    • 火焰 does 100 dmg/s × 1.5 = 150 dmg/s × 0.65 = 97.5 → 97 dmg/s.
  • Sources of DoT are 火焰, 燃烧 重型, LAS-17 Double-Edge Sickle Burn, 铝热弹, 毒气, 酸, Thorn Bushes and Barbed Wire and 流血 (绝地潜兵 only)

Elemental Multipliers

Elemental 伤害 and 状态效果 operate via 伤害 multipliers, the most common being 火焰, though there are few 敌人 with 弧度 and 毒气 status 效果 伤害 multipliers that are also affected. Such as Fleshmobs receiving a 1.8x 电弧伤害 倍率 or 绝地潜兵 with a 1.3x 毒气伤害 倍率. These multipliers operate are based on enemy size; the larger the enemy the higher the 倍率 and vice versa, with 更小 敌人 having a 更小 倍率.

  • 火焰伤害倍率 examples: 武斗虫 have a 0.8x 倍率, Brood 指挥官 have a 1x 倍率, Chargers have a 1.5x 倍率, and Bile Titans have a 1.7x 倍率,.
  • Robotic and large 敌人 have higher status thresholds and take longer to ignite than 有机.
