机器人 MG 阵地s are 武器 often found at 机器人 structures, such as 机器人 哨站. Broken up into 2 distinct variants, being the 轻型 Fusion Cycler and 重型 Fusion Repeater. The single barreled Fusion Cycler has a high 射速, while the double barreled 重型 Fusion Repeater version has a lower 射速, but shoots through both barrels at the same time.

Both also have metal gunshields, protecting the user from incoming 火焰. 绝地潜兵 must have precise aim or use explosives if they wish to defeat the enemy behind it. When no 敌人 are using it, 绝地潜兵 can 控制 either 机器人 MG 阵地 similarly to the 重机枪阵地.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • Both the single-barreled and double-barreled variants of the 机器人 MG 阵地 can very quickly kill an unaware 绝地潜兵.
    • Because of this, it is recommended to note any manned MG Emplacements before assaulting the area.
  • If it is 不可能 to defeat the MG 阵地 itself, a 爆头 towards the 机器人 manning it can render it 无用.
  • 绝地潜兵 are only recommended to use the MG 阵地 when there is no other option, due to its 低伤害 and lack of 护甲 穿刺 rounds.
  • If the MG 阵地 is used for too long, it will overheat and be rendered 无用 for a few moments.

已知问题[edit | edit 来源]

  • Due to a bug, the 装甲兵 can have its personal gun stuck on its back after getting off the 阵地. Attempts to shoot 绝地潜兵 result in bullets hitting the ground. It will still try to 近战 any approaching 绝地潜兵.
