描述[edit | edit 来源]

“

这颗星球处于永恒的寒冬之中。星球上冰冷的山峰隐约反射着遥不可及的恒星之光。

Icy Glaciers is an arctic 生物群系 that features thick snow coverage and lots of ice. The 浮出地面 is divided between two sections: rocky, and icy. The rocky 浮出地面 is made up of hills, rocks and rocky mountains covered with snow. The icy 浮出地面 is made up from the frozen waters of the 行星, featuring glacial mountains, rocks, up-facing icicles, and slippery ice, which is all slightly covered by snow. Ravines can also rarely be spotted on the 浮出地面.

The thick snow on the 浮出地面 slows 绝地潜兵 down slightly whenever they walk on it. The snow will also be pushed and crumpled down whenever anything walks or slides over it, allowing for the creation of "snow angels". Another 特性 exclusive to the Icy Glacier's snow is the formation of Snowballs, which will rarely form whenever looking down at the snow. They can be picked up and thrown, but deal no 伤害.

While due to the 极其 low temperatures of the Icy Glaciers, most of the water has frozen over, some liquid water can also still be found on the 浮出地面. This water is deep enough to drown a careless 绝地潜兵.

While 自然 fauna seems to be completely missing, some flora like the Frost Flower and 小型 grass still manage to grow on the 浮出地面 of these 极其 cold planets. Dead, frozen ferns can also 偶尔 be seen.

Foreign objects such as  终结族 Horde biomatter, crates, and High-Value Pickups are covered by a thin sheet of snow.

Colonies[edit | edit 来源]

Colonies on Icy Glaciers planets 特性 asphalt streets covered by patches of snow, as well as a thin sheet of snow covering everything else from crates, to rooftops, to corpses.

Metropolises[edit | edit 来源]

In parks located in Metropolises, the usual snowy 地形 can be found. Although the 生物群系 seemingly has no trees, pine trees will still generate, along with Frost Flowers. Thin snow covers almost every 浮出地面, including cars, streets, and enemy installations.

Megafactories[edit | edit 来源]

In the exterior of Megafactories, the snowy 地形 will be almost completely flat, with 小型 hills and dunes. External 地形 also still features large ice rocks and ravines, but always lacks mountains, gigantic rocks, and water. Frost Flowers and ice spikes can still 偶尔 be found.

The same 施加 to the interior of Megafactories, with the exception of Frost Flowers and ice spikes generating. The interior also features a mix of dirt/gravel and snow for the 地形, and 有时 ice will appear in place of rocks for edges. As is the case for this 生物群系, every 浮出地面 is covered by a thin sheet of snow.
