    这颗星球处于永恒的寒冬之中。星球上冰冷的山峰隐约反射着遥不可及的恒星之光。

    Icy Glaciers is an arctic 生物群系 that features thick snow coverage and lots of ice. The 浮出地面 is divided between two sections: rocky, and icy. The rocky 浮出地面 is made up of hills, rocks and rocky mountains covered with snow. The icy 浮出地面 is made up from the frozen waters of the 行星, featuring glacial mountains, rocks, up-facing icicles, and slippery ice, which is all slightly covered by snow. Ravines can also rarely be spotted on the 浮出地面.

    The thick snow on the 浮出地面 slows 绝地潜兵 down slightly whenever they walk on it. The snow will also be pushed and crumpled down whenever anything walks or slides over it, allowing for the creation of "snow angels". Another 特性 exclusive to the Icy Glacier's snow is the formation of Snowballs, which will rarely form whenever looking down at the snow. They can be picked up and thrown, but deal 否 伤害.

    While due to the 极其 low temperatures of the Icy Glaciers, most of the water has frozen over, some liquid water can also still be found on the 浮出地面. This water is deep enough to drown a careless 绝地潜兵.

    While 自然 fauna seems to be completely missing, some flora like the Frost Flower and 小型 grass still manage to grow on the 浮出地面 of these 极其 cold planets. Dead, frozen ferns can also 偶尔 be seen.

    Foreign objects such as  终结族 Horde biomatter, crates, and High-Value Pickups are covered by a thin sheet of snow.

    在……的外部。 巨型工厂, the snowy 地形 will be almost completely flat, with 小型 hills and dunes. External 地形 also still features large ice rocks and ravines, but always lacks mountains, gigantic rocks, and water. Frost Flowers and ice spikes can still 偶尔 be found.

The interior also features a mix of dirt/gravel and snow for the 地形, and 有时 ice will appear in place of rocks for edges. As is the case for this 生物群系, every 浮出地面 is covered by a thin sheet of snow.

    • 暴风雪 – Intense 暴风雪 reduce mobility and moderately reduce visibility for both enemy and friendly units.

    环境危害

    • Frost Flowers will 爆炸 into a mist that slows any 绝地潜兵 or Enemy inside if damaged.
    • Slippery Ice 降低 the friction of the ground, negatively impacting driving while on the ice.
    • Water deep enough to drown a careless 绝地潜兵.

    天气

    • 清楚
      • Clear 天气 is the base state of the Icy Glaciers. The skies are lightly covered by thin veil-like clouds, and features little fog. 轻型 wind and 轻型 snowfall also occurs passively during clear 天气..

    • Snowstorm
      • Snowstorm是天气 效果 where 重型 snowfall and wind begins to occur while fog quickly begins to set in, dropping the visibility dramatically.
      • Snowstorms both negatively and positively affect 绝地潜兵 by decreasing walking and running speeds, while also significantly decreasing enemy detection 射程.
