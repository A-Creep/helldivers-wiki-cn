Icy Glaciers is an 极地 生物群系 that features 厚 雪 coverage and lots of 冰. The 浮出地面 is divided between two sections: rocky, and icy. The rocky 浮出地面 is made up of hills, rocks and rocky mountains covered with 雪. Ravines can also 很少 be spotted on the 浮出地面.

The 厚 雪 on the 浮出地面 slows 绝地潜兵 down slightly whenever they walk on it. The 雪 will also be pushed and crumpled down whenever anything walks or slides over it, allowing for the creation of "雪 angels". Another 特性 exclusive to the Icy Glacier's 雪 is the formation of Snowballs, which will 很少 形成 whenever looking down at the 雪. They can be picked up and thrown, but deal 否 伤害.

While due to the 极其 低 temperatures of the Icy Glaciers, 最多 of the 水 has 冰冻 over, 一些 liquid 水 can also still be found on the 浮出地面. This 水 is 深 enough to drown a careless 绝地潜兵.

While 自然 动物群 seems to be completely missing, 一些 植物群 点赞 the 霜冻 Flower and 小 grass still manage to grow on the 浮出地面 of these 极其 寒冷 planets. Dead, 冰冻 ferns can also 偶尔 be seen.

Foreign objects such as biomatter, crates, and 高-数值 Pickups are covered by a 薄 sheet of 雪.
