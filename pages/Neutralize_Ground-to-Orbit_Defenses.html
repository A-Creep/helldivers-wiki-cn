目标 Steps[edit | edit 来源]

标准机器人 Megafactory

“

轨道加农炮就位于此处。潜入此地点，使用地狱火炸弹将其摧毁。

— 目标 概述

“

此处部署有反轨道加农炮。

— 目标 概述

  • Call down and defend a Hellbomb until it explodes to 摧毁 the 轨道加农炮.
    • Only Hellbombs, B-100 Portable Hellbombs 和 SEAF 大炮: Mini-Nuke shells can 摧毁 the 轨道加农炮.

战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • One configuration of the 轨道加农炮 is defended by three ground-level Cannon 炮塔.
  • 几个 机器人 Fabricators also surround each 轨道加农炮.
  • The outpost is moderately defended 亲戚 to the 难度 level with a mix of 装甲兵, 蹂躏者, 巨型者，以及 侦察纵步者. In addition, all 轨道加农炮 are surrounded with 机器人 MG 炮台.
  • 绝地潜兵 should be advised of the 燃烧 pit below the bridge in one configuration of the 轨道加农炮, as it is an 瞬间 death to fall into it.

Configurations Of Note[edit | edit 来源]

  • 目标 with 1 of the 3 cannon 炮塔 shown. This is the most 危险 one, as it is close to an open 章节 of wall the player walks by inside the area to plant a hellbomb
  • 目标 with a bridge and 燃烧 pit below. There is a narrow path obscured by the rock on the left of the image that 玩家 may walk across to bypass the bridge.
