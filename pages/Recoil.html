Weapon 后坐力

In 绝地潜兵 2, most 武器 have 后坐力 - the kick received by the crosshair with each shot. With each shot, the crosshair and player camera are kicked upwards; additionally, the crosshair is randomly kicked to the left or right.

Stance 后坐力 Modifiers

Weapon 后坐力 is modified by your stance, the latest modifiers have been made public via this blogpost.

The 后坐力 modifiers for shooting from the hip - without aiming at all - are 未知, but it's clear the 后坐力 is significantly 已增加.

It's currently unclear if - some? - 武器 have their own 后坐力 modifiers for shooting in third and first person, but it does feel like some 武器 have less 后坐力 in first person.

护甲被动

工程包 和 强化 reduce weapon 后坐力 by 30%. The in-game 描述 incorrectly states that the 后坐力 reduction is applied only while crouched or prone. In actuality, the 后坐力 reduction is applied across all stances.
