破坏 Air Base is a 任务 that can appear on 机器人-controlled planets. It tasks 绝地潜兵 with destroying one or more parked Dropships and, on 困难 and above, destroying an Air Base 控制 Tower. This is reflected in the 任务’s icon, overriding the 运输舰 icon if the Air Base 控制 Tower is present.

Starting at 自杀任务, 绝地潜兵 are 必需 to 摧毁 two Air Base 控制 Towers and one group of Dropships, on 绝地潜兵难度, this is 已增加 to two air bases and two groups of Dropships.

  • However, this does not apply to 城市 missions. In Cities, 自杀任务 and above will 要求 one Air Base 控制 Tower and two groups of Dropships to be destroyed.

任务 Walkthrough[edit | edit 来源]

摧毁航空基地控制塔[edit | edit 来源]

标准机器人 Megafactory

“

All 运输舰 通讯 is routed through this 控制 Tower. Destroying it will disable any 远距离 运输舰 deployments.

— 目标 概述

“

该控制塔是敌方在此区域空中作战的指挥中枢。

— Megafactory 目标 概述, Cyberstan 任务

  • 定位并解锁系统访问端口
    • 绝地潜兵 must find and interact with a SSSD 容器 found inside the base
  • Use 战略配备: SSSD 运送
    • 绝地潜兵 must call down the SSSD by inputting:

  • 收集 the SSSD 硬盘
  • 将超级固态硬盘置入插槽
    • The receptacle can either be found 下一个 to the 终端 present in the air base, or 下一个 to the Air Base 控制 Tower itself.
  • Hack into the 终端 to 破坏 控制 Tower
    • 绝地潜兵 must find and interact with the 终端 found inside the base
  • 绕过终端安全系统
    • 绝地潜兵 are presented with a puzzle, requiring them to match 机器人 letters into the 正确 order, shown by the key on the right side of the 终端.

  • 使用终端破坏控制塔
    • 绝地潜兵 are 必需 to activate three different switches, similar to a SEAF SAM Site
  • 使用终端完成破坏
    • 绝地潜兵 are 必需 to press a single switch, doing so will start a 10 second 倒计时 after which the Air Base 控制 Tower will 爆炸 and the 目标 will be completed.
    • The 爆炸 is similar to that of a NUX-223 Hellbomb, so 绝地潜兵 are advised to stay away from the Air Base 控制 Tower during the 倒计时 顺序.

Disclaimer: Each highlighted part with a count has its own 生命值. Parts without a count share 生命值 pools.

1) Parts with their HP listed as "Main" do not have their own individual 生命值. All 造成伤害 to these parts is transferred to the enemy's 主生命值 pool.
2) Refer to 护甲 values and enemy 耐久度 as defined in 伤害 for more information.
3) 造成伤害 to a part will also be transferred in part or in full to 主生命值. If set to 0%, no 伤害 is transferred.
4) If set to "Yes", 伤害 transferred 对主血量 is capped by the combined 生命值 and 构造 of the part.
5) 构造 acts as a secondary 生命池 that, once Main or limb 生命值 has been depleted, may decay. The first number is the total 构造 and the second is the 衰退速率. If set to 0/s there is no decay and the 构造 behaves as extra 生命值.
6) ExDR means 爆炸 伤害抗性, and can 射程 from 0 to 100%. If set to 100%, the limb cannot take 爆炸伤害; The 爆炸伤害 will instead 目标 Main. Refer to 伤害 for more information.
  • Note: This 机械师, known as Affected By 爆炸 can only occur once per 爆炸, regardless of 100% ExDR parts hit, and the 爆炸's 护甲穿透 must match or exceed 主体护甲 to deal 伤害 对主血量. However, if the same 爆炸 has already damaged Main, this 机械师 will not trigger.


战术信息[edit | edit 来源]

  • The Dropships in the "摧毁 Dropships" 目标 are not the same as their flying counterparts as they have significantly less 生命值 at 1,000 vs 3,500, and the thrusters are not 分离 parts of the fuselage.
  • Eagle 战略配备 例如 Eagle 500kg Bomb, “飞鹰”空袭，以及 “飞鹰”机枪扫射 can 摧毁 多个 dropships.
  • Any Weapon/战略配备 with  反坦克 I 护甲穿透, preferably with high 耐久伤害 such as the PLAS-45 Epoch or LAS-99 类星体加农炮, can 摧毁 the grounded Dropships.
    • 1 shot with any 反坦克.
    • 1 shot from the GP-20 Ultimatum or 2 from the P-33 制导手枪
    • 1 G-123 铝热弹 or G-48 Giga 手榴弹, or 2 TM-1 Lure Mine
    • 2 fully-charged shots with the PLAS-45 Epoch.
    • 3-4 99% charged shots with the RS-422 磁轨炮.
  • Conventional 攻击 cannot 摧毁 the Air Base 控制 Tower. You must call down the 目标 装备, insert it, and engage the 终端 to self-destruct the tower.
