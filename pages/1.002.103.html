概述

Hello everyone! It’s been an “interesting” week with a 补丁 that had a few unexpected slip-ups. After testing and balancing, the wrong versions of some files for our new 战争债券 items were shipped, which wasn’t part of the plan. Misaligned scopes also made an unwelcome return, albeit in a new form - some bugs really are persistent, aren’t they? (Silently stares off into the 距离…) So, we even double-checked the scale of the LAS-16 Sickle, just to be sure. On to the key points for this 补丁:

  • The LAS-17 Double-Edge Sickle was meant to start with 轻型护甲穿透 and build up to 中型 as more heat built up, but it launched with 中型护甲穿透 right away, even with zero heat.


  • The GP-31 Ultimatum also gained extra 弹药 from the previously-a-bug-but-now-a-特性 Siege Ready 护甲 被动 and we feel that this specific combination is too strong.
  • We’re huge fans of big booms booming big (to 引文 our fabulous Design 导演) so we didn’t want to make the weapon less satisfying to use - we still want it to bring democratic tears to your eyes every time you use it, like it does for us. However, we will be addressing how 容易 it is to access extra ammunition for it, ensuring it requires a bit more effort to use it to the full 效果.


It’s not our intent to release 战争债券 items that need immediate balancing, and we understand that any 变更 we make can evoke strong feelings. We want to assure you that we’re actively listening to your feedback and, as with any of our 上一个 updates, we’ll keep monitoring the situation closely. Your input is invaluable, so please 继续 to share your thoughts on these 变更. We’re always open to making further adjustments if needed!
