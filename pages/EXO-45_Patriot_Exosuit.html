战术信息

爱国者 Specific

  • The 爱国者's 迷你机枪 is a 致命 weapon against 无护甲 or lightly 有护甲 敌人 such as 追猎虫 or Troopers, but can also be used to 伤害 致命 components of 敌人 such as the 通风口 on the back of an Annihilator Tank's turret. The 迷你机枪 has a short wind-up time, but also has a short wind-down time that can be used to conserve ammunition and keep the 迷你机枪 spinning while changing targets rapidly.
  • Despite the 初始速度 being 标注为 200m/s, the rocket 实际上 flies at 70m/s. This is the same flight 速度 as the MLS-4X 突击兵's missiles.
  • It is ill-advised to use the 爱国者's rockets against bug nests and bot fabricators due to their 有限 ammunition pool. It is better utilized to kill 重型 敌人, like Chargers. The rockets also suffer from 伤害 fall-off, 含义 that at longer ranges, certain breakpoints like 强袭虫 heads will not be met.
    • One well-placed shot to the head can kill a 强袭虫, except the Behemoth 变体, which requires two shots. Another tactic to conserve rockets against Behemoth 强袭虫 is to use one rocket to strip the 护甲 off a leg, then use the 迷你机枪 to 摧毁 the leg flesh or to hit the butt.
    • Two shots to the head will kill a 吐酸泰坦.
    • Two shots to the head or three shots to a foot of a 移动工厂 will kill it.
    • A single well-placed shot to the 暴露 head will kill an 穿刺虫.
  • It is possible to conserve 弹药 for both the 火箭发射器 and 迷你机枪 when fighting 小型 敌人 by stomping them with the 爱国者's legs.

基本信息 外骨骼机甲

  • The hull of the 爱国者 is quite 耐久 against 食腐虫 和 武士 近战 攻击, as well as 小型 arms 火焰 from the 机器人.
  • The rotational mobility of the 外骨骼机甲 is 有限. Don't expect to quickly spin around to kill flanking 敌人.
  • 外骨骼机甲 are highly 抵抗 to 火焰-based 武器 and hazards, such as 火焰 Tornadoes.
  • Other hazards such as Tremors, Ice Plants, Shrubs, and 爆炸 Cones can be ignored or trampled. Barriers such as trees, 轻型 fencing, and certain rocks can also be cleared away when advancing with the 爱国者.
  • When boarding the 爱国者, the 玩家 regain 耐力 and can Stim while inside the 外骨骼机甲.
  • It is possible to reach slightly 更高处 if dismounting from the 爱国者 by positioning the back of the 外骨骼机甲 下一个 to a reachable raised platform, such as buildings found near Minor Places of Interests or certain raised rock formations.
  • Equipping a SH-32 防护罩生成包 while inside the 爱国者 allows the 屏障 to block certain 攻击 aimed at the 外骨骼机甲 from the 正面. 攻击 that aim at the waist and below or behind will still deal 伤害 to the 外骨骼机甲.
  • The 近战 stomp 攻击 is capable of opening Cargo Containers found at 兴趣点.
  • Due to the long 冷却 period of 外骨骼机甲, it is advised to bring them to DSS-occupied planets as it 降低 the 冷却 by 35%.
  • 绝地潜兵 can take only one 外骨骼机甲 during 战略配备 selection stage. The exception of having more than one 外骨骼机甲 is during specific Galactic Event.

琐事

  • the EXO-45 爱国者 外骨骼机甲 was first added to the game at Minor Places of Interests on Tien Kwan and was permanently added upon the completion of the "Liberate Tien Kwan" 重要指令 on March 24th.
  • the EXO-45 爱国者 外骨骼机甲 appears to be a revamped EXO-44 Stomper 外骨骼机甲 from 绝地潜兵1.
  • When originally revealed in the Report for Duty trailer, the EXO-45 爱国者 外骨骼机甲 was called EXO-44 Walker 外骨骼机甲 MK3.
  • 绝地潜兵 请参阅 the 外骨骼机甲 as "Walkers" when called in and pinged.
  • Wrecked 外骨骼机甲 can be seen at various Minor Places of Interests and in certain parts of the game's world ever since the game's launch (as can wrecked M-102 Gunner FRVs), though their models differ somewhat from the 爱国者; notably, the 火箭巢 has fewer tubes.
