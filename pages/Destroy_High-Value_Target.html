霸王虫[edit | edit 来源]

  • The 霸王虫 has the largest 主生命值 pool in the game by a significant margin, at 150,000. But all body parts are 0% 耐久, so it takes full 标准伤害 from all sources.
    • Due to its 极其 large 生命池 and wide sweeping 致命 攻击, it is advised that the entire squad either avoids the 霸王虫 entirely or 坐标 to take it down.
  • The 霸王虫's body is completely encased in  Tank I 护甲, with the exception being  Tank II 护甲 on the head and it's  轻型 有护甲 open mouth.
    • Its 身体护甲 can be broken with sustained 反坦克 火焰, revealing the  轻型-有护甲 flesh beneath. When a plate of 护甲 is close to being destroyed, it will visibly show cracks and breaks.
      • The inner flesh is completely immune to 爆炸伤害, so 爆炸武器 like the MS-11 Solo Silo should be avoided.
    • Most of the toughness of the 霸王虫's plate 护甲 comes from its 庞大, non-decaying bleedout pool. It behaves as extra 生命值, for a total of 50,000 有效 生命值 per 章节.
  • 绝地潜兵 who wish to 击倒 a 霸王虫 should consider bringing as much  反坦克 I firepower as possible in the form of 战略配备 和 支援武器.
    • “飞鹰”机枪扫射 和 轨道加特林火力网 are both highly 克制 the 霸王虫, as they can 伤害 多个 spinal 护甲 plates due to the 霸王虫's 庞大 size.
    • the GR-8 无后坐力炮, FAF-14 Spear，以及 EAT-17 消耗性反坦克武器 allow for focusing on breaking 护甲 plates between 轨道武器 and Eagle cooldowns.
    • the EXO-45 爱国者 外骨骼机甲 is one of the most 有效 tools against a 霸王虫. Its 导弹 arm can help strip an 护甲 plate with rockets, dealing up to a total of 17,500 伤害 to an 护甲 plate, allowing its 加特林 arm to quickly inflict 庞大 伤害 upon the stripped 章节. With a full 弹匣, the chaingun can deal up to 121,500 伤害 in this manner.
      • However, due to their 有限 mobility and large frames, 外骨骼机甲 are 易受 the 霸王虫's large 攻击.
    • the Bastion is likewise 克制 a 霸王虫 due to its high HP pool, allowing it to withstand 多个 bile 攻击, and its large cannon and HMG 弹药 reserves can 摧毁 a plate and deal enough 伤害 to 干掉 the 霸王虫 with ease. However, the driver must maneuver the tank away from 钻地中 Hive Lords to prevent the 霸王虫 from flipping the tank.
    • the B-100 Portable Hellbomb can inflict an 极难 amount of 伤害 if 绝地潜兵 can position it correctly. The best time to use it is 通常 when the 霸王虫 attempts to spew bile, as this 攻击 does not hit at point-blank 射程 and keeps it in place long enough for the Hellbomb's 倒计时 to finish.
  • Once the 护甲 is broken, 高每秒伤害 武器 can be used to shred its 主生命值 pool by firing into the 暴露  轻型 有护甲 flesh. 武器 such as the AR-61 Tenderizer, M-105 盟友, MGX-42 Bullet Storm，以及 M-1000 Maxigun perform well at this.
    • the LIFT-850 喷射背包 or LIFT-182 Warp Pack can be used to avoid the 霸王虫's 攻击. 轻型护甲 and the 试验性注射剂 增益 further 增加 移动速度 on foot, and combining these options together can 改进 odds of survival.
  • The 霸王虫's movements will cause tremors in the ground that slow all nearby units. Close proximity should be avoided when considering the presence of other 终结族
    • The 霸王虫 is 免疫 to all 效果 that would 眩晕弹 or 困惑 it, whilst still being prone to direct 毒气伤害 and ignition by 火焰.
  • When engaging or withdrawing from the 霸王虫, 绝地潜兵 should 维护 距离 from one another. The size of the 霸王虫's 攻击 makes it 危险 for them to remain close to each other, as one strike could potentially kill all of them.
  • Most of the 造成伤害 by the bile of a 霸王虫 is 爆炸, so bringing 护甲 with the 加固 被动 can help save 绝地潜兵 from these 攻击 or even make their 伤害 negligible.
