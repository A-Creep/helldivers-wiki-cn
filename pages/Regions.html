概述

Regions are unlock-able 操作 area(s) of a 行星 that offer unique 解放 options to 绝地潜兵. Their 机制 are largely independent from the overall 行星's progress, but provide a 庞大, one-time 解放 boost once they are successfully liberated. Depending on the 行星's 百分比 progress, a Region may be unlocked to 绝地潜兵.

超级地球-controlled Regions utilize the Metropolis 生物群系, regardless of their classification. This 生物群系 does offer unique 玩法 opportunities, ranging from unique missions, to to a large 解放 boost to the 行星. Mega Cities have unique 操作 Modifiers, with Civilians and SEAF in Area being active, regardless of 难度. These modifiers can also extend to 敌人, as they may include unique 敌人/subfactions and 已增加 enemy 类型.

Regions are divided into four different 类型 with varying HP amounts:

  1. 定居点 (★): 100k HP
  2. Town (★★): 200k HP
  3. City (★★★): 400k HP
  4. Mega City (★★★★): 600k HP

NOTE: 机器人 Megafactories are 机密 differently, but 功能 identically to their 超级地球 equivalents.

超级地球's Regions are more noteworthy since they serve as the capitals of each of the 行星's 星区.

Mega City 1 - Port Mercy

“

自终极大战中庇护数百万忠诚难民起，仁慈港便成为所有A级公民危难时的安全港湾。

— City 操作 描述 on Port Mercy

Port Mercy serves as the 首都 of 分区 1.

It was the 6th Mega City to be attacked and 4th 落入……之手 光能者 invaders of The Great Host 在以下期间 First Battle for 超级地球.

Mega City 2 - 平等-On-Sea

“

Named for the 历史 conference where 人类 was found to be the most Equal species in the Galaxy.

— City 操作 描述 on 平等-On-Sea

平等-On-Sea serves as the 首都 of 分区 2.

It was the 第3 Mega City attacked by the 光能者 during the First Battle for 超级地球.

Due to a 庞大 community-driven effort by Chinese 玩家, 平等-On-Sea was one of two cities left standing at the end of the First Battle for 超级地球. This is despite it being amongst the first to be invaded.

Mega City 3 - Prosperity City

“

我们引以为傲的首都既是民主委员会的所在地，也是进步、生产力和爱国主义的璀璨灯塔。

— City 操作 描述 on Prosperity City

Prosperity City serves as the 首都 of 分区 3.

It was the 7th Mega City attacked by the 光能者 during the First Battle for 超级地球.

It is the federal 首都 of the 联邦 of 超级地球. It is one of the two Mega Cities that were not destroyed by forces of The Great Host during the First Battle for 超级地球.

Mega City 4 - Administrative Center 02

“

行政中心神圣大厅经手的表格和申请均印有“时代印章”，应当摆放在家中显赫位置。

— City 操作 描述 on Administrative Center 02

Administrative Center 02 serves as the 首都 of 分区 4.

It was the 4th Mega City to be attacked and 第2 to fall to the 光能者 during the First Battle for 超级地球.

Mega City 5 - Eagleopolis

“

这座伟大商贸中枢因宏伟的购物商场和繁华的娱乐中心而闻名整个银河系。

— City 操作 描述 on Eagleopolis

Eagleopolis serves as the 首都 of 分区 5.

It was the 第1 Mega City to be attacked and fall to the 光能者 during the First Battle for 超级地球.

Mega City 6 - Gun

“

Risen from the ashes of an 古代 city, Rememberance (sic) is a 纪念碑 to the Perseverance of Humankind.

— City 操作 描述 on Remembrance before its renaming to Gun

Gun serves as the 首都 of 分区 6.

It was the 第2 Mega City to be attacked and 第3 to fall to the 光能者 during the First Battle for 超级地球.

Initially named as 缅城, it was later renamed to 枪 via 绝地潜兵 vote as its reconstruction began.

  • A poll was held in the 官员 绝地潜兵 Discord Server to decide the new name.
    • The available choices were;
      • New Remembrance
      • Everlasting Memory
      • Peacebeacon's Bloodlight
      • Winds of Change of Tides of War
      • 枪
    • 枪 was selected as the new name for the destroyed 首都 by 72.54% of the votes. [1]

It is the first 超级地球 Mega City to be rebuilt following the Battle for 超级地球.

Mega City 7 - New York Supreme

“

约克大纷争结束后，一座城市力压群雄，誓要统御其他约克市，终结彼此间的流血纷争。

— City 操作 描述 on York Supreme

New York Supreme serves as the 首都 of 分区 7.

It was the 5th Mega City to be attacked and fall to the 光能者 during the First Battle for 超级地球.

Initially named as 棒约克, it was later renamed to New York Supreme via 绝地潜兵 vote as its reconstruction began.

  • A poll was held in the 官员 绝地潜兵 Discord Server to decide the new name.[2]
    • The available choices were;
      • New York Supreme (accumulated 62,674 votes)
      • York Undying(accumulated 2,449 votes)
      • 复仇(accumulated 1,973 votes)
      • Libertasium, Torch to the Stars(accumulated 3,362 votes)
      • Bernard(accumulated 53,480 votes)
    • New York Supreme was selected as the new name for the destroyed 首都 by 50.57% of the votes. [3]

It is the second 超级地球 Mega City to be rebuilt following the Battle for 超级地球.

琐事

  • Each 超级地球 Mega City seems to be based on the 位置 of real-world cities:
    • Port Mercy seems to be located in Southeast Asian archipelago, which contains major cities such as Singapore and Jakarta.
    • 平等-On-Sea seems to be located in Shanghai, China.
    • Prosperity City seems to be located in Stockholm, Sweden.
    • Administrative Center 02 seems to be located in Brussels, Belgium.
    • Eagleopolis seems to be located in the southeast region of the Mediterranean Sea, which contain locations such as Cairo, Jerusalem, and the West Bank.
    • Gun (formerly Remembrance) seems to be located in Buenos Aires, Argentina.
    • New York Supreme (formerly York Supreme), as the name suggests, seems to originate New York, and built on the American East Coast.
  • The Mega City Gun (formerly Remembrance) may be a 参考 to the movie, 星河战队.
    • In the movie, an 攻击 on Buenos Aires is used to justify the war the movie follows.
    • It is located near Buenos Aires and is said to have been rebuilt from a destroyed city.
  • The 质量 防御 of 平等-On-Sea lead to some controversy among Chinese 玩家, who due to a 翻译 error leading them to believe that the 光能者 campaign on the Mega City would be end once it was 100% liberated.
  • Mega Cities categories can be identifiable by the number of stars above their icon:
    • Settlements - one star,
    • Towns - two stars,
    • Cities - three stars,
    • Mega Cities - four stars.
  • There are 大约 164 Megacities spanning throughout the entire 联邦 of 超级地球.
  • During the 生化斯坦之战, 机器人 Megafactories served as Regions that the 绝地潜兵 were tasked with liberating and destroying to approach the main Megafactory, Transcendence.
    • Cyberstan could only be attacked through their Megafactories, with each one unlocking more Megafactories for 绝地潜兵 to dive on. This 攻击 涂装 is similar to the Battle of 超级地球, when the 光能者 attacked 超级地球, and 绝地潜兵 could only dive on the seven Mega Cities.
