爆破力与结构

All destructible structures in- game have a 爆破 Threshold between 10 and 60.

In order to 摧毁 a 建筑, the user needs to be using a weapon/战略配备 that has enough 爆破力. Explosions have 爆破力 fall-off in the 外圈半径. 护甲穿透 is irrelevant to this check, as is 伤害, and whether or not the 攻击 is 爆炸. When the threshold is met, the 建筑 will be destroyed.

The exceptions to this are enemy spawners; 虫洞, 机器人构筑者, 巨型构筑者 和 停靠的跃迁舰, which 要求 a 最小 爆破力 of 20 and the 爆炸 property to 摧毁 via their open hole, 通风口 and door; as they have Ballistic Demolition Resistance, or BaDR. 弹道 爆破 抗性 means that the 建筑(s) resists 爆破力 from non-爆炸 projectiles such as bullets or 电 arcs. For 示例, an E/MG-101 重机枪部署支架 which has a 爆破力 of 20, but is not 爆炸. Therefore, it will not 摧毁 the 建筑.

Note: Certain structures such as 孢子喷涌虫 和 尖啸虫巢穴, are not labeled as structures that 要求 爆破力 to 摧毁. These entities are considered non-moving enemy units with low 护甲 Value, high HP, and 耐久度. The only exceptions to this are 机器人 Fabricators, Bulk Fabricators and Grounded Warp Ships, which use both 生命值, 护甲, and 爆破力 systems.
