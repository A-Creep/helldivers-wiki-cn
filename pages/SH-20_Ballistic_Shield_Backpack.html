功能

To use the 防弹盾牌, the player must equip a weapon with the 单手操作 trait, such as the CB-9 Exploding Crossbow, all submachine guns except the SMG-32 Reprimand and any of the 副武器. When using a compatible weapon, the 防弹盾牌 will expand and player will hold it in their left hand. Aiming the weapon will cause the player to raise the shield directly in 正面 of themselves, granting forward coverage that protects most of the player's body.

When the shield is equipped, the weapon's sights are unusable and first-person aim is a view through the shield's viewport, requiring the player to aim using the weapon's laser pointer (if present) or otherwise eyeballing it.

Equipping or switching to a two-handed weapon, picking up a two-handed carriable object such as a SEAF 大炮 shell, interacting with two-handed 目标 such as the cranks on the 雷达站 or E-710 Pumps, or picking up an 目标 that requires 运输 in the off-hand such as an SSSD 硬盘, will 折叠 and stow the shield on the player's back.

The 防弹盾牌 blocks projectiles at all times, including when stowed on the back or even left on the ground.

战术信息

  • Crouch to minimize exposure to enemy 火焰. While prone, however, the shield is held nearly flat off to the 侧面, severely limiting its effectiveness.
  • The in-game 描述 is slightly misleading, as the shield will block 任何 enemy 火焰, including the 侦察纵步者's guns, 碎裂坦克 quad laser repeaters, 巨型炙炎者 flamethrowers, and the miniguns of a 移动工厂.
    • The shield is capable of blocking the 伤害 of some explosions if the player is in the 外圈半径 of the 爆炸.
    • 爆炸 projectiles should be dodged entirely, for while the shield will technically block 爆炸 projectiles, the 爆炸 will still 伤害 and ragdoll the player.
    • As it has 生命值 and 护甲, the 防弹盾牌 can be eventually destroyed, at which point it will automatically 掉落 to the ground, appearing warped and broken.
  • The 防弹盾牌 cannot 保护 the wielder from 火焰, or 弧度 电击.
    • While the 防弹盾牌 does not 保护 the wielder from 火焰 on the ground it does 保护 the wielder from flamethrowers.
  • As of 补丁 1.002.101, the 防弹盾牌 can now 保护 the user from 近战 攻击 and, though undocumented, 终结族 bile.
    • The shield is capable of absorbing 近战 blows that would otherwise instantly kill 绝地潜兵 like 强袭虫 和 吐酸泰坦 stomps, or the Hulk Scorcher's buzzsaw. Be aware that these 攻击 will 摧毁 the shield with two hits or less due to their 极难 伤害.
    • While the 防弹盾牌 will block 终结族 bile, it does not 保护 the user from the area-of-效果 伤害 that accompanies it.
    • The shield offers great 保护 against 光能者 敌人, foremost the 监视者, whose 远程 and 近战 攻击 cannot penetrate it.
      • It is also capable of blocking a 猎杀器's laser. However, due to the 庞大 伤害 output of this weapon, the shield will be destroyed in roughly one second.
  • The player can switch to a two-handed weapon to deliberately stow the shield on their back, providing 保护 during retreats.
