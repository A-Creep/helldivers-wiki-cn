武斗虫
“

武斗虫 are the 自然 end result of a species optimized for mindless 扩展包. In controlled environments, they have eviscerated 无辜 baby cows in under a second. One can only imagine what they would do to 无辜 baby humans.

— 绝地潜兵 2 PlayStation Store Page

The 武士 is a 中型-sized 终结族 that is sturdier than its 更小 counterparts, suiting it better for 近距离 combat.

生成

武斗虫 can be found on all difficulties, 生成 from Bug 突破, within patrols, guarding Bug Nests and 目标, or from 虫穴. They can also be spawned in a trio by Brood 指挥官.

Starting on 挑战, they can be found guarding Minor Places of Interest.

行为

武斗虫 will simply approach 绝地潜兵, climbing over 地形 and 障碍 in an attempt to 攻击 with their claws at 近距离. They can strafe sideways, and will attempt to cut off 绝地潜兵 trying to run past them. When their head is destroyed, 武斗虫 will enter a "狂暴" state, where they gain 已增加 移动速度 but continuously 流血 out.

偶尔，当绝地潜兵超出其攻击范围时，它会钻入地下，然后突然出现并攻击绝地潜兵。

武斗虫能够向空气中释放信息素云，引发虫族破口。

战术信息

  • 武斗虫可以用任何武器杀死，但高射速或爆炸性武器可以迅速消灭成群的它们。
  • The fastest way to kill a 武士 is by destroying its head, though destroying it will cause it to enter its "狂暴" state. 绝地潜兵 can shoot the 武士 more to kill it, or they can avoid it until it fully bleeds out.
    • 摧毁它的任何一条腿都将阻止其移动速度增加。
    • Dealing enough 伤害 will cause the 武士 to 爆炸 in a shower of green goo, destroying its corpse. This can be seen when using grenades, 电弧武器, or some 战略配备.
  • 武斗虫即使没有头部也能引发Bug 突破，不过它们很可能在完全召唤之前就流血身亡。
  • 武斗虫通常很慢，但绝地潜兵应警惕它们的横移能力，这可能妨碍从其它终结族手中撤退。
