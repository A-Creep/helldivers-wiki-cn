Background[edit | edit 来源]

操作 Swift Disassembly was coming to an end. Phase III of the 操作 was successful.

绝地潜兵 liberated Maia a couple of days ago, Tibit was liberated soon after Maia. Now it was time for Durgen.

策略 and importance[edit | edit 来源]

Durgen was the last 机器人 行星. Liberating it would mean the eradication of 机器人.

战斗[edit | edit 来源]

The siege started on Apr. 7th.

The 行星 is holding a record of over 350k 绝地潜兵 on it at the same time. Battle didn't last too long, it ended a couple of hours later.

Over 37.5 Million 绝地潜兵 were killed by 机器人 on Durgen during the last hours of war against them.

Unfortunately, just 2 days after the siege succeeded, 机器人 returned with a fleet 10 times larger than the 机器人 that were eradicated after the siege.
