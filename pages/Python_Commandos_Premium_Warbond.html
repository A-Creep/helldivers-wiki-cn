概述[edit | edit 来源]

玩家 can use Medals to claim various items. These 奖励 are spread across three pages, each requiring increasingly more Medals (ranging from 2 to 110). 玩家 start on page one, unlocking subsequent pages as they spend more Medals, gradually expanding their customization options.

备注[edit | edit 来源]

There are also 蟒蛇突击兵 themed items in the Rotating 目录 在……上 超级商店.

琐事[edit | edit 来源]

  • The 营销 概述 image has the cape names switched, with are correctly named in-game.
  • the Python Commandos 尊享战争债券 appears to be a sequel, or similar theme to the 毒蛇突击兵 尊享战争债券, as both take 灵感 to US Vietnam War-era 突击兵 movies.
    • The 战争债券 appears to be an homage to the 1987 film 捕食者, in which a group of Commandos with similar 武器库 fight an extraterrestrial 武士 hunting them down.
