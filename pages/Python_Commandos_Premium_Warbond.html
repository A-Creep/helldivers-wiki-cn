蟒蛇突击兵高级战争债券

Python Commandos is a 尊享战争债券 available for 1,000 超级货币 in the 战争债券 tab of the 获取 Center.

概述

玩家 can use 奖章 to claim various items. These 奖励 are 散射 across three pages, each requiring increasingly more 奖章 (ranging from 2 to 110). 玩家 start on page one, unlocking subsequent pages as they spend more 奖章, gradually expanding their customization options.

备注

There are also Python Commandos themed items in the Rotating 目录 on the 超级商店.

琐事

  • 营销概览图中的披肩名称弄反了，而游戏内名称是正确的。
  • The Python Commandos 尊享战争债券 appears to be a sequel, or similar theme to the 毒蛇突击兵 尊享战争债券, as both take 灵感 to US Vietnam War-era 突击兵 movies.
    • The 战争债券 appears to be an homage to the 1987 film 捕食者, in which a group of Commandos with similar 武器库 fight an extraterrestrial 武士 hunting them down.
