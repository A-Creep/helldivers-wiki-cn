补丁 Highlights

难度 Level 10 - 超级绝地潜兵难度

A new, 极难 难度 level designed for the most experienced and skilled 绝地潜兵, featuring formidable new 敌人 and intense challenges.

New 任务 目标

Two new secondary 目标 will soon be active on a 行星 near you! These 阵营-specific optional 目标 will have you assaulting 机器人 human processing plants to 摧毁 their evil Bio-处理器, and venturing deep into 终结族 领土 to 撤离 with a living larva sample that the bugs will do anything to get back!

Bigger enemy 哨站

特攻 终结族 strongholds and 机器人 fortresses in the new 难度 超级绝地潜兵难度. The challenge will be great but the 奖励 even 更大. Search the conquered 哨站 thoroughly for the 机密数据 and make sure to 撤离 with it. May liberty guide your path.

New Bugs and Bots

New and enhanced threats to freedom emerge. Guess who’s back to bug you again? The 穿刺虫 returns, 钻地中 its head and popping tentacles from the ground to catch you off guard. Watch out for its 致命 limbs, but don’t miss its 弱点 when it digs in - it seems like self-preservation still isn’t its strong suit! The 穿刺虫 however is not all. Prepare for the rest of the formidable foes! The 火箭坦克 rolls in with 重型护甲 and devastating firepower. The Reinforced 侦察纵步者 now has its previously 脆弱 spot 强化 and boasts two rockets on each 侧面 for 更大 firepower! The Spore 强袭虫 fearlessly invades through the thick acidic fog, accompanied by an 阿尔法指挥官, watch out for its friends!

New swamp 行星

Prepare to dive down into an eerie and foggy version of the swamp 行星. Keep your wits about you and do not let fear take hold.

New 环境危害

酸雨风暴 - Temporarily lowers 护甲 for 敌人 and the player. Make use of this but stay on high alert 绝地潜兵.
