描述

Note: This 生物群系 does not exist internally and Cyberstan simply 使用次数 the Boneyard 生物群系, though Cyberstan itself differs from the original 生物群系.

This 生物群系 is a 变体 of the Boneyard 生物群系.

The 浮出地面 of Cyberstan is very rocky, often featuring large rock formations and valleys. The 浮出地面 is also almost completely covered with snow, and 小型 ice spikes can 偶尔 be found. 有时, patches of dirt will be 可见 through the snow, revealing brown, possibly dead grass.

Unlike the Boneyard 生物群系, there are 否 bones or otherwise any osseous structures to be found anywhere. And unlike Boneyard planets which have entire lakes and seas, Cyberstan features 无.

The skies of Cyberstan are severely polluted, thanks to the Megafactories built across the 浮出地面. The space above is still 可见 during the night, including the planets orbiting Cyberstan's local system, but they are blocked out by the thick smog most of the time.

Like the 超级地球 Metropolis 生物群系 generating Metropolises, every map will be generated with an 机器人 Megafactory covering 60-70% of the 任务 area.

Outside the Megafactories of Cyberstan, 更小 manufacturing districts are present all over the 浮出地面. Like on 超级地球, these lights emitted by the manufacturing districts are 可见 from space, though instead of yellow, the ones on Cyberstan are red.
